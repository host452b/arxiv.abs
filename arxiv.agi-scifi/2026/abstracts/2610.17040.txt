Title: Beyond Model Bias: Cross-Entropy Based Rankings in Literary and Academic Texts Remain Stable across Small Language Models
Authors: Ankit Bhattacharjee, Indrasabarna Hari, Krityapriya Bhaumik, Arka Das, Piyush Sarangi
ID: 2610.17040
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17040

Abstract:
While language models provide powerful mechanisms for evaluating textual
predictability, it remains unclear whether the relative extents of LM surprisal
remain stable across models. This study presents empirical evidence of the
invariance of ranking based on cross-entropy related measures (e.g. mean,
variance of each data-point), across four distinct small scale transformer-based
models, for a given dataset of text sources. We consider two stylistic
environments: a corpus of contemporary poetry and a corpus of academic research
papers. We demonstrate that mean and variance of cross-entropy maintain robust
cross-model rank correlation ($ρ> 0.90$) across both regimes. To test whether
this agreement merely reflects generic differences in textual complexity, we
compare it against six parameter-free compression baselines. The extent of
agreement among language models is found to be substantially higher than that
among model-baselines, for most of the measures. Geometric analysis of the
structurally constrained academic regime reveals that higher-order moments are
sensitive to specific models, although strong agreement persists. The findings
suggest that relative extents of textual unpredictability may be independent of
the language model they are computed against.
