Title: Real2Worlds2Real: Scaling Self-Improving Agentic Real2Sim2Real for Robot Manipulation
Authors: Jinzhong Gu, Jiakai Chen, Xinyi Cao, Yan Shen, Hangke Sui, Shutian Zheng, Xiang Xiao, Haoran Lu, Fan Xu, Lu Shi et al.
ID: 2611.31098
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31098

Abstract:
Scaling robot manipulation learning remains challenging due to the high cost of
collecting real-world interaction data. Real-to-Sim-to-Real (Real2Sim2Real)
offers a promising route by enabling policy learning in simulation grounded in
real-world observations, yet scaling this paradigm across diverse tasks and
physical interactions remains difficult. We introduce Real2Worlds2Real, a self-
improving agentic framework that integrates scalable simulation-world
construction with policy development. Given limited visual observations and task
descriptions, its tool-orchestration harness constructs faithful digital twins
and expands them into diverse, physically executable digital cousins. The agent
adaptively selects tools and revisits construction decisions, using visual and
execution feedback to refine geometry and physical behavior. Building on these
worlds, the agent orchestrates policy development by coordinating data
generation, policy learning, and refinement based on task requirements and
simulation rollout feedback. Across both stages, the agent distills validated
procedures into reusable skills that guide subsequent tasks. Experiments
demonstrate improved world fidelity and successful real-world policy transfer,
with performance gains from increasing world diversity and interaction-data
volume. Skill reuse further improves world-building efficiency and long-horizon
task success.
