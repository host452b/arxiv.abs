Title: Optimized Bragg Grating Liquid Crystal Solar Sail
Authors: Sai Keshav Sasanapuri, Xiulian, Shan, Grover A. Swartzlander
ID: 2610.28942
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28942

Abstract:
We have designed a thin 3-layer sun-facing diffractive Bragg reflector comprised
of a chiral polymeric liquid crystal film. Using Rigorous Coupled-Wave Analysis
(RCWA), the stack was optimized to maximize the transverse radiation pressure
force across the wavelength band 0.47 - 1.67 micrometers. The optimized
structure operates on a single polarization state of light. A two structure
stack, each operating on a different state of polarization, is predicted to
provide a transverse momentum transfer efficiency of 51%. This study
demonstrates how a wavelength selective Bragg grating may be used both as a
sunshade to scatter sunlight away from the Earth, and as a solar sail for
maintaining a prescribed orbit (such as the Earth-Sun L1 point) owing to the
momentum transfer efficiency.
