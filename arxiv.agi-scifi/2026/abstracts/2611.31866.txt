Title: The Oracle Is Not Enough: Secure Settlement for Prediction Markets
Authors: Jiayu Liu, Sen Yang, Xueyuan Zhao, Kartik Nayak, Fan Zhang
ID: 2611.31866
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31866

Abstract:
Prediction markets are becoming an important mechanism for aggregating
information and forecasting uncertain real-world events. As they grow in scale
and value, reliable settlement becomes critical because settlement determines
the winning outcome and allocation of market collateral. Yet many heterogeneous
markets require market-specific evidence and interpretation, and incorrect
settlement can create direct financial incentives for manipulation. In this
paper, we ask how to settle heterogeneous markets correctly and efficiently at
scale. We first compare existing settlement designs in their support for
heterogeneous markets, reliance on direct operator intervention, and resistance
to manipulation, motivating a layered architecture that combines incentivized
crowdsourcing with stake-backed voting and selective post-settlement review.
Building on this architecture, we develop a stake-backed settlement protocol
that provides economic security and settlement liveness under market-specific
verification costs. Specifically, we prove that manipulation is unprofitable for
every settled market and give two constructions that support sufficient self-
enforcing participation with controlled reward expenditure. Using historical
Polymarket and UMA data, we evaluate the protocol under real-world conditions.
We find that 99% of admitted markets can be settled with modest participation
rewards even under high verification costs.
