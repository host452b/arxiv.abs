Title: Sharp Local Entropy Bounds in the Wasserstein Space
Authors: Chang Jun Im, Jeong Min Jeon
ID: 2611.20685
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20685

Abstract:
Fréchet regression extends regression analysis to metric space-valued responses
such as probability distributions. Its asymptotic theory often relies on local
entropy conditions, which control the complexity of small balls in the response
space. A commonly used condition requires the associated local entropy integral
to remain bounded as the balls shrink, but this requirement can fail when the
response space is infinite-dimensional. We determine the exact rate at which
this integral diverges in the Wasserstein space of probability distributions
supported on a compact interval. Specifically, for a broad class of ball
centers, we establish matching upper and lower bounds for the local covering
entropy and show that the local entropy integral grows at the rate of the
reciprocal square root of the ball radius. This rate persists when the response
space is restricted to a large class of continuous distributions. The same rate
holds for uniform local entropy integrals, with the supremum taken over ball
centers. These results quantify the local complexity of the Wasserstein space
and provide sharp benchmarks for entropy assumptions in statistical problems
with distribution-valued responses.
