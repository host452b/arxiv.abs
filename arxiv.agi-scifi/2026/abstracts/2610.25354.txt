Title: Entropy hypocoercivity and query complexity for the bouncy particle sampler
Authors: Jianfeng Lu, Yinchen Luo
ID: 2610.25354
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25354

Abstract:
We study convergence in relative entropy of the bouncy particle sampler with
Gaussian velocity refreshment, for a target $e^{-U}$ on $\mathbb R^d$ with $U$
$m$-strongly convex and $L$-smooth, and condition number $κ=L/m$. For initial
laws with bounded density relative to equilibrium, we show that the relative
entropy decays exponentially up to an arbitrary additive error $δ$, with a rate
that depends only logarithmically on the density bound and on $δ$. From a
Gaussian cold start and with a suitably chosen refreshment rate, the relative
entropy falls below $δ$ after time $\widetilde O(\sqrt{d/m})$. Combined with
exact simulation by windowed thinning, this gives an expected gradient-query
complexity of $\widetilde O(d\sqrtκ)$, improving the $\widetilde O(d^2\sqrtκ)$
bound based on $L^2$ hypocoercivity.
