Title: A Cognitive Agentic AI Framework for Interactive Process Analysis and Optimization
Authors: Trung Ngoc Trinha, Roman Tschentscher, Trong Dong Nguyen, Trung Thanh Nguyen, Anna Trubetskaya
ID: 2611.27725
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27725

Abstract:
Complex multivariable process analysis and optimization require coordinated
access to simulation, structured data, machine learning (ML), optimization, and
scientific literature, often across disconnected software tools. We present a
Cognitive Agentic AI Framework (CAAF) that enables interactive, iterative
process investigation through natural-language prompts within an established
flowsheet. Following harness-engineering principles, CAAF assigns task
interpretation, planning, knowledge synthesis, and context management to a large
language model while delegating numerical calculations to specialized tools.
Dedicated providers connect databases, scientific-document retrieval, ML-
assisted Bayesian optimization (BO), and COCO process simulation. The framework
is demonstrated on methyl acetate production. Guided by successive prompts, an
engineer explored multivariable relationships, conducted 4,000 BO iterations to
maximize carbon conversion yield (C-Yield) and minimize specific energy cost
(C-SEC), refined operating bounds, and verified selected candidates through
process simulation. Re-simulation of 16 candidates within the restricted domain
showed close agreement with ML predictions, with a mean absolute C-Yield error
of 0.0085 (0.85 percentage points). The highest BO-derived C-Yield, verified by
COCO, was 0.921 versus approximately 0.81 in the initial dataset, an improvement
of about 11 percentage points. CAAF also detects and reports non-converged
simulations, excludes invalid outputs, and continues subsequent evaluations. The
results demonstrate how human-guided agentic coordination can combine
engineering tools for iterative analysis and optimization with rigorous
simulation-based verification under a fixed process topology.
