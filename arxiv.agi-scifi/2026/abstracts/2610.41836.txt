Title: 6G-Enabled Cooperative Connected and Automated Mobility: Are We There Yet?
Authors: Mate Boban, Alessandro Bazzi, Claudia Campolo, Alexey Vinel, Mario Castaneda, Luca De Nardis, Eirini Liotou, Jérôme Härri, Nikolaos Koursioumpas, Lina Magoula et al.
ID: 2610.41836
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41836

Abstract:
Connected, cooperative, and automated mobility (CCAM) is a key enabler of future
intelligent transportation systems, requiring seamless integration of
communication, sensing, computing, and automation technologies. This paper
surveys the state of the art in CCAM, focusing on key use cases and performance
indicators, enabling technologies, system architectures, and standardization and
regulatory developments relevant to large-scale deployment. Particular attention
is given to cooperative perception, collective awareness, artificial
intelligence and machine learning as fundamental building blocks of future CCAM
ecosystems. The paper further identifies major research challenges related to
scalability, interoperability, and real-time operation in highly dynamic mobile
environments. Finally, it outlines future research directions toward achieving
large-scale, safe, and resilient CCAM systems supported by next-generation
communication networks.
