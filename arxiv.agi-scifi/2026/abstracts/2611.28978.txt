Title: World Models for Ocean Engineering: A Critical Review of Maritime Autonomy, Adaptive Latent Prediction, Digital Twins, and Biodiversity Monitoring
Authors: Basma Dokkara, Esteban W. G. Cluab, Luiz M. G. Goncalves, Paolo Spagnolo, Cosimo Distante
ID: 2611.28978
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28978

Abstract:
Marine robots face partial observability, non-stationary dynamics, and sparse
communication, motivating WMs that learn compact predictive states for planning.
This review develops a functional taxonomy of WMs for ocean engineering,
focusing on Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) and AdaJEPA's test-
time adaptation within model predictive control, mapping them to navigation,
adaptive sampling, digital twins, and biodiversity monitoring, and separating
demonstrated evidence from prospective applications. The synthesis identifies
key Gaps: benchmarks, uncertainty calibration, safe adaptation and proposes a
research agenda for physics-informed, deployment-adaptive marine WMs validated
through simulation and field missions.
