Title: Efficient VLA Reinforcement Learning via Subchunk Rollouts in Latent World Models
Authors: Nan Tang, Han-Tao Zhang, Jing-Cheng Pang, Hao-Nan Wang, Hao-Xiang Ren, Yi-Fei Sheng, Jun-Yin Ye, Tian Xu, Fan-Ming Luo, Hao-Xin Lin et al.
ID: 2611.27645
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27645

Abstract:
Model-based reinforcement learning (MBRL) can improve pretrained vision-
language-action (VLA) policies through imagined experiences. However, existing
VLA-MBRL methods primarily rely on large-scale image or video generation world
models, incurring large computational overhead. Latent world models offer a more
efficient alternative but face two challenges: compounding errors during
recursive prediction and incompatibility with VLAs' visual input interfaces. In
this work, we propose LS-MBPO, a lightweight residual MBRL framework that
leverages latent world models to improve upon pretrained VLAs. LS-MBPO
introduces two key techniques: (1) Hierarchical Forcing, which combines dense
causal supervision with autoregressive feedback to mitigate compounding
prediction errors; and (2) Latent Subchunk Rollouts, which leverage action
chunks from a frozen VLA policy and a latent residual policy for successive
subchunk refinement, bridging the gap between latent imagination and VLA policy
execution. We further introduce a Progress-Conditioned Branch-Start Curriculum
that progressively shifts rollout starting points from later to earlier task
stages, enabling effective policy learning from short imagined rollouts.
Extensive experiments on LIBERO, RoboTwin, and a real-world ALOHA robot show
that LS-MBPO achieves superior task performance and training efficiency compared
to existing methods.
