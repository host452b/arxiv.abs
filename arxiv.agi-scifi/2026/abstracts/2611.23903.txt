Title: AI Surge Accelerates the Transition to Liquid Cooling: Rethinking Energy and Water Use in Data Center Cooling
Authors: Yingbo Zhang, Eric Masanet, Shengwei Wang
ID: 2611.23903
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23903

Abstract:
The power density of AI workloads is rapidly driving shifts to liquid cooling.
This study rethinks and assesses the global impact on energy and water demand of
this cooling transformation. Results suggest that liquid cooling can reduce
cooling energy use by 25-56% relative to air cooling, offering substantial
energy savings for GW-scale AI data centers. Best-practice PUE values could be
as low as 1.07-1.08 for today's systems and 1.03-1.04 for emerging designs, and
WUE values range from 0.38 to 2.63 L/kWh, depending on temperature conditions.
We identify global free-cooling rack inlet temperature thresholds of 33
$^\circ$C for evaporative systems and 49 $^\circ$C for dry systems,
demonstrating the practicality of 100% free cooling in many locations. The model
finds that minimum PUEs do not necessarily coincide with minimum total energy
use, because warmer coolant raises server power. Results quantify the large
sustainability potential of liquid cooling and provide actionable targets for
data center design and policies.
