Title: Fourier Entropy Bounds via Approximations
Authors: Manaswi Paraashar, Nitin Saurabh
ID: 2611.06451
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06451

Abstract:
We establish upper bounds on the Shannon entropy $\mathbb H(\widehat f^{\,2})$
of the Fourier distribution of Boolean functions. We prove that $$ \mathbb
H(\widehat f^{\,2}) =O\!\left(\log\bigl(1+\|\widehat f\|_{1,1/3}\bigr)\right),
$$ where $\|\widehat f\|_{1,1/3}$ denotes the minimum spectral norm of a
pointwise $1/3$-approximant to $f$. This implies $\mathbb H(\widehat
f^{\,2})=O(\mathsf R^\oplus(f))$, where $\mathsf R^\oplus(f)$ is bounded-error
randomized parity-query complexity. We also bound Fourier entropy by the smaller
of the two one-sided average unambiguous parity-certificate complexities. More
generally, for any exact representation of indicator of $f$ as $\sum_C
w_C\mathbf1_C$, with nonnegative weights and affine subspaces $C$, we prove $$
\mathbb H(\widehat f^{\,2}) =O\!\left(\sum_C w_C\,2^{-k_C}k_C\right), \qquad
k_C=\operatorname{codim}(C). $$ The quantity on the right is the average conical
junta degree of the representation. These results strengthen earlier bounds of
(Arunachalam et al. 2021) by extending approximation-based entropy estimates to
Shannon entropy and allowing one-sided and overlapping exact representations.
The proofs use an entropy bound from a sequence of increasingly accurate
approximants of $f$ with \emph{small} spectral norm. Our techniques also find an
application in quantum communication complexity, where it improves a lower bound
for the bounded-error quantum communication complexity of XOR functions.
