Title: AGI: Agility Index for Power Systems Dynamic Performance, Inspired by Reynolds Number
Authors: Maria Vabson, Amir Sajadi, Bri-Mathias Hodge
ID: 2611.18986
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18986

Abstract:
Multiple established metrics and indices exist to quantify the dynamic
performance of power systems. With the growth of inverter based resources, these
mostly conventional metrics and indices may not encompass all important elements
and variables that hold information about the grid performance, especially the
damping properties. This paper proposes the Agility Index (AGI), which is a
Reynolds number-inspired metric for power system dynamics relating the ratio of
inertia to damping. With the current trends towards high-power inverter assets
and reduced-inertia grids, explicit attention is necessary in monitoring inertia
and damping together. This paper shares insights the AGI presents about the
dynamic performance of power systems.
