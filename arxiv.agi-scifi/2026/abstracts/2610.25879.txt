Title: Cling: Supporting Cross-Page Coordination with Human-Directed, In-Situ, and Small-Step Automation
Authors: Zhicheng Huang, Yining Cao, Haijun Xia
ID: 2610.25879
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25879

Abstract:
Web-based information work spans independently developed pages, with goals and
coordination needs often emerging through browsing. Manual coordination requires
repeated low-level actions, yet explicitly specifying operations for automation
can interrupt engagement with information that shapes users' next steps. Our
approach constructs reusable, schema-based page representations and identifies
coordination actions through correspondences across schemas. We instantiate this
approach in Cling, which surfaces coordination opportunities in situ for users
to select, configure, and execute incrementally while directing the broader
task. A technical evaluation demonstrates accurate identification of useful
opportunities across diverse domains. A 12-participant study found that Cling
reduced task durations by more than half compared with both manual interaction
and an end-to-end AI agent, while requiring 61% fewer clicks and 80% fewer tab
switches than manual interaction. Its small-step automation supported control
and verification as actions unfolded, while accommodating intent as it emerged
and evolved through information encountered on the page.
