Title: Machine Learning for Near Real-Time Identification of Wildfire Burned Areas
Authors: Khoi Nguyen, Adam W. Bargteil, Milton Halem
ID: 2611.20036
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20036

Abstract:
In this article, we present a near real-time method that estimates burned
probability anywhere in the world using satellite images. Our approach takes
advantage of the state-of-the-art pix2pix model to translate a preprocessed
index calculated from Landsat 8/9 images into an approximate of USGS Burn
Probability indicating per pixel burn probability. Since this is a machine
learning-based approach, it can be automated with a minimum amount of human
intervention with significantly less time delays and geographic restrictions
compared to other contemporary methods. We include results from our experiments
and compare our approximation with the USGS Burn Probability using RMSE score.
