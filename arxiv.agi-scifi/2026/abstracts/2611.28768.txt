Title: A high-speed and high-resolution 3D velocity map imaging system with a neuromorphic camera
Authors: Sulaiman Abubakar, Blessed Oguh, Akaninyene Udoikono, Hackim Musah, Suk Kyoung Lee, Wen Li
ID: 2611.28768
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28768

Abstract:
An improved camera-based 3D-velocity map imaging (VMI) system was demonstrated
with a neuromorphic camera. We show a machine vision event-driven camera can
achieve an unprecedented latency of 2 microseconds when detecting single
ion/electron with a microchannel plate imaging detector, which boosts the speed
of the momentum imaging system to more than 100 kHz, while the system with a
conventional frame camera is limited to a few kHz. The high spatial resolution
of the camera offers additional performance boost while maintaining excellent
multi-hit capability and a high time resolution.
