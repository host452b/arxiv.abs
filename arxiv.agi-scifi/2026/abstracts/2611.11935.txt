Title: AI-Driven Discovery of Entanglement Witness in Spin Hamiltonians
Authors: Jiwei Tian, Mouyang Cheng, Mingda Li
ID: 2611.11935
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11935

Abstract:
Entanglement is a crucial resource in quantum science and engineering, but
detecting it in many-body states from experimentally accessible observables
remains challenging. Here we introduce EntNet, an AI-driven framework to
discover compact combinations of spin observables that accurately distinguish
entangled from fully separable many-body mixed states in one-dimensional
spin-$1/2$ chains governed by short-range Hamiltonians. We construct local-
unitary-invariant representations of measurable Pauli correlators up to four-
body order, sparsely select 16 candidate invariant feature channels, and learn
their spatial structure using a Transformer architecture with rotary positional
embeddings. Using only expectation values of these low-order observables, EntNet
achieves $>98\%$ recall on certified entangled states with near-zero false-
positive rates on separable states. Remarkably, it exhibits transferability
across system sizes and distinct Hamiltonian families, including frustrated and
disordered spin models, with minimal loss of accuracy. Beyond detection, EntNet
provides physical insight into entanglement detection by revealing a selective
spatial organization of pair correlations, while its finite-temperature decision
boundary tracks the excitation gap of Jordan-Wigner quasiparticles. These
results establish a transferable and physically interpretable framework for
data-driven discovery of entanglement witnesses from local correlation
functions.
