Title: Towards bio-inspired visual navigation: a neuromorphic navigation paradigm for MAVs
Authors: Junjie Jiang, Ankang Zhou, Zheng Fang
ID: 2610.21978
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21978

Abstract:
Biological visual navigation achieves remarkable robustness at extremely low
power, whereas frame-based robotic systems relying on artificial neural networks
remain limited in both respects, particularly for energy-constrained micro
aerial vehicles (MAVs) operating in complex environments. Inspired by the event-
driven, sparse, and asynchronous principles of biological visual systems,
neuromorphic sensing and computation provide a promising alternative for robust
and energy-efficient navigation. In this paper, we propose the first end-to-end
event-driven neuromorphic paradigm for MAV visual navigation in complex
environments by integrating neuromorphic vision, spiking neural networks, and
neuromorphic processors. The proposed method first extracts the environmental
representation from event streams using a spiking perceptual representation
module. This representation is fed into a spiking navigation module, where
guidance learning and population coding enable efficient policy learning with
few time steps. The navigation module then outputs a waypoint converted into
executable trajectory points. Experiments show that the proposed neuromorphic
method achieves navigation performance comparable to ANN-based methods while
markedly reducing power consumption. The spiking perceptual module consumes only
19.57% of the power of its ANN counterpart, while the spiking navigation module
deployed on Intel Loihi consumes only 12.37% of that required by its ANN
counterpart running on NVIDIA Orin NX. Beyond prior neuromorphic systems focused
mainly on relatively simple navigation subtasks, we demonstrate a complete
neuromorphic visual navigation system spanning simulation and real-world
deployment. Our results establish the feasibility of the neuromorphic paradigm
for autonomous navigation and provide a bio-inspired pathway toward robust and
low-power MAV navigation.
