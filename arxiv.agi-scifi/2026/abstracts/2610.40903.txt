Title: Metadevices that compute: mathematical foundations and experimental implementations for neuromorphic nanophotonics
Authors: Xian Hadia, Odysseas Tsilipakos, Dimitrios C. Tzarouchis, Raphaël Pestourie
ID: 2610.40903
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40903

Abstract:
Wave-based analog computing has re-emerged as a compelling alternative to
digital electronics for specific linear-algebraic and machine-learning
workloads, promising massively parallel, high-bandwidth, and energy-efficient
information processing. Metamaterials, metasurfaces, and integrated
metastructures (collectively, metadevices) now perform mathematical operations,
solve integral and differential equations, multiply matrices, and implement
trainable photonic neural networks directly in the wave domain. This focused
review consolidates the mathematical foundations that underpin such devices,
i.e., the mapping between linear operators and wave scattering, the feedback
architectures that realize equation inversion, and the inverse-design and
scientific machine-learning toolchains that make their synthesis and training
tractable, and connects them to the rapidly growing body of experimental
demonstrations, from free-space diffractive processors to reconfigurable guided-
wave metastructures. We conclude with open problems and opportunities for this
emerging neuromorphic nanophotonics ecosystem.
