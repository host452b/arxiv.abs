Title: Neuromorphic Computing Based on Parametrically-Driven Oscillators and Frequency Combs
Authors: Mahadev Sunil Kumar, Adarsh Ganesan
ID: 2604.21861
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.21861

Abstract:
Parametrically driven oscillators exhibit distinct dynamical regimes that
strongly influence their computational capability as physical reservoirs. Here,
we demonstrate reservoir computing in a two-mode system exhibiting 2:1
parametric resonance using Mackey--Glass, Rössler, and Lorenz chaotic time-
series prediction tasks. Mapping the normalized mean squared error over
parameter space reveals that the lowest prediction errors consistently occur
within the parametric resonance regime, where nonlinear interactions are
activated while temporal coherence is preserved. In contrast, although
frequency-comb states increase spectral dimensionality, prediction performance
degrades in the chaotic comb regime due to loss of phase coherence. We further
show that the modulation depth $Δ\text{F}$, detuning $κ$, damping ratio
$γ_{21}$, and input data rate systematically reshape the accessible dynamical
regimes and thereby control the reservoir performance. These results identify
parametric resonance as a robust operating regime for oscillator-based
neuromorphic computing and establish dynamical regime engineering as a route
toward physical reservoir optimization.
