Title: Scaling laws for linear-elastic two-variant martensitic phase transitions in general domains
Authors: Sergio Conti, Oleksandr Misiats
ID: 2611.04329
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04329

Abstract:
We consider a two-well problem within geometrically linear elasticity, on a
bounded Lipschitz set in dimension $n\ge 2$. We assume that the two wells are
compatible through a symmetrized rank-one connection and that the displacement
matches their average on the boundary, and we include a regularization based on
the total surface energy. We show that there are two distinct regimes, with the
energy scaling differently in terms of the surface-energy coefficient $\eps$: it
is proportional to $\eps^{2/3}$ if there are two distinct compatible twin-normal
directions, and to $\eps^{4/5}$ if there is only one. This matches previous
findings in rectangles and other special geometries, mostly in two dimensions,
and the lower bounds obtained by a Fourier method by Raiţă, Rüland, Tissot and
Tribuzio (2024). Our results are obtained by explicit constructions, which
contain a self-similar refinement of oscillations close to the boundary, and by
a short real-space proof of the matching lower bounds.
