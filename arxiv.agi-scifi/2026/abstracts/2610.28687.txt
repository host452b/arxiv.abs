Title: Solar System Surface Technosignatures: Searching for Signs of Artificial Design on Extraterrestrial Terrain
Authors: Ana Henrique, Sofia Sheikh, Benjamin Fields
ID: 2610.28687
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28687

Abstract:
Artificial radio emissions have historically been a major focus of
technosignature searches. Here, we apply an anthropological lens to expand this
scope to surface technosignatures: non-random, intentional patterning on
planetary terrain, similar to long-lasting human-made landscapes and monuments
on Earth. On geologically inactive bodies such as the Moon and Mars, artificial
traces and patterns could potentially remain visible for hundreds of millions of
years. We discuss two recent developments that make this search timely: high-
resolution imaging datasets, such as those produced by the HiRISE camera on the
Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO), and ML-based anomaly-detection methods.
Finally, we list two possible approaches to developing a model capable of
searching for these surface technosignatures: a supervised and an unsupervised
one. Complemented by LiDAR, ground-penetrating radar, and reflectance
spectroscopy, these methods would enable the first quantitative upper limits on
surface technosignatures while yielding ancillary benefits for planetary
science.
