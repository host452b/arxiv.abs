Title: Low-Data OOD Prediction of SAW Resonators with Simulation-Compensated Transfer Learning
Authors: Zhijie Ma, Xingyu Liu, Shuhan Chen, Fangsheng Qian, Junyan Zheng, Nan Jun Kim, Taek Joo Lee, Hyung Kook Yang, Yansong Yang
ID: 2611.15140
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15140

Abstract:
Surface acoustic wave (SAW) resonators are foundational to modern radio-
frequency (RF) front-end architectures. However, models trained on limited
fabricated devices generalize poorly to unmeasured design regions because of
process-induced variations, while finite-element method (FEM) simulations,
although inexpensive, exhibit systematic offsets from measurements. To predict
device performance before physical prototyping, this work presents a simulation-
compensated transfer-learning framework that uses computationally efficient
2.5-D FEM simulations as physics-based anchors and learns their calibration to
the measurement domain. Using 4,236 measured lithium tantalate (LiTaO3) SAW
resonators and 816 unique simulated configurations, five transfer strategies are
systematically benchmarked under seven out-of-distribution (OOD) protocols
spanning frequency, geometry, and material shifts. The proposed framework
reduces OOD prediction error by 83.54% on average (up to 97.57%) relative to the
Measurement-only baseline, with the best strategy selected per case, and raises
the pooled R2 of frequency prediction to 1.000, offering an economically
scalable route toward physics-guided, fabrication-aware machine learning for
microelectromechanical-system (MEMS) acoustic-device design.
