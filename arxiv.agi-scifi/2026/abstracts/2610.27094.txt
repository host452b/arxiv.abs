Title: Binary Black Hole Puncture Initial Data with Physics-Informed Neural Networks
Authors: Thiago Assumpção, Ignacio Brevis, Irene Pitsiladi, Martin Zinzen, Miguel Bezares
ID: 2610.27094
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27094

Abstract:
We present Pinndorama, a physics-informed neural network (PINN) code for solving
the Hamiltonian constraint in the puncture formalism. The networks are trained
solely on the governing equation and boundary conditions, without an analytical
or perturbative guiding solution, with collocation points sampled in curvilinear
coordinates. Training combines two optimization algorithms: an initial stage
using adaptive moment estimation (Adam), followed by the self-scaled Broyden
quasi-Newton method. We apply this approach to a spherically symmetric test
problem with a known exact solution, axisymmetric binary punctures with unequal
spins, and a fully three-dimensional GW150914-like configuration with unequal
spins and orbital momenta. Comparisons with the NRPyElliptic solver yield
relative $L^2$ errors of order $10^{-8}$ in axisymmetry and $10^{-5}$ in three
dimensions. In addition, we reduce the hyperbolic-relaxation time in
NRPyElliptic by a factor of approximately $4.6$ by training a parametric network
that takes the puncture spin as input and provides a physics-informed initial
guess to the solver. These results demonstrate that PINNs can provide accurate
binary-puncture initial data and complement conventional elliptic solvers in
numerical relativity.
