Title: BPU-SpikeMM:Exact Matrix Multiplication via Spatio-Temporal Spike Encoding on Real Neuromorphic Hardware
Authors: Rongyuan Guo, Yufeng Zhang, Haotian Wang, Canming Yao, Yuxiang Huan, Wangdong Yang, Kenli Li
ID: 2611.18275
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18275

Abstract:
Matrix multiplication is the dominant computational kernel in deep learning, yet
its growing scale strains conventional von Neumann hardware in energy efficiency
and bandwidth. Neuromorphic architectures offer a promising alternative, but
mathematical equivalence between matrix multiplication and spiking computation
does not guarantee direct execution on real neuromorphic hardware, as the
specific hardware paradigm may still require additional data recovery, bit-
weight reconstruction, or state readout after neuromorphic execution. This paper
presents BPU-SpikeMM (Brain Processing Unit-based Spike Matrix Multiplication),
an exact spatio-temporal spike encoding framework for matrix multiplication on
real neuromorphic hardware. We prove the exact equivalence of spike-encoded
matrix multiplication under the BPU execution paradigm, distinguish mathematical
equivalence from native direct-readout equivalence on BPU hardware, and build a
spatio-temporal offloading pipeline supporting multiple encoding schemes.
Furthermore, we extend the framework to a multi-dimensional encoding design
space and evaluate the applicability of different encodings on real BPU hardware
in terms of correctness, temporal resource cost, spatial resource cost, and
event-driven metrics. The results confirm native direct-readout equivalence
across all evaluated encoding schemes and show that their applicability to BPU
execution depends on joint trade-offs among temporal cost, spatial resource
consumption, and event activity.
