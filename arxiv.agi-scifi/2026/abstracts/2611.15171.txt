Title: Quantum Computing in Power Systems:Current Landscape and Future Roadmap with AI
Authors: Yijun Xu, Yongbing Yao, Wei Gu, Chengjun Liu, Qi Lou, Lamine Mili, Mohammadreza Saghafi, Mert Korkali, Shuai Lu
ID: 2611.15171
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15171

Abstract:
Modern power systems are characterized by greater penetration of renewable
energy resources, extensive use of power electronics, and more diverse loads.
This leads to a dramatic increase in computational complexity, which, in turn,
compromises solution efficiency and fails to satisfy the real time demands of
system analysis. Recently, quantum computing techniques, attributed to their
unique physical properties, such as superposition and entanglement, hold the
potential to offer new solutions for power systems. To this end, this paper not
only provides a comprehensive landscape of quantum computing in power systems,
but also investigates its roadmap with AI enabled evolution. This paper begins
with an introduction to the fundamental concepts of quantum computing, followed
by a review of several approaches to power system simulation and optimization.
Subsequently, we further investigate the potential quantum AI-enabled evolution
in power systems. In addition, the current development of quantum software and
hardware platforms is elaborated. Finally, this paper synthesizes the prevailing
challenges and prospects for future research directions.
