Title: Solving the MAX-$k$-CUT problem for hypergraphs with quantum computing
Authors: V. S. Yarmus, Kh. P. Gnatenko
ID: 2610.41678
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41678

Abstract:
We investigate the MAX-$k$-CUT problem on hypergraphs by considering their
representation as spin systems with Ising interactions. The corresponding cost
Hamiltonian for MAX-$k$-CUT problem on hypergraphs is reduced to an Ising
Hamiltonian with pairwise interactions. This reduction simplifies the solution
of the MAX-$k$-CUT problem using quantum computing. As illustrative examples, a
3-uniform tight-star hypergraph and a sunflower hypergraph are considered. The
corresponding Hamiltonians are constructed, and quantum protocols for the
MAX-$k$-CUT problems are presented. The MAX-3-CUT and MAX-4-CUT instances are
investigated for these hypergraphs by varying the number of Quantum Approximate
Optimization Algorithm (QAOA) layers from (p=2) to (p=15) with quantum computing
on IBM's Qiskit AerSimulator.
