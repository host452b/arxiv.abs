Title: Caging Vision-Language Navigation for Legged Robots via Barrier-Certified Safety Architecture
Authors: Abhijeet M. Kulkarni, Saimouli Katragadda, Yuze Du, Zijun Sha, Guoquan Huang
ID: 2611.04972
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04972

Abstract:
By harnessing the inherent commonsense capabilities of pretrained Large Language
Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs), current Vision-Language
Action(VLA) models for legged robots excel at interpreting language-based
navigation commands but lack the physical grounding and explicit safety
assurance required for real-world operation. Therefore, this paper aims to cage
Legged-Vision-Language Navigation (VLN) through a safety architecture that
provides physical grounding and ensures navigation safety. The key idea is not
to abandon the powerful capabilities of end-to-end VLA planning, but rather to
augment and cage them. In particular, we integrate a high-level VLA planner with
a low-level physical-grounding layer that leverages efficient state estimation,
in-situ 3D spatial perception, and an Signed Distance Field (SDF) representation
to generate executable trajectories. We provide a navigation planner with
provable collision-avoidance guarantees under the stated assumptions by
combining a low-dimensional Bezier-parameterized MPPI planner with Discrete
Exponential Barrier Function (dEBF) constraints. The proposed Legged-VLN system
is modular, making it compatible with various VLM/VLA models and agnostic to the
robot platform. The proposed system is validated with extensive real-world
experiments on humanoid and quadrupedal robots, demonstrating versatile,
efficient, and safe vision-language driven navigation capabilities.
