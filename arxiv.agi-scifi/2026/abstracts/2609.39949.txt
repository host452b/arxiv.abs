Title: Scaling law of variational quantum algorithms
Authors: Ningfeng Wang, Zhengwei Liu
ID: 2609.39949
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39949

Abstract:
We study scaling law of variational quantum algorithms by large-scale numerical
experiments on combinatorial optimizations. On experiments up to n = 5000 qubits
with O( log(n)) circuit depth with a classically simulable ansatz, the converged
energies consistently differ by no more than 0.5% from the optimal or reference
energies reported in the benchmarks.
