Title: Spatiotemporal Dynamics of Conway's Game of Life in the Entropy--Complexity Plane
Authors: Lucas Malacarne Astore, Alejandro C. Frery, Petrônio Cândido de Lima e Silva, Frederico Gadelha Guimarães
ID: 2610.31708
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31708

Abstract:
We investigate the dynamics of Conway's Game of Life (GoL) through the lens of
Ordinal Pattern analysis. Each GoL generation is transformed into a one-
dimensional time series, from which ordinal patterns are extracted to
characterize its underlying dynamics. The resulting ordinal distributions are
then represented in the Entropy--Statistical Complexity ($H \times C$) plane,
enabling a quantitative analysis of the system's evolution. We evaluate
different encoding strategies, including patch sizes and linearization methods,
and assess their impact on the resulting $H \times C$ trajectories. Finally, we
characterize the emergent dynamics in terms of convergence behavior, trajectory
patterns, and statistical complexity, showing how the proposed entropy--
complexity framework provides insights into the dynamics of cellular automata.
