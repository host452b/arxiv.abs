Title: Predictive Neuromorphic Control of a Scanning Mirror System: An Event-Centric Framework
Authors: Changrui Liu, Robin Wroblowski, Anil Alan, Shengling Shi, Rodolphe Sepulchre, Bart De Schutter
ID: 2611.11634
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11634

Abstract:
Organizing control around events can reduce sensing and actuation demands while
directly linking control decisions to the occurrence and timing of desired
system behaviors. This paper proposes a novel event-centric model predictive
control (MPC) framework that unifies event sensing, event prediction, and event
regulation. Events define the feedback information, prediction horizon, and
event-timing objectives, placing them at the center of predictive control
design. The framework is developed for a scanning mirror system equipped with a
baseline neuromorphic controller composed of spiking neurons that supports
sparse actuation. The control objective is to achieve prescribed zero-crossing
times and a desired scanning range while minimizing the mechanical energy
transferred between the actuator and the mirror. A sampling-based algorithm is
presented to obtain suboptimal solutions of the event-centric MPC optimization
problem without requiring an explicit event-to-event prediction model. By
grounding predictive optimization in events, the proposed framework opens a new
direction for MPC design and its integration with neuromorphic control.
