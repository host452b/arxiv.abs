Title: Stabilizer-Restricted Antidistinguishability in Quantum Computation with Magic States
Authors: Selman Ipek
ID: 2611.21123
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21123

Abstract:
An antidistinguishing measurement for a family of preparations excludes at least
one preparation for every possible outcome, constraining 'classical'
representations of these processes, known as ontological models. We develop this
connection within quantum computing with magic states (QCM), a universal model
of quantum computation. Our constructions use adaptive Pauli measurements on
tensor products of single-qubit non stabilizer states, including families
associated with the Clifford hierarchy. These constructions yield stabilizer-
restricted versions of the Pusey-Barrett-Rudolph theorem: any ontological model
reproducing these measurement statistics and satisfying preparation independence
must assign disjoint ontic supports to the preparations considered. We
demonstrate this obstruction to preparation independence in a concrete
ontological model based on convex polytopes used for the classical simulation of
QCM.
