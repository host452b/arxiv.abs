Title: ViTrust: Learning 3D Observation Trust for Deformable Physical World Models
Authors: Zhihao Guo, Zhihao Li, Xu Ge, Lijian Zhong, Yu Lu, Haitao Wu, Longqing Zhao, Yue Ma
ID: 2611.28815
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28815

Abstract:
Learning deformable physical world models requires dynamics that generalize
beyond observed motion. Accurate reconstruction alone does not ensure accurate
future prediction, motivating us to learn how observations should influence
physical identification. However, visual reliability alone does not determine
how informative an observation is about physical dynamics. We propose ViTrust to
learn 3D observation trust, determining how strongly each tracked point
influences physical identification. Its key design is a visual-contact trust
adapter that combines pointwise spatiotemporal evidence with interaction history
on a persistent contact graph to predict time-varying observation weights. A
single-stage, shared-forward objective jointly optimizes these weights and
physical parameters through weighted geometry and correspondence losses, with
constraints to prevent observation-weight collapse. All learning uses only the
observed prefix. The fitted physics remains fixed for future prediction under
supplied controls. Experiments on real-world sequences demonstrate improved
future prediction over the evaluated baselines, alongside qualitative
demonstrations of novel-control rollouts and cross-object transfer.
