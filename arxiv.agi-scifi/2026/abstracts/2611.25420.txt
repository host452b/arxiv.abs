Title: Structural Fine-Tuning under Economic Restrictions
Authors: Yi Niu, Yuecheng Su, Ethan Yang
ID: 2611.25420
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25420

Abstract:
How should a predictor trained on historical data be adapted to a new economic
environment or task when few target observations are available? We propose
structural fine-tuning, a framework that introduces economic restrictions to
guide the adaptation of pretrained predictors. The framework does not require a
complete structural model; instead, it utilizes constraints based on economic
knowledge, ranging from simple, ubiquitous assumptions to particular workhorse
models. Theoretically, we characterize conditions when economic restrictions
improve prediction over the original model and ordinary fine-tuning. Then, we
illustrate the framework through a simulation exercise of a credit issuer's
credit-limit decision following a Medicaid-style expansion in medical payments.
Imposing restrictions based on eligibility, outstanding medical bills, cash
flows, and group-level risk bounds, our framework lowers the root mean squared
error of predicted risk by 28 to 39 percent and the issuer's policy regret by 3
to 10 percent relative to ordinary neuron gating, LoRA, and full fine-tuning.
