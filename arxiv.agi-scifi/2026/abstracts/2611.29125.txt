Title: SilentWAM: Planning in the Masked State of a Diffusion World Model
Authors: Ziyu Zhao, Casey Sun, Zeyu Wang, Shicong Ma, Emily T Zhang, Cheng Lu
ID: 2611.29125
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29125

Abstract:
World-action models learn from future prediction, but accessing their future
representations directly at test time can require costly frame generation.
Methods that generate future frames before planning incur additional forward
passes, while future co-training methods omit explicit future predictions at
inference. We introduce \method, a planner that reads the hidden states of
masked future tokens without decoding future frames. We pretrain MMaDA-
Parallel-M on driving video using frame-level masked world modeling, which
predicts a masked suffix of future frames and explicitly includes inputs with
all future frames masked. Learned planning queries attend to these masked
positions and the observed context, and a continuous head predicts the
trajectory in the same forward pass. With a single front camera, \method reaches
92.0 PDMS on NAVSIM v1 and 89.3 EPDMS on NAVSIM v2, with an end-to-end latency
of 60.3\,ms.
