Title: Supersymmetric Neuromorphic FitzHugh--Nagumo model
Authors: D. Maroulakos, A. Wal, S. K. Mishra, Z. Toklikishvili, L. Chotorlishvili
ID: 2611.03312
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03312

Abstract:
We develop a supersymmetric spectral description of stochastic FitzHugh--Nagumo
dynamics motivated by an effective antiferromagnetic neuromorphic model. A local
normal-form reduction of the adopted tangential Néel-vector dynamics relates
magnetic control parameters to fast activation and slow recovery coordinates.
Freezing the recovery variable yields a one-dimensional Fokker--Planck equation
with a quartic potential, whose similarity-transformed generator admits an exact
supersymmetric factorization. The first nonzero relaxation eigenvalue is
determined by the ground state of the partner operator; in the weak-noise
bistable regime, it is approximated by the sum of the two interwell Kramers
rates. Projecting the similarity-transformed probability density onto the two
lowest modes defines a normalized auxiliary two-state amplitude. We derive its
localized-basis coherence in terms of both the time-dependent basin populations
and their equilibrium weights. Its maximum corresponds to equal normalized
amplitude weights, which generally differ from equal basin populations. The
fixed-recovery example exhibits a pronounced coherence pulse during monotonic
relaxation without a spike. Conversely, the illustrated deterministic spiking
trajectory repeatedly leaves the bistable domain and passes through regions with
insufficient barrier separation, restricting the validity of the localized two-
state approximation. These results connect stochastic relaxation spectra to an
occupation-based coherence diagnostic and delimit its application to
neuromorphic spiking. The diagnostic characterizes an auxiliary amplitude
representation of classical stochastic dynamics and does not by itself establish
microscopic quantum coherence.
