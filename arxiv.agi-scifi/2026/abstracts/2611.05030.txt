Title: On Revolutionizing Power Systems: Suitability of Quantum Computing-Ready Pipelines for Stochastic Unit Commitment Optimization
Authors: Ying-Yi Hong, Dylan Josh Domingo Lopez
ID: 2611.05030
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05030

Abstract:
Modern unit commitment (UC) scenarios, particularly those involving
uncertainties from demand fluctuations and renewable energy sources, pose
significant challenges to traditional optimization methods due to the large
search spaces they encompass. This paper proposes a quantum computing-ready
optimization pipeline for deterministic and stochastic UC, integrating
classical, quantum-inspired, quantum annealing, and hybrid quantum-classical
solvers across test systems, including the IEEE 118-bus system. A novel
Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization (QUBO) reformulation accommodates
generator scaling and 24-hour scheduling horizons with compact Boolean logic
penalties for startup, cooldown, and spinning reserve constraints. A divide-and-
conquer strategy and Quantum Computing Monte Carlo (QCMC) simulation address
scalability across 27,730 uncertainty scenarios, reducing compilation time by up
to two orders of magnitude. Experimental results demonstrate the pipeline's
ability to achieve efficient, scalable, and accurate UC optimization under
uncertainty: the hybrid quantum method approach matches the classical baseline,
reduces stochastic UC runtime from 40.76 to 14.79 hours, and characterizes pure
quantum annealing as limited to small systems with 0.22% to 2.18% encoding
errors and constraint violations. These results provide an analysis of the
scalability and computational practicality of hybrid quantum optimization for
generator scheduling in UC.
