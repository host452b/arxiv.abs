Title: WiFo-D: Wireless Foundation Model Based on Discrete Representations
Authors: Xinyu Pan, Boxun Liu, Chen Chen, Xiang Cheng
ID: 2611.25378
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25378

Abstract:
Wireless foundation models have shown strong generalization capabilities across
heterogeneous wireless tasks and configurations. However, most existing models
rely on continuous representations, limiting ultra-low-overhead channel state
information (CSI) feedback, few-shot generalization, and robustness to noise. To
address these limitations, this paper proposes a wireless foundation model based
on discrete representations (WiFo-D), which learns multi-granularity discrete
CSI representations. It comprises a CSI encoder, multi-codebook vector
quantization, and a modulation-based decoder, with the learned token maps and
quantized features supporting multi-rate CSI feedback, CSI reconstruction, and
diverse CSI-related downstream tasks. Extensive experiments demonstrate strong
CSI feedback performance at an ultra-low latent compression ratio of 1/32 with 3
bits per token, reducing normalized mean square error (NMSE) by more than 4.7 dB
compared with the strongest baseline. Furthermore, for frequency-domain
prediction with limited training samples, WiFo-D improves generalization and
noise robustness over continuous-representation-based methods. The results also
demonstrate its superiority across downstream tasks, including beam prediction,
wireless localization, and line-of-sight/non-line-of-sight identification.
