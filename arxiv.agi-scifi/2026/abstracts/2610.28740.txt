Title: A Hybrid Neural Network Emulator for M1 Neutrino Reaction Rates in Binary Neutron Star Merger Simulations
Authors: Yi Qiu, David Radice, Raffaele Ferrari, Albino Perego
ID: 2610.28740
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.28740

Abstract:
We present a neural network emulator for the bns_nurates library, which computes
neutrino reaction rates used in binary neutron star merger simulations.
Following a hybrid strategy, we emulate only the computationally dominant two-
dimensional reactions---pair processes, bremsstrahlung, and inelastic neutrino-
electron scattering---while evaluating the inexpensive one-dimensional processes
still with bns_nurates. We find that the emulator reproduces the opacities to
within a few percent across the relevant thermodynamic conditions and
accelerates their evaluation by roughly an order of magnitude. When deployed in
a global Athenak binary neutron star merger simulation, it recovers the remnant
structure, neutrino luminosities, and ejecta composition of a reference run
using the full bns_nurates library, at approximately half the overall cost
