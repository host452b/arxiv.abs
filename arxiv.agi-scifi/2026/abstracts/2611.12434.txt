Title: Frontier AI Safety and Governance 2026: Risk Understanding and Coordinated Governance
Authors: Zeng Yi, Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh, Cao Gongce, Lu Enmeng, Chen Yu, Han Kaiyu, Li Kai, Wang Zhengqi, Chang Yao, Xie Jiawei et al.
ID: 2611.12434
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12434

Abstract:
Frontier AI Safety and Governance 2026: Risk Understanding and Coordinated
Governance is a key outcome of the AI Safety and Governance Program of World
Internet Conference Specialized Committee on AI in 2026. Drawing on the latest
developments in frontier AI capabilities and applications, the report
systematically examines the evolving risk landscape, analyzing key risk areas,
their severity and response priorities. Building on a review of existing global
governance practices and an assessment of frontier AI governance readiness, it
identifies key governance gaps and weaknesses and proposes directions and
practical mechanisms for coordinated responses. The report provides a reference
for government departments, international organizations, businesses, research
institutions and other stakeholders. It aims to strengthen the international
community's understanding of and consensus on frontier AI risks, enhance
preparedness and collective response capacity, and advance the safe, reliable
and responsible development of frontier AI to better serve the common well-being
of humanity.
