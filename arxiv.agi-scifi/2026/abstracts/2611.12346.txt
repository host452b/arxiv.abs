Title: ReVMap: Vectorized Global Mapping via Connectivity-Aware Local Map Fusion
Authors: Hongjae Shin, Jungho Kim, Donghyuk Kwak, Myeongjun Kim, Seunghoon Yu, Jiyong Oh, Jun Won Choi
ID: 2611.12346
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12346

Abstract:
Large-scale annotation of vectorized high-definition (HD) maps is labor-
intensive and costly, motivating local-to-global auto-labeling approaches that
incrementally construct global maps from local vectorized predictions. However,
existing approaches often overlook prediction uncertainty and rely on heuristic
map integration, potentially propagating errors and introducing topological
inconsistencies. To address these limitations, we propose ReVMap, a reliable
local-to-global framework that iteratively refines local map predictions and
global map construction through uncertainty-guided prior utilization and
connectivity-guided global map revision. Specifically, ReVMap leverages point-
wise localization uncertainty to incorporate global map priors into local map
prediction, improving robustness against inaccurate prior information. It then
predicts local-to-global connectivity and selectively updates the global map
through uncertainty-aware fusion based on the reliability of local predictions.
By iteratively coupling local prediction and global revision, ReVMap
progressively improves both local map accuracy and global map consistency.
Extensive experiments on the nuScenes and Argoverse 2 benchmarks demonstrate
that ReVMap achieves state-of-the-art performance in both local map prediction
and global map construction.
