Title: A Large-Scale Technosignature Survey of the Anti-Solar Point with the Allen Telescope Array: Survey Design and Pilot Results
Authors: Sofia Z. Sheikh, Eleanor Hort, Bárbara Cabrales, Isabel Gerrard, Wael Farah, Nick Tusay, David R. DeBoer, Saul Balcarcel-Salazar, Joe S. Bright, Luigi F. Cruz et al.
ID: 2611.17376
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17376

Abstract:
In the search for technosignatures, it can be useful to narrow down the cosmic
haystack by prioritizing time-coordinate ``Schelling Points'' at which a beacon
may be more likely to be transmitted. One such Schelling Point is the Anti-Solar
Point (ASP) the place and moment at which Earth would lie directly between a
hypothetical transmitter in the Earth Transit Zone (i.e., ecliptic) and the Sun.
In this work, we describe a campaign to survey the ASP for radio
technosignatures using the Allen Telescope Array, report the results of three
initial nights of data from a pilot study in Summer 2023, and discuss the future
of the campaign. In the pilot study, we find no sky-localized narrowband 1-2.4
GHz technosignatures within an RA range of approximately 20.266-21.312 hrs
during the ASP, setting an upper limit flux density threshold of 115-936 Jy.
