Title: Efficient Iterative Symmetrization algorithm for quantum computing and hadron applications
Authors: Nahia J. Dios-Bilbao, Adrián Castaño-García, J. J. Gálvez-Viruet, Felipe J. Llanes-Estrada, Mario Logrosán-Álvarez, Nicolás M. Arenaza
ID: 2611.18091
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18091

Abstract:
Efficient algorithms for the (anti)symmetrization of quantum registers are
necessary for upcoming few- and many-body physics applications, from atomic to
particle-physics scales. We propose a recursive algorithm (anti)symmetrizing the
last particle added to memory, each time one is created or destroyed, respect to
the rest of already (anti)symmetrized ones. Given a generalized Dicke state
(GDS) of $(j-1)$ quantum registers, each assigned to a particle, the algorithm
produces another GDS of $j$ registers (of $n_{qb}$ qubits each) using a mere
$\mathcal{O}(j\log_2^2 j + j n_{qb})$ $\mathtt{CNOT}$ and single-qubit gates,
and $3j+\lceil\log_2j\rceil+\mathcal{O}(\max(n_{qb},\log_2 j))$ clean auxiliary
qubits. The (anti)symmetrizing operator constructed for this purpose depends on
the quantum numbers of the additional particle being introduced or ``created''.
The algorithm improves upon the construction presented in Appendix A of
\cite{Galvez-Viruet:2024hry} by almost two powers of $j$ and is on par with the
most efficient methods to date, while ideally suited for dynamical memory
management with variable particle number. We exemplify its applicability with a
small selection of problems from hadron physics which are expected to be
addressable with upcoming quantum computers.
