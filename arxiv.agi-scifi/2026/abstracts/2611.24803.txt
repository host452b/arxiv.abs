Title: Cross-Dataset Inference of Central-Satellite Classification and Host Halo Masses from Galaxy neighbours with Machine Learning
Authors: Alice Chen, Camille Avestruz, Niayesh Afshordi, Yen-Ting Lin
ID: 2611.24803
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24803

Abstract:
The classification of galaxies as centrals or satellites gives insight into the
galaxy-halo connection. This, together with halo mass estimates, can be used to
connect observed galaxy populations to underlying dark matter structure. To (1)
classify galaxies as centrals or satellites and (2) estimate the host halo mass
for centrals, we train XGBoost models on IllustrisTNG (TNG100) galaxies, using
the distances (3D or projected (2+1)D redshift space) and $M_*$ of their ten
nearest neighbours. We then test the model on EAGLE to measure its robustness
after applying a simple domain-alignment step that corrects for a relative shift
in the $M_*$ distribution between TNG100 and EAGLE. We further augment the
training data by combining feature sets from neighbour samples with varying
lower $M_*$ thresholds. On the TNG100 test set, the central/satellite classifier
reaches purity and completeness $=0.97$ (0.95) in 3D (projected redshift) space
at the threshold where purity=completeness, and the halo-mass regressor achieves
$r^2$=0.98 (0.93) when evaluated on true centrals and $r^2=0.93$ (0.93) when
evaluated on inferred centrals. Applied to EAGLE after alignment, the classifier
achieves purity/completeness $=0.95$ (0.95), while halo-mass inference yields
$r^2=0.91$ ($0.87$) for true centrals and $r^2=0.83$ (0.77) for inferred
centrals; training-data augmentation most improves the halo-mass estimation
task. Satellites misclassified as centrals underpredict the true masses of host
halos, especially if they are closer to a lower mass halo's central than its own
host's central and/or has a significantly lower $M_*$ than its own central.
These results show that survey-accessible information such as projected
separations and stellar masses - combined with simulation-trained models and
domain alignment - can enable reliable central identification and host halo mass
estimation to an $r^2\geq 0.85$.
