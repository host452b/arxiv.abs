Title: The Robots are Coming: Artificial Intelligence Searching for Extraterrestrial Intelligence
Authors: Steve Croft
ID: 2610.04449
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04449

Abstract:
Breakthrough Listen, humanity's most ambitious search for technosignatures, or
evidence of extraterrestrial technology, is carrying out surveys across the sky
and across the electromagnetic spectrum, in images, spectra, time series,
catalogs, and alerts. Classical techniques still play a major role in the
search, but the recent rapid growth in the capabilities of artificial
intelligence is enabling new and more powerful algorithms and approaches to be
deployed across enormous datasets. Surveys designed to look for technosignatures
are still a major focus for the Breakthrough Listen program, but excitingly, the
new strategies also enable comprehensive mining of archives and of general-
purpose real-time data streams. We outline how artificial intelligence and
machine learning are being used to scale up the search, including with the
newly-commissioned Vera C. Rubin Observatory, in addition to radio telescopes
around the planet. The chances of a detection have never been better.
