Title: eCAS: An Orbital Embedding Method for Quantum Computing
Authors: Anna Brünisholz, Markus Reiher
ID: 2610.41517
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41517

Abstract:
Future fault-tolerant quantum computers will allow us to calculate electronic
structures of atomistic systems. However, these systems will be intrinsically
limited in size, requiring embedding theories for many systems because of the
unfavorable scaling of the number of terms describing the electron-electron
interaction in the second-quantized electronic Hamiltonian. Quantum embedding
theories address this challenge by partitioning a quantum system into a region
of interest, the impurity, and its (larger) complement, the environment.
However, currently available quantum embedding methods place restrictive demands
on near-future quantum computers, such as the need to measure reduced density
matrices or the need for repeated evaluations. Here, we introduce the new
orbital embedding method eCAS that steers clear of these pitfalls by
constructing a multi-CAS problem, the active spaces of which are localized and
can be solved independently of each other with any quantum algorithm. This also
allows for the development of comparatively simple gradient expressions. eCAS
leverages the Schmidt decomposition to construct initial impurity and bath
orbitals and augments them with localized environment orbitals, based on a
mutual correlation criterion. The mutual correlation criterion makes eCAS
systematically improvable without the need for any iteratively determined
quantities.
