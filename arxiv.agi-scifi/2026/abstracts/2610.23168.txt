Title: Consistency Checks for Λ positive Quantum Cosmology
Authors: S. P. Miao, N. C. Tsamis, R. P. Woodard
ID: 2610.23168
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23168

Abstract:
In this paper we improve and further analyze the recently derived quantum
gravity equations which apply in accelerating cosmological spacetimes and whose
solutions re-sum leading logarithms from all orders of perturbation theory. We
show the consistency of these upgraded equations against 1-loop non-trivial
exact perturbative computations. The upgraded equations retain their remarkably
simple form.
