Title: DriveDreamer-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement of Driving Perception and Agentic Data Generation
Authors: Dingyi Yao, Tingdong Yu, Guosheng Zhao, Chaojun Ni, Xiaofeng Wang, Jiming Mao, Guan Huang, Lihui Peng, Zheng Zhu
ID: 2611.30671
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30671

Abstract:
Controllable generation can expand training data for driving perception, but
visual realism alone does not ensure learning gains. Effective generation must
adapt both what data to generate and how to construct them as the perception
model evolves. In this paper, we present DriveDreamer-RSI, a framework for
recursive self-improvement of driving perception and agentic data generation
through feedback between perception learning and data construction. Its outer
loop implements Gap-Guided Target Adaptation, generating labeled videos from
current perception gaps and updating targets after training. Its inner loop uses
a vision-language agent to iteratively edit scenes through observation and
execution feedback. Across rounds, Learning-Feedback-Driven Strategy Refinement
derives reusable construction advice from execution experience and downstream
learning outcomes. On the nuScenes validation set, DriveDreamer-RSI surpasses
the dataset-replay baseline by 4.09 mAP and 4.13 NDS points using approximately
half the synthetic frames.
