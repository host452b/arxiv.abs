Title: Differentiable Digital Twins for Optimization, Calibration, and Characterization of Neutral-Atom- and Ion-based Quantum Computers
Authors: Marco Rossignolo, Lorena C. Bianchet, Anurag Saha Roy, Shai Machnes
ID: 2611.09779
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09779

Abstract:
Quantum technologies based on individually controlled atoms are among the most
promising platforms for scalable quantum computing, owing to their favorable
coherence properties and controllability. Nonetheless, achieving reliable and
high-fidelity gate operations remains challenging due to the sensitivity of
these systems to noise and hardware imperfections. In this work, we introduce a
comprehensive software toolset designed to model atomic quantum systems with
high precision and simulate qubit gate dynamics. By integrating quantum optimal
control and benchmarking techniques with automatic differentiation for efficient
and accurate gradient-based optimization, the toolset enables significant
improvements in gate performance. We demonstrate its capabilities through
detailed case studies involving both neutral atoms and trapped ions,
highlighting its effectiveness in enhancing quantum gate fidelities, and
providing valuable insights for experimental implementation.
