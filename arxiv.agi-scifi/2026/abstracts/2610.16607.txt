Title: From Earth to Mars and Beyond: AI-Driven Cosmic-Ray Validation and Prediction with CorsikaAgent
Authors: Zhuo Chen, Zhizheng Zhao, Jinhong Shen, Leyun Gao, Cheng-en Liu, Qite Li, Zijian Wang, Qiang Li
ID: 2610.16607
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.16607

Abstract:
We calculate the sea-level cosmic-ray muon flux with CORSIKA and develop
CorsikaAgent, an auditable large-language-model-based system for configuring,
executing, and validating air-shower simulations, and for predicting particle
fluxes in unexplored environments. The physics calculation uses proton primaries
described by the solar-minimum spectrum of Papini et al., with UrQMD at low
interaction energies and either Sibyll 2.3e or QGSJET-III at high energies. The
predicted momentum spectra at approximately 0 and 75 degrees are compared with
experimental measurements of the atmospheric muon flux and with commonly used
analytic parametrizations. The two interaction-model configurations give similar
spectra and describe the principal momentum and zenith-angle dependences of the
data. The agent independently reconstructs the same observable through a
different sampling strategy and keeps every steering card, executable, seed,
log, and analysis script bound to the result it produced; its output is
consistent with the direct calculation at the level resolved by the present
comparison. Transferred to Mars, the workflow reproduces the CORSIKA 8
benchmark, and transferred to Venus it produces the first prediction of the
surface muon flux, an environment for which no measurement exists. The agent
thus extends the workflow from auditable reproduction to first-principles
prediction: it autonomously reconfigures the simulation(atmosphere, magnetic
field, electric field)for new environments, while the proton-only primary model,
limited high-momentum statistics, and an incomplete systematic-uncertainty
treatment bound the physical precision that can currently be claimed.
