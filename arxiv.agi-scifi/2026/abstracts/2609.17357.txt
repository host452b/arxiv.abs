Title: A Spatiotemporal Extension of the Neuromorphic DBSCAN Implementation
Authors: Charles P. Rizzo, James S. Plank
ID: 2609.17357
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17357

Abstract:
DBSCAN is an algorithm that denoises and clusters data. In prior work, we
implemented the DBSCAN algorithm neuromorphically, introducing two constructions
termed ``flat'' and ``systolic''. The ``flat'' construction prioritizes
throughput, while the ``systolic'' construction trades time for space resulting
in a smaller, more hardware-friendly architecture at the cost of throughput. In
this work, we offer spatiotemporal extensions of these two constructions to
better leverage the spatiotemporal nature of event sensor data. Moreover, as in
our prior work, we discuss partial or segmented implementations that further
leverage time for space when hardware resources are constrained. All network
constructions are provided as open-source implementations.
