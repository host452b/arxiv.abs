Title: A Theory-Guided LLM Pedagogical Agent Supporting Open-Ended STEM+C Learning
Authors: Clayton Cohn, Surya Rayala, Siyuan Guo, Hanchen David Wang, Naveeduddin Mohammed, Umesh Timalsina, Shruti Jain, Ryan Li, Angela Eeds, Menton Deweese et al.
ID: 2605.30539
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30539

Abstract:
Open-ended learning environments (OELEs) can support STEM+C (science,
technology, engineering, mathematics, and computing) inquiry through activities
such as computational modeling, which requires students to translate
disciplinary knowledge across domains and coordinate scientific concepts with
computational representations. However, this is often challenging for students,
many of whom require support during problem solving. In this exploratory study,
we present CoSMoAgent (Computational Science Modeling Agent), a GPT-5-based
multi-agent knowledgeable peer for high-school kinematics modeling grounded in
the Evidence-Decision-Feedback (EDF) framework. CoSMoAgent goes beyond dialogue:
it infers students' strategies, learner states, and knowledge gaps from
environment logs and evolving model states. These inferences guide dialogue
policies that prioritize probing and hints over direct answers. We deployed
CoSMoAgent with 11 dyads across three modeling tasks (33 dyad-sessions), using a
mixed-methods design to examine learning outcomes, processes, and adaptive
scaffolding. As task mastery increased, students verbalized their understanding
more successfully and sought help less often, and the agent's policies shifted
from probing toward suggesting actions and extending reasoning. Two contrasting
case-study dyads followed markedly different problem-solving paths, yet achieved
similar learning gains despite substantial differences in task mastery and
CoSMoAgent interaction. Students valued the agent's questions but were often
frustrated by its withholding of answers. The study contributes a design pattern
and classroom evidence for LLM scaffolding grounded in environmental activity
rather than chat alone within an open-ended modeling environment.
