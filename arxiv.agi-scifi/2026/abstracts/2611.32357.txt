Title: CriticJEPA: World Models Are Better Critics Than Optimizers
Authors: Xvyuan Liu, Jianjie Fang, Honglin Zhang, Zhaolu Wang, Xin Zhang, Wei Wu, Fengli Xu, Chen Gao, Yong Li
ID: 2611.32357
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32357

Abstract:
Visual world models enable goal-directed planning by predicting action outcomes.
Gradient-based planners optimize actions to lower predicted goal cost, but
continued optimization can exploit prediction errors after execution stops
improving. Comparing plans requires informative relative scores; improving them
also requires reliable update directions. Exact-state execution reveals that a
frozen hierarchical world model ranks goal-conditioned proposals even when its
rankings among outputs of prolonged gradient search are uninformative. Effective
planning pairs goal-directed candidates with predictions that distinguish their
outcomes. We introduce CriticJEPA, which learns goal-conditioned flow proposals
from reward-free trajectories and uses frozen rollouts to select among them at
every hierarchy level. Forward selection forms a complete planner; short
anchored updates refine candidates before selection. Across visual navigation
and manipulation, CriticJEPA increases success at a fraction of the planning
compute, transferring shared settings across independently trained models,
hierarchy depths, and unseen tasks while keeping every world-model weight fixed.
