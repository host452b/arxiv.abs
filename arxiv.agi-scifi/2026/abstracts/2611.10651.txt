Title: The Physics of Interstellar Travel
Authors: Coryn A. L. Bailer-Jones
ID: 2611.10651
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10651

Abstract:
How can we send an uncrewed spacecraft to the nearest stars within a human
lifetime? What propulsion methods are available? Will it survive impacts in the
interstellar medium? What science will it do at its destination? How can it send
back data over enormous distances? In this short semi-popular article, I examine
various aspects of the physics of interstellar travel, its challenges and
possible solutions. I focus on nuclear rockets, laser sails, dust impacts, and
communication. These, as well as other issues such as navigation and onboard
power, are explored in more detail in my book, The Physics of Interstellar
Travel (2026).
