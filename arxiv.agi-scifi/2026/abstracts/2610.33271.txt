Title: Neuromorphic Decision Dynamics for Deadlock-free Safe Navigation in Congested Environments
Authors: Himani Sinhmar, Vaibhav Srivastava, Naomi Ehrich Leonard
ID: 2610.33271
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33271

Abstract:
Safe navigation in congested environments requires both collision avoidance and
coordinated passage. We introduce neuromorphic decision dynamics (NDD), a
decentralized, event-based controller, that provides navigation in congested
spaces with guarantees on safety, liveness, robustness, and scalability. NDD
makes safe-and-live deconfliction a controllable, locally tunable emergent
property of local agent interactions, without requiring communication,
preassigned priorities, models of neighbors' objectives or future actions, or
estimates of group size or global network properties. By design, NDD couples the
dynamics of internal decision and attention states with changing physical states
and locally observed relative motion. Each agent continuously modulates its
speed along its prescribed path, responding as soon and as much as conflict
resolution requires. Agents self-organize into disjoint, locally engaged groups
that resolve conflicts concurrently. When any group's conflict becomes safety-
critical, decision-action feedback destabilizes collective indecision, and
coordinated conflict-resolution emerges that stabilizes a safe traversal order
within the group. Order persists as conflicts clear, but groups may split or
merge as agents autonomously adapt to new conflicts and those with resolved
conflicts resume their nominal motion. We derive explicit conditions on sensing
range, feedback gains, response time scales, and actuation limits that guarantee
collision avoidance and traversal-order formation in finite time with no
deadlock or livelock for finite, heterogeneous agent populations of arbitrary,
changing size. Computational cost scales with the number of local interactions
sensed by each agent. Large-scale simulations, comparisons with existing
approaches, and hardware experiments demonstrate NDD in complex congested
scenarios and validate its scalability.
