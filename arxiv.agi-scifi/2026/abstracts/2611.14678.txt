Title: CosmoSim: Lens-parameter estimation of the Einstein Cross NGC 6505 using machine learning
Authors: Ben David Normann, Oda Hjemli, Hans Georg Schaathun
ID: 2611.14678
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14678

Abstract:
CosmoSim is an open-source tool for simulating gravitational lenses and
reconstructing lens mass distributions, either by ray tracing a parametric lens
model or through a Roulette expansion of the lens potential. It is also used to
generate the large, labelled synthetic image sets needed to train machine-
learning (ML) parameter estimators. We present the first application of CosmoSim
to real data. A convolutional neural network trained only on CosmoSim images
estimates Singular Isothermal Ellipsoid lens and source parameters directly from
observed images. We test the pipeline on 21 images of 19 strong lenses from
Euclid and the AGEL survey, after removing the foreground lens light with a
custom decontamination tool built on AstroPy. As a case study we reconstruct the
Einstein Cross NGC 6505 both from the lens model published by the discovery team
and from parameters predicted by our ML model. Both reconstructions recover the
observed four-image structure, and the ML-based reconstruction performs at least
as well as the literature-based one: it scores better on masked structural
similarity, intersection-over-union and Chamfer distance, with an essentially
equal Hausdorff distance. Across the ensemble, the predicted Einstein radii are
consistent with catalogued values within the quoted uncertainties (mean relative
difference 0.08 for Euclid and 0.19 for AGEL lenses), and the enclosed masses
agree to within a median of 0.12 dex. The predicted ellipticities differ
systematically from the catalogued values, while the comparison of orientations
is inconclusive. These results establish CosmoSim as a tool that can be applied
directly to observational data.
