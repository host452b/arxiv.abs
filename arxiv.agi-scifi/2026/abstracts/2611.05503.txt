Title: Neuromorphic Camera for Multi-Target Pointing Acquisition and Tracking in Free Space Optical Communication
Authors: Francesco Nardo, Nigitha Selvaraj, Klaus Schertler, Peter Krötz, Ricardo Barrios, Sebastian Houben, Sebastian Randel
ID: 2611.05503
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05503

Abstract:
Pointing, Acquisition, and Tracking (PAT) in Free-Space Optical Communication
(FSOC) applications must achieve both low jitter and a large Field of View (FoV)
to support beacon acquisition and multi-target tracking. While conventional
frame-based sensors provide the necessary angular coverage, their performance is
limited by latency, available frame-rate, and motion blur. This work explores
and demonstrates the application of an event-based vision sensor for PAT tasks
in FSOC. The system employs a neuromorphic sensor that asynchronously records
brightness changes (events) with microsecond-level temporal resolution. The main
contribution of this paper is a comparative analysis of event-based tracking
algorithms for the simultaneous acquisition and tracking of multiple optical
beacons that operate directly on this asynchronous stream. The performance of
these strategies is validated on an experimental dataset representing realistic
operational conditions for dynamic High-Altitude Platform (HAP) and Unmanned
Aerial Vehicle (UAV) networks. The analysis compares key metrics, such as
tracking accuracy and processing latency, under various signal-to-background
conditions.
