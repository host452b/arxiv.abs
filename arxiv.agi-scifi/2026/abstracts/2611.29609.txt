Title: JEPA-Shield: Safe Diffusion Planning with Latent World Models via Conformal Shielding
Authors: Taekyung Kim, Ege Yuceel, Steven Hong, Georgios Fainekos, Dimitra Panagou, Bardh Hoxha
ID: 2611.29609
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29609

Abstract:
Latent world models enable planning from images, but their predictions do not
directly establish whether a robot will satisfy physical constraints. We present
JEPA-Shield, which connects a frozen action-conditioned Joint-Embedding
Predictive Architecture (JEPA) world model to task-space safety enforcement. Two
downstream models predict relative motion from imagined latent rollouts and
decode unsafe geometry from the current image. Split conformal prediction turns
end-to-end motion-prediction errors into multi-horizon uncertainty tubes. A
backup-based shield checks these tubes against physical constraints and screens
both candidate trajectories and final proposals in diffusion-inspired sampling-
based model predictive control. Under the stated exchangeability and initial-
backup assumptions, we establish probabilistic constraint satisfaction at
action-block boundaries for a returned plan executed without replanning. We also
introduce VParkBench, with geometric car and tractor-trailer parking tasks and a
editable photorealistic Gaussian-splatting environment. In closed-loop
evaluation, JEPA-Shield achieves success rates of 84.4%, 82.3%, and 99.0%,
respectively, with no observed constraint violations. Ablations show
complementary benefits from shielding and uncertainty calibration. The results
demonstrate the importance of grounding latent world-model predictions in task-
space quantities and explicitly accounting for prediction uncertainty in safety-
critical planning.
