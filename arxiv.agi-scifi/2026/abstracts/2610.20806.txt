Title: Four-dimensional QCD equation of state with a critical surface from contours of constant entropy density
Authors: Tristan Gyure, Hitansh Shah, Lorenzo Formaggio, Francesco Di Clemente, Mauricio Hippert, Claudia Ratti, Volodymyr Vovchenko
ID: 2610.20806
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20806

Abstract:
We present a new lattice-QCD-based equation of state in the four-dimensional
space of $(T,μ_B,μ_Q,μ_S)$ which features a critical surface. This is obtained
by generalizing the expansion of constant entropy density contours across the
multiple conserved charges of QCD. Since this expansion permits the presence of
a first-order phase transition, this is the first lattice QCD based equation of
state in four dimensions with a critical surface. We parametrize the chemical
potential space using spherical coordinates $(μ,θ,\varphi)$, reducing the
problem to a one-dimensional extrapolation in $(T,μ)$ at fixed angles that,
together, span the full 4D space. By integrating the extrapolated entropy
density, we reconstruct the pressure, from which we obtain the conserved charge
densities, their susceptibilities and the speed of sound. Our results agree
quantitatively with other lattice QCD constructions at low chemical potentials
in the crossover region, and predict the emergence of a first-order phase
transition at higher chemical potentials. We study equation of state properties
in detail under heavy-ion collision (HIC) conditions for the strangeness and
electric charge densities ($n_S=0,~n_Q=0.4n_B$), where we find the CP location
at $(T_c,μ_{B,c},μ_{Q,c},μ_{S,c})=(115\pm7,646\pm72,-12\pm3,117\pm16)$ MeV. In
particular, we find a strong non-monotonic dependence of baryon number
susceptibilities along the chemical freeze-out line at $\sqrt{s_{NN}} \lesssim
7.7$ GeV. Finally, we outline further applications of the 4D equation of state,
including charge-neutral matter relevant to neutron stars and large isospin
density relevant to cosmic trajectories in the early Universe.
