Title: Synthesis Smuggling: Maliciously Hiding Inferred Personal Attributes in LLM Outputs
Authors: Gautam Galada, Supreeth Shastri, Sagnik Ray Choudhury
ID: 2611.17280
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17280

Abstract:
We propose Synthesis Smuggling, a novel threat model in which an adversarial LLM
infers sensitive information about a user from routine assistance-seeking
interactions and steganographically encodes that into innocuous-looking model-
generated content. The user may then unknowingly transmit that information by
sharing the generated artifact, say as an email, social-media post, or message,
through ordinary communication channels. An adversary monitoring these channels
can discover this information. We demonstrate this threat by training two open-
weight models to infer gender and age-group labels from conversational cues and
encode their predictions using invisible characters or word choices. These
results show that user-shared model outputs can become a covert channel for
personal inferences, even when the model itself has no direct means of
contacting an adversary.
