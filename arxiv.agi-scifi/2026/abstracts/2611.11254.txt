Title: Probing the Supersymmetric Grand Unified Theories with Anomaly Mediation at the Future Proton-Proton Colliders and Hyper-Kamiokande Experiment
Authors: Waqas Ahmed, Tianjun Li, Shabbar Raza
ID: 2611.11254
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11254

Abstract:
With the grand desert hypothesis, we investigate the Supersymmetric (SUSY) Grand
Unified Theories (GUTs) with the minimal anomaly mediated supersymmetry breaking
(AMSB) as a continuation of our program of studying SUSY GUTs at the future 100
TeV proton-proton Collider such as $\rm FCC_{hh}$ and SppC as well as proton-
decay experiment such as Hyper-Kamiokande experiment. We show that for the GUT
scale $M_{GUT} \ge 0.84 \times 10^{16}$ GeV, the upper mass bounds on the gluino
and first two generation squarks are 8 TeV and 6 TeV, respectively. Therefore,
the gluino and first two generation squarks can be probed at the $\rm FCC_{hh}$
and SppC. In addition, we discuss the mass spectra for the colored sparticles,
sleptons, pseudo-scalar Higgs and electroweakinos. We also shed light on the
possible search strategies of gluino and winos at the future 100 TeV colliders.
Moreover, for $\rm M_{\rm GUT} \le 0.84 \times 10^{16}$ GeV, the proton lifetime
for dimension-six proton decay via heavy gauge boson exchange is less than or
around $5 \times 10^{34}$ years, and thus we can probe it at the Hyper-
Kamiokande experiment. To be concrete, we present a couple of benchmark points
representing the above two mentioned GUT conditions on $\rm M_{\rm GUT}$.
