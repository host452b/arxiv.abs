Title: $Z$-sight: Unifying Vision-Language-Action and Latent World Modeling
Authors: Junjie Ye, Yiqi Zhao, Muchen Xu, Celina Shiyu Wang, Hao Dong, Ruohai Ge, Rong Xue, Benjamin J Kolligs, Aditya Dutt, Sasanka Nagavalli et al.
ID: 2611.24940
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24940

Abstract:
We introduce $Z$-sight, a unified robot foundation model that couples vision-
language-action (VLA) and generative latent world modeling. Video-based world
action models (WAMs) show the value of joint future-and-action generation for
control, yet inherit the heavy cost of video generators. Recent latent-space
policies avoid pixel generation, yet typically align intermediate features or
rely on frozen target encoders, decoupling future prediction from the VLM's
native feature space. $Z$-sight instead predicts the future directly in the
visual token space in which its VLM perceives the present. Coupling a foresight
expert and a continuous action expert with the VLM in a mixture-of-transformers,
$Z$-sight jointly denoises future spatial tokens and actions at inference
without pixel reconstruction or external encoders. Trained on human video and
robot trajectories, $Z$-sight achieves 81.9% success on SIMPLE (+9.6 points over
the strongest baseline) and 73.3% across six Unitree G1 tasks (+20.0 points) at
110 ms per chunk, while remaining competitive on tabletop benchmarks.
