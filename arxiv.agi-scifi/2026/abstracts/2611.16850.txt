Title: A scaling law for numerical computation of fracture stress intensity factors
Authors: Ronghao Cui, Jan Martin Nordbotten, Ivar Stefansson, Inga Berre
ID: 2611.16850
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16850

Abstract:
Numerical simulations of fracture propagation require accurate computation of
stress intensity factors (SIFs) to determine whether a pre-existing fracture
will propagate. Even though displacement solutions from well-established
numerical methods generally converge with mesh refinement, the convergence
behavior of SIF estimates remains less understood. In this work, we propose a
scaling law to improve the performance of SIF extraction using two-point
displacement correlation methods. This scaling law relates sampling distance
from the fracture tip to mesh resolution. It is developed by balancing the
asymptotic truncation error against the numerical discretization error. Three
numerical discretization methods--standard quadratic finite elements, quadratic
finite elements with quarter-point elements, and a multi-point stress
approximation finite volume method--are employed to validate the proposed
scaling law on fracture benchmark problems under pure-mode and mixed-mode
loading. We find that the conventional nearest-tip sampling strategy fails to
compute convergent SIF estimates. In contrast, the proposed scaling-based
approach yields decreasing errors in the SIF estimates as the mesh is refined.
Across all three discretization methods, the proposed scaling law captures the
sampling locations required for convergent SIF extraction, suggesting a general
numerical characteristic of near-tip SIF extraction.
