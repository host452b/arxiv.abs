Title: Benchmarking AI risk behaviors
Authors: Luhan Mikaelson, Robert Adragna, Emmett Brosowsky, Hayley Clatterbuck
ID: 2611.16318
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16318

Abstract:
We evaluate LLM risk propensities across a range of contexts and task demands.
We evaluate models for how risk averse or risk tolerant they are in various
conditions, as well as how stable and consistent their behavior is across
conditions. We present a toolkit for developing model risk profiles that can be
used to predict model behavior and which can be adapted to include new
evaluations of risk propensities. We also examine how these properties have
changed across model generations and families. We observe that 2026 state-of-
the-art models tend to be more stable, more consistent, and more risk neutral
than older models. These trends appear to be related; newer models are more
stable and consistent because they are more likely to use expected value
maximization as a decision procedure.
