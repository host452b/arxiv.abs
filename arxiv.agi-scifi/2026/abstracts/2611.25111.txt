Title: DSFlow: A Neuro-Symbolic Data Workflow Agent with Statistical Diagnostic Guardrails
Authors: Junfeng Nie, Xiaohui Chen
ID: 2611.25111
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25111

Abstract:
Data science workflows often require tools that presume non-trivial technical
knowledge. Large language models (LLMs) reduce this barrier, but a gap remains
between flexible general-purpose agents and statistically controlled pipelines.
We introduce DSFlow, a LangGraph-based neuro-symbolic data-agent platform that
combines reusable, LLM-selected skills with deterministic diagnostic guardrails.
Skills provide extensible task knowledge. Symbolic validation can accept a
proposed method, request clarification or replanning, override an unsuitable
method, or supply a safe fallback. Regression and clustering case studies
illustrate how DSFlow uses diagnostic evidence to correct method choices and
documents those decisions in auditable reports.
