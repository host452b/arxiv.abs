Title: Temporary gas turbines powering a US AI data center created a cross-state air pollution hotspot
Authors: Will W. Qiang, Bo Huang, Xingjian Liu, Harry F. Lee
ID: 2611.06115
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06115

Abstract:
Data centers increasingly use on-site gas turbines that regulators can treat as
temporary. Two satellites show the fleet powering SpaceXAI's Colossus 2 raised
local nitrogen dioxide columns by about half as it expanded, the most anomalous
increase among 656 urban locations nationwide since 2020. Mississippi authorized
the fleet, but two-thirds of the hotspot's 116,000 mostly Black residents live
in Tennessee, and the hotspot contains no regulatory nitrogen dioxide monitor.
The fleet causes an estimated four premature deaths yearly, valued at $49
million, over 80% outside Mississippi and nearly all from units without
selective catalytic reduction. On new turbines, this control's benefits exceed
costs over the service life, but not over temporary stays or for in-state
damages alone. Standards following the equipment could recover these benefits.
