Title: Holography at finite resolution: zero-point length in AdS/CFT
Authors: Ankit Anand, Kimet Jusufi, Amir A. Khodahami, Adrinil Paul, Ahmad Sheykhi
ID: 2611.24779
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24779

Abstract:
We explore the zero-point length $l_0$ of T-duality-inspired effective field
theory in holography, distinguishing two readings. As a boundary resolution
scale, $l_0$ filters the source of a bulk scalar in anti-de Sitter space and
yields an exactly solvable boundary response that is ultraviolet finite without
a hard cutoff and inherits the power-law tails of the zero-point-length kernel.
We show that this filter is the source-normalized bulk-to-boundary transfer
function of a $ν=1$ scalar evaluated at radial depth $z=l_0$. Anchoring Ryu-
Takayanagi surfaces on the same radial surface gives finite entanglement
entropies that retain the perimeter law and the universal sphere free energy.
For two intervals in AdS$_3$, the prescription yields the mutual-information
connectivity transition in closed form for arbitrary interval lengths; equal
intervals maximize the connectivity window at fixed total length, and in the
subresolution regime the critical separation exhibits a universal scaling law
with a rescaled mutual information collapsing onto a piecewise-linear profile.
As an AdS-invariant bulk proper length, $l_0$ renders the one-loop tadpole
finite while preserving the conformal form of boundary two-point functions. Its
exact Källén-Lehmann decomposition, however, has negative continuum weight
beginning at threshold, obstructing a unitary interpretation of this propagator.
The ratio $L/l_0$ identifies a crossover scale of the form factor rather than a
derived spectrum of a unitary completion.
