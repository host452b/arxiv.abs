Title: Quantum Computing Enhanced Search for Wavelike Dark Matter
Authors: Yunjia Bao, Yu Cheng, Liang Jiang, Lian-Tao Wang
ID: 2611.14629
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14629

Abstract:
Identifying a weak, stochastic signal hidden within a broad frequency band is a
central challenge in the search for wavelike dark matter. We explore quantum
computing enhanced sensing based on the Grover search algorithm to address this
problem and find that a long integration time stabilizes the accumulated sensing
phase, reducing quantum signal processing (QSP) errors induced by dark matter
fluctuations. We further propose alternating the signs of the QSP angles between
oracle calls to suppress the coherent accumulation of the intrinsic QSP
approximation errors during the search. Combining these strategies in a
transmon-based benchmark, we obtain a projected sensitivity to axion dark matter
beyond existing astrophysical limits. This opens access to substantial
unexplored parameter space across the $10$-$100\,μ\mathrm{eV}$ mass range and
offers an accelerated discovery channel for wavelike dark matter. With $10^6$
entangled sensing qubits, the projected sensitivity reaches the QCD axion band.
Although illustrated with transmons, the protocol can be adapted to other
experimental platforms for wavelike dark matter detection.
