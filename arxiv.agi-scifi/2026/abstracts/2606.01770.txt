Title: Adaptive Auto-Harness: Sustained Self-Improvement for Agentic System Deployment on Open-Ended Task Streams
Authors: Zewen Liu, Zhan Shi, Yisi Sang, Bing He, Minhua Lin, Tianxin Wei, Dakuo Wang, Benoit Dumoulin, Wei Jin, Hanqing Lu
ID: 2606.01770
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.01770

Abstract:
Auto-harness systems improve LLM agents by adapting prompts, skills, tools, and
supporting infrastructure from execution feedback. Sustaining these gains on
open-ended task streams requires handling growing histories, heterogeneous
tasks, and shifting distributions. We introduce Adaptive Auto-Harness, a unified
framework that separates persistent evolution workspaces from deployable
harnesses and supports interchangeable evolution procedures. We compare
configurations adapted from five prior approaches on recorded streams of
prediction markets, security challenges, and event forecasts. With the same
Sonnet solver, our full system reaches 80.9\% market all-task accuracy and
50.2\% security Pass@1, versus 22.2\% and 37.2\% without evolution; market
accuracy also exceeds a market-following control's 72.0\%. Our analyses show
that artifact accumulation does not guarantee sustained gains, and knowledge
that transfers across tasks can still lose value as the task distribution
changes. Continued evolution can therefore help preserve earlier gains through
revision of accumulated knowledge. Code: https://github.com/A-EVO-Lab/a-evolve.
