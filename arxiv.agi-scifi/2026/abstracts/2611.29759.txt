Title: What Should World Models Plan Over? Learning Geometry-Aligned Representations of Body Motion
Authors: Renhao Zhang, Abhranil Chandra, Bruno Castro da Silva
ID: 2611.29759
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29759

Abstract:
We introduce MotionPort, which learns low-dimensional latent representations of
body motion for accurate dynamics prediction and effective long-horizon
planning. Existing world-model planners often search over action representations
learned for prediction, yet we show that predictive accuracy alone does not
ensure that optimization can discover successful trajectories. MotionPort
addresses this mismatch by representing body trajectories rather than action
chunks and organizing the search space by motion similarity, helping predicted
improvements translate into successful execution. Without fine-tuning state
encoders, MotionPort raises average goal-reaching success from 37.9% to 87.8%
across three backbones and seven settings in four domains. On the evaluated
AntMaze-OGBench tasks, it achieves 72% success, compared with 4% for the
strongest baseline.
