Title: Enhancing Mixed Oil Length Prediction through Graph Representation Learning
Authors: Xinyi Hong, Hai Shu, Zhaoyang Zhang, Lei Chen, Yanfeng Yang, Ziqi Chen
ID: 2611.20660
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20660

Abstract:
During the pipeline transportation of refined oil products, the inevitable
formation of mixed oil segments poses considerable economic challenges to
pipeline networks. Therefore, it is important to accurately predict the length
of these mixed oil segments. Although previous methods have achieved relatively
accurate predictions by integrating machine and deep learning algorithms, they
ignore the statistical dependencies that remain among real-world mixed oil
samples, which degrades model accuracy. To address this issue, we propose a
novel Sample Dependencies Incorporated Graph Representation Learning (SDI-GRL)
model. By representing samples and pipeline features as nodes of a graph, SDI-
GRL connects dependent samples through their shared features, allowing message
passing to propagate information across related samples. This graph-based
framework allows our model to capture the statistical dependencies among samples
absent in prior works. A case study on a mixed oil dataset demonstrates that our
SDI-GRL model surpasses the strongest existing approach, reducing both the RMSE
and the MAE by more than 10%.
