Title: Semiclassical Scaling Laws of Magnus Quantum Algorithms under Harmonic Confinement
Authors: Di Fang, Samuel Orellana Mateo
ID: 2611.17055
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17055

Abstract:
The simulation of semiclassical Schrödinger dynamics is often hindered by rapid
oscillations, where classical numerical methods and quantum simulation
algorithms can suffer severe precision loss as the semiclassical parameter
$\varepsilon \to 0$. In this work, we uncover a surprising robustness of high-
order Magnus quantum algorithms in the semiclassical regime. We establish two
important results. First, we extend the theory of Magnus superconvergence from
free-particle reference dynamics to a general class of Schrödinger operators
whose reference Hamiltonian includes harmonic confinement. Second, and more
importantly, we reveal a nontrivial semiclassical scaling law: while the
expansion remainder term bound suggests an error prefactor of
$O(\varepsilon^{-(p+1)})$ for a $p$th-order method, we prove that the loss is in
fact order-independent and collapses to $O(\varepsilon^{-1})$ for all $p$. The
key analytical mechanism is the exact Egorov transport associated with quadratic
flows in semiclassical Weyl quantization, which enables a symbol-level analysis
of the interaction-picture Hamiltonian and its Magnus commutators. Our results
show that high-order Magnus quantum algorithms remain far more robust in the
semiclassical regime than naive series remainder term counting would suggest.
