Title: Event-based analysis of neuromorphic feedback systems
Authors: Alessandro Cecconi, Michelangelo Bin, Lorenzo Marconi, Rodolphe Sepulchre
ID: 2611.13832
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13832

Abstract:
This article investigates the existence and stability of periodic behavior
arising in the feedback interconnection of neuromorphic systems, which evolve
according to continuous dynamics but interact only through discrete events.
Exploiting the event-driven nature of this interaction, each system is modeled
by an operator that maps the history of events to the delay of its response.
Building on the fading-memory properties of these operators, the article
develops a small-gain result establishing the existence, uniqueness, and
robustness of periodic one-to-one rhythms.
