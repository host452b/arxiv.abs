Title: Spiking Neuromorphic Control for Periodic Orbits Stabilization in Underactuated Robots: A Greedy Lyapunov-Based Approach
Authors: Maksim Surov, Oleg Sumenkov
ID: 2610.37310
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37310

Abstract:
This work investigates the use of event-based spiking controllers, implemented
as spiking neural networks, for trajectory tracking in underactuated robots.
Specifically, we address the problem of stabilizing a prescribed periodic orbit.
It is well known that, by applying the method of transverse linearization to the
robot dynamics in a neighborhood of a periodic trajectory, the dynamics can be
approximately described by a linear time-varying system with periodic
coefficients (a periodic LTV system). The problem of orbital stabilization can
therefore be reduced to stabilizing the trivial solution of this LTV system. We
show that a previously proposed Lyapunov-based greedy design, originally
developed for LTI systems, can also be extended to the corresponding linearized
transverse dynamics, which take the form of a periodic LTV system. This enables
the construction of a feedback controller for orbital stabilization of the
robot. The effectiveness of the proposed method is demonstrated on the
trajectory stabilization problem for the butterfly robot.
