Title: A Machine Learning Approach to Finslerian Cosmological Dark Energy Model
Authors: Shruti Seth, R. Chaubey
ID: 2610.26882
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26882

Abstract:
Modified extensions of the standard cosmological models often introduce complex
parameter degeneracies that severely impact sampling convergence of the
parameter space while analyzing the model observationally. In this work, we
present an accelerated Bayesian inference technique using a Heteroscedastic Deep
Ensemble with Monte Carlo Dropout incorporating distance-gated local residual
corrections to emulate complex likelihood surfaces and avoid extrapolation
beyond the trained region. We demonstrate this methodology on a unified dark
energy cosmological model equipped with Finsler geometry. The model is studied
using the recent observational datasets, including Cosmic Chronometers (CC),
Pantheon$+$SH$0$ES, and DESI DR$2$ BAO measurements. While the conventional
sampling procedure struggles with slow mixing of parameter chains
($τ_{max}=1578.3$), the neural network, specially trained densely in high
likelihood regions, yields converged and highly resolved posterior distributions
of the model parameters without significantly compromising the minimum chi-
square value. We further discuss the results for physical inferences and also
compare the present dark energy model with $Λ\text{CDM}$ statistically using AIC
and BIC.
