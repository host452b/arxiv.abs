Title: Advancing a Global Framework for AI Safety and Governance for the Well-being of Humanity
Authors: Zeng Yi, Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh, Wang Zhengqi, Lu Enmeng, Chen Yu, Cao Gongce, Guo Xiaoyang, Kang Yanrong, Han Kaiyu, Fan Jinyu et al.
ID: 2611.13968
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13968

Abstract:
The Report "Advancing a Global Framework for AI Safety and Governance for the
Well-being of Humanity" is an important outcome of the AI Safety and Governance
Program of World Internet Conference Specialized Committee on Artificial
Intelligence in 2025. This report provides a systematic review of current global
explorations and practices in AI safety and governance, identifies key issues
that need to be addressed, and draws upon multilateral governance experiences
from other domains. Centered on mechanism design, it explores how to build a
global AI safety and governance system that ensures safety and security,
promotes inclusiveness, clarifies responsibilities and accountabilities,
strengthens effective coordination, and ensures authoritative and efficient
governance. Guided by the principles of multilateralism and the vision of
building a community with a shared future for humankind, the report proposes
institutional measures and policy recommendations to establish a United Nations-
centered global AI safety and governance framework. It aims to serve as a
reference for governments, international organizations, enterprises, and
research institutions. The report is to contribute insights and momentum to
advancing people-centered and beneficial AI governance worldwide, ensuring that
AI technologies truly serve the well-being of all humanity.
