Title: Decomposing the Associative Chain: Schema-Based Mediation Supports Expression and Understanding in Human-AI Music Co-creation
Authors: Songruoyao Wu, Wanying Wu, Shuyu Li, Chongjun Zhong, Ziyi Huang, Kejun Zhang
ID: 2611.19203
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19203

Abstract:
Users cannot reliably translate their creative intents into AI-generated music.
We contend that the root of this difficulty lies in associative divergence:
associations between narratives and musical features are overly remote, and
users lack a visible mediation structure to negotiate them. To address this, we
introduce five core schema groups as cognitive mediation and propose a
bidirectional schema-based framework. Its forward pathway decomposes narrative-
to-music associations into staged mappings, while its reverse pathway provides
structured interpretations from music back to narrative. Findings from two user
studies(N = 188; N = 60) show that the framework led to clearer creative
expression and greater perceived control, improved the comprehensibility of
generated music, especially for unconventional pieces. Furthermore, it supported
cultural and affective thinking while helping users locate associative
divergence. Our work points toward better creative intent alignment in human-AI
music co-creation.
