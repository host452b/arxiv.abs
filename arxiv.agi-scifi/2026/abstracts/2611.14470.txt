Title: TanCRAFT: Context-Reasoned Anticipatory Feedback for Assembly Tasks
Authors: Sassan Mokhtar, Lars Doorenbos, Marius Bock, Sina Mokhtar, Katharina Beckh, Dominik Bach, Juergen Gall
ID: 2611.14470
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14470

Abstract:
Preventing human error in physical assembly tasks requires assistance systems
that can intervene before mistakes are made. This is especially challenging in
settings where users are free to choose the construction order and correctness
depends on the evolving assembly rather than a fixed next step. We present
TanCRAFT, a four-stage real-time predictive assistance system that tracks the
scene state, reasons about the current partial assembly, forecasts the near-
future motion of manipulated pieces, and intervenes when the predicted placement
is incompatible with the target. We instantiate our framework and confirm its
effectiveness in a controlled tangram assembly task through offline benchmarks
and a within-subject user study. Our findings show that the predictive feedback
significantly reduces mistakes relative to unguided and reactive sessions, while
yielding the lowest mean perceived workload and shortest mean completion time.
Overall, these results demonstrate the value of predictive feedback for error
prevention and provide a step toward cost-aware assistance for automation and
human-robot workcells.
