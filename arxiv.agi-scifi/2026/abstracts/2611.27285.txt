Title: SIR-Agent: Skill-Augmented Image Restoration Agent with Recursive Self-Improvement
Authors: Yijian Wang, Qingsen Yan, Weiyu Zhou, Mingde Zhang, Axi Niu, Haokui Zhang, Jiantao Zhou
ID: 2611.27285
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27285

Abstract:
Agents for Image Restoration (IR) aim to leverage Multimodal Large Language
Models (MLLMs) to autonomously perceive image degradation and invoke IR tools.
However, existing IR agents typically rely on fine-tuning MLLM parameters to
adapt to new IR tasks, leading to catastrophic forgetting of IR knowledge. To
address this limitation, we propose a skill-augmented IR agent, dubbed SIR-
Agent, that maintains IR knowledge in a scalable skill library and uses
Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) to facilitate efficient skill-library
evolution. Specifically, we design a Chain Feedback-driven Skill Revision (CFSR)
to evolve the skill library. The CFSR analyzes restoration chains to identify
and revise weak operations in existing skills. The compatibility of a revised
skill with known tasks decides if it replaces the original skill or is appended
independently. Furthermore, to advance CFSR's skill-library evolution, we
present an RSI-driven skill meta-learning mechanism, which identifies CFSR's
inefficient operations and autonomously refines its revision strategy. Extensive
experiments across 14 IR benchmarks, covering 6 single-degradation and 8 mixed-
degradation scenarios, demonstrate SIR-Agent's superior IR performance. Further
evaluation on 7 unseen tasks validates SIR-Agent's generalization to new IR
tasks.
