Title: Quantum computing for plasma physics in magnetic-confinement fusion
Authors: Chenjia Zhu, Shuai Xu, Yousheng Zhang, Yunfeng Liang, Yue Yang
ID: 2610.25885
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25885

Abstract:
Plasma simulations for magnetic-confinement fusion (MCF) involve high-
dimensional kinetic dynamics, strong nonlinear coupling, complex geometries, and
multiscale spatiotemporal behavior, imposing severe computational bottlenecks.
While quantum computing offers a potential alternative for accelerating plasma
simulation problems, its practical advantage for fusion research has not yet
been demonstrated. We review existing quantum computing studies across kinetic
dynamics, wave interactions, equilibrium, and macroscopic stability, evaluating
their hardware implementations and operational bottlenecks. Current studies
remain largely restricted to linearized equations, reduced models, or isolated
computational subroutines, leaving a substantial gap from realistic fusion
simulations. We summarize key problem characteristics, including high
dimensionality, structured dynamics, and measurement requirements, under which
plasma applications are most likely to yield a quantum advantage. Guided by
these considerations, we highlight potential approaches and present a roadmap
toward practical quantum advantage of simulating plasma in MCF.
