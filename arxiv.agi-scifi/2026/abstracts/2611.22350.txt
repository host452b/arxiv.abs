Title: An AI-Driven Assistant for Human-Centric Building Digital Twin Systems: Design, Implementation, and Comparative User Evaluation
Authors: Leonardo Messi, Erika Anneli Pärn, Juan Diego Castaño Molina, Oleksandr Anyshchenko, Dijar Selami, Peihang Luo, Borja García de Soto
ID: 2611.22350
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22350

Abstract:
Smart buildings generate streams of operational data, yet this richness rarely
translates into accessible, decision-ready information for stakeholders who
inhabit and manage them. Current Building Digital Twin Systems (BDTS) often
remain oriented toward technical specialists and rely on static, one-size-fits-
all interfaces. This paper presents the design, implementation, and comparative
user evaluation of an AI-driven conversational assistant integrated into a
human-centric BDTS for Indoor Environmental Quality monitoring, combined with
adaptive information presentation and auto-suggest functionality. The prototype
was deployed at New York University Abu Dhabi. A controlled, two-arm, between-
subjects, quasi-experimental study with wave-based, non-randomized assignment
compared the Baseline interface with the Experimental interface. Results showed
significant reductions in task-completion time and perceived workload, while
accuracy and usability improved but did not meet the adjusted significance
criterion. The study contributes an implemented and empirically evaluated
architecture that separates generative interaction from deterministic
environmental assessment and coordinates conversational responses with adaptive
visual presentation.
