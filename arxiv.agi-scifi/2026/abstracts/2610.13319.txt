Title: Developing Competency in AI Ethics: A training framework
Authors: Alexandra Prégent, Alexei Grinbaum
ID: 2610.13319
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13319

Abstract:
We present a training framework for developing core competencies in the
practical operationalisation of AI ethics: (1) identifying ethical concerns; (2)
determining which standard ethics requirements they implicate; (3) selecting and
prioritising concrete technical and organisational measures to address them; and
(4) recognising ethics tensions and reasoning about trade-offs where such
tensions cannot be fully resolved. The framework is intended for researchers and
engineers in AI, Research Ethics Committee members, policymakers, and students,
providing them with practical tools for translating abstract ethical
requirements into concrete actions. The framework provides a step-by-step
approach organised around four interconnected tasks: issue identification,
requirement mapping, measure selection, and tension identification. These tasks
are supported by a suite of training materials, including an Ethics Checklist
and a Portfolio of Measures. Here, we complement them with a set of practical
exercises of three different types as well as trainer guides. We also discuss
lessons learned in pilot training sessions and offer recommendations for
trainers.
