Title: PACE-WM: Parallel World Modeling with Action History for Visual Planning
Authors: Haishan Liu, Shijie Lian, Bin Yu, Yao Zhang, Changti Wu, Fanping Liu, Jianzheng Liu, Lei Xie, Cong Huang, Kai Chen
ID: 2611.27173
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27173

Abstract:
JEPA-style world models support visual planning by predicting future
representations conditioned on future actions. However, similar observations can
conceal different motion states, making temporal context essential for
interpreting future action effects. We propose PACE-WM, a parallel world model
that uses executed-action history to contextualize future prediction and
planning. Specifically, PACE-WM maintains a recurrent memory of executed actions
and uses this memory to condition a causal Transformer. PACE-WM predicts
multiple future representations in parallel without recursive predicted-state
feedback within each prediction window. We further introduce Reference-Anchored
Minimum Progress (RAMP) for action selection during planning. RAMP scores
candidate actions by their predicted goal improvement along direct and split
paths relative to a shared reference. Across four visual planning tasks, PACE-WM
with RAMP achieves an average success rate of $98\%$ and plans approximately
$7$-$9\times$ faster than autoregressive LeWM under matched CEM settings. We
also construct four controlled benchmarks to evaluate PACE-WM's capabilities in
motion disambiguation, goal-relative motion, and goal adaptation. Project page
is available at https://github.com/LHS180/PACE-WM.
