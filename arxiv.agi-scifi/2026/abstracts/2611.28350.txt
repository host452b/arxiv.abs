Title: Two Actions from One State: Fork Futures Teach JEPA World Models to Compare
Authors: Yihao Wu, Runjia Zeng, Tongyan Wang, Renzhong Huang, Chongzhou Fang, Qifan Wang, Fan Heng, Dongfang Liu
ID: 2611.28350
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28350

Abstract:
JEPA world models are trained to predict logged transitions but used to compare
counterfactual actions from one state. The logged behavior data show one action
per state, and under a local linear approximation we prove the action response
is unidentified along any direction the policy never varies within a state. We
therefore supplement behavior data with measured outcomes of alternative actions
from the same state. Our fork futures execute two actions from one restored
state, and their outcome difference cancels the state term, identifying the
action response on the sampled span. The resulting model, Fork-JEPA, matches
predicted to measured differences, anchors the logged successor with behavior
prediction, and adds a lookback head that keeps the predecessor recoverable, all
at training time, leaving planning unchanged. On three visual control tasks,
behavior-only training leaves the predicted action effect no better than a
constant guess. Fork-JEPA makes it readable and raises planning success by up to
14\% over LeWM, its base model. Where the action response changes across hidden
zones, it nearly doubles cross-zone success, and 1,024 fork pairs beat 1.2M
extra behavior steps. Our project is available at https://fork-jepa.github.io/.
