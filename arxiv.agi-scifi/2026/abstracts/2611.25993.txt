Title: Holographic Tessellations Tensor Networks $\{p,q\}$ from Entangled Quantum Tetrahedron in Loop Quantum Gravity
Authors: Deden M. Akbar, Freddy P. Zen, Jusak S. Kosasih
ID: 2611.25993
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25993

Abstract:
The relationship between spin network states in loop quantum gravity and bulk--
boundary duality is further explored using the tessellated holographic tensor
network. We develop a holographic tensor network construction based on quantum
entanglement between tetrahedra in loop quantum gravity (LQG). This construction
relates four-valent intertwiner spaces and Bell states dressed with $SU(2)$
holonomies to effective tensors on hyperbolic $\{p,q\}$ tessellations. For the
$\{5,4\}$ tessellation, six tetrahedra are assembled into a single effective
cell with five network legs and one logical index. Provided that the microscopic
transfer map has full row rank, a right inverse and a completion on the kernel
space are employed to construct an isometric embedding, yielding an effective
tensor proportional to a perfect tensor. The construction is generalized to the
$p=2k-1$ family, with local dimensions supporting absolutely maximally entangled
states. On finite networks satisfying a radial orientation condition, an
appropriate normalization produces an isometric bulk--boundary map. For cuts
satisfying the isometry condition on both sides, the boundary entropy decomposes
into a link contribution $\sum_{\ell}\log(2j_{\ell}+1)$ and the bulk entropy,
thereby providing a discrete realization of the quantum Ryu--Takayanagi
structure. Applications to the black hole interior are formulated via
postselection-based non-isometric encoding. Assuming additional Haar statistics,
this framework is used to analyze the preservation of overlaps on average and
the competition between entropy branches underlying the Page Curve. These
results establish a constructive link between the geometric degrees of freedom
of LQG and holographic codes, while clarifying the conditions required to
connect them to geometric area in LQG, the location of the quantum extremal
surface, and evaporation of black hole.
