Title: Evaluating and improving AI agents for sequencing protocol understanding
Authors: Chi-Lam Poon, Michael Goneos, Zezheng Song, Xi Chen
ID: 2610.37851
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37851

Abstract:
Existing scientific-agent benchmarks rarely evaluate whether agents can
reconstruct the exact molecular structures and generation workflows specified by
sequencing protocols, a capability critical for analyzing customized sequencing
assays. We introduce LibStructBench, a document-grounded benchmark for
reconstructing sequencing-library structures and molecular workflows. Our
experiments show that frontier agents often produce approximately correct
reconstructions but remain unreliable at exact reconstruction, particularly in
molecular-state representation and workflow connectivity. Switching the
underlying language model produced larger average performance differences than
switching agent harnesses, with a statistically significant difference only for
molecular-state reconstruction. Additional knowledge improved oligo-family
recovery in a small pilot without improving mean molecular-workflow scores.
Autonomous accumulation of procedural rules and cross-protocol examples showed
non-monotonic validation performance and no mean held-out molecular-workflow
improvement in the tested trajectory. These findings highlight the need for more
controlled and verifiable approaches to scientific reasoning about molecular
processes in complex experimental protocols.
