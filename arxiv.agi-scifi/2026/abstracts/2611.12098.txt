Title: A Universal Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing Architecture with Globally Driven Atom Arrays
Authors: Zhihao Zhang, Shuaifan Cao, Xinyang Yu, Yanyan Chen, Yueyang Min, Yingzhou Li, Zhongcheng Yu, Lei Feng, Xiaopeng Li
ID: 2611.12098
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12098

Abstract:
Scalable quantum computation requires reconciling programmability with simple,
high-fidelity control. Here, we propose a universal fault-tolerant architecture
using globally driven dual-species atom arrays. In a hybrid neutral-atom system,
interspecies Rydberg interactions enable qubit addressability without locally
addressed quantum gates. We construct a universal native instruction set, Global
Rydberg Atom Instruction Language (GRAIL), and show that crosstalk errors can be
suppressed to the $10^{-4}$ level. We compile surface-code error correction and
magic state cultivation into GRAIL, and parallelize the compilation to suppress
crosstalk. Under a hardware-inferred circuit-level noise model, we obtain an
error-correction threshold of $1.43\%$ for the resulting quantum memory and a
logical error rate below $10^{-9}$ for magic states cultivated at an
experimentally accessible Rydberg Rabi frequency. By replacing local gate
addressing with atom-mediated selectivity, this architecture removes the
associated calibration requirements, offering a route to simpler control for
large-scale fault-tolerant neutral-atom computation.
