Title: FlashT2S: GPU-Accelerated Tensor-to-Spike Conversion Operators for Heterogeneous Neuromorphic LLM Backends
Authors: Rongyuan Guo, Haotian Wang, Yufeng Zhang, Wangdong Yang, Kenli Li
ID: 2610.17035
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17035

Abstract:
Artificial intelligence faces two growing challenges: computational bottlenecks
and energy inefficiency inherent to the von Neumann architecture. Neuromorphic
computing offers a promising alternative, yet its immature ecosystem still
relies on conventional systems for data supply, requiring efficient conversion
between tensor and spike dataflow. We observe a clear mismatch, where data
conversion is slow while downstream computation is fast. As data size grows
quadratically with large language models, this mismatch becomes a major
bottleneck. In this work, we examine the data-conversion components that appear
on the critical path of training and inference in neuromorphic LLMs. The work
presents FlashT2S (Flash Tensor-to-Spike): GPU-Accelerated Tensor-to-Spike
Conversion Operators, which applies architecture-aware and data-centric
optimizations to reduce conversion latency. Experimental results show that
FlashT2S delivers an average speedup of 850.78x over the native operators across
individual operators and 802.83x in realistic workloads. Additionally, compared
with GPU kernels automatically generated by torch.compile, our kernels achieve
5-11x speedups. Notably, it achieves near-optimal absolute performance: large-
scale conversions are reduced to tens of microseconds (10-99 us), while small-
scale conversions reach single-digit microsecond levels (less than 10 us).
Furthermore, its standardized output enables seamless integration across
heterogeneous backends, including diverse neuromorphic hardware.
