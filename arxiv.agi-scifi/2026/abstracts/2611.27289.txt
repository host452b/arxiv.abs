Title: InterBand-EMG: A Cross-Band Foundation Model for Generalizable Hand-Pose Estimation
Authors: Zongheng Guo, Tao Chen, Dalong Du, Yijian Yang, Heng Liu, Lun Shu, Chen Luo, Junhao Tu, Lei Shu, Mingzhou Wu et al.
ID: 2611.27289
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27289

Abstract:
Surface electromyography (sEMG) enables continuous hand-pose estimation, but
learning representations that generalize across users and movements remains
challenging. We introduce InterBand-EMG, which combines band-specific
quantization with cross-band reconstruction and structured masked code
prediction to learn transferable EMG representations for hand-pose tracking. We
also introduce SynapMyo, an eight-channel EMG-to-pose dataset comprising
1,227.74 hand-hours from 499 users across 68 movement categories. Experiments on
emg2pose and SynapMyo show that InterBand-EMG outperforms state-of-the-art
baselines in joint-angle accuracy across all three generalization settings on
both datasets. The source code and the SynapMyo dataset will be publicly
released upon acceptance of this paper.
