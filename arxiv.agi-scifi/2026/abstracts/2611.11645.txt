Title: A software infrastructure proposal for mathematical research with AI agents
Authors: Mario Vago Marzal, Enric Cosme Llópez
ID: 2611.11645
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11645

Abstract:
In 2026, AI agents began to produce mathematical results. They formalize
complete theorems and settle open problems faster than a research group can
review the results. The difficulty is shifting to organizing that output,
deciding which results to trust and understanding them as a group. Current tools
address particular problems or assist individual researchers, and none of them
is designed as the shared working environment of a research group. We propose
the design of a self-hosted environment in which a group of people and agents
does its daily mathematical work. It consists of a small open core, which each
group deploys with the resources it has and adapts through extensions. The core
records who did what and gives every message and result a stable address. We
also outline how the formal libraries of different groups, maintained mostly by
agents, can form a network. We describe Stemma, an early prototype that
implements part of the proposal, and illustrate it with a synthetic example.
