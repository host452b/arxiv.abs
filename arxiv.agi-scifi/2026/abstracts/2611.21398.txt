Title: Self-attention is a Dyson equation for many-body machine learning
Authors: Jarvist Moore Frost
ID: 2611.21398
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21398

Abstract:
Why does symmetric sum-pooling (DeepSets) predict ground-state potentials to
sub-meV accuracy, yet fail for correlated wavefunctions and excited states? Sum-
pooling is a mean-field (Hartree) model: its $O(1/N)$ off-diagonal Jacobian
suffices for nearsighted ground-state energies, but erases nonlocal two-body
correlations. We show that self-attention is a real-space Dyson iteration whose
Boltzmann (softmax) kernel acts as a two-body self-energy. Because physical
Hamiltonians act pairwise, Löwdin's 2-RDM theorem and the Galitskii--Migdal
relation guarantee that iterating a two-body propagator captures the full
energetics of correlated matter. When fitting a fractal backflow determinant,
DeepSets blurs the nodal surface onto a mean-field envelope, whereas self-
attention recovers the fractal attractor. Seen through this lens, the
unreasonable effectiveness of attention is not an accident; in a universe built
from pairwise interactions, two-body attention is all you need.
