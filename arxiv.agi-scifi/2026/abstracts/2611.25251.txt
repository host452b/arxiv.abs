Title: WEVO: World Model-Enabled VLA Optimization for Whole-Body Loco-Manipulation
Authors: Qianzhong Chen, Muyuan Lin, Yuyin Sun, Mac Schwager, Cheng-Hao Kuo, Roberto Martín-Martín
ID: 2611.25251
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25251

Abstract:
Reinforcement learning (RL) can improve vision-language-action (VLA) policies
beyond imitation, but humanoid exploration is unsafe and simulator RL can
exploit residual sim-to-real gaps. We present WEVO, a teleoperation-free
framework for post-training a whole-body humanoid VLA in an action-conditioned
video world model (WM). Simulated manipulation and locomotion play data improve
contact-dynamics and viewpoint-change prediction. A VLA trained on synthetic
demonstrations collects 20 autonomous real robot rollouts per task to align the
WM with deployment observations. The VLA, whole-body controller (WBC), and video
WM then form a closed-loop RL environment with dense progress rewards. Across
three real robot tasks, play data improves every evaluated WM metric and
downstream policy success; video WM RL raises mean success from 23.3% to 75.0%,
compared with 48.3% for real robot RL. We therefore leverage the complementary
strengths of both environments: simulation provides policy initialization and
broad coverage, while the video WM offers a deployment-aligned environment for
policy improvement. Project website: https://qianzhong-
chen.github.io/wevo.github.io/.
