Title: Machine-accelerated extraction of experimental superconducting parameters for physical insight and prediction across thousands of materials
Authors: Mihir Manium, Benjamin Geisler, Peter J. Hirschfeld, Amalia I. Coldea
ID: 2611.15627
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15627

Abstract:
New superconductors could unlock next-generation technologies from nuclear
fusion to lossless electronics. Machine learning can predict candidate
superconductors but lacks validated experimental training data beyond the
critical temperature. Here we present AutoSuperCon, a database of eight
superconducting parameters, containing 20354 entries for 6793 superconductors.
The data were extracted from over 47000 arXiv preprints using a large language
model and validated against reference datasets ($R^2 = 0.96$ with NIMS
SuperCon). Using AutoSuperCon, we show that median gap ratios rise from 3.6 in
conventional superconductors to 6.4 in cuprates, 28% of upper critical fields
exceed the Pauli limit, and critical temperatures lie below a ceiling
proportional to the superfluid density. Further, we train composition-based
predictors for the critical temperature ($R^2_{\log}=0.83$) and the upper
critical field ($R^2_{\log}=0.69$), boosted by transfer learning. Within known
superconducting families, composition alone is sufficient to screen candidates
for both parameters. AutoSuperCon thus enables physical insight and data-driven
discovery across thousands of superconductors.
