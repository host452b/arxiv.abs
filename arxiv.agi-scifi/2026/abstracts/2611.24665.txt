Title: Uniformly positive entropy and mixing in minimal systems
Authors: Shuhao Zhang, Xiaomin Zhou
ID: 2611.24665
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24665

Abstract:
Every minimal system with uniformly positive entropy of order two is
topologically mixing. In a minimal system, mixing along an infinite set
$A\subset\N_0$ is equivalent to $\dist(T^nU,T^nV)\to0$ along $A$ for all
nonempty open $U,V$, and to unbounded complexity of each standard binary open
cover along every infinite subset of $A$. For $A=\N_0$ it is also equivalent to
$Δ^*$-transitivity. A local distance condition characterizes finite cyclic
decompositions with mixing return maps. Such a decomposition exists precisely
when, for each infinite $S\subset\N_0$, the closure of some proximal cell along
$S$ has positive measure for some invariant probability. In a minimal weakly
mixing system, for fixed infinite $S$, these cells are either all dense or all
have closures of measure zero for every invariant probability. We also give
proximal-cell criteria for Furstenberg families and mild mixing.
