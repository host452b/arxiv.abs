Title: Privacy Protection in Self-Evolving Personal Agents: Persistent Memory and Cross-Carrier Transfer
Authors: Zibin Lin, Taotao Wang, Shengli Zhang
ID: 2611.26983
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26983

Abstract:
Personal agents carry private experience into later decisions through persistent
memory. This thematic survey compares memory admission, maintenance, release,
and withdrawal by protected change, observer, and validation setting. Direct
evidence in the screened corpus comes from controlled memory workflows. Three
worked scenarios follow one private fact through booking, feedback-driven
learning, and withdrawal. We show when prompt revision and memory reads compose,
when skills inherit a private head's bound, and why reader non-use does not
establish cleanup of learned state. Diagnostic experiments measure the utility
costs of wider label protection and broad memory deletion, and test how missing
lineage obscures descendants.
