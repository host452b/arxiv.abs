Title: Agentic Wireless Optimization: Workflow Automation, Algorithm Design, and Multi-Timescale Network Operations
Authors: Yuan Guo, Zixiang Ren, Ruihua Luo, Yilong Chen, Ya-Feng Liu, Xianghao Yu, Derrick Wing Kwan Ng, Jie Xu
ID: 2611.05887
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05887

Abstract:
Optimization is fundamental to wireless communications, from characterizing
channel capacity and designing transmission schemes to allocating network
resources. However, conventional workflows require substantial expertise and
manual effort, particularly for high-dimensional or nonconvex problems, black-
box objectives, and interdependent decisions across time scales. Recent advances
in agentic artificial intelligence (AI), particularly agents based on large
language models (LLMs), offer new opportunities to address these challenges by
integrating reasoning, tool use, and numerical feedback. This article presents
an overview of agentic AI for wireless optimization, focusing on workflow
automation, algorithm design, and multi-timescale network operations. First,
LLM-based agents streamline optimization workflows by automating problem
formulation, code generation, numerical solution, and result verification in a
closed loop. Second, they facilitate algorithm design by combining and
configuring existing numerical methods and refining learning-based optimizers,
using numerical feedback to improve solution quality and computational
efficiency for nonconvex and black-box problems. Third, they support multi-
timescale network operations by decomposing coupled problems into interdependent
subtasks and coordinating their solutions to satisfy network-level objectives
and service requirements. A multicast beamforming case study demonstrates
performance gains from agent-developed solvers, particularly in execution time,
and a network energy-saving example illustrates how agents coordinate dynamic
base station activation, user association, and beamforming across time scales.
Finally, we discuss open challenges and research opportunities in agentic
wireless optimization.
