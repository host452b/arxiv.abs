Title: Local information processing capacity: anatomy of computation in neuromorphic systems
Authors: Jiaxuan Chen, Daiki Nishioka, Yicheng Song, Ryo Iguchi, Sota Hikasa, Hina Kitano, Akira Hirose, Takashi Tsuchiya
ID: 2611.05959
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05959

Abstract:
Many neuromorphic systems possess rich internal computational structures shaped
by their physical dynamics. However, existing metrics characterize only global
computation, leaving these local structures unresolved despite their importance
for system interpretation, optimization and miniaturization. Here, we introduce
local information processing capacity (L-IPC), which represents individual
dynamical states as vectors in an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space and
decomposes their computational properties through subspace projections onto
nonlinearity orders and memory steps, with the residual quantifying
computational irrelevance. Applied to three systems with comparable global
capacities (an echo state network, an ion-gating reservoir and a spin-wave
reservoir), L-IPC reveals markedly different distributions of computation,
ranging from homogeneous to spatially, temporally and spectrally structured
patterns. These local profiles further guide task-specific pruning,
outperforming standard approaches at the same size in the structured systems.
These results establish L-IPC as a general framework for anatomizing computation
and guiding task-adaptive design in neuromorphic systems.
