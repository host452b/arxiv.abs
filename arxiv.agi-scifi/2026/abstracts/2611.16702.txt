Title: Transferable Neuromorphic Protection for Distribution Networks
Authors: Saurabh Prabhakar, Yubo Song, Pascal Schirmer, Bijaya Ketan Panigrahi, Subham Sahoo
ID: 2611.16702
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16702

Abstract:
Protection of converter-dominated distribution networks is challenging due to
limited fault currents, operating-mode transitions, bidirectional power flow,
and heterogeneous network configurations. This paper proposes a scale-invariant
neuromorphic protection framework to enable transferable protection decisions
across such networks. The framework integrates asset-based normalization, multi-
domain disturbance representation, event-driven spike encoding, leaky integrate-
and-fire (LIF) dynamics, adaptive threshold regulation, and directional
supervision within a unified mathematical formulation. Local voltage, current,
active power, and reactive power measurements are transformed into dimensionless
disturbance representations, while latency encoding and membrane integration
capture disturbance intensity and temporal evolution. The framework is evaluated
across four heterogeneous network configurations: a two-DER inverter-interfaced
microgrid, an islanded ring microgrid, the IEEE 13-node feeder, and the CIGRE
European medium-voltage benchmark. Across 480 cases, it achieves a median
operating time of 7.6 ms, with 94.2 % of cases operating within one power cycle.
The results demonstrate selective protection across different electrical ratings
and network topologies using a common parameterization without network-specific
retuning, highlighting the potential of neuromorphic processing for physically
interpretable, communication-free protection of converter-dominated distribution
networks.
