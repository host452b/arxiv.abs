Title: Understanding Human-Robot Collaboration through Joint Action
Authors: Chengwen Zhang, Jianyang Wang, Chang Liu, Yukun Wang, Yuan Xu, Zhoutong Ye, Jie Cai, Chun Yu, Yuanchun Shi
ID: 2610.37298
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37298

Abstract:
Human-robot collaboration requires partners to coordinate interdependent
contributions toward a shared goal. Joint Action theory provides concepts for
understanding this coordination, but how these concepts are instantiated and
combined across empirical research remains fragmented. We conducted a systematic
review following PRISMA 2020, identifying 83 empirical papers published between
2021 and 2026. Through theory-driven framework analysis, we organized the
literature into nine constructs across three capacities: Representation,
Prediction, and Integration. We analyze how these constructs are distributed and
co-occur across studies, revealing where empirical evidence concentrates and
where it is absent. By mapping empirical HRC research onto a unified Joint
Action framework, this review offers a coherent overview of HRC studies,
identifies gaps in current evidence, and provides guidelines for future
research.
