Title: AI Learning and Conceptual Transfer in the Game of Hidden Rules
Authors: Christo Mathew, Wentian Wang, Jacob Feldman, Lazaros K. Gallos, Paul B. Kantor, Vladimir Menkov, Hao Wang
ID: 2608.21372
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21372

Abstract:
This report summarizes the work conducted on the Game of Hidden Rules (GOHR),
focusing on reinforcement learning agents trained to infer hidden rules from
trial-and-error feedback, representation design, rule difficulty analysis,
transfer learning, generalization, and pseudo-bot-assisted human learning
analysis. The report focuses on the Transformer-based A2C framework, Feature-
Centric and Object-Centric representations, experimental findings, and
classification of human learning data.
