Title: An Open-Architecture Dual-Core STM32-Based Neural Interface with Low-Latency On-Device AI for Neuromodulation Applications
Authors: Sipeng Liang, Kun Cheng, Xiao Wu, Lyu Wang, Savanna Blade, Jiaxi Wei, Ruoheng Wang, Zhecheng Yin, Johnson Song, Tristan J. Lawson et al.
ID: 2611.15942
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15942

Abstract:
We present an open-architecture bidirectional neural interface for closed-loop
neuromodulation built around a dual-core STM32H747 microcontroller and
commercial Intan recording and stimulation front-ends. The system supports up to
128 recording channels at 20 kSps and 64 stimulation channels while enabling
low-latency on-device AI inference through a dual-core processing architecture
and a two-stream memory hierarchy. To validate the machine learning pipeline, we
demonstrate two neural decoding models, a compact multilayer perceptron
executing entirely from on-chip memory and a larger temporal neural decoder
utilizing external memory staging. Both models successfully decode fingertip
velocity from neural spiking activity. As a proof of concept for closed-loop
operation, we implement spike-triggered stimulation and achieve an end-to-end
latency of $189 \pm 14\,μ$s, representing an improvement of three orders of
magnitude over a host-PC-mediated closed-loop pathway. These results demonstrate
that resource-constrained embedded platforms can support high-channel-count
neural interfacing, flexible on-device AI, and sub-millisecond closed-loop
neuromodulation.
