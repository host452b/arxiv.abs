Title: Principal Trait Analysis: Identifying User Behavioral Traits in Human-AI Collaboration
Authors: Hunter McNichols, Fareya Ikram, Kai Du, Andrew Lan
ID: 2608.11460
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11460

Abstract:
Human-artificial intelligence (AI) collaboration takes many forms and occur in
both professional and educational scenarios. It is important to identify how
human users interact with AI and how their behavior affects task success and
learning outcomes. Existing approaches often rely on predefined behavioral
categories or derive patterns without establishing their relationship to
outcomes. In this work, we propose Principal Trait Analysis (PTA), a data-driven
method for identifying interpretable behavioral traits from human-AI
collaboration dialogues. Our method analyzes large corpora of human-AI
collaborative session traces, derive common traits across the dataset, scores
each human collaborator on how well their interaction aligns with these traits,
and selects a set that covers the most variance across users. While unsupervised
and data-driven, our method also enables domain expertise to be injected during
trait identification. We evaluate PTA on two human-AI collaborative coding
datasets and investigate how the traits predict future task success, learning
outcomes, and evolve over time.
