Title: A Revealed Preference Framework for AI Alignment
Authors: Elchin Suleymanov
ID: 2603.27868
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27868

Abstract:
Human decision makers increasingly delegate choices to AI agents, raising a
natural question: does the AI implement the human principal's preferences or
pursue its own? To study this question using revealed preference techniques, I
introduce the Luce Alignment Model, where the AI's choices are a mixture of two
Luce rules, one reflecting the human's preferences and the other the AI's. I
show that the AI's alignment (similarity of human and AI preferences) can be
generically identified in two settings: the laboratory setting, where both human
and AI choices are observed, and the field setting, where only AI choices are
observed.
