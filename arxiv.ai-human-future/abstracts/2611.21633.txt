Title: Measuring Human-AI Alignment as an Early Indicator for Effective Collaboration in Hybrid SE Teams
Authors: Andreas Metzger
ID: 2611.21633
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21633

Abstract:
As AI agents increasingly integrate into software engineering (SE) processes,
ensuring effective collaboration in these hybrid teams becomes a critical
challenge. While existing metrics focus on observing dynamic run-time
performance, there is a lack of systematic, static measurements for early human-
AI alignment -- the degree to which an AI's actions and granularity map to human
expectations. This position paper proposes a research agenda to develop an SE-
specific alignment metric suite drawing principles from software architecture,
organizational psychology, and human-computer interaction. Furthermore, it
outlines a validation methodology using concepts and techniques from business
process monitoring (BPM) and introduces static assessment tools leveraging
declarative BPM languages to ensure agents adhere to specific project
constraints.
