Title: What Does AI Success Look Like? A Mixed-Methods Analysis of Supranational AI Governance Instruments
Authors: Delaram Golpayegani, Marta Lasek-Markey, Monique Munarini, Arjumand Younus, Aphra Kerr, Dave Lewis
ID: 2610.31030
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31030

Abstract:
In the last decade and in particular since 2018, supranational institutions have
published policies, guidelines, and most recently regulations to govern
development and use of Artificial Intelligence (AI). This paper aims to
investigate the themes and dimensions of ``AI success'' set forth by instruments
of AI governance issued by supranational bodies. To this end, we use a mixed-
methods approach, combining manual thematic analysis and automated topic
modelling approaches, to analyse a curated corpus of 13 documents issued by
supranational bodies, including the European Union bodies, OECD, G20, UNESCO,
United Nations, and the Council of Europe between 2018-2025. Based on the
analysis and through the lens of sociology of expectations, we propose a novel
categorisation scheme for AI success themes emerging from supranational bodies'
documents. Drawing on the findings, we advance a set of policy recommendations,
setting forward the need to move beyond high-level principles toward more
coordinated approaches to AI governance.
