Title: Fundamental Impossibility Results in AI Safety: Trading Off between Desiderata
Authors: Ida Mattsson, Ratip Emin Berker, Emanuel Tewolde, Ioannis Anagnostides, Kiriaki Fragkia, Xinyu Liu, Alexander Heckett, Jiayuan Liu, Eden Hartman, Vojtěch Kovařík et al.
ID: 2610.27373
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27373

Abstract:
Is there a fundamental tradeoff between desiderata for safe AI systems, such as
harmlessness and helpfulness? We answer by formalizing the interaction between
AI systems and users as a game with three players: a defender and an attacker
that are computationally or informationally bounded, and an AI that provides
public advice and is less constrained. Our main result shows that there is a
fundamental tradeoff in providing advice that helps the defender while advice
remains public. We also consider whether the information that the AI shares can
be manipulated to benefit only the defender and conclude with a discussion of
how we can use these results in designing safer AI systems.
