Title: IntentWeaver: Sustaining Coherence across Evolving Intents in Human-AI Co-Writing
Authors: Yuanhao Zhang, Xinru Cheng, Baiqiao Zhang, Xinqi Zhang, Assem Zhunis, Shuai Ma, Xiaojuan Ma
ID: 2611.08214
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08214

Abstract:
Writers hold intents at many levels, from overarching goals to concrete wording,
and evolve them by periodically popping back up from the text to higher-level
intents. Generative AI co-writing disrupts this practice. Dense drafts capture
attention and trap writers in local editing; higher-level intents stall while
lower-level ones drift from them unnoticed, a breakdown we term intent
incoherence. Our formative study traced this breakdown from attention capture
through silent drift to failed reconciliation. We present IntentWeaver, which
supports intent evolution while sustaining coherence in human-AI co-writing. It
derives an intent hierarchy from the draft and writer interactions, evaluates
each intent's fulfillment and cross-level coherence at every change, and
propagates consequences across intent levels. In a within-subjects study (N=24)
against a baseline, participants using IntentWeaver revisited and evolved
higher-level intents more often, with fewer unnoticed deviations, and produced
texts better realizing them. We further discuss design implications for emergent
intents.
