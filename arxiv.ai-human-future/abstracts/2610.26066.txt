Title: FinSafeGuard: A Quality-Driven Synthetic Dataset for Financial AI Safety
Authors: Joseph Thomas Thacil, Reetu Raj Harsh, Krati Saxena, Bhaskarjit Sarmah, Stefano Pasquali
ID: 2610.26066
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26066

Abstract:
Generating high-quality synthetic datasets at scale remains a fundamental
challenge for training specialized AI systems in data-scarce domains. We present
a comprehensive methodology that produces 14.3 million quality-filtered
synthetic training examples from a structured taxonomy of 632,000 scenario
specifications. Our four-stage pipeline ad- dresses critical challenges in
synthetic data quality, diversity, and realism through: (1) systematic pre-
generation research establishing optimal generation parameters via diminishing
returns analysis, (2) conditional auto evolution mechanisms that remediate low-
diversity batches during generation, (3) a two-layer semantic deduplication
architecture combining lexical and embedding-based methods, and (4) LLM-as-Judge
quality evaluation filtering samples based on behavioral correctness and tuple
fidelity. Empirical analysis across three domains (Banking, Insurance, Financial
Services) demonstrates domain-specific deduplication patterns ranging from 9.9%
to 40.6%, with output generation modes exhibiting highest redundancy. Our batch
size optimization reveals mode-specific diversity saturation thresholds,
enabling principled allocation strategies. The complete pipeline reduces 26.4M
raw samples to 14.3M high-confidence examples through merge (20.9% reduction),
semantic deduplication (18.7% reduction), and quality-based filtering (15.9%
reduction), providing a reproducible framework for domain-specific synthetic
data generation with rigorous quality control.
