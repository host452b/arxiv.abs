Title: Designing with Cinematic Language: An Intent-Driven Approach to Human-AI Video Clip Co-Creation
Authors: Yi He, Zhengxi Yu, Feilin Han, Chuer Chen, Shengqi Dang, Nan Cao
ID: 2611.07377
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07377

Abstract:
Generative video models have made video creation more accessible, yet creators
still struggle to translate high-level narrative intentions into coherent
cinematic expressions. Existing systems primarily rely on prompt refinement or
expose isolated camera controls, leaving a gap between what to express and how
to film it. We present CineAssist, a human-AI co-creation system that treats
cinematic language as an interactive design medium connecting creative
intentions with executable video controls. We construct a Cinematic Language
Design Space and an annotated dataset from 107 filmmaking sources and 1,436 film
clips, linking communicative intentions to cinematic parameter values.
CineAssist combines a multi-agent workflow for intent alignment and strategy
recommendation with an interactive cinematic editor and a context-sensitive
preference learner for user-controlled personalization. We evaluate the system
through three studies examining end-to-end usability, cinematic quality, and
preference evolution. Results show that CineAssist facilitates efficient,
controllable, and personalized intent-driven cinematic video creation.
