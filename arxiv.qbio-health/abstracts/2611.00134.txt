Title: Hydrodynamic flow in the gut hinders the fixation of beneficial mutants but accelerates their invasion
Authors: Arthur Alexandre, Claude Loverdo, Anne-Florence Bitbol
ID: 2611.00134
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00134

Abstract:
Gut bacteria experience a spatially structured environment with hydrodynamic
flow. Using a branching-process approach, we show that beneficial mutants
arising randomly upon division have a reduced fixation probability compared to
the well-mixed case. The suppression factor is set by the spatial heterogeneity
of mutant fate across the gut. In contrast, the invasion of mutant lineages that
avoid early stochastic extinction is accelerated in the gut compared to a
matched well-mixed case. Stronger advective flow decreases fixation
probabilities while accelerating mutant invasion.
