Title: Tabra Blends: Improving the Accuracy--Latency Trade-off with Complementary Tabular Models
Authors: Jing Jia, Guanyang Wang
ID: 2611.32859
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32859

Abstract:
Tabular foundation models make different trade-offs between prediction accuracy
and latency. We study whether a compact ensemble can deliver higher accuracy in
less time. Across 816 TabArena splits, our blend of four-estimator Kumo and one
LimiX pipeline reaches 2063.8 Elo, 45.4 points above the top published entry in
the same fit. Its normalized fit + predict time is 15% lower than Kumo-16's and
98% lower than full LimiX's. We find that greater prediction diversity between
the two models helps explain the reduction in error.
