Title: Emerging International Best Practices for AI Model Specs
Authors: Edward Kembery, Nathan Lambert, Robert McCarthy, Jinge Wang, Peng Xu, Naying Hu, Clement Neo, Xu Jia, Miro Pluckebaum, Seán Ó hÉigeartaigh et al.
ID: 2611.32768
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32768

Abstract:
Frontier AI developers from around the world have begun to publish documents
that describe how their AI models are intended to behave. Although these 'model
specifications'-or 'model specs', for short-are increasingly important in AI
development and governance, little independent guidance exists for writing them.
This paper addresses this gap in five main sections. First, we outline the
advantages of writing a model spec for AI developers and society at large.
Second, we introduce a taxonomy for model specs that developers can take
inspiration from. Third, we propose guidance for writing and implementing a spec
in practice. Fourth, we note eight open problems related to writing model specs
for safe and desirable behaviour. We conclude with recommendations for frontier
AI companies, governments, and research and civil society organisations.
