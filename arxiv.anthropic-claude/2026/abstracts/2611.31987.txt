Title: InCue: Making Better Use of What Multimodal LLMs Already See at Test Time
Authors: Shuwei Wu, Siyue Yu, Longin Jan Latecki
ID: 2611.31987
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31987

Abstract:
Even when multimodal large language models (MLLMs) answer incorrectly, their
internal cues often point to the relevant region. A widely used remedy re-
presents the cue-selected region as an enlarged crop to supply finer detail.
However, we find that much of its gain requires no new detail: it largely
survives when the crop comes from the already-seen low-resolution input, and
partly even when the region's cached tokens are merely copied. Part of re-
presentation's gain thus comes from drawing attention to evidence the model
already holds. We propose InCue, a test-time framework that makes better use of
this evidence without additional visual input. InCue treats visual attention and
predictive-entropy gradients as Internal Cues, couples them into a spatial
target, and optimizes only an offset on the existing visual tokens to steer
attention toward it. Under matched visual-token budgets, InCue achieves state-
of-the-art performance, consistently outperforming recent test-time methods and
improving four MLLMs by up to 8.9% on V$^*$ and 23.4% on its hard samples. Its
gains carry over to eight general benchmarks. Code will be available at
https://github.com/paul-swu/InCue.
