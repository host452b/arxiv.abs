Title: LLM-Assisted Verilog Code Refinement for Efficient RTL Clock Gating
Authors: Geuna Chang, Seohyun Kim, Youngsoo Shin
ID: 2611.29461
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29461

Abstract:
RTL clock gating is a popular technique for reducing clock power at the early
design stage. Iterative updates of Verilog code, clock-gating inference, and
analysis of clock-gating efficiency (CGE) are time-consuming and pose challenges
to RTL designers. LLM-assisted automatic refinement of Verilog code for
improving CGE is proposed. Since CGE and power depend on switching activity,
LLMs generate Idle, Typical, and Peak workloads of the design, one of which is
selected by the user as the optimization target. Four LLM clock-gating agents,
each given a guideline for a particular clock-gating technique, then
sequentially refine the Verilog code, focusing on the registers with low flop-
gating efficiency (FGE) under the selected workload. Code updates are accepted
only if they are functionally equivalent and reduce post-synthesis power without
violating area and timing constraints. Experimental results with five RTL
designs show an average FGE improvement of 12.1 percentage points and an average
power reduction of 23.6% (up to 59.3%) under the selected workloads, compared to
the original Verilog code, outperforming a commercial tool and an LLM baseline,
which achieve average power reductions of only 4.1% and 8.6%, respectively.
