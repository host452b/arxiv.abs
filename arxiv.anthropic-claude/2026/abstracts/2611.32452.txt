Title: The Teacher Is Already Inside: Self-Distilling Looped Language Models Beyond Their Last Loop
Authors: Linghua Zhang
ID: 2611.32452
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32452

Abstract:
Looped language models apply one shared transformer stack several times per
token, and every loop is readable through the shared language-model head. Post-
training them is hard. Reward-only reinforcement learning needs a verifier and
learns from one sparse signal per response; spreading the reward over loops
collapses the entropy of every loop; and cross-loop self-distillation, as in
LoopOPD, can only teach intermediate loops, because the last loop cannot learn
from its own frozen copy: the loss is zero at initialisation. We observe that a
looped model already contains a policy that decodes better than its last loop:
the next-token distribution extrapolated from the first loop through the last.
ELPSD (Extrapolated-Loop Policy Self-Distillation) turns this distribution into
a self-distillation target: a frozen copy of the model computes it, and the
model's own last loop is distilled towards it on its own rollouts, using the
exact reverse KL at every visited prefix. Teacher and student are the same
model, so ELPSD needs no reward, no external teacher and no privileged context,
and it leaves rollouts and decoding unchanged. We show that the objective pulls
the last loop towards the base model's last loop with weight 1+w and away from
the base model's first loop with weight w, and that the teacher must stay
frozen, since an idealised teacher recomputed from the current model
extrapolates without bound. We evaluate ELPSD on Ouro-1.4B-Thinking across five
math benchmarks against the base model, GRPO, RLTT and OPSD. Without any reward,
ELPSD raises the average avg@8 from 61.15 to 63.69 and is the best method on all
five benchmarks, ahead of GRPO (62.32), which uses a verifier, and the self-
distillation method OPSD (61.77), which needs reference solutions.
