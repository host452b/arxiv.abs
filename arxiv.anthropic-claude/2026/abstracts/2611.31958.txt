Title: SPAR: Self-Referenced World-Model Rewards for Physical Alignment in Video Generation
Authors: Junyi Zhang, Qichao Wang, Wenzhe Qian, Yuanchuan Wang, Min Zhang
ID: 2611.31958
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31958

Abstract:
Generating videos with physically plausible dynamics remains challenging despite
advances in visual quality. Latent world models offer predictive signals for
evaluating physical plausibility, but higher agreement between predicted and
observed features may reflect easier-to-predict scene content rather than more
plausible dynamics. We propose \textbf{S}elf-Referenced \textbf{P}hysical
\textbf{A}lignment \textbf{R}ewards (\textbf{SPAR}), a framework that uses
world-model predictions for inference-time physical alignment. SPAR compares
each video's prediction error with that of static clips constructed by repeating
its own frames, providing a content-matched reference for evaluating generated
dynamics. To account for the uneven spatial and temporal distribution of
physical interactions, we introduce a unified \textbf{spatiotemporal reward
refinement} strategy. \textbf{Spatial refinement} emphasizes localized
prediction discrepancies, while \textbf{temporal refinement} moderates brief
error peaks and supplements prediction errors with agreement between predicted
and observed feature changes. Evaluations across multiple benchmarks demonstrate
that SPAR improves the physical plausibility of both \textbf{text-to-video
generation} and \textbf{image-to-video continuation}, bringing generated
dynamics into closer alignment with the physical regularities governing real-
world events. Additional results and resources are available at https://gavin-
jy.github.io/spar-page/
