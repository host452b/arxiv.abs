Title: LLM-Assisted Co-Optimization of RTL and Synthesis Algorithms via Bottleneck Analysis
Authors: Yikang Ouyang, Yang Luo, Dongsheng Zuo, Xiaoxiao Liang, Yuzhe Ma
ID: 2611.29582
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29582

Abstract:
Post-synthesis power, performance, and area (PPA) depend jointly on the
register-transfer-level (RTL) code and the synthesis algorithms. However, the
benefits of RTL optimization may be limited by bottlenecks in synthesis
algorithms, and vice versa, leaving opportunities for better PPA unexplored by
methods that optimize either level alone. In this work, we present a large
language model (LLM)-assisted framework for regional co-optimization of RTL and
synthesis algorithms via bottleneck analysis. A key insight behind our approach
is that RTL structures and the Boolean networks used in synthesis expose
complementary opportunities for addressing bottlenecks that lead to suboptimal
PPA in different regions of the circuit. Our bottleneck analysis uses a Gaussian
process (GP) to assess whether optimizing RTL or synthesis algorithms is more
promising for each region. The GP learns from regional graph representations and
PPA feedback. Using the GP's predictions and predictive uncertainty, Bayesian
optimization (BO) provides guidance that an LLM Router combines with circuit
analysis to choose how to address each region's bottlenecks. Self-improving LLM
workers then optimize the RTL or synthesis algorithms in their assigned regions,
and their regional changes are greedily integrated into the current design
configuration. Experiments on 16 RTL designs show average improvements of
103.27% in worst negative slack (WNS), 1.23% in area, and 13.26% in power
relative to the initial designs. These improvements exceed those of the
strongest baseline for each metric by 47.80, 2.75, and 13.17 percentage points,
respectively.
