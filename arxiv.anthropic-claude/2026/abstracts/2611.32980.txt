Title: A Strongly Subquadratic Algorithm for Unique Assembly Verification in the Abstract Tile Assembly Model
Authors: Tim Wylie
ID: 2611.32980
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32980

Abstract:
We show, for the first time, a strongly sub-quadratic algorithm for Unique
Assembly Verification (UAV) in the 2D Abstract Tile Assembly Model (aTAM) with
any constant temperature. UAV in the 2D aTAM is solvable in
$\widetilde{O}(n^{71/36})$. Further, in 3D, we give a logarithmic improvement
for UAV to $O(n^2/\sqrt{\log(n+2)})$, and then show that even for assemblies
with a constant height of two in 3D (barely 3D), no strongly sub-quadratic
algorithm can exist under the Orthogonal Vectors Conjecture (OVC) by showing it
is coOV-hard. Further, we show that UAV for barely 3D assemblies is P-complete
and analyze the result with relation to Circuit-SETH. We show that for UAV, any
3D aTAM system can be simulated by a barely 3D aTAM system. This provides a
clear conditional separation between the 2D and 3D problems (even with only two
layers), and improves the time complexity for one of the most fundamental
problems in algorithmic self-assembly in both 2D and 3D.
