Title: LM2 Compiling a Language Model into Another Model Weights
Authors: Naman Chawla, Chhavi Dhiman
ID: 2611.29577
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29577

Abstract:
Deploying a conversational model and a specialist together could avoid
maintaining a separate specialist service. We present LM2, which embeds a
trained story generator into a larger conversational model. The larger model
handles the conversation and invokes the generator for story requests. We
constrain the generator arithmetic during training so its calculations can be
compiled directly into added model layers. We compare this approach with QwenPU,
which executes the stories260K language model on an embedded processor. LM2
requires one host token per generated character, compared with approximately
25,000 for QwenPU. However, its story generator predicts held out text less
well, i.e. 1.145 bits per character versus 0.894 for stories260K, where lower is
better. The Qwen3.5, 0.8B implementation occupies 18.1 GB and generates the same
characters as a standalone integer implementation of the inner model in the
tested runs. These results illustrate a tradeoff between host-token cost and
text-prediction performance.
