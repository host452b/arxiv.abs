Title: Provably Near-Optimal PQ in the Extended Zador Model
Authors: Edo Liberty, Ashwin Padaki, Amir Ingber
ID: 2611.31579
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31579

Abstract:
This paper investigates Product Quantization (PQ) Optimization with Gaussian
data. It offers three key contributions (i) a proof that SVD is the optimal
preconditioning rotation for PQ; (ii) a new Extended Zador (EZ) estimator of the
$k$-means quantization cost that is accurate in low bit rates and low
dimensions; and (iii) a novel segmentation algorithm based on 1-d bin packing of
global bit allocations. The result is a simple algorithm (EZPQ) with strong
approximation guarantees. It matches OPQ's empirical accuracy while providing a
per-instance distortion lower bound certificate under the Gaussian EZ cost
model. On typical real embedding spectra, EZPQ with 2 bits per coordinate is
often certifiably within 1% of the optimum in this model. It also consistently
achieves a lower reconstruction error than all random-rotation-based algorithms
such as EDEN, E-RaBitQ, and TurboQuant.
