Title: OmniX: A first jet and event model
Authors: Anna Hallin, Gregor Kasieczka, Henning Rose
ID: 2611.31403
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31403

Abstract:
Foundation models are a new approach to building machine learning algorithms for
particle physics. Their central idea is that a single model can be adapted to a
variety of downstream tasks, amortizing training costs. So far, most foundation
model prototypes have operated on the constituents of a single jet. This limits
their usefulness for physics problems at the scale of full events, such as
distinguishing different processes, and restricts their performance, as global
correla- tions are invisible to the model. Here, we introduce OmniX, an
architecture that operates on both single jets and whole events within a single
model. Using the jet-level JetClass and Top-tagging datasets and the event-level
Collide-2V dataset, we demonstrate that OmniX trained jointly on single jets and
full events matches or exceeds otherwise equivalent jet-only and event-only
models in generation and classification.
