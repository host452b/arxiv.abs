Title: MSRGF: Multi-Source Reconstruction with Reference-Guided Fusion for Incomplete Multimodal Sentiment Analysis
Authors: Yan Zhuang, Minhao Liu, Yanru Zhang, Li Ma, Weilong Chen, Xingyu Wu, Jiawen Deng, Fuji Ren
ID: 2611.32860
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32860

Abstract:
Incomplete multimodal sentiment analysis (MSA) predicts sentiment from textual,
acoustic, and visual features when some observations are missing. Existing
reconstruction methods estimate missing features from available modalities and
combine the resulting information for sentiment prediction. However, different
available modalities can produce distinct estimates of the same modality, while
direct fusion often leaves the relationships among these estimates underused. We
propose MSRGF (Multi-Source Reconstruction with Reference-Guided Fusion), which
retains separate estimates and exploits their relationships during fusion.
Specifically, MSRGF comprises three components: (1) Source-Wise Reconstruction
(SWR), which uses modality-specific queries and a shared generator to produce a
separate estimate of each modality from every available modality; (2) Reference-
Guided Fusion (RGF), which uses relationships among estimates and available
observations to construct a weighted reference, selectively refines it using
their differences, and incorporates observed features when available; and (3)
Global Context Modulation (GCM), which uses global context to adjust modality
summaries for sentiment prediction. Extensive experiments on three datasets show
that MSRGF outperforms existing methods, achieving average accuracy improvements
of 1.9% on MOSEI under random within-modality missingness, 0.6% under random
whole-modality missingness and 1.3% under fixed whole-modality missingness.
