Title: ESGPT: A Data-Centric ESG Language Model with Unified Instruction and Benchmark Resources
Authors: Jaeyoung Lee, Misuk Kim
ID: 2611.30052
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30052

Abstract:
ESG analysis requires complex language understanding across diverse sources,
including corporate reports, news, and disclosures, for tasks such as ESG
relevance detection, action interpretation, impact analysis, and question
answering. However, existing ESG datasets are fragmented by task and source,
with heterogeneous formats and annotation schemes, limiting their use for
training general-purpose ESG LLMs and unified evaluation. In this work, we
construct ESG-Instruct, a 30,000-instance ESG instruction dataset built from
public ESG resources, and ESGU-Bench, an ESG understanding benchmark covering
six representative ESG tasks. Based on these resources, we further develop
ESGPT, an 8B ESG-specialized LLM, and evaluate it against diverse open-source
and API-based LLMs. Experimental results show that general-purpose LLMs exhibit
substantial performance variation across ESG tasks, while ESGPT, despite its
relatively small scale, achieves the best performance on multiple ESG tasks.
This work provides unified data and benchmark resources for training and
evaluating ESG-specialized LLMs.
