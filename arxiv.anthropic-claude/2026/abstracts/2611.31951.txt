Title: User-Adaptive Fusion of Multiple Foundation Models for Recommender Systems
Authors: Sunwoo Kim, Hyunjin Hwang, Geon Lee, Kyungho Kim, Kijung Shin
ID: 2611.31951
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31951

Abstract:
Can recommender systems benefit from combining a collection of foundation models
within and across modalities? While different foundation models may capture
complementary aspects of item metadata, we find that naively combining their
representations yields only marginal gains over using a single foundation model.
We make a key observation: the users who benefit vary across foundation models.
Motivated by this observation, we introduce UF2O, a user-adaptive approach for
fusing multiple foundation models for recommendation. In a nutshell, UF2O models
the contribution of each foundation model as user-specific by maintaining
separate recommendation views and adaptively combining their scores for each
user. By performing fusion at the score level, UF2O can also fuse heterogeneous
foundation models without directly aligning their representation spaces.
Extensive experiments that jointly combine four foundation models on four
datasets show that UF2O outperforms existing fusion approaches in both static
and sequential recommendation settings, with improvements of up to 7% over the
strongest fusion baseline. The code, datasets, and supplementary materials for
our work are available at https://github.com/kswoo97/UFFO.
