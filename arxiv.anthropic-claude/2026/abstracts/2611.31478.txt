Title: Which Way Is Up? Rotated Photos Silently Hurt Multi-View 3D Foundation Models
Authors: Yukun Shi, Minglun Gong
ID: 2611.31478
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31478

Abstract:
Multi-view 3D foundation models estimate camera poses and 3D points from a set
of photos in one forward pass. Many cameras store a portrait shot sideways, with
an orientation tag telling viewers how to turn it, and this tag can be ignored,
lost or wrong. We measure how rotated photos affect five such models on three
datasets. A few rotated photos lower camera pose accuracy for every model, and
the sideways photos already in a standard benchmark do the same. Even the most
robust model still loses accuracy. The failure is also silent: the models'
confidence does not reliably flag it, and most official image loaders ignore the
tag. The fixes we test each correct only part of the problem. These findings
call for orientation checks in reconstruction pipelines and benchmarks.
