Title: Ground, Guide, Act: Improving VLA Spatial Generalization with Multimodal Foundation Models
Authors: Guangting Zheng, Shaoxuan Wang, Yu Yuan, Zhipeng Tang, Jiajun Deng, Yanyong Zhang
ID: 2611.31452
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31452

Abstract:
Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models effectively learn action policies from robot
demonstrations, but struggle to generalize to changes in object positions and
task goals. General multimodal foundation models (MFMs) offer complementary
scene understanding and spatial reasoning, but remain slow and unreliable for
direct fine-grained control. To understand how MFMs can improve VLA spatial
generalization, we conduct a systematic capability analysis across multiple VLA
architectures using atomic-stage interventions, revealing that many failures
stem from poor target grounding and global spatial routing, while local
manipulation skills remain largely usable. Based on these findings, we propose
MFM-VLA, a selective collaboration framework in which an MFM provides target
grounding and spatial guidance, controllers execute free-space motion, and the
VLA retains control over fine-grained manipulation. We fine-tune the VLA with
action-chunk-level dense geometric captions and perturbed-state recovery data to
improve local guidance following and recovery from unfamiliar handoff states. A
development-calibrated capability router selects between direct VLA execution
and MFM-assisted execution to avoid unnecessary intervention. Across LIBERO-PRO,
ALOHA-Sim-PRO, RoboCasa365, and real-world chemistry tasks, MFM-VLA improves
success rates by 14.9, 74.0, 3.6, and 30.9 percentage points, respectively, over
the corresponding base VLA. The project page is available at https://tom-
zgt.github.io/MFM-VLA
