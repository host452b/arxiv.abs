Title: Spec$^2$R: Recovering Emergent Properties in Supervised Vision Transformers
Authors: Adam Wróbel, Jacek Tabor, Bartosz Zieliński, Marcin Przewięźlikowski
ID: 2611.31589
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31589

Abstract:
Vision Transformers (ViTs) have achieved strong performance across computer
vision tasks, but the quality of their learned representations differs
drastically depending on the training paradigm. While self-supervised ViTs often
demonstrate emergent properties, such as object-aligned patch representations
and attention maps, supervised ViTs typically lack these properties, even when
trained with strong data augmentations. Understanding the origin of this
difference is important for explaining and controlling the object-level
structure. To investigate it, we perform a spectral analysis of supervised and
self-supervised models, and identify a key distinction: MLP layers in supervised
ViTs introduce high-frequency variations into patch representations, disrupting
their spatial organization. Motivated by this finding, we propose Spec$^2$R, a
lightweight spectral regularization method that fine-tunes the MLP layers of
pretrained supervised ViTs. Spec$^2$R recovers object-aligned patch
representations and attention maps, while improving adversarial robustness and
preserving competitive classification accuracy.
