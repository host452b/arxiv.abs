Title: Energy and Performance Characterization of UxNBs: Toward AI-Driven Non-Terrestrial Open RAN Control
Authors: Ali Srour, Farzad Veisi, Sami Taktak, Vania Conan
ID: 2611.32258
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32258

Abstract:
UAV-mounted base stations (UxNBs) can provide ondemand 5G coverage, but their
limited onboard energy makes power consumption the main operational constraint.
AI-driven Open RAN controllers are a promising solution, yet they are trained
almost exclusively on analytical models and simulations, since no public dataset
captures the real energy behavior of a flying 5G base station (BS). As a first
step toward closing this gap, we present a UAV testbed hosting a 5G picocell
that supports a 20 MHz carrier and up to 100 Mbps throughput. The testbed
measures BS system metrics, including power consumption, CPU load, and
temperature, alongside UAV flight telemetry and detailed per-layer vRAN
processing metrics. To our knowledge, this is the first demonstration that
jointly exposes the energy and RAN behavior of a flying 5G BS, providing the
real measurements needed to train and validate energy-aware AI controllers.
