Title: ThinkMorph-SSL: Scaling Interleaved Multimodal Reasoning with Pretext Tasks
Authors: Jiawei Gu, Linjie Li, Yiming Liu, Yunzhuo Hao, Huichen Wang, Guanzheng Chen, Zhichao Peng, Luxin Xu, Xinyu Zhang, Li Luo et al.
ID: 2611.32183
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32183

Abstract:
People reason with pictures as well as words. Interleaved multimodal reasoning
gives models a similar way to solve problems by creating intermediate images and
using them to guide further reasoning. Training models with this ability usually
relies on task-specific demonstrations, which are difficult to collect. We
introduce ThinkMorph-SSL, a two-stage training recipe that uses images
themselves as a source of supervision. The first stage, pretext reasoning,
converts visual pretext tasks, such as image colorization and denoising, into
instruction-free interleaved traces. Models describe a corrupted or transformed
image, generate a restored or canonical image, and interpret the result. The
second stage uses automatically constructed demonstrations for question-driven
instruction tuning. At the same instruction-tuning budget, models trained with
pretext reasoning outperform those trained with instruction tuning alone. The
full recipe improves over the base model by an average of 11.33 accuracy points
across twelve benchmarks. These results show that supervision derived from
images can build the visual skills needed for interleaved reasoning and improve
learning from task-specific demonstrations.
