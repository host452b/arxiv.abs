Title: Blockchain-IoT Convergence for Spatiotemporal Data Systems: Challenges, Solutions, and Research Gaps
Authors: Natalia Chaudhry, Muhammad Murtaza Yousaf
ID: 2611.33019
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33019

Abstract:
Blockchain in conjunction with the Internet of Things (IoT) is changing the
management of spatiotemporal data in a way that has never been possible before
now. In the process of gathering geospatial and time-stamped data from a large
network of IoT devices, data authenticity, accuracy, and timely processing are
major issues. Spatiotemporal indexing in a blockchain environment is necessary
for ensure quick data retrieval; supporting real time data processing
requirement. Blockchain with its decentralized consensus mechanism is a possible
solution for preventing data fabrication, unauthorized access, and single point
of failure in IoT based spatiotemporal applications. This paper aims to describe
how Blockchain and IoT indexing can be combined to enhance the spatiotemporal
data management (specifically indexing and querying of data). Literature
solutions will be reviewed and compared to highlight challenges and insights for
future blockchain-integrated IoT in resilient spatiotemporal data ecosystems.
