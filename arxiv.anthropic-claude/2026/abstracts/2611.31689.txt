Title: GeoMVGen: Joint Appearance-Geometry Modeling for Multi-View Video Generation in Robotic Manipulation
Authors: Qiaoqiao Jia, Guile Wu, Dongfeng Bai
ID: 2611.31689
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31689

Abstract:
Robotic manipulation requires predicting how robot-object interactions evolve
over time, encompassing both visual appearance and scene geometry. In multi-
camera settings, such predictions need to capture the evolving 3D scene
consistently across viewpoints, while supporting controllable generation under
different robot actions and camera configurations. Existing video generation
models provide strong priors for visual appearance and motion but typically lack
explicit geometric modeling. Meanwhile, recent advances in geometry-aware and
multi-view generation have explored joint appearance-geometry modeling and
cross-view consistency, yet generating temporally coherent appearance and
geometry across multiple viewpoints under robotic manipulation conditions
remains challenging. In this work, we propose GeoMVGen, a framework for joint
multi-view RGB and pointmap video generation in robotic manipulation. Built on a
video diffusion transformer, our framework employs modality-specific adaptation
modules to capture the distinct characteristics of appearance and geometry,
while using cross-modal residual connections to facilitate information exchange
between the two modalities. To establish geometric correspondences across
viewpoints, we further introduce a cross-view projection mechanism with
complementary geometric supervisions, combining pointmap consistency with
geometry foundation model-guided feature alignment. These designs enable
controllable generation of temporally evolving multi-view observations with
coherent appearance and geometrically consistent scene structures. Extensive
experiments on 4D video generation dataset comprising simulated and real-world
robotic manipulation scenarios demonstrate that GeoMVGen outperforms state-of-
the-art methods in video quality, geometric accuracy, and cross-view
consistency.
