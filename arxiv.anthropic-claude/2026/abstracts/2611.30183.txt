Title: PCC: Pivotal Channel Compensation for Layer Pruning of Large Language Models
Authors: Xiangqian Wang, Yucheng Zhang, Zhaohong Jia, Kun Jing, Chunchun Li
ID: 2611.30183
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30183

Abstract:
Treating independent layers as the minimal pruning unit allows layer pruning to
efficiently eliminate many redundant parameters and achieve high compression
rates. Layer pruning has become a promising and prevalent structured pruning
technique for large language models (LLMs). However, multi-layer removal can
lead to significant performance degradation for LLMs. Although some works have
explored various compensation methods for pruned layers, they are inefficient
and costly. Observing that the expression of a large proportion of information
is concentrated on a small number of pivotal channels, we propose a novel post-
pruning compensation method for layer pruning, called pivotal channel
compensation (PCC). PCC can achieve significant performance improvement by
compensating for a small number of channels through learnable linear
transformations at the pruning interface. Besides, we propose a new evaluation
metric, called channel-wise average performance improvement (CAPI), which can
measure the efficiency of channel-wise compensation. Our experimental results
across various LLMs demonstrate that PCC can achieve significant and consistent
improvements and high CAPI on language modeling and zero-shot question answering
for the state-of-the-art cross-layer and consecutive-layer pruning methods.
Compared with existing compensation methods, PCC can achieve competitive results
with fewer additional parameters and less training cost.
