Title: First-Hit Photoconsistency for Neural Surface Reconstruction
Authors: Gabriel Lenain, Edmond Boyer, Jean Ponce
ID: 2611.32998
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32998

Abstract:
Recent neural implicit and Gaussian splatting approaches have achieved
impressive results in multi-view surface reconstruction. However, jointly
optimizing geometry and appearance introduces ambiguities. A parametric
appearance model can compensate for geometric errors, reproducing the input
images accurately without an accurate surface. We demonstrate accurate neural
implicit surface reconstruction using only multi-view patch photoconsistency
with geometry-based visibility, without volumetric rendering or parametric
appearance models. For each reference ray, sphere tracing computes the first
surface intersection. Its position and normal define a local patch whose
photoconsistency is evaluated across visible neighboring views using colors
sampled directly from the input images. For efficient sphere tracing, we use a
1-Lipschitz multi-layer perceptron. We use a single photoconsistency loss,
without foreground masks or auxiliary geometric regularization. Experiments show
that our method achieves the lowest mean Chamfer distance on BlendedMVS (BMVS)
among compared methods and matches the geometric accuracy of leading
reconstruction methods on DTU.
