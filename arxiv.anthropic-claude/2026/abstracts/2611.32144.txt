Title: CSC-Mamba: Bridging multimodal convolutional sparse coding and state space models for multi-contrast MRI super-resolution
Authors: Iman Marivani, David Van Hamme, Jan Aelterman, Wilfried Philips
ID: 2611.32144
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32144

Abstract:
Multi-contrast MRI super-resolution accelerates clinical protocols by
reconstructing high-resolution (HR) target modalities from low-resolution (LR)
inputs, guided by easily acquired reference contrasts. While purely data-driven
deep neural networks achieve strong performance via rich representation
capacity, they rely on heuristic fusion mechanisms (e.g., naive concatenation or
black-box cross-attention) that lack theoretical grounding. Alternatively,
model-based deep unfolding networks enforce principled cross-contrast fusion
through explicit priors, yet their unrolled steps remain constrained to shallow
convolutions, lacking modern deep blocks to capture global context. To bridge
this gap, we propose CSC-MAMBA, unifying deep-unfolded multimodal convolutional
sparse coding with the Vision Mamba state-space backbone. By fusing common
structural priors from the guidance modality via unfolded sparse coding and
globally refining contrast-specific representations with a Mamba back- bone, our
approach benefits from interpretable, model-guided fusion and long-range spatial
context. Experiments on the IXI dataset demonstrate that CSC-MAMBA outperforms
state-of-the-art methods in reconstruction quality, achieving up to a 0.62 dB
gain in PSNR while remaining computationally efficient.
