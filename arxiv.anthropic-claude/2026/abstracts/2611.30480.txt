Title: Test-Time Latent Computation to Improve Visual Perception in Multimodal Large Language Models
Authors: Chen Yang, Zheng Fang, Wenqi Fan, Jun Xia
ID: 2611.30480
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30480

Abstract:
Recent work on Large Language Models (LLMs) suggests that reasoning capabilities
can be enhanced at test time not only through longer token-level reasoning
traces, but also through additional computation in latent space. This raises a
natural question for Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs): can additional
computation in latent space improve visual perception at test time? In this
work, we first verify that MLLMs contain stable reusable layers that can be re-
forwarded at inference time without disrupting normal generation. Based on this
observation, we propose \textbf{Lo}ok \textbf{Ba}ck in the \textbf{L}atent
\textbf{S}pace (\textbf{Lobals}), a training-free decoding method that
identifies the contiguous layer window with the strongest visual-token attention
and re-forwards through it, allowing the model to revisit visual evidence in
latent space. We demonstrate that this latent-space revisit strategy can improve
MLLMs' performance across diverse multimodal settings, including general vision-
language tasks, visual hallucination tasks, and vision-centric tasks.
