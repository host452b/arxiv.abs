Title: The sharp uniqueness threshold for the antiferromagnetic Potts model on regular trees
Authors: Wei Wu, Yicheng Xu, Kuan Yang
ID: 2611.32635
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32635

Abstract:
We study Gibbs uniqueness for the $q$-state antiferromagnetic Potts model on the
infinite rooted $d$-ary tree and the infinite $(d+1)$-regular tree, where
$d\geqslant1$ and $q\geqslant2$. An edge has weight $p\in[0,1]$ if its endpoints
have the same color and weight $1$ otherwise. We prove that there is a unique
Gibbs state if and only if $p\geqslant p_c:=\max\{0,1-q/(d+1)\}$, except when
$(q,p)=(d+1,0)$, thus completes the uniqueness thresholds for all $d\geqslant1$
and $q\geqslant2$. Our proof use a convex potential for the tree recursion and
show that it strictly decreases away from the uniform distribution. Together
with the result of Gu, Wu and Yang, our theorem gives the exact critical power
law whenever $p_c>0$.
