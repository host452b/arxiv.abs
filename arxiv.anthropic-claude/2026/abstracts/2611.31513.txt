Title: How to build and train your recurrent-depth model: A fixed-point perspective
Authors: Thomas Pethick
ID: 2611.31513
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31513

Abstract:
Recurrent-depth models are trained with truncated backpropagation, yet it
remains unclear why this works. We show that input injection, commonly used in
recurrent-depth models, is exactly Halpern iteration, and that a slowly
vanishing stepsize provably converges with a backpropagation window
polylogarithmic in the depth. The guarantees require a Lipschitz operator, which
for SwiGLU leads to a weight constraint in both row and spectral norm, while
only a row norm constraint for gates. Based on these constraints, we develop an
optimizer tailored to each parameter group, Sparrow, which provides a drop-in
replacement of Muon/MuonH with no overhead, that speeds up training even beyond
recurrent models.
