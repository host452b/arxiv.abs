Title: RoFE: Rotated Frozen Embeddings for Low-Data Multimodal Tabular Learning
Authors: Yihao Qiao, Jiajian Ma, Wushuang Rui, Abhishek Jha, Aarti Darji, Yiqiu Shen
ID: 2611.31049
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31049

Abstract:
Multimodal tabular learning combines tabular data with images and text, often in
settings where labeled data are scarce. Tabular foundation models such as prior-
data fitted networks (PFNs) offer a promising solution via in-context learning
without task-specific training. However, modern image and text encoders produce
embeddings with hundreds or thousands of dimensions, far exceeding the number of
features encountered during PFN pretraining. Existing approaches bridge this gap
using principal component analysis (PCA) to compress frozen embeddings.
Surprisingly, we find that this PCA interface can underperform simple linear
classifiers when labeled examples are scarce, even though PFNs are designed for
small datasets. We identify the underlying failure mechanism: the PFN's per-
column standardization removes the variances of the PCA scores. When the PCA
keeps every direction of the labeled examples, the examples collapse into a
regular simplex in which all pairwise distances are equal. To address this
problem, we propose Rotated Frozen Embeddings (RoFE), a training-free method
that rotates PCA scores before PFN inference. We theoretically show that
rotation makes the column variances nearly equal, so that standardization
approximately preserves the embedding geometry. Across image and text
classification tasks, encoders, and tabular foundation models, RoFE improves
low-data performance over the PCA interface and outperforms baselines that
require task-specific training. The improvements also extend to regression.
