Title: CHICO: Bridging Sparse Metadata and Multimodal Evidence in Cultural Heritage Collections
Authors: Vagner Inácio de Oliveira, Paula Dornhofer Paro Costa
ID: 2611.30923
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30923

Abstract:
Cultural heritage aggregators provide access to millions of digitized items. Yet
their catalog records often contain sparse, heterogeneous metadata, with
associated media links that may not lead to accessible images. Structuring and
validating these resources for multimodal AI research can support reproducible
experimentation. To this end, we present CHICO (Cultural Heritage Images and
Catalog Objects), a multimodal dataset derived from Europeana that brings
together curated catalog fields, images, and contextual information to support
evidence-grounded description generation while retaining the original Europeana
records. It comprises more than 60,000 cultural heritage records with associated
images. Although originally developed for that purpose, it can also support
multimodal information retrieval, metadata enrichment, and evidence-based
evaluation.
