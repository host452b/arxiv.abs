Title: Open-Set Radio Frequency Fingerprint Identification with Feature Fusion and Vision Transformer
Authors: Jie Ma, Junqing Zhang, Guanxiong Shen, Linning Peng, Alan Marshall
ID: 2611.31636
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31636

Abstract:
Radio frequency fingerprint identification (RFFI) is a promising device
authentication technique for the Internet of Things (IoT) that leverages
hardware impairments to uniquely identify devices. However, most existing RFFI
systems operate under a closed-set assumption and lack the ability to detect
rogue devices, which poses significant security risks. In this paper, we propose
an open-set RFFI approach that jointly performs rogue device detection and
legitimate device classification. Specifically, we designed a multi-task RFFI
system that integrates an autoencoder (AE) for rogue device detection and a
fully connected (FC) layer for classification. To enrich the feature
representation, we generated two channel-independent spectrograms (CISs) with
distinct window lengths from the I/Q signals, capturing complementary time-
frequency characteristics. These CISs were processed in parallel by two Vision
Transformer (ViT) networks, and their outputs were fused via feature
concatenation to construct a device fingerprint for both tasks. We built a
testbed involving ten legitimate LoRa devices and five rogue devices. We
conducted a comprehensive experimental evaluation and compared our approach with
several benchmark algorithms. Experimental results show that our fused feature
multi-task RFFI consistently outperformed the baselines in both legitimate
device classification and rogue device detection. Our proposed approach achieved
an accuracy of 91.1% and an area under the curve (AUC) of over 0.72 in a
realistic wireless scenario where the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) was 20 to 30
dB.
