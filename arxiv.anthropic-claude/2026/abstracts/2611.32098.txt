Title: ActiveBridge: Scaling Active Prior for Robots through Egocentric Perception-Interaction Modeling
Authors: Zixiao Gu, Qiren Qin, Yu He, Zhengyang Liu, Da Huang, Hongquan Zhang, Simo Wu, Peng Zhou, Guangnan Ye, Yunbo Wang et al.
ID: 2611.32098
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32098

Abstract:
Robotics foundation models are now widely used for manipulation, and egocentric
videos offer rich data for scaling. However, existing methods overlook the human
active priors captured in egocentric videos, which limits performance in
unstructured scenes. They act on given observations instead of actively seeking
information as humans do. We propose ActiveBridge, a training framework that
learns these human active priors and transfers them to robots through world-
action modeling. We design a robot that actively controls its viewpoint through
head actions to acquire the visual information needed for manipulation using a
single head camera, without wrist cameras. During human training, an
instruction-conditioned model jointly predicts future egocentric videos and
synchronized head-wrist action trajectories, with head actions providing
explicit supervision for active perception. Experiments show that ActiveBridge
outperforms baselines on active manipulation tasks, while ablations demonstrate
the benefit of joint head-wrist action supervision.
