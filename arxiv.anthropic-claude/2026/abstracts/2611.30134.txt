Title: WASP: Continual Weight Adaptation for Streaming LLM Personalization
Authors: Linxi Xie, Chonghua Liao, Mengxuan Wu, Guandao Yang, Zhangyang Wang, Kai Wang
ID: 2611.30134
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30134

Abstract:
Personalized large language models (LLMs) should continually adapt to users'
evolving preferences. Yet extracting preference changes from noisy interaction
histories while supporting inexpensive updates remains challenging. We introduce
WASP (Weight Adaptation for Streaming Personalization), a recurrent framework
that captures fine-grained preferences directly from raw user interactions and
generates personalized LoRA adapters without expensive per-user optimization.
Specifically, WASP achieves this by training a shared hypernetwork that
maintains a compact user representation and outputs adapter weights in a single
forward pass. As new interactions arrive, WASP updates this representation
without reprocessing full interaction history, enabling continual adaptation
with per-update costs independent of history length. Experiments on
personalization benchmarks and chronological interaction streams show that WASP
sustains high-quality personalization over long horizons under fixed compute and
storage budgets. Together, these results support efficient streaming
personalization and facilitate privacy-preserving on-device deployment.
