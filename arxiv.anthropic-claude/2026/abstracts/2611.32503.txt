Title: Analyzing Single-Bit Flips on LLMs and Their Backbone-Matched VLMs
Authors: Xiaomeng Wang, Martha Larson, Zhuoran Liu, Zhengyu Zhao
ID: 2611.32503
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32503

Abstract:
Open-weight large language models (LLMs) enable offline weight analysis and are
reused as backbones in vision-language models (VLMs), increasing the risk of
bit-flip attacks that manipulate stored weight bits to induce malicious model
behavior. An adversary could identify vulnerable bits on a public LLM and later
flip the same locations in a deployed LLM or its backbone-matched VLMs. A
single-bit flip is particularly practical in this setting because it is easier
to achieve under hardware fault attacks than multi-bit flips. However, existing
selectors are mostly limited to multi-bit flips, without in-depth analysis of
single-bit flips. In this paper, we adapt representative data-free and data-
based bit selectors to identify candidate bits. We evaluate each bit
independently on source LLMs and at the same logical coordinates in backbone-
matched VLMs (without re-search), considering both performance degradation and
prediction patterns across multiple inputs. Results show impact of single-bit
flips varies across selectors on LLMs and their backbone-matched VLMs. A single-
bit flip can make the model tend to produce a repeated output string across
different inputs. Our robustness analysis reveals vulnerabilities in terms of
performance and prediction patterns.
