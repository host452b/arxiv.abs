Title: Is Prosody Encoded in the Neural Audio Codec Mimi?
Authors: Nicolas Ballier, Artem Saloev, Erin Pacquetet, Taylor Arnold
ID: 2611.32795
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32795

Abstract:
Neural audio codecs (NACs) have become a fundamental infrastructure for modern
speech-language models, discretizing continuous speech signals into token
sequences that enable large language models to process and generate audio. Most
research evaluates codec quality at the decoding phase, assessing the
naturalness of reconstructed speech. However, little research investigates what
phonetic information is captured during encoding. In this paper, we investigated
the potential correlates of prosody in the NAC Mimi, a codec used by
conversational speech agents such as Moshi and Sesame and used in downstream
tasks such as speech-to-speech translation (Hibiki).
