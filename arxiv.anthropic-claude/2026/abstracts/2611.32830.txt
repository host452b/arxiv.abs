Title: PtychoFM: A Foundation Model for Ptychographic Imaging
Authors: Aileen Luo, Emon Dey, Ming Du, Tao Zhou, Peco Myint, Himanshu Goel, Shashank Subramanian, Xiangyu Yin, Tekin Bicer, Siniša Veseli et al.
ID: 2611.32830
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32830

Abstract:
Ptychography provides high-resolution lensless imaging across X-ray, electron,
and optical microscopy, but reconstructing images from diffraction measurements
generally requires computationally intensity iterative phase retrieval. These
methods often require experiment-specific analysis, limiting real-time feedback
as data acquisition rates increase. Here, we demonstrate PtychoFM, a physics-
aware foundation model for ptychographic imaging that combines large-scale pre-
training with experiment-specific fine-tuning to reconstruct complex object
images directly from individual diffraction patterns. Trained using a
differentiable forward model without object-space labels, PtychoFM transfers
from step-scan training data on one sample to fly-scan measurements of other
samples with different feature sizes, morphologies, and refraction indices. At a
synchrotron beamline, diffraction frames were streamed from the detector to a
single GPU for inference at up to 200 frames per second and returned for real-
time visualization. PtychoFM provides a practical route to rapid, transferable
ptychographic reconstruction and live experimental feedback under nonideal
conditions.
