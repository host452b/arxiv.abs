Title: Look Before You Commit: Visual-Evidence-Guided Decoding for Multimodal Diffusion Language Models
Authors: Tongxiao Hu, Yikai Zhao, Qiyan Zhao, Xiaosong Yuan, Xuhang Chen, Pengzhou Cheng, Xiaofeng Zhang
ID: 2611.31677
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31677

Abstract:
Diffusion multimodal large language models rely on confidence to decide when to
commit candidate tokens during generation. In multimodal tasks, however,
confidence cannot distinguish whether a candidate originates from image evidence
or the language prior. We perform paired forward ablation and find that nearly
nine out of ten high-confidence commits are insensitive to the image, while
degenerate and hallucination samples exhibit significant increases in the
entropy of deep-layer attention organization. Based on this diagnosis, we
propose CD-APC, a training-free decoding method. CD-APC performs evidence-
adjusted scoring within a base-supported pool and uses dual gating based on
model acceptability and contrastive concentration to decide whether to commit,
together with an inverse-window mechanism that adapts to inter-step difficulty.
Across two backbones and five benchmarks, CD-APC consistently improves
performance, indicating that stable visual evidence improves model outputs.
