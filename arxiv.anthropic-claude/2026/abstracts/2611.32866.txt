Title: Weight Scale Rebalancing: Transferable Training Recipes for Transformers
Authors: Yikun Hou, Naomi Graham, Alp Yurtsever
ID: 2611.32866
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32866

Abstract:
Training Transformers requires careful hyperparameter tuning, which becomes
increasingly costly for large models. We introduce UDV-Rebalance (UDVR), a
reparameterization that inserts an auxiliary diagonal matrix $D$ between
naturally paired weight matrices $(U,V)$ appearing throughout Transformer
architectures. UDVR periodically rebalances the factors to maintain unit-norm
columns in $U$ and unit-norm rows in $V$, with $D$ absorbing the corresponding
scales. With rebalancing, a single hyperparameter setting of UDVR remains
effective across model scales, architectures, and data distributions. This
yields a tuning-free rule that adapts to each weight matrix using only its
dimensions. We fit this rule once on ViT models trained on CIFAR-10 and
CIFAR-100, and it remains valid when the activation function or the optimizer is
changed. The same rule transfers to new image domains in DomainNet, to ViT,
Swin, and MaxViT on ImageNet, and to language-model pretraining with up to 1.3B
parameters. UDVR consistently outperforms Weight Normalization and $σ$Reparam
and matches a vanilla baseline whose hyperparameters are swept on each target
model.
