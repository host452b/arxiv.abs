Title: Near-Far Attention: Region-Aware Sparse Retrieval for Long-Context LLM Decoding
Authors: YuanShuo Wang, Jianchao Tan, Lei Gong, Teng Wang, Wenqi Lou, Xuehai Zhou
ID: 2611.30554
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30554

Abstract:
Sparse attention reduces the cost of long-context decoding, but token selection
introduces additional scoring and ranking overhead. Moreover, uniform page
scoring overlooks differences in relevance between near and far context. We
propose Near-Far Attention (NFA), which adapts retrieval to regional attention
patterns while keeping candidate scoring lightweight. During prefill, the
Lightweight Gate Estimator identifies regions that can be skipped during
decoding, adding less than 1% to dense-prefill time. For the remaining regions,
the Calibration-Free Subspace Extractor supplies compact features shared by
page-level and token-level scoring without offline calibration. The Distance-
Aware Two-Level Selector then scores near and far pages differently and jointly
ranks candidate tokens under a shared budget. Evaluations across model families,
parameter scales, and GQA/MLA architectures show aggregate task quality
comparable to full attention. On Qwen3-8B at 512K context length, NFA achieves
up to $5.49\times$ operator speedup and $2.97\times$ whole-model decoding
speedup over FlashInfer, including selection overhead.
