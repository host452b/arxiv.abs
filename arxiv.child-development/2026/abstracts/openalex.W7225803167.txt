Title: How Kindergarten Teachers Enact a Structured Computational Thinking Intervention in Early Childhood Education: An Experience Report
Authors: Tobias Bahr
ID: openalex.W7225803167
URL: https://doi.org/10.1145/3795860.3846227

Abstract:
Computational thinking (CT) has been integrated into early childhood education
(ECE) in numerous research projects, yet teachers' experiences of enacting CT
activities themselves remain underexplored. This experience report describes how
22 kindergarten teachers at seven German kindergartens enacted a structured,
eight-activity CT intervention with preschool-age children (5–6 years old) over
eight weeks, following a single 90-minute preparatory workshop. Data consisted
of structured observation protocols completed by the teachers after each
session, cross-validated with field notes from external researchers, and were
analysed using thematic analysis. Teachers found the materials age-appropriate
and easy to enact, adhered closely to the provided sequence while making small
context-sensitive adaptations, and identified concrete challenges (e.g.,
children's left/right confusion when taking the robot's perspective) and success
factors (e.g., clear, symbol-based materials that freed teachers to focus on
scaffolding rather than explaining the tools). This poster provides lessons
learned for practitioners and designers of CT materials for ECE, including
recommendations on which activities to revise or drop, and reflects on the
potential of brief, low-cost professional development for ECE teachers.
