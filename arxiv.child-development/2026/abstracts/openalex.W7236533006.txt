Title: TRANSFORMATIONAL LEADERSHIP AND TEACHERS’ PEDAGOGICAL LEARNING IN ISLAMIC KINDERGARTEN EDUCATION: A QUALITATIVE STUDY OF TWO INSTITUTIONS
Authors: Entin Nuryati, Ayi Najmul Hidayat, Maman Suherman, N. Dede Khoeriah
ID: openalex.W7236533006
URL: https://openalex.org/W7236533006

Abstract:
Background: Transformational leadership offers a framework for examining how
principals support teachers’ professional learning through role modelling,
communication of educational purposes, intellectual stimulation, and
individualized consideration. Purpose: This study examines the enactment of the
four dimensions of transformational leadership, the organizational conditions
shaping support for teachers’ pedagogical learning, and the implications for
practice at TK Aisyiyah 1 Kebayoran Baru and TK Bunayya Islamic Pre School
Kebayoran Lama, South Jakarta. Methods: This qualitative descriptive study used
in-depth interviews, observations, and document analysis. The two institutions
were purposively selected because of their different organizational
characteristics and educational services. Data were analyzed through data
condensation, data display, and conclusion drawing and verification. Results:
The leadership practices discussed include professional role modelling,
communication of educational purposes, reflection and supervision,
collaboration, and individualized guidance. TK Aisyiyah 1 is characterized by a
family-oriented work culture and centre-based learning, whereas TK Bunayya
integrates two curricula and provides inclusion support, stimulation classes,
and day care. These differences identify organizational contexts relevant to
understanding leadership support. Conclusion: Transformational leadership needs
to be considered in relation to teachers’ pedagogical responsibilities and
institutional characteristics. The findings provide contextual insight into
professional support but do not establish measurable improvement in pedagogical
competence or a causal relationship
