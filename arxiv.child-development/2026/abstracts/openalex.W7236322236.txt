Title: Curriculum Design for Kindergarten Toddler Classes under China's Integrated Childcare and Preschool Education Model: Theoretical Foundations and Implementation Pathways
Authors: Xiao Wang
ID: openalex.W7236322236
URL: https://doi.org/10.70088/fcpdny52

Abstract:
China is expanding childcare for children under three at remarkable speed, and
opening toddler classes in kindergartens has become a central strategy under the
integrated childcare and preschool education model. Most kindergartens, however,
have no curriculum built for two- to three-year-olds, and simply pushing the
preschool curriculum downward risks placing academic pressure on very young
children. This study asks what theoretical principles should anchor curriculum
design for kindergarten toddler classes, how these principles can be organized
into an operational framework, and what conditions implementation requires.
Drawing on an analysis of national policy documents issued between 2019 and 2025
and a structured synthesis of 34 journal studies, we designed an integrated
relational curriculum framework. The framework rests on attachment theory,
sociocultural theory, the educare tradition, and developmental neuroscience. It
organizes provision around four components: secure-base relationships maintained
by a key person system, care routines treated as curriculum, responsive play-
based learning, and family-kindergarten co-construction, with an observation and
assessment cycle running through all four. Implementation pathways cover
workforce preparation, transition and continuity, home partnership, and quality
assurance. The framework offers kindergartens a workable scaffold for toddler-
class provision and a reference for a much needed national curriculum guideline.
