Title: From potential to practice: A critical realist exploration of GenAI use in Australian early childhood education and care
Authors: Ali Tehrani, Parian Madanipour, Caroline Cohrssen, Frada V. Burstein, Emma Forsyth
ID: openalex.W7225728294
URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compedu.2026.105791

Abstract:
The rapid uptake of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) tools has driven
significant change across the education sector. Despite this, empirical
understanding of how early childhood education and care (ECEC) educators use
GenAI in their professional practice remains limited. This pioneering study used
the Context-Mechanism-Outcomes (CMO) schema as an organising framework to
explore how Australian ECEC educators use GenAI tools, including the Contexts in
which they are used, the Mechanisms of use, and the associated Outcomes. Using
an online qualitative survey of 75 participants across Australia, guided by
critical realism and abductive reasoning, this exploratory study shows that
GenAI users outnumbered non-users in this sample, and four distinct, plausible
explanatory use patterns emerged. Further, the identified Mechanisms reveal that
educators’ use of GenAI tools for non-contact work activity domains is
associated with benefits and constraints, and with some tensions in the
Outcomes. In light of these findings, our study highlights the need for sector-
specific policy development, professional guidance, and further research to
support educators’ informed use of GenAI.
