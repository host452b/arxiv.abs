Title: Influencing Factors of Adolescent Identity Formation Difficulties: Literature Review Based on the Perspective of Identity Development
Authors: Haiyi Zhao
ID: openalex.W7220966473
URL: https://doi.org/10.54254/2753-7048/2026.37525

Abstract:
Adolescence is a critical period in which individuals develop a coherent sense
of self and work toward identity formation. In this process, teenagers integrate
their own interests, abilities, values, expectations and ideals of others to
form a relatively coordinated and stable self-identity. However, affected by
rapid physical changes, social role changes and future social choices, not all
teenagers can smoothly integrate their identity, and some fail to complete the
task of identity construction well. Teenagers' exploration of self-identity
affects their current mental health level and relates to the normal development
of future education, employment and social functions. Erikson pointed out that
the main task of youth development is to solve the crisis of identity and
confusion. If this crisis is not properly handled, it is easy for people to
experience the state of self-identity crisis, loss of life goals and failure to
form a stable belief commitment. Later, Marcia's identity state model also
emphasized that only through exploration and commitment can a complete sense of
identity be achieved. Nowadays, the growth environment of middle school students
is increasingly diverse and complex, so the difficulties in identity
construction result from diversified factors rather than a single one. Based on
identity development theoretical framework, this paper adopts literature
analysis method to build a comprehensive cause analysis model of adolescent
identity crisis and elaborate the influence and interaction of family, personal
and social factors on adolescent identity construction.
