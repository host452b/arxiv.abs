Title: Teachers’ Practices of Play-Based Learning and Learners’ Early Childhood Development
Authors: Margie P. Cusay, Ninfa C. Osias
ID: openalex.W7225085759
URL: https://doi.org/10.54536/ajahs.v5i4.8862

Abstract:
Play-based learning has become an important pedagogical approach for promoting
learners’ engagement, inclusion, and holistic development during the early
childhood year. The study examined the extent of teachers’ practices of play-
based learning and learners’ early childhood development in select districts of
Balingasag, Division of Misamis Oriental, during School Year 2024-2025.
Specifically, the study aimed to find out the extent of teachers’ practices of
play-based learning, examine the learners’ level of early childhood development,
determine the significant relationship between the teachers’ practices of play-
based learning and the learners’ early childhood development, and test the
significant difference in the teachers’ practices of play-based learning when
grouped according to their profile. The study involved 150 Kindergarten teachers
selected using the universal sampling technique. The study employed a
descriptive-correlational research design and used a researcher-made
questionnaire to gather data. The study utilized descriptive and inferential
statistics to analyze the data. The findings revealed that most respondents have
highly practiced structured play while their learners are highly advanced in
physical domain. Overall, the data showed no significant relationship across
variables. However, there was no significant difference in the teachers’
practices of play-based learning when grouped according to their profile. In
conclusion, the play-based learning serves as a critical pedagogical approach
for fostering holistic child development. It is recommended that teachers’
support may optimize play-based learning practices, with set of rules to follow
for future research in early childhood education.
