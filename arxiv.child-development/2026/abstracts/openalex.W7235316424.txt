Title: COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS OF MATERNAL AND PATERNAL ISLAMIC PARENTING PRACTICES: EVIDENCE FROM THE DEVELOPMENT OF THE ISLAMIC PARENTING STYLE SCALE
Authors: Maryam Noor, Muhammad Jahanzeb Khan
ID: openalex.W7235316424
URL: https://doi.org/10.57144/hi.v49i3.1453

Abstract:
This study aimed to explore the development of standards for Islamic parenting
practices, emphasizing the distinct yet complementary roles of fathers and
mothers. Drawing on psychological research, Islamic teachings, and Muslim
scholars’ perspectives, this study examines how religious considerations shape
the parenting styles of both mothers and fathers. Muslim parents need evidence-
based, successful intervention programs, but the creation of trustworthy and
valid measuring tools, like the Islamic Parenting Style Scale (IPSS), is
essential to the program's success. The study's two main goals were to (1)
establish the framework of the Islamic parenting style with a father and mother
version and (2) create IPSS norms for parents in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. After EFA
and inter-item correlation, 64 items for the father version (N=409) and 66 items
for the mother version (N= 409) were retained to be included in the final
analysis of CFA. EFA suggested a three-factor structure for the IPSS, including
the cognitive/reflective, guidance/supervision, and compassionate domains. CFA
confirmed these three factors, with 28 items for the father version (N=250) and
25 items for the mother version (N=250). As a psychometrically robust tool, the
IPSS provides future users with a highly valid and reliable way to measure three
major aspects of Islamic parenting. Findings add to understanding gender-
specific forms of Islamic parenting, extending earlier scale validation work
through comparison analysis.” It is predicted that the IPSS will expand
academics' understanding of Islamic parenting techniques among Muslim
populations. This presents considerable promise for accurate evaluations and
successful, focused parenting interventions in the Muslim community of Khyber
Pakhtunkhwa.
