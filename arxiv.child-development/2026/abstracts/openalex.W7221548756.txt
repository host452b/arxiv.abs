Title: Finding Pride Under the Rainbow: Exploring Middle Adolescents’ Experiences in Self-Discovery and Identity Formation
Authors: Guia Andrea Huertas, Cris John Bryan C. Dela Cruz
ID: openalex.W7221548756
URL: https://doi.org/10.70838/pemj.631006

Abstract:
This transcendental phenomenological study explored the lived experiences of
middle adolescents as they navigated self-discovery and identity formation in
Isulan, Sultan Kudarat. The study involved 10 participants aged 14-17 who
identified as gay, lesbian, or bisexual. They were purposively selected, and
their experiences were gathered through in-depth semi-structured interviews.
Thematic content analysis was used to identify common patterns and themes across
their stories. The findings showed that family, peers, school, community, and
societal expectations played an important role in how participants understood
and expressed their identities. Their narratives reflected moments of confusion,
self-doubt, fear or rejection, and emotional difficulty as they tried to
understand themselves while responding to the expectations around them. However,
their experiences also reflected gradual self-acceptance and growing confidence
in expressing who they are. Support from other, greater awareness of gender
diversity, and personal reflection helped participants make sense of their
experiences and move toward accepting themselves. Overall, the study shows that
identity formation is a personal and ongoing process shaped by both individual
experiences and relationships with others. The findings emphasize the value of
creating supportive and inclusive spaces in families, schools, and communities
where LGBTQ+ adolescents can feel accepted, respected, and safe as they explore
and develop their identities. The study offers insights that may help educators,
parents, guidance counselors, and other stakeholders better understand the
experiences of adolescents navigating self-discovery and identity formation.
