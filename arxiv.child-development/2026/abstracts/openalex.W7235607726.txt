Title: Digital Transformation Management in Fostering Learning Innovation in Kindergartens
Authors: Nita Nurbaeti, Okke Rosmaladewi
ID: openalex.W7235607726
URL: https://doi.org/10.63922/ijevss.v5i04.7453

Abstract:
The availability of digital technology in early childhood education does not
necessarily lead to learning innovation without appropriate management. This
study aims to analyze the management of digital transformation in fostering
learning innovation at TK Kartika XIX-34 Purwakarta and TKAT Miftahul Huda
Purwakarta from the perspective of the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) cycle, including
supporting factors, inhibiting factors, and differences in management between
the two institutions. The study employed a qualitative approach with a
comparative case study design. Data were collected through in-depth interviews
with principals, teachers, and parents, classroom observation, and
documentation, and were analyzed using the interactive model together with
within-case and cross-case analysis. The results show that both institutions
follow a PDCA pattern with different emphases. Planning was based on learning
needs and involved teachers; implementation was supported by training,
mentoring, and supervision; evaluation drew on supervision, teacher reflection,
children’s responses, and parental input; and follow-up took the form of
revising teaching modules, media, and training, as well as standardizing good
practices. TK Kartika XIX-34 stood out in infrastructure development and direct
supervision, whereas TKAT Miftahul Huda emphasized joint evaluation, media
development, and the integration of Qur’anic values. Supporting factors included
leadership, teachers, training, collaboration, devices, and internet access,
while barriers related to teacher competence, connectivity, devices, and time.
The study concludes that PDCA functions as a mechanism linking the management of
digital transformation to the emergence of learning innovation, and that its
application is contextual, depending on institutional characteristics
