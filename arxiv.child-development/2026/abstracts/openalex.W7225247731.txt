Title: Perinatal mental health care: current evidence and future directions
Authors: Tiago Miguel Pinto, Raquel Almeida Costa
ID: openalex.W7225247731
URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpobgyn.2026.102786

Abstract:
Perinatal mental disorders are a major contributor to maternal morbidity
worldwide and represent an important public health challenge. This narrative
review aims to provide a global perspective on women’s mental disorders and care
during the perinatal period. Literature was identified through database searches
and backward citation searching, prioritizing systematic reviews and meta-
analyses, and studies published within the last five years. We have reviewed the
evidence on (1) the epidemiology of perinatal mental disorders; (2) the burden
of these disorders for women, infants, healthcare systems, and society,
including adverse obstetric outcomes, bonding difficulties, and multi-
generational developmental impacts; (3) unmet needs and treatment gaps in mental
healthcare; (4) barriers and facilitators influencing access to and engagement
with care; (5) international clinical guidelines; and (6) the challenges
associated with its implementation across diverse healthcare settings. Future
priorities for research, practice, and policy in perinatal mental health were
discussed, including the potential role of digital innovations and artificial
intelligence in enhancing risk-stratification and improving global access to
care.
