Title: A Digital Intervention Reduces Childhood Obesity Risk Behaviors: Analysis of Secondary Outcomes of the Greenlight Plus RCT
Authors: Charles T. Wood, Eliana M. Perrin, Melissa C. Kay, Aihua Bian, Jonathan S. Schildcrout, William J. Heerman, H. Shonna Yin, Russell L. Rothman, Kori B. Flower, Lee Sanders et al.
ID: openalex.W7235266616
URL: https://doi.org/10.1002/oby.70324

Abstract:
OBJECTIVE: This study examined key behavioral outcomes from the Greenlight Plus
RCT. METHODS: Greenlight Plus was a comparative effectiveness trial from birth
to 2 years of life that enrolled 900 dyads from October 2019 through January
2022 across six US medical centers. The clinic-only group received counseling at
visits, and the clinic + digital group received tailored, responsive text
messages. We assessed 14 parent-reported behaviors selected a priori. We tested
overall effects, then estimated individual outcome intervention effects with
logistic regression and proportional odds logistic regression models. RESULTS:
We analyzed 857 dyads (424 in the clinic + digital group and 433 in the clinic-
only group) and found a significant effect on behaviors overall (Χ2 = 40.54, df
= 14, p = 0.0002), including greater breastfeeding at 6 months (adjusted odds
ratio [aOR] 1.46, 95% CI: 1.02, 2.10), lower sugar-sweetened beverage
consumption at 9 (aOR 0.60, 95% CI: 0.40, 0.90) and 12 months (aOR 0.58, 95% CI:
0.40, 0.86), lower screen time at 9 (aOR 0.59, 95% CI: 0.45, 0.79) and 15 months
(aOR 0.61, 95% CI: 0.45, 0.82), and more "tummy time" at 4 months (aOR 1.46, 95%
CI: 1.12, 1.92). CONCLUSIONS: The Greenlight Plus intervention led to
significant improvements in breastfeeding, sugar-sweetened beverage consumption,
screen time, and tummy time. TRIAL REGISTRATION: ClinicalTrials.gov:
NCT04042467.
