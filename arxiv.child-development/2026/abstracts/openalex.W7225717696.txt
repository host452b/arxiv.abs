Title: Internet Connectivity Quality and its Technology Integration Practices of Kindergarten Teachers
Authors: Em-em Alejo, Anna Rose Hallazgo, Ellen Linogo, Lady-ann Lomod, Arien Sotillo
ID: openalex.W7225717696
URL: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24096259

Abstract:
This study determined the relationship between internet connectivity quality and
technology integration practices of kindergarten teachers. Specifically, it
assessed the respondents’ profile, the level of internet connectivity quality in
terms of accessibility, reliability, speed, and stability, the level of
technology integration practices in lesson preparation, classroom instruction,
and assessment and communication, and the significant relationship between the
two variables. The study involved 41 kindergarten teachers in the Division of
Tanjay City. A descriptive-correlational research design was used, and the data
were analyzed using frequency, percentage, mean, standard deviation, and
Spearman’s Rank Correlation Coefficient.The findings revealed that most
respondents were female, had 7–10 years of teaching experience in Early
Childhood Education, had earned Master’s units, and had attended trainings
related to digital teaching tools. The level of internet connectivity quality
was interpreted as Moderate Quality, with an overall composite mean of 2.92.
Among the dimensions, stability obtained the highest mean, while speed received
the lowest mean, indicating that internet speed remains a major concern.
Meanwhile, the level of technology integration practices was interpreted as
Moderate Practice, with an overall composite mean of 3.26. Assessment and
communication obtained the highest mean, followed by lesson preparation and
classroom instruction. Furthermore, the results showed a strong positive and
significant relationship between internet connectivity quality and technology
integration practices, with a Spearman’s rho value of 0.659 and a p-value of <
.001. This indicates that better internet connectivity quality is associated
with higher levels of technology integration among kindergarten teachers. Based
on the findings, an ICT support plan was proposed to improve internet speed and
stability through upgraded internet services, optimized network infrastructure,
backup connections, and offline instructional resources. The study concludes
that reliable internet connectivity plays an important role in supporting
effective technology integration in kindergarten teaching and learning.
