Title: Play-Based Learning for Emotional Regulation in Kindergarten: Assessing Effectiveness and Teachers' Implementation Insights
Authors: Jessica D. Limbaga
ID: openalex.W7239365258
URL: https://doi.org/10.38124/ijisrt/26nov224

Abstract:
This experimental study investigated the impact of play-based learning on the
emotional regulation of kindergarten learners at Nabhang Elementary School,
Ayungon District 2, Negros Oriental, during the school year 20252026. Two
groups, control and experimental, were assessed using an adopted questionnaire
to measure developmental profiles, literacy and numeracy skills, and emotional
regulation parameters, including emotional control, emotional expression,
frustration tolerance, social interaction, and self-soothing recovery. Data were
analyzed using simple percentage, frequency count, weighted mean, and test of
differences.
