Title: Teacher Scaffolding in Kindergarten Drawing Activities: A Rural-Urban Comparison in Jiangxi Province, China
Authors: 蔡今中
ID: openalex.W7237361653
URL: https://doi.org/10.54097/zsxk4945

Abstract:
Teacher assistance does not necessarily constitute scaffolding. In drawing
activities, support must be interpreted through children’s intentions, responses
and subsequent control. This article develops a rural–urban comparison framework
for kindergarten teaching in Jiangxi Province, China. It distinguishes verbal,
demonstrative, material, affective and peer-related support from the process
characteristics of contingency, fading and transfer of responsibility. An event-
based design links child cues, teacher responses, child action and subsequent
adjustment within the parent study’s observation framework. Independent
synthetic counts demonstrate the importance of observation-time and eligibility
denominators. Hypothetical episodes distinguish brief demonstration from
takeover and attentive waiting from ignored requests. The framework contributes
operational rules for comparing support without equating more intervention or
more open questions with higher quality. Actual group differences and sustained
learning outcomes require original records and further evidence.
