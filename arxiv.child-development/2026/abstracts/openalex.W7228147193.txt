Title: CHANGES IN KINDERGARTEN CHILDREN'S SOCIAL SKILLS FOLLOWING GROUP TRADITIONAL GAMES: A ONE-GROUP PRETEST–POSTTEST STUDY
Authors: Razulaini Razulaini, Atika Wirdasari
ID: openalex.W7228147193

Abstract:
Abstrak Keterampilan sosial anak taman kanak-kanak sering belum berkembang
optimal. Penelitian ini mendeskripsikan perubahan skor keterampilan sosial anak
setelah kegiatan permainan tradisional kelompok di TK Mutiara Agam, Kecamatan

butir dalam lima dimensi. Rata-rata skor naik dari 46,00 menjadi 66,00, dengan

Abstract Kindergarten children's social skills are often not yet optimally
developed. This study describes changes in children's social skills scores
following group traditional games at TK Mutiara Agam, Tanjung Mutiara District.
A one-group pretest–posttest pre-experimental design was used with ten children
aged 5 years 2 months to 5 years 6 months. The activity was one session of about
60 minutes in which the children played ular naga and gobak sodor (galah
panjang); the pre-test was conducted on 7 October 2026 and the post-test on 8
October 2026. The class teacher rated each child with a 20-item, five-dimension
observation questionnaire. The mean score increased from 46.00 to 66.00, a mean
change of 20.00 points (95% CI 16.05–23.95), t(9) = 11.46, p < .001, dz = 3.62
(95% CI 1.86–5.37). Scores increased for every child and in all five dimensions,
and the result held when one pair matched without a name was excluded. However,
the absence of a comparison group, the single session, the one-day interval, and
a single rater who also led the activity mean that the change cannot be
interpreted as a causal effect. The findings are preliminary and require testing
with a comparison group and independent observers. Keywords: traditional group
games; social skills; preschool children; cooperative play; pretest–posttest
study
