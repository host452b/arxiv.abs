Title: MedICL: Modality-Anatomy-Aware Visual In-Context Learning for Medical Visual Question Answering
Authors: Ji-jun Park, Soo-joon Choi, Seungmin Han, Haeun Kwon, Jiwon Jeong, Taeyang Yoon
ID: 2611.00067
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00067

Abstract:
Training-free adaptation of medical vision-language models (LVLMs) is a
deployment requirement, yet visual in-context learning (ICL) injects modality-
and style-mismatched demonstrations into medical contexts, teaching a frozen
model the wrong answering style. We present MedICL, which curates demonstrations
instead of retraining the model. MADS selects modality-anatomy aligned
demonstrations that cover complementary interpretation dimensions; W2C curates
them with weak retrieval signals and a strong verification vote that checks
demonstrations rather than the model's own outputs; ECC answers with W anchored
demonstration sets, aggregates by abnormality-aware signatures, and re-answers
once on conflict. On VQA-RAD, SLAKE, and PathVQA under the LLaVA-Med zero-shot
protocol, MedICL improves the strongest zero-shot baseline by +11 - +14 points
on open-ended and +7 - +14 on closed questions, recovers 71% of fine-tuned open-
ended performance on VQA-RAD at zero training cost, and stays robust to shifted
or corrupted demonstration banks.
