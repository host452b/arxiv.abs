Title: OrionMoE-v1.0: A Mixture-of-Experts Foundation Model for Tabular In-Context Learning
Authors: Mohamed Bouadi, Nassim Bouarour, Aditya Tanna, Vinay Kumar Sankarapu
ID: 2610.20418
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20418

Abstract:
Tabular foundation models enable in-context learning by predicting unseen
observations from a labeled support table without task-specific parameter
updates. Despite recent advances, most existing approaches rely on dense
architectures that apply the same transformations across features and datasets.
We introduce OrionMoE-Small-v1.0, a compact tabular foundation model that
combines distribution-aware representations with dataset-conditioned sparse
mixture-of-experts computation. Built on a dual-axis transformer architecture,
OrionMoE-Small selectively activates experts with routing decisions informed by
both individual cell representations and the broader dataset context. This
design enables adaptive allocation of computation across heterogeneous tabular
problems while retaining a shared computational pathway across tasks. The model
is pretrained entirely on synthetic classification tasks generated from a
structured causal prior and performs downstream prediction without gradient-
based adaptation. Experiments across multiple tabular benchmarks demonstrate
competitive performance against substantially larger foundation models, with
particularly strong results on small-context and high-dimensional datasets.
Further analysis reveals favorable relative performance under class imbalance
and highlights the importance of feature composition.
