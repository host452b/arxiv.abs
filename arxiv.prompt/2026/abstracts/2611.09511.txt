Title: V-STORM: A Structure-Aware Grounded Chain of Thought Dataset for Faithful and Fine-Grained Spatio-Temporal Video Reasoning
Authors: Dian Jiao, Yuqian Yuan, Jiaqi Zhu, Mingjian Gao, Siwen Tan, Yunqi Cao, Jinhui Li, Sijing Li, Zhenxuan Fan, Haoyang Wang et al.
ID: 2611.09511
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09511

Abstract:
While MLLMs can often identify what happens in a video, they still struggle with
fine-grained spatio-temporal reasoning, which requires precise grounding when
and where the supporting evidence appears and composing such evidence into a
coherent reasoning chain. This challenge is exacerbated by the lack of scalable
data construction and evaluation pipelines that provide granular, verifiable
supervision linking reasoning steps to explicit spatio-temporal evidence.To
address this gap, we present V-STORM, a structure-aware framework that
transforms videos into Spatio-Temporal Scene Graphs (STSGs) and derives Grounded
Chain of Thought (GCoT) from validated evidence paths, making each reasoning
step traceable to structured and verifiable spatio-temporal evidence.Under this
framework, we construct a 260K-sample training set and a 3K-sample human-
verified benchmark, both featuring GCoT annotations that link reasoning to
explicit spatio-temporal evidence.Experiments reveal a clear separation between
evidence localization and evidence composition: current MLLMs can often identify
relevant spatio-temporal cues, yet still fail to integrate them into correct
multi-step reasoning, exposing a bottleneck that answer-only evaluation largely
obscures. Moreover, fine-tuning with V-STORM substantially improves both answer
accuracy and grounding faithfulness, validating its effectiveness as supervision
for faithful and evidence-grounded video reasoning. Our dataset, benchmark, and
source code will be publicly released.
