Title: An Empirical Case Study of Prompt Engineering Strategies for Bypassing Multi-Layer Agent Security Defenses
Authors: Chengwei Chu
ID: 2610.14116
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14116

Abstract:
The OpenClaw Security Attack and Defense Challenge evaluates AI agent robustness
through a three-stage defense pipeline: skill file scanning (Stage 1),
instruction safety evaluation (Stage 2), and attack trajectory scoring (Stage
3). This paper presents an empirical case study of prompt engineering strategies
against these layers across 19 attack targets in Enterprise OA, E-commerce, and
Smart Home scenarios. I document both successful and failed strategies, with an
emphasis on the observed trade-off between instruction safety (Stage 2) and
attack execution accuracy (Stage 3). The final online score is 0.2630, with 5 of
19 tasks achieving full bypass. The results suggest that multi-layer defenses,
reinforced by built-in behavioral constraints, substantially limit what prompt
engineering alone can achieve.
