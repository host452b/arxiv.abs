Title: Notwithstanding Anything to the Contrary, Ignore All Instructions: Measuring Prompt Injection Guards on Legal Contract Review Attacks
Authors: Mark Heimann, Katherine Taylor, Bruno Jacob
ID: 2611.08834
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08834

Abstract:
Specialized prompt injection guards are deployed in front of document analysis
systems, but no false positive rate has been published for any of them on legal
contracts. We measure eleven open-weights guards on two corpora of legal
contracts and find that existing guards struggle: document-level false positive
rates are as high as 92.39%; two guards fall below chance on paired AUC; and a
five-regex baseline is not Pareto-dominated by any guard in the panel.
Controlled probes locate model-specific as well as general failure modes.
Prompted LLMs resolve the tested failures, including a second threat category
(unauthorized scope claims) on which every guard scores zero. A cascaded LLM
solution, where the positives of a mid-tier model are verified by a frontier
model, is 6.5x cheaper than using exclusively a frontier model and approaches
its performance.
