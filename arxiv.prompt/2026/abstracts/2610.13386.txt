Title: MoPrO-SD: Modular Prompt Optimization for Stance Detection
Authors: Meysam Sabbaghan, Nikesh Gyawali, Arman Zareian Jahromi, Sebastian Loera-Lozano, Doina Caragea, Cornelia Caragea
ID: 2610.13386
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13386

Abstract:
Stance detection remains challenging for large language models (LLMs) due to
implicit opinions, sarcasm, and context-dependent expressions of stance.
Although automatic prompt optimization offers an alternative to fine-tuning
without updating model parameters, existing structured approaches are not
specifically designed around stance-label semantics and class-specific
ambiguities. We introduce MoPrO-SD, a stance-specific prompt optimization
framework that decomposes prompts into functional semantic modules and refines
them sequentially using LLM-based feedback. We study three refinement
strategies: instruction-only optimization (I), instruction with reasoning (I+R),
and instruction with per-example reasoning (I+R+E), using class-conditioned
errors for stance-label refinement. We also introduce VaccineStance, a new
human-annotated benchmark for vaccine stance detection. Experiments across four
datasets and five open-weight LLMs show that MoPrO-SD improves upon the human-
written Base prompt in many settings. On SemEval-2016, class-conditioned
feedback achieves higher mean Macro-F1 than pooled feedback for four of five
backbones. Both reasoning-based strategies, I+R and I+R+E, achieve strong
performance, with their relative effectiveness varying across models and
datasets, while remaining competitive with recent prompt optimization methods.
The MoPrO-SD code and VaccineStance dataset are available on GitHub.
