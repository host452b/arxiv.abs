Title: Can Jev Improve Demonstration Retrieval for In-Context Learning?
Authors: Rui Song, Yingji Li, Hao Xu
ID: 2611.00737
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00737

Abstract:
Demonstration selection plays a crucial role in the effectiveness of in-context
learning (ICL) for large language models (LLMs). Extensive research has explored
various retrieval strategies to identify informative demonstrations and improve
downstream performance. Recently, the emergence of Jev, a decision-oriented
language model capable of producing probabilistic judgments, offers a new
perspective on evaluating demonstration utility. This motivates us to
investigate a fundamental question: Can Jev improve demonstration retrieval for
in-context learning? In this paper, we demonstrate that Jev can effectively
enhance demonstration retrieval by directly assessing the utility of candidate
demonstrations for a given query. However, applying Jev to large-scale retrieval
introduces substantial computational overhead, as each candidate requires an
additional decision-making inference. To address this limitation, we propose a
simple yet effective knowledge distillation approach that transfers Jev's
utility estimation capability to a lightweight multilayer perceptron (MLP). By
learning to approximate Jev's probabilistic judgments, the distilled model
enables efficient demonstration ranking while maintaining competitive retrieval
performance. Our study highlights the potential of decision-oriented language
models for demonstration selection and provides an efficient framework for
integrating their decision-making capabilities into ICL.
