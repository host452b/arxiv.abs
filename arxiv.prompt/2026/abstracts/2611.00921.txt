Title: Solvable dynamical model of in-context learning
Authors: Ruoran Bai, Haiping Huang
ID: 2611.00921
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00921

Abstract:
Pretrained Transformer architectures display a surprising in-context learning
ability without additional training. How the learning process guides the network
to achieve this intriguing capability remains unsolved. In this study, we focus
on a single-layer single-head linear attention model, where an empirical risk
minimization is defined for learning. Given a finite number of training
examples, we determine the exact solution of network-parameter dynamics, which
is linked to the training error and generalization error, characterizing how
memorization and generalization emerge from learning and how these transitions
are related to curvatures of the empirical risk landscape. Furthermore, we
calculate a data-dependent free energy, from which the training and
generalization errors in the long time limit of learning dynamics can be well
predicted. Our work adds insights toward understanding the mechanism of in-
context learning especially from a learning dynamics perspective.
