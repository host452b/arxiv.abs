Title: Genesis: Visual Augmentation Primitives for Multimodal Chain-of-Thought Reasoning
Authors: Fan Yang, Lifeng Chen, Yipu Wang, Han Wang, Feilong Chen, Yidong Ma, Zhiyang Chen, Chaoyang Zhao, Lingxiang Wu, Hanqi Jiang et al.
ID: 2610.15565
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15565

Abstract:
Recent multimodal large language models achieve promising results on visual
reasoning tasks, approaching human-level performance on existing STEM
benchmarks. However, these capabilities degrade under complex real-world scenes.
We identify that chain-of-thought reasoning remains largely confined to the
linguistic space, rarely anchoring each step in visual evidence. The main cause
is the absence of such data during training. In this work, we introduce a
training data augmentation pipeline, \textbf{Visual Augmentation Primitives}
(VAP), which leverages state-of-the-art vision generative models to synthesize
diverse photorealistic training samples and corresponding reasoning text. VAP
introduces some basic operators and applies them to a seed image and gets a
variant. It also constructs coherent multimodal reasoning chains throughout the
generation process. Such training data force models to perceive visual content
and perform logical reasoning according to them at every step. We further
introduce multimodal chain-of-thought rubric-based reinforcement learning to
enhance this. It rewards not only text reasoning but also visual reasoning by
scoring intermediate reasoning steps for logical validity, completeness, and
consistency. Moreover, we construct the UNREAL benchmark to systematically
evaluate visual reasoning on hyperrealistic scenes across controllable
difficulty levels. Experiments show that a 4B model built on this framework
surpasses 8B baselines, with performance improving consistently as the model
scales to 32B, validating the scaling law for visual reasoning.
