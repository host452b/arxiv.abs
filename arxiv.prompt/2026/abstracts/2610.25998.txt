Title: Internalizing Chain-of-thought into Looped Transformers
Authors: Kehang Zhou, Pinyan Lu
ID: 2610.25998
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25998

Abstract:
Chain-of-thought (CoT) enables constant-bit-size transformers to perform
general-purpose computation through generated tokens (Li and Wang, 2025a),
whereas looped transformers carry out the same computation by recurrence in
hidden states (latent thought). We construct a family of constant-bit-size
looped transformers that interpolates between CoT and latent thought. For a
prescribed loop count $L=L(n)$ and a fixed integer $k\ge1$ satisfying
$s(n)^{1/k}=Ω(L)$, our transformer simulates a time-$t(n)$, space-$s(n)$ Turing
machine using a context window of $O(s(n)/L)$ and $O(t(n)s(n)^{1/k}/L)$
generated tokens, reducing both resources by a factor of $L$ relative to the
corresponding unlooped queue-machine simulation. The construction also holds
with softmax attention at constant bit-size, under the finite-precision kernel
specified in this paper.
