Title: Extracting System Prompt of LLM Services in Complex (Agentic) Settings: Hierarchical Optimization and Reliable Evaluation Metrics
Authors: Shuaidong Li, Xinshan Yang, Yufei Chen, Chenhao Lin, Chao Shen, Zhengyu Zhao
ID: 2611.04803
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04803

Abstract:
System prompt is a critical intellectual property of large language model (LLM)
services. To analyze its leakage, prompt extraction attacks have been
extensively studied. However, we validate that existing attacks substantially
degrade on recent LLM services, whose complexity has evolved in both the prompt
itself and system-level constraints. Specifically, (1) system prompts become
longer and more structured to contain pre-defined tool schemas, execution logic,
safety guardrails, etc., and (2) only the final post-processed outputs (e.g.,
agent's actions) but not the raw textual response can be observed. In this
paper, we address the above increased complexity by proposing Extraction with
Hierarchical Optimization (ExtraHerO), which (1) decomposes the prompt
extraction into a hierarchical reconstruction process with a pre-defined
structural prompt template, and (2) iteratively updates the adversarial query
with guidance of feedback-template differences to observe diverse outputs. In
particular, we monitor the information gain of attack optimization to early stop
or reset it for a good trade-off between effectiveness and efficiency. We also
find that existing evaluation metrics for prompt fidelity can be misleading
because they do not account for structural information. Therefore, we rely on
global textual similarity and prioritize structural keywords. Moreover, we
introduce a system-level metric, adversarial utility, to measure the help of
prompt extraction for real-world downstream attacks. Experimental results
demonstrate the consistent superiority of ExtraHerO, with average improvements
of 12.5\% (fidelity), 2.3\% (functionality), and 6.0\% (adversarial utility) on
chatbots, and 12.2\%, 34.3\%, and 30.5\% on agents.
