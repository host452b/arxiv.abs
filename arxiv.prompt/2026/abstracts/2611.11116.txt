Title: Query-Only Prompt-Agnostic Attacks on Visual In-Context Learning
Authors: Xiao Hu, Muhammad Ibrahim, Hao Wen, Pin-Yu Chen, Jianliang Wu, Antonio Bianchi
ID: 2611.11116
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11116

Abstract:
Visual In-Context Learning (VICL) enables promptable segmentation by
conditioning RGB predictions on visual demonstrations, making it attractive for
auto-labeling, but its robustness under query-only access and unknown deployment
prompts remains underexplored. We study query-only, prompt-agnostic targeted
attacks where the attacker observes only returned RGB outputs, without access to
model internals or deployment prompts. Treating prompt-conditioned RGB rendering
as a distinct attack surface, we introduce two appearance-space attacks: Color
Hijack (CH) drives foreground colors toward the background to suppress
visibility, while Task Hijack (TH) collapses instance-distinguishing colors
toward a shared color assignment. To transfer across unseen prompts, we propose
a Self-Prompt Surrogate (SPS) constructed from the clean query and its output.
In a paired, matched-budget SegGPT comparison, SPS outperforms the Random-Prompt
Surrogate (RPS), reducing CH instance foreground retention from 0.918 to 0.155
and TH palette-aware tolerance distance from 0.345 to 0.225. Across four VICL
models on COCO and HierText, CH suppresses foregrounds across semantic-,
instance-, and text-style outputs, while TH collapses instance colors under
held-out deployment prompts. After palette-based decoding, TH also reduces the
decoded instance count and mask retention while increasing instance merges.
Finally, at 30% corruption, CH-corrupted annotations from a publicly accessible
VICL-based auto-labeling pipeline reduce downstream YOLO-seg mAP50 from 0.761 to
0.281.
