Title: TAC-CoT: Tool-Empowered Implicit Chain-of-Thought via Interleaving Explicit Tokens
Authors: Juseon Do, Sangmin Bae, Jeesu Jung, Hwanjun Song
ID: 2611.01926
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01926

Abstract:
LLM-based agentic systems are increasingly combined with CoT reasoning to solve
tasks that require accurate reasoning, external knowledge, and multi-step
deliberation. Yet, explicit CoT incurs substantial generation overhead, while
implicit CoT, which replaces textual rationales with compact continuous tokens,
does not naturally support the explicit communication interface required for
agentic tool-use. We propose TAC-CoT, a tool-empowered adaptive implicit CoT
framework that replaces explicit reasoning traces with compact latent reasoning
segments while retaining textual tokens only for tool calls, observations, and
final answers. This interleaved design enables agentic tool-use without
verbalizing every intermediate reasoning step. Experiments on agentic math and
QA benchmarks show that TAC-CoT improves accuracy by up to 8.8 points while
reducing model forward passes by up to 6.8x. Furthermore, analyses show that
TAC-CoT improves tool-use quality in both single- and multi-tool settings.
