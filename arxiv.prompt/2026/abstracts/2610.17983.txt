Title: Breaking the Average: Overcoming Convergent Defaults in Robotic Manipulation via Diagnostic Chain-of-Thought and Structured Guidance
Authors: Mohammed Daba, Waleed Abdulrahman Saleh AlAkwa
ID: 2610.17983
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17983

Abstract:
Recent Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, such as $π_{0.5}$, have demonstrated
impressive generalization capabilities by scaling behavior cloning across
massive datasets. However, these models frequently suffer from distributional
convergence, a phenomenon where the policy regresses to "safe" but suboptimal
mean behaviors due to mode averaging in the training data. This often results in
generic grasp strategies, Convergent Defaults, that fail in constrained or long-
tail scenarios. In this work, we propose a novel framework to identify and
correct these behaviors without parameter updates. We introduce a three-stage
methodology: (1) Probing for Defaults, identifying task-specific mode collapse;
(2) Diagnostic Root Cause Analysis, utilizing the VLA's latent reasoning
capabilities to verbalize the logic behind suboptimal actions; and (3)
Structured Guidance Injection, where concrete, distinct alternatives are encoded
into reusable "Lens Prompts" that shift the sampling distribution. We validate
this approach using $π_{0.5}$ in ManiSkill3 simulation environments. We
hypothesize that our method outperforms vanilla $π_{0.5}$ and standard Chain-of-
Thought prompting by effectively breaking convergent defaults in complex
manipulation tasks.
