Title: Beyond Discrete Ancestry Groups: Distribution-Aware In-Context Learning for Cancer Outcome Prediction Across the Genetic Ancestry Continuum
Authors: Weizhi Li, Yan Cui
ID: 2611.10464
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10464

Abstract:
Machine learning models for omics-based prediction of cancer outcomes are
commonly built on aggregated data without distinguishing ancestry groups, or
under an ancestry-discrete design, which partitions samples into ancestry groups
and fits a separate model within each group. Recent evidence shows that this
approach is inherently limited: predictive accuracy declines with genetic
distance from the training samples, even within a single ancestry group, because
the data distribution shifts continuously rather than only between predefined
categories. In this paper, we propose distribution-aware in-context learning
(DA-ICL) for ancestry-continuum modeling. Our method extends conventional
ancestry-discrete modeling to ancestry-continuum modeling, capturing the
nonstationary omics-endpoint relationship across the genetic continuum while
enabling efficient modeling through an in-context learning foundation model. We
provide theoretical analysis demonstrating the superiority of the ancestry-
continuum over ancestry-discrete modeling, along with empirical results
supporting the proposed framework. Source code is available at
https://github.com/ai4pm/DA-ICL-Continuum.
