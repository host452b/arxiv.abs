Title: The Surprising Expressivity of a Single Neuron with Autoregressive Chain-of-Thought
Authors: Roey Magen, Nirmit Joshi, Gal Vardi
ID: 2610.17283
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17283

Abstract:
The success of autoregressive language models trained with Chain-of-
Thought~(CoT) supervision raises a fundamental question: How simple can a next-
token predictor be while still enabling the efficient simulation of general
computation through its autoregressively generated CoT? Surprisingly, we show
that a single neuron suffices. For a broad family of activations, including
threshold and ReLU, a single autoregressive neuron can simulate any feedforward
circuit with the same activation. Specifically, a size-$s$ circuit on $n$ inputs
can be simulated by a neuron of dimension $O(ns+s^2)$ using $O(s^2)$ generation
steps, with both bounds independent of circuit depth and tight under natural
assumptions. For threshold activations, this improves upon the simulation of
Joshi et al. (COLT 2025), whose bounds scale exponentially with circuit depth.
Consequently, autoregressive threshold neurons can simulate polynomial-time
Turing machines, while remaining efficiently learnable from CoT supervision. Our
main expressivity result relies on a novel connection to Sidon sets from
additive combinatorics. As an experimental illustration, we efficiently train a
single autoregressive ReLU neuron to mimic a neural network teacher with
nontrivial test accuracy on CIFAR-10.
