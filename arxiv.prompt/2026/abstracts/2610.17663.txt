Title: The Image Still Has a Say: Final-Answer Repair for Multimodal Chain-of-Thought
Authors: Yulan Pan, Xiaoyu Xu, Zhihong Shen
ID: 2610.17663
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17663

Abstract:
Although chain-of-thought prompting has improved multimodal reasoning, it is
still difficult to produce correct final answers, as the reasoning can drift
away from the image. Besides, the trace is already fixed when the answer is
generated, leaving no room for self-correction. Considering the image is still
available at the final-answer step, we think it is reasonable to fix the answer
there without rewriting the reasoning. As such, the problem becomes to correct
the final answer using the image under fixed reasoning. Unlike methods that
improve trace generation through training, resampling, or decoding-time
grounding, we repair the final-answer readout while keeping the reasoning trace
fixed. The motivation is based on our finding that a wrong option can become
prematurely committed in the reasoning while the correct option still has
stronger image support in middle-to-deep layers. Thus, we propose Text-to-Visual
Evidence Reallocation (TVER), a training-free method for final-answer repair.
Specifically, TVER selects answer-related text and question-matched image
patches, and moves bounded attention from text to patches. Furthermore, the
patches are chosen in a middle-to-deep layer window based on question relevance.
Our method improves answers across benchmarks and backbones, with gains in both
multiple-choice and open-answer evaluations.
