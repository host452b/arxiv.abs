Title: ICLHerd: Herding-Based Data Selection for LLMs with In-Context Learning as a Proxy
Authors: Yuqiao Wen, Lili Mou
ID: 2610.27142
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27142

Abstract:
Data selection plays a central role in modern large language model training, as
the quality of training data strongly influences downstream performance. A
common approach is to curate a development set that encodes the desired skills
(e.g., reasoning or multilinguality) and select data that are similar to them.
Existing methods compute similarity using cosine distance over either the
model's hidden states or gradients. However, we observe that this max-similarity
strategy induces a mode-seeking bias: the selected data clusters tightly around
the development samples. To address this, we propose ICLHerd, a data selection
framework that leverages the von Mises-Fisher kernel to balance mode-seeking
with mode-averaging. We further introduce in-context learning as a validation
proxy, enabling efficient hyperparameter search over the balance trade-off.
Results show that ICLHerd outperforms existing LLM data selection algorithms,
highlighting the importance of balancing mode-seeking and mode-averaging.
