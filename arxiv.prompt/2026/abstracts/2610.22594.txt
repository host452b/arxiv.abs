Title: The Model Knows Better: Defending Medical Multi-Agent Systems Against Prompt Injection
Authors: Fardin Ahsan Sakib, Ziwei Zhu, Ozlem Uzuner
ID: 2610.22594
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22594

Abstract:
Medical multi-agent systems, in which LLM agents discuss a case, are
increasingly studied for clinical decision support. Prompt injection, text that
an attacker adds to a model's input, can manipulate LLMs into wrong decisions.
How it affects these systems, and how to protect them, remains unclear. Across
two systems, two model families and three benchmarks, a single injected passage
succeeds in up to 61% of cases, and collaboration does not consistently protect
against it. Existing defenses help only partly: they miss injections that look
like ordinary clinical text. Yet when asked to check the injected text against
the patient's findings, a model that followed the injection usually rejects it.
We therefore propose a training-free defense that verifies the clinical
judgments behind the system's decision. Following clinical practice, it
separates the patient's findings from clinical judgments, such as diagnoses,
recommendations and instructions, and checks each judgment against the findings
alone, so the decision rests only on supported judgments. Our defense
outperforms all baselines on every benchmark, lowering attack success on MedQA
to 4-7% while maintaining accuracy on cases without an attack, and remains
effective with a medical model and against defense-aware and adaptive attacks.
