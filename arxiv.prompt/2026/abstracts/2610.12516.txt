Title: TraceICL: Thread-Resolved Demonstration Curation with Consistency Calibration for Robust Multimodal In-Context Learning
Authors: Yigit Yilmaz, Elena Petrova, Mehmet Kaya, Lucia Rossi, Amir Rahman
ID: 2610.12516
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12516

Abstract:
Interleaved multimodal in-context learning (ICL) lets frozen large vision-
language models (LVLMs) acquire new tasks from a handful of image-text-answer
demonstrations, yet it remains fragile: demonstrations bind to the wrong images,
redundant exemplars crowd out informative ones, and noisy answers are copied
verbatim into predictions. We present AVA, a training-free framework that
organizes the demonstration pipeline of multimodal ICL into three composable
stages. A Thread-Resolved Decomposer (TRD) segments the interleaved context into
per-sample threads and re-stitches them with explicit boundaries, restoring
image-text binding. An Influence-scored Demonstration Selector (IDS) estimates
each candidate's marginal influence with one extra forward pass, removes
redundancy, and orders the survivors by descending value. A Weak-to-Strong
Consistency Calibration (WSC) module scores each demonstration by a weak sibling
model's reconstruction confidence and softly reweights its contribution during
decoding. On five vision-language benchmarks with interleaved 4-shot prompts,
AVA improves the strongest same-setting baseline by +1.7 points on average, with
gains of up to +2.4 points on VizWiz, shrinks the require-visual performance gap
on ScienceQA-IMG, and retains most of its gain under 30% noisy demonstrations,
at a 26% latency overhead that an offline cache amortizes. No gradients are ever
computed; the LVLM stays frozen.
