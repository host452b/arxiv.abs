Title: Mind the Intent Gap: A Methodology for Collaborative Agent-Driven Software Engineering
Authors: Nicolas Tacheny, Tom Mens, Saïd Mahmoudi
ID: 2610.37340
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37340

Abstract:
AI agents have entered software teams, first as tools and now as team members.
They produce in minutes what used to take days, yet the expected gain in
delivery often fails to appear. We argue that they are entering processes
designed for people, which quietly relied on people to cover three of their
weaknesses. The team's intent was never fully written down, because colleagues
filled the gaps. An AI agent fills them too, though by guessing, and drifts from
what the team had in mind. The reasoning behind each deliverable was already
lost from one step to the next, and AI agents now inflate and deflate the
documents passed along. The reasoning people did alone was partly private and
now moves into AI tools that keep it private by nature. We present CAIA, for
Collaborative Agentic Intent Alignment, a methodology for software teams that
brings agents into the way they work. A knowledge base describes the product
that a project maintains. For each Change, a micro-team aligns on its intent
before agents evolve the product and propose updates to the Knowledge Base. The
proposed CAIA methodology emerged from an industrial need and is already in use
at Ni2, with a platform built to support it. We present the methodology and
outline our plans to evaluate it further.
