Title: AIstatLab at BraTS-GoAT 2026 Task 3: Quality-Controlled Consensus Pseudo-Labeling and Dense Expert Adaptation
Authors: Heeseung Shin, Changwon Lim
ID: 2611.25477
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25477

Abstract:
BraTS-GoAT Task 3 evaluates brain-tumor segmentation across heterogeneous
entities using shared ET, TC, and WT regions. We present a 3D
ResidualEncoderUNet trained with quality-controlled consensus pseudo-labels, a
training-only nested-region objective, and a four-expert bottleneck adapter.
Five-fold ResEnc-M/L teachers gener ated predictions for 1,138 unlabeled cases;
fusion, hierarchy enforcement, and confidence-and-agreement screening retained
983 for training with 1,351 annotated cases. The final five-fold ResEnc-M entry
achieved mean DSC 0.834, mean NSD 0.510, and Acalc = 0.6720 on the validation
server. Component effects were modest and region-dependent, with the clearest
gains on ET. Source code is available at https://github.com/HeeSeung-
Shin/Brats2026-Goat-HS.
