Title: Ontology of Software Maintenance
Authors: Luis Olsina, Pablo Becker, Maria Fernanda Papa
ID: 2611.25196
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25196

Abstract:
Software maintenance is often a long and costly phase of the software lifecycle,
encompassing activities such as defect correction, adaptation, code improvement,
and functionality enhancement. Conceptual inconsistencies and terminological
variation continue to hinder effective communication among researchers and
practitioners. To address this gap, this paper presents the Software Maintenance
Top-Domain Ontology (SwMaintTDO), a high-domain ontology developed within a
five-tier ontological architecture. This ontology was constructed using
competency questions, ontological analysis of documented maintenance processes,
artifacts, and resources, and alignment with established software engineering
standards. SwMaintTDO provides a unified and extensible conceptual model for
representing key maintenance processes, roles, resources, artifacts, goal
purposes, and the transition of target modifiable and context entities from
current to updated maintenance situations. The applicability and value of
SwMaintTDO are demonstrated through its application -via its concepts and
relationships- to the process specification of a corrective maintenance
strategy.
