Title: Automatic Assistance to Reduce Information Overload during Software Issue Report Understanding
Authors: Salma Begum Tamanna, Gias Uddin, Junaed Younus Khan, Lan Xia, Longyu Zhang
ID: 2611.07109
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07109

Abstract:
Modern issue tracking systems are used to log an issue and track it from its
inception to closure. Issue reports with diverse discussions from stakeholders
can be large and verbose, making them challenging to analyze quickly. Our
developer interviews revealed that they struggled with the diverse information
in issue reports and demanded automated techniques to classify and summarize
information. We designed a tool, iSum (issue summarizer), that helps with the
automatic classification and summarization of information types discussed in
issue reports. The summarizer offers two views: 1. Developer View takes an issue
report as input and outputs visualized summaries of information in the report.
2. Manager View takes a suite of all issue reports under a given software
project as inputs and shows the prevalence and trends of different information
types across the reports. We evaluated iSum through three studies. 1. In an
empirical study over two benchmarks, iSum offers, on average, 11-13% higher
F1-scores than state-of-the-art baselines to detect information types. 2. We
conducted a user study in task-based setup with 30 developers to determine
iSum's effectiveness in issue exploration scenarios. Results showed increased
productivity and accuracy with iSum compared to manual inspection. 3. We
assessed iSum's feasibility for development and management tasks. It proved
useful to both developers and project managers for issue comprehension and trend
monitoring.
