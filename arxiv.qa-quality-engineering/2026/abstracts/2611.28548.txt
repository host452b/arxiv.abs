Title: X-AQA: Unifying Action Quality Assessment with Multimodal Large Language Models and Multi-Domain Learning
Authors: Kaili Zheng, Wei Ding, Yuchen Ding, Kaiwen Wang, Xun Zhu, Chenyi Guo, Yudong Zhang, Ji Wu
ID: 2611.28548
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28548

Abstract:
Action Quality Assessment (AQA) aims to evaluate the quality of human actions,
yet existing methods are predominantly developed and evaluated on individual
datasets with different task formulations and assessment criteria. We introduce
X-AQA, the first unified training and evaluation framework for general-purpose
AQA with multimodal large language models (MLLMs). We construct X-AQA-40K, a
training corpus comprising 40,163 samples from 22 data sources across four
domains: competitive sports, fitness exercises, rehabilitation, and general
skills. We convert heterogeneous annotations into a unified question-answering
format, incorporating dataset-specific annotation guidelines and official
scoring rules when available. Using this corpus, we fine-tune Qwen3.5-9B to
develop a single MLLM capable of handling diverse AQA tasks. We further
establish X-AQA-Bench, which evaluates both in-distribution (ID) performance on
datasets represented during training and out-of-distribution (OOD)
generalization on four held-out datasets. The resulting model outperforms all
evaluated open-source MLLMs on X-AQA-Bench and achieves competitive performance
relative to proprietary MLLMs. Ablation studies further demonstrate that multi-
domain joint training and explicitly providing assessment criteria in prompts
both improve OOD generalization. X-AQA establishes a unified foundation for
advancing general-purpose AQA across diverse real-world domains. Data and models
are released at
\hyperlink{https://huggingface.co/collections/Kelly0510/x-aqa}{here}.
