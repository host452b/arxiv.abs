Title: Generalization Testing of Grid Foundation Models for Power Flow and Optimal Power Flow
Authors: Ayushi Jolotia, Parikshit Pareek
ID: 2611.19194
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19194

Abstract:
There is growing interest in foundation models (FMs) for power grids,
particularly for power flow and optimal power flow. Their usefulness depends on
generalization accuracy, which existing works test on their own benchmarks. We
propose a three-axis framework to test their generalization limits: changed
admittance matrices of training grids, load levels, and unseen grids. We test
GENCO, LUMINA and GridSFM against the DC baseline, alongside their reported
limitations, and discuss theoretical reasons for their failures. Experiments
reveal that adding a line degrades all FMs more than removing one, and longer
lines eventually make LUMINA and fine-tuned GENCO worse than DC. All three
degrade beyond their training load range, where the loading-voltage curve
steepens. On unseen grids, LUMINA degrades sharply even inside its training
range, and GridSFM fails on the grid farthest from it. Thus, beyond training
conditions, FMs behave more like meta-models requiring test-time fine-tuning
data and compute.
