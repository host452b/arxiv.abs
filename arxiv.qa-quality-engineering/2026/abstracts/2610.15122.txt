Title: Targeting the Right Situations: Mutation Testing of Fuzzy-Powered Situation Hyperspace
Authors: Nawshin Mannan Proma
ID: 2610.15122
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15122

Abstract:
Ensuring the safety of autonomous Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) operating in
underground mines is challenging due to the vast and uncertain Operational
Design Domain (ODD). Traditional validation approaches, such as random testing,
often result in inefficient situation coverage, allocating computational
resources to low-risk scenarios while failing to expose critical edge cases.
Building on prior work in risk-prioritized Situation Coverage, this paper
investigates whether Fuzzy Logic-based risk estimation effectively correlates
with real system failures. To this end, we conduct a mutation testing study
within our SCALOFT simulation framework. A fixed set of software mutants is
systematically injected and evaluated under two testing strategies: (i)
uniformly random situation generation and (ii) Fuzzy-Guided risk-prioritized
situation generation. Experimental results show that the Fuzzy-Guided approach
achieves a higher mutation score and is more effective at exposing difficult-to-
detect faults compared to random testing.
