Title: Stress Testing a Multimodal Large Language Model Grading Assistant on Kinematics Graph Reading
Authors: Ons Zammel, Carlos Soares, Flávia Carvalhido, Gerd Kortemeyer
ID: 2611.22726
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22726

Abstract:
Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) can recognize graph features yet award
credit without sufficient graphical support. This exploratory study examines
selected grading outputs for synthetic responses to four kinematics exercises,
generated through visual distortion and progressive transformation toward
reference graphs. Different model and prompt configurations were used. Some
judgments correctly distinguished instantaneous zero velocity from rest over a
finite interval and recognized positive curvature. Yet a severely distorted
velocity--time graph received full credit despite disconnected and branching
traces and an unclear zero-velocity reference. Other submissions graded under
the same configuration lost substantial credit. Numerical cutoffs intended to
distinguish meaningful features from drawing irregularities were supplied
without a coordinate convention for unnumbered graphs, leaving the basis of
exact partial-credit awards unresolved. Stress testing can expose unsupported
awards before classroom use. For the configurations examined, we do not
recommend accepting scores as final grades without instructor review, including
review of full-credit responses.
