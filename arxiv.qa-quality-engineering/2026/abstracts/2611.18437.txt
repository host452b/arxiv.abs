Title: Accounting for Trust and Safety: Bounding Content-Moderation Quality from Records You Already Hold
Authors: Aayush Agarwal, Daron Veer Singh Sohi, Linda Cornelison
ID: 2611.18437
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18437

Abstract:
Platforms use expert audits to measure content-moderation quality. This paper
develops a formal method for checking those claims using records platforms
already maintain, such as appeal reversals and confirmed reports. These records
provide one-sided bounds: they can show that a stated quality target was missed,
but cannot prove that it was met. The paper presents the theory, limitations,
constructed examples, and a prospective protocol for future evaluation. It
reports no analysis of Meta operational data, no experiment, and no production
result.
