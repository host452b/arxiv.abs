Title: Efficient Robust Optimization via an Adaptive Mono-Level Gaussian Process Surrogate
Authors: Julius Schultz, Mustafa Essou, Ulrich Römer
ID: 2611.22467
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22467

Abstract:
Optimization under uncertainty seeks designs that perform robustly despite
varying operating conditions or uncertain model parameters. However, identifying
such designs typically requires solving an optimization problem with a nested
uncertainty analysis, resulting in a high computational burden. Surrogate models
can alleviate this burden and enable optimization under uncertainty even with
computationally expensive simulation models. This paper proposes a novel
surrogate-based approach that reduces the number of costly model evaluations
required for robust optimization. The approach adopts a mono-level strategy, in
which a single Gaussian process surrogate is constructed and adaptively refined
for both optimization and uncertainty analysis. Refinement in the design space
is guided by a sampling-based uncertainty indicator that propagates the
surrogate's epistemic uncertainty to the robustness measure. Efficient
uncertainty estimation is crucial for the scalability of the algorithm and
achieved through pathwise sampling combined with a weight-space representation
of the Gaussian process. The proposed approach is evaluated on several
analytical benchmark models and requires fewer model evaluations than a state-
of-the-art bi-level surrogate approach while achieving comparable solution
accuracy. Once trained, the mono-level surrogate can be reused to optimize
different robustness measures at reduced cost. Moreover, the approach is
successfully applied to a structural optimization problem with constraints and
design variable uncertainty.
