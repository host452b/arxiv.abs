Title: Process Mining-Enabled Federated Anomaly Detection for the Industrial IoT
Authors: Francesco Vitale, Salvatore Bramante, Francesco Flammini, Hangli Ge
ID: 2611.17869
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17869

Abstract:
While federated anomaly detection enables the integration of local data insights
across diverse nodes to reduce false positives, it inherently struggles with
non-IID data. In practice, industrial IoT plants often operate under varying
conditions and experience diverse environmental phenomena, resulting in
significant heterogeneity in the monitored time series. In such settings,
standard federated learning approaches fail to perform nuanced aggregation that
accounts for these cross-node differences, ultimately leading to increased false
positive rates. In this paper, we propose a process mining approach to evaluate
the similarity of local node data against a reference provided by a global node.
The global node utilizes these similarity metrics to dynamically weight local
models during aggregation, producing personalized updates distributed across the
network. We evaluate our approach using industrial IoT time-series data
collected from manufacturing plants operating under distinct conditions.
Experimental results demonstrate that our process mining-enabled federated
anomaly detection pipeline achieves superior performance, yielding a 46.52% and
11.99% relative improvement in F1-score compared to isolated local training
baselines and standard FedAvg, respectively, alongside a 51.24% relative
reduction in false positives over standard federated aggregation.
