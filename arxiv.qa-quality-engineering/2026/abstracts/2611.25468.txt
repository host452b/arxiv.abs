Title: Improved Constants in the Kautz-Singleton Construction for Probabilistic Group Testing
Authors: Trung-Khang Tran, Jonathan Scarlett
ID: 2611.25468
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25468

Abstract:
We consider the probabilistic nonadaptive group testing problem, in which a
uniformly random defective set of size $d$ is to be identified among $N$ items
using a binary test matrix. For the explicit Kautz--Singleton construction, it
is known that order-optimal scaling $T=Θ(d\log N)$ for high-probability recovery
can be achieved in the regime $d=Ω(\log^2 N)$. However, the leading constant in
this scaling remains uncharacterized even for standard low-complexity decoders.
In this work, we revisit the probabilistic analysis of this construction and
improve the leading constant for the COMP decoder, as well as establishing the
same bound for the DD decoder. Our approach relies on a sharper analysis of the
number of distinct roots associated with the defective items, yielding tighter
bounds on the decoding error probability. In particular, we show that both COMP
and DD achieve vanishing average error probability with $T\le (1/\ln
2+\varepsilon)d\log N$ tests for any $\varepsilon>0$, in the admissible regime
$N=q^k$ for some prime power $q = Θ(d)$ and integer $k \ge 2$. For COMP, this
leading constant matches that of the random near-constant column weight design,
whereas for DD, a gap remains except in the sparse regime $d = N^{o(1)}$.
