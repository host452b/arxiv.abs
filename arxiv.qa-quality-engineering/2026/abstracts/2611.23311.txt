Title: Post-Quantum Cryptography Education for Software Developers: A Multivocal Literature Review
Authors: R. D. N. Shakya, C. P. Wijesiriwardana, S. M. Vidanagamachchi, Nalin A. G. Arachchilage
ID: 2611.23311
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23311

Abstract:
Quantum computing threatens widely deployed public-key cryptography, making
post-quantum cryptography (PQC) adoption a software engineering challenge.
However, it remains unclear how well existing educational provision prepares
software developers for secure PQC implementation and migration. We conducted a
multivocal literature review of 8 research publications and 15 professional
training offerings, examining competency coverage, practical software
engineering tasks, pedagogical approaches, learner support, and evaluation. All
23 sources covered PQC foundations and algorithms, but coverage of practical
software-engineering tasks was uneven. Our analysis identifies six gaps
concerning developer focused evaluation, authentic software engineering tasks,
task competency alignment, pedagogical design and learner support, security-
focused assessment, and long term validation of learning outcomes and transfer.
We derive recommendations for strengthening PQC education and better preparing
software developers to implement and migrate cryptographic systems securely.
