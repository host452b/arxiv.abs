Title: GaborFlow: Hardware-Software Co-Optimization of Gabor Filter Accelerator Design
Authors: Priyanka Agarwal, Pruthvi Parate, Raghav Khurana, Madhav Rao
ID: 2610.15003
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15003

Abstract:
Brain tumor classification from MRI scans represents a critical challenge in
medical imaging, where accuracy directly impacts patient outcomes. This paper
introduces GaborFlow, a novel hardware-software co-optimization framework that
revolutionizes the design of the Gabor Filter accelerator for generating feature
maps to enhance brain tumor detection. Unlike conventional approaches that rely
on fixed filter parameters, our method employs trainable Gabor coefficients
within a carefully optimized range, integrated with an EfficientNet-B0 backbone
CNN architecture. The innovation lies in the synergistic combination of software
optimization through Bayesian hyperparameter tuning and hardware efficiency
through precision-optimized multipliers. Our GF baseline accelerator achieves
remarkable results, with 98.6% classification accuracy, 28.08% power benefits,
and 48.83% footprint savings compared to state-of-the-art implementations,
demonstrating that medical AI can be both highly accurate and energy-efficient.
The hardware design files are made freely available for easy adoption and
further usage by the researchers and designers.
