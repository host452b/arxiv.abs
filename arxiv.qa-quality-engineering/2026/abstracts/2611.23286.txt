Title: The Illusion of Relief: How GenAI Redistributes Cognitive Workload in Software Organizations
Authors: Chamari Dasanayake, Vladimir Mandić, Rahul Mohanani, Burak Turhan
ID: 2611.23286
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23286

Abstract:
This study explores how Generative AI (GenAI) reshapes software practitioners'
cognitive work across software development activities. We analyzed questionnaire
responses from 44 practitioners across nine organizations using descriptive
quantitative methods and qualitative analysis of relevant open-ended responses.
Practitioners reported productivity and time savings, but also effort associated
with providing context, prompting, reviewing, correcting, verifying, and
integrating AI-generated outputs. Human judgment and accountability remained
central, with practitioners anticipating a shift from direct code production to
guiding, evaluating, validating, integrating, and coordinating AI-supported
work. Thus, GenAI appears to redistribute cognitive work toward oversight rather
than simply eliminate it, although whether overall cognitive workload increases
or decreases remains undetermined.
