Title: Sub-100KB On-Device CNN Training via Dynamic Sparse Updates and Hardware--Software Co-Design
Authors: I-Hsuan Li, Tian-Sheuan Chang
ID: 2611.15236
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15236

Abstract:
The increasing demand for real-time processing and personalized deep learning
has made edge-device training essential but challenging due to high memory and
computational costs. To address this, we propose a Convolutional Neural Network
(CNN) training accelerator with a dynamic sparse update mechanism that
selectively updates important parameters using a random channel strategy,
avoiding costly evaluations. Combined with activation pruning, our approach
reduces activation storage to just 3.4\% of the original. Our hardware features
a fused forward dataflow that concurrently processes group normalization and
convolution, reducing training time by 45\% through computation reordering and
efficient zero-skipping. Implemented in TSMC 28nm at FP8 precision, the design
operates at 800 MHz, achieving 8 TOPS peak throughput, 31.13 TOPS/W energy
efficiency, and 14.8 TOPS/mm$^2$ area efficiency, with power consumption ranging
from 257 to 272 mW. The design enables on-device training within a sub-100KB on-
chip SRAM budget (95KB in silicon).
