Title: When Agents Play Differently: Localizing Human--Agent Behavioral Divergence with Conditional Distributional Testing
Authors: Oleg Smirnov, John Pertoft, Sara Karimi
ID: 2611.26012
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26012

Abstract:
Evaluating whether artificial agents behave like humans requires comparing not
just average outcomes but entire behavioral distributions across diverse
contexts. We propose a stratified framework that computes nonparametric
discrepancy statistics within contextual cells, and discovers subgroup rules
identifying where agent behavior departs from human play. We show that session-
level metrics dominated by win/loss outcomes offer little signal beyond mean
comparisons, whereas process-level metrics such as move efficiency reveal
genuine behavioral differences. Applied to a commercial match-3 game, it
uncovers patterns invisible to aggregate metrics: a playtesting bot is diverging
substantially from humans in certain game modes, while showing no detectable
divergence in others. We release our implementation as open source to support
reproducibility and adoption.
