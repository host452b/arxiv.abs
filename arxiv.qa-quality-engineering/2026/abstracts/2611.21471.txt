Title: Subprocess-Constrained Markov Decision Processes
Authors: Jiarui Gan, Debmalya Mandal
ID: 2611.21471
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21471

Abstract:
Constrained Markov decision processes (CMDPs) typically bound expected
cumulative cost from an initial state. Such a guarantee can be insufficient when
a policy must remain feasible after every history it may encounter. We introduce
\emph{subprocess-constrained MDPs} (S-CMDPs), which require the expected
discounted cost of each continuation, conditional on any reachable history, to
satisfy a bound determined by the current state. This stronger requirement
changes the structure and complexity of the problem: we show that stationary
policies can be strictly suboptimal, and finding even an approximately optimal
stationary policy is NP-hard. We adapt a recently introduced value-set iteration
framework to show that history-dependent policies surprisingly overcome both
obstacles. Our polynomial-time algorithm represents such a policy implicitly:
its expected reward is within $ε$ of the optimal feasible value, it violates
each subprocess cost bound by at most $δ$, and its action distribution can be
computed in polynomial time for any given history.
