Title: MQCODet: Modality Quality-Guided Collaborative Optimization for UAV-Based RGB-T Tiny Pedestrian Detection
Authors: Hui Li, Jianbo Ma, Hui Luo, Guanglin Qi, Zhixing Wang, Haorui Zuo, Jianlin Zhang
ID: 2611.28850
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28850

Abstract:
Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-based RGB-thermal (RGB-T) remote sensing provides
complementary visual and thermal information for pedestrian perception under
complex aerial observation conditions. However, detecting tiny pedestrians in
UAV-based RGB-T imagery remains challenging due to cross-modal spatial
heterogeneity, manifested as modality quality imbalance and spatial position
misalignment. Existing RGB-T detectors primarily address these challenges at the
feature fusion stage, without establishing an effective coupling between feature
fusion and object localization, potentially resulting in suboptimal detection
performance. To address this limitation, we introduce a Modality Quality-Guided
Collaborative Optimization Detector (MQCODet), which extends modality quality
from a fusion prior to a localization supervision cue. Specifically, we propose
a Dynamic Calibration and Region-Aware Fusion (DCRF) module that progressively
refines cross-modal spatial alignment and performs region-aware fusion to
generate reliable multi-scale features and a scene-level modality quality prior.
We further design an Efficient Contextual Interaction (ECI) encoder to enhance
multi-scale feature interaction while preserving fine-grained spatial details.
Moreover, we propose a Modality-Aware Adaptive (MAA) loss that incorporates the
modality prior and object scale to adaptively balance spatial consistency and
geometric similarity for localization supervision. Extensive experiments
demonstrate that MQCODet surpasses state-of-the-art methods on two UAV-based
RGB-T tiny pedestrian detection benchmarks while achieving real-time inference
on the edge platform.
