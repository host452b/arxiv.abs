Title: Deliberate Pacing for Human Oversight in Agentic Software Development
Authors: Tobias Reichel, Michèle Stassen, Anamaria Mojica-Hanke, Maria Teresa Baldassarre, Sebastian Baltes, Alexander Serebrenik, Steffen Herbold
ID: 2611.09550
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09550

Abstract:
Agentic AI increasingly enables software development activities to proceed
without continuous human involvement. While this autonomy may accelerate
development, it also raises questions about when developers can meaningfully
oversee software development done by agents. We propose deliberate pacing:
intentionally adjusting when and how autonomous development proceeds to preserve
opportunities for meaningful oversight. Drawing on an exploratory multivocal
literature review of 20 sources, we identify three factors motivating this
vision: changing relative costs of human involvement, the timing of oversight
opportunities, and interdependencies between pacing decisions across the
software development life cycle. We argue that neither uninterrupted autonomy
nor frequent human intervention is universal. Instead, pacing should balance
opportunities to understand and influence consequential decisions against costs
in human attention, development time, and rework. We outline a research agenda
to investigate when deliberate pacing is beneficial, how intervention timing
affects oversight, and how pacing decisions interact across development
activities.
