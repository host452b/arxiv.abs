Title: Complete Test Suites for Effectful Automata
Authors: Bálint Kocsis, Rick Koenders, Joshua Moerman, Jurriaan Rot
ID: 2611.26535
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26535

Abstract:
Complete test suites for automata are (finite) sets of words that enable testing
an implementation against a specification: when a (black-box) automaton passes
the test suite, completeness guarantees that it is equivalent to the
specification on all (infinitely many) words. A well-known method for
constructing complete test suites for deterministic automata is the W-method,
used in conformance testing and automata learning. Based on a recent categorical
generalisation of the W-method, we propose a framework for~the construction and
analysis of test suites for a certain class of effectful automata defined by
commutative monads. By working in the Eilenberg-Moore category of the associated
monad at hand, we obtain a generic notion of effectful W-method, together with a
completeness theorem. We show how to apply the framework and derive complete
test suites for non-deterministic and weighted automata.
