Title: Small but Mighty: Test Suites as Improvement Signals for Self-Evolving Agents
Authors: Yutong Cheng, Wei Cheng, Wenchao Yu, Dawei Zhou, Haifeng Chen
ID: 2610.15964
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15964

Abstract:
A self-evolving agent rewrites its own harness and must decide which rewrites to
keep, so its improvement signal is the check a rewrite has to pass. Verifying a
rewrite by agent rollout is expensive and subjective, while executable checks
such as the repository's own tests are too sparse to catch behavior-changing
rewrites, rejecting only a quarter of them. We instead take test feedback as the
executable signal, and search for test suites that are both small and strong
with a quality-diversity evolutionary algorithm, so that the signal is dense and
informative. TeSearch treats suite size as a behavior descriptor, so that
quality is pursued at every size and a small, strong suite is reachable, and
learns a transition kernel during the search that denoises the LLM as an
actuator on that descriptor. Across 129 real-world modules and three backbones,
TeSearch delivers the highest-quality suites of any method compared, LLM-driven
evolutionary search included, and the smallest of any LLM-based one. Used as the
improvement signal of a self-evolving agent, it rejects 59.9% of behavior-
changing rewrites against 25.3% for the repository's tests, at a 4.0% false-
alarm rate and 3.5 s per decision. The code and data are available at
https://github.com/ccsnow127/TeSearch.
