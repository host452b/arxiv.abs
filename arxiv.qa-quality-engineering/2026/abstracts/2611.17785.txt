Title: CHARMING: Parallel Edge AI Acceleration Across Tensor Processing and In-Memory Computation
Authors: Md Omar Faruque, Jingang Jin, João Paulo C. de Lima, Shouzhi Fang, William C. Tegge, Peipei Zhou, Qinru Qiu, Alex K. Jones
ID: 2611.17785
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17785

Abstract:
Edge AI increasingly relies on specialized accelerators for low-latency
inference under tight resource and energy constraints, yet their efficiency
remains limited by data movement. Prior work, CHARM, mitigates this overhead by
overlapping data movement with computation. We observe that in-memory computing
can further supplement tensor processing as a parallel accelerator while
eliminating certain data movements entirely. We present CHARMING, a
heterogeneous edge AI architecture that uses accuracy- and quantization-aware
partitioning to distribute and concurrently execute inference workloads across
tensor processing and in-memory computation. CHARMING partitions attention heads
between an AIE array and an in-DRAM compute unit, balancing execution according
to model architecture, sequence length, and precision while selecting heads to
minimize accuracy degradation. CHARMING also integrates quantization into the
AIE projection stage to further reduce data movement. We validate CHARMING end-
to-end in hardware on AMD Versal VCK5000 and Alveo U200 platforms and evaluate
LLM and video-generation models across multiple precisions using measured
hardware results and simulation of the latest in-memory compute hardware.
CHARMING improves prefill performance by up to 23.6 percent and decode
performance by up to 44 percent over AIE-only acceleration. With int4 x int4
fixed-point in-memory computation, CHARMING reduces per-layer energy by up to
7.6 percent and improves energy-delay product by up to 24.7 percent, while
maintaining accuracy similar to a 32-bit floating-point baseline.
