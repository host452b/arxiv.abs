Title: Testable Testing: Distribution-Robust Property Testing
Authors: Xi Chen, Renato Ferreira Pinto, Nathaniel Harms, Rocco A. Servedio
ID: 2611.27033
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27033

Abstract:
We introduce a new model for property testing of Boolean functions, which we
call distribution-robust testing. Distribution-robust testing is motivated by
the hope of designing testers whose output can be trusted even when the
underlying probability distribution does not satisfy the assumptions of the
tester, while still being more efficient than distribution-free testers that
make no assumptions at all. The purpose of this paper is to perform a broad
initial exploration of this new model. In particular, we present a variety of
general algorithmic techniques, which we apply to show that several well-studied
properties admit distribution-robust testers that are significantly more
efficient than their distribution-free counterparts. For example, we obtain
constant-query distribution-robust testers for conjunctions, decision lists, and
unions of intervals, all of which are known to require significantly more
queries in the distribution-free model. These encouraging results suggest a
range of questions for future work.
