Title: Testing Settlement Flags after Late Evidence and Process Restart
Authors: Zengjian Liu, Tommy He, ELE Zhao, HongLiang Sun
ID: 2611.26665
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26665

Abstract:
A settlement flag can survive an application restart while the economic effects
remain ambiguous. We define a contract linking the fixed plan, source admission
at two cutoffs, identity-keyed ledger effects and post-invocation state. We
generated 504 synthetic histories, ran each through a file implementation and a
separately coded SQLite implementation, and observed 672 controlled process
exits. The file implementation missed 54 same-identity payment-date conflicts,
including 27 that credited cash early, and a monitor using run flags, artifact
presence and purchase counts accepted all 54. The same contract distinguishes a
purchase awaiting closure from a correct quote wait for a single writer on a
running kernel and filesystem.
