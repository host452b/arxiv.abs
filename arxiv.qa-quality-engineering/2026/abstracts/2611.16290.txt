Title: Parallel Computing Systems for the Optimal Selection of Process Equipment
Authors: Andrey B. Borisenko
ID: 2611.16290
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16290

Abstract:
This monograph addresses the optimal selection of equipment for chemical-
engineering systems of multiproduct batch plants, a large-scale NP-hard discrete
optimization problem in which, for each processing stage, a unit of standard
size must be chosen so that the total capital cost of the equipment is minimized
while the system remains operable and produces the required products within a
given time horizon. We present the mathematical model of system operation and
analyze its computational properties. We then develop a sequential branch-and-
bound algorithm and its parallel versions for shared-memory systems (OpenMP),
distributed-memory clusters (MPI, master-worker), and graphics processing units
(CUDA), including recursive and iterative tree traversal, shared-memory
optimization, and branch-divergence reduction. To overcome the exponential
growth of the run time, we propose a hybrid metaheuristic in which Ant Colony
Optimization finds a feasible initial solution and Simulated Annealing improves
it, and we implement it on GPUs with thousands of independent threads. Finally,
we propose random, constant, and multi-constant approaches to tuning the
parameters of the ant colony algorithm based on statistical analysis of many
parallel runs, and we study the program on GPUs of three generations and on
nodes with several GPUs. Computational experiments on multi-core processors,
clusters, and GPUs show that the parallel hybrid algorithm finds solutions
within 0.01% of the branch-and-bound optimum in tens of seconds instead of tens
of hours.
