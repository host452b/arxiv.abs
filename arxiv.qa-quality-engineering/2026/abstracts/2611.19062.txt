Title: Log-optimal two-sample testing by betting
Authors: Aaditya Ramdas
ID: 2611.19062
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19062

Abstract:
For each standard Borel observation space, we construct a single anytime-valid
two-sample procedure that is asymptotically log-optimal for every pair of
unknown probability laws and every limiting ratio of diverging sample sizes,
including zero and infinity. For independent balanced pairs, the optimal log-
wealth rate is $2\JS(P,Q)$, where $\JS$ is the Jensen--Shannon divergence.
Unequal sizes yield weighted Jensen--Shannon information and the one-sample
limit $\log W(n_1,n_2)/n_2\to\KL(Q\|P)$ when $n_1/n_2\to\infty$. Refining finite
partitions and logarithmic-loss learning with measurable characteristic kernels
provide universal constructions. Our main contribution is the generality of our
construction, and its connections to classical batch efficiency notions.
Practical implementations allow continuous classifier fine-tuning, as
demonstrated on finite alphabets, Gaussian data, and handwritten digits.
