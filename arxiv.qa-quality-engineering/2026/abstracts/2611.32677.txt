Title: Testing Expansion without Connectivity via Return Probabilities
Authors: Nikolas Melissaris
ID: 2611.32677
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32677

Abstract:
We study whether every connected component of a bounded-degree graph is an edge
expander, given access to its neighbor lists. Dinur and Goldreich give a tester
using $\mathrm{poly}(1/\varepsilon)n^α$ queries for every fixed $α>2/3$. We
obtain \[ \widetilde O_{d,c,β}\!\left(\frac{n^{1/2+β}}{\varepsilon}+\frac{\sqrt
n}{\varepsilon^4}\right) \] queries for every fixed $0<β\le1$. The tester
accepts graphs whose components have edge expansion at least $c$ and rejects
graphs $\varepsilon$-far from having edge expansion $Ω(βc^2/d)$ in every
component, each with probability at least $2/3$. No structural promise is
imposed on the input. The tester compares pairwise collision probabilities at
two walk lengths and starts further walks at vertices where sampled endpoints
coincide. These additional samples control the contribution of vertices with
larger return probabilities. We show that the resulting conditions force the
longer walk to be nearly uniform on an induced expander with few edges leaving
it. We also prove an $Ω(\sqrt{n/\log n})$ lower bound for Cayley graphs at
infinitely many input sizes.
