Title: OpenDraco: A Platform for Engineering and Experimenting with Configurable Multi-Agent Architectures for Software Engineering
Authors: Xiang Feng Ye Pan, Vincenzo De Martino, Matias Martinez
ID: 2610.32686
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32686

Abstract:
Experimenting with LLM-based multi-agent systems (MAS) involves an iterative
cycle of modeling configurations, executing and evaluating them, comparing
results, and refining system designs. Existing approaches provide limited end-
to-end support for this experimentation lifecycle. We present OpenDraco, an
open-source platform that integrates these activities into a single workflow.
OpenDraco represents agents, prompts, tools, models, parameters, and
communication topology in exportable, versioned JSON specifications, translated
into executable LangGraph workflows supporting different interaction structures.
Users can modify and re-evaluate configurations using local or cloud-based LLMs.
We instantiate OpenDraco for automated program repair (APR) and evaluate a
sequential baseline and seven reconstructed configurations on synthetic and
real-world SWE-bench Lite issues. The results reveal substantial cost
differences among configurations with similar repair outcomes, while the
baseline resolves 12 of 77 SWE-bench issues using open-source LLMs. Additional
proof-of-concept tasks illustrate applicability beyond APR. OpenDraco supports
iterative experimentation with heterogeneous MAS configurations, enabling
researchers to examine their outcomes and costs within a common environment.
