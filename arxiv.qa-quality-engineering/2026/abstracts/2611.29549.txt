Title: CompareDR: Benchmarking Deep Research Reports from Individual Quality to Comparative Merit
Authors: Wanli Wu, He Zhu, Yue Xu, Haolin Ren, Yan Chen, XunLiang Cai, Fan Yang, LingChuan Liu, Shizhu He
ID: 2611.29549
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29549

Abstract:
Deep research systems can produce reports with different evidence, analyses, and
conclusions for the same query. A reader deciding which findings to rely on
needs to know how well each report meets the task requirements and which
differences matter to the answer. A score for a single report can summarize its
quality, but it does not identify which contributions make one report more
useful than another. We introduce CompareDR, a benchmark that combines task-
specific pointwise audits with guideline-based pairwise comparisons. We use
passage-linked report representations to locate relevant differences and
selective evidence verification to check claims that could affect the judgment.
The benchmark comprises 40 Chinese and English tasks from three existing
benchmarks, 360 reports from nine systems, 1,099 human-reviewed evaluation
criteria, and human preferences elicited through blinded comparisons of 80
report pairs. The hybrid evaluator matches 69 of these 80 preferences (86.25%,
Cohen's $κ=0.746$). The benchmark supports both comparisons of research systems
and examination of the evidence and analyses behind those judgments.
