Title: RAIN: Agentic Proof Generation for Rocq-based Software Verification
Authors: Jinyi Zhou, Zenan Li, Yuan Yao, Jun Ma, Taolue Chen, Xiaoxing Ma
ID: 2610.30759
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30759

Abstract:
Interactive theorem provers can provide strong correctness guarantees for
software, but constructing the proofs remains a challenging and labor-intensive
task. Inspired by the recent progress of modern coding agents, we present RAIN,
a lightweight proof-specialized agent for Rocq proof generation. RAIN augments
an LLM with proof engineering capabilities, including repository exploration,
iterative refinement, and checker feedback. This design allows the agent to
retrieve lemmas autonomously, revise complete proof scripts instead of isolated
tactics, and iteratively refine its proof strategy using enriched proof-state
information. Evaluated on a subset from CoqStoq, RAIN shows substantial
improvements over the existing baselines, achieving over 90% prove success rate
with at least 24.7% improvements. We also compare with general-purpose coding
agents, the results show that RAIN maintains comparable prove success rate but
with significant lower cost.
