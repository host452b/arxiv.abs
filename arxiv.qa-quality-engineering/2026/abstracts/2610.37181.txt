Title: LitUbik: open-source software for human-led systematic reviews with traceable AI assistance
Authors: Adolphe J. Bequet, Sebastien Laurent, Baptiste Bouvier
ID: 2610.37181
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37181

Abstract:
LitUbik is an open-source, self-hosted tool for conducting systematic reviews,
from the registered protocol to the PRISMA 2020 diagram. LitUbik covers
calibrated search equations, automated searching, deduplication, blinded multi-
reviewer screening, full-text retrieval, snowballing, appraisal, extraction and
reporting, on one server or across disconnected instances. At each stage,
optional AI assistance from commercial or local language models offers proposals
that a named reviewer accepts or rejects. Every AI call is logged, so the AI-use
disclosure is written from a record. A systematic review of 31 papers on review
tools, conducted in LitUbik, compares it with existing tools and illustrates its
use. LitUbik is distributed under AGPLv3.
