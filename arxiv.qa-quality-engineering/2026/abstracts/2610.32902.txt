Title: EVSEFuzz : A Tool for Automated Testing of Protocol Implementations
Authors: Soham Gumaste, Michael Khan, Roland Varriale, Rigel Gjomemo
ID: 2610.32902
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32902

Abstract:
The rapid adoption and development of electric vehicle (EV) networks has
required the expansive deployment of EV charging infrastructure (EVCI) which
relies on communication protocols such as the open charge point protocol (OCPP)
to coordinate data transfer between charging network operators and the EV supply
equipment. In this paper, we present EVSEFuzz, a tool for automatically building
fuzzers to test the security of protocol implementations. To achieve this we
make a novel use of Python datatype validation packages and tools to
``understand'' datatypes and message structures and generate both compliant and
non-compliant messages. To date, EVSEFuzz has been tested successfully with
several open and closed-source implementations and has found several
vulnerabilities leading to the publication of two CVEs.
