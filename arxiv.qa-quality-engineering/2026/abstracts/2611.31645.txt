Title: A Flexible and High-Performance Framework for Distributed Dynamic Graph Processing
Authors: Kaiyu Chen, Junhua Zhang, Dong Wen, Lantian Xu, Wentao Li, Long Yuan, Xubo Wang, Ying Zhang, Lu Qin
ID: 2611.31645
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31645

Abstract:
The proliferation of massive, constantly evolving graphs in domains such as
social and financial networks necessitates efficient and scalable dynamic graph
processing. Existing distributed systems, such as Ingress, attempt to
automatically adapt static algorithms to dynamic updates, but they face critical
limitations: overly conservative strategy selection, inability to leverage
algorithm-specific optimizations, and limited inference capabilities that often
force fallbacks to slow, generic methods. To overcome these limitations, we
propose a new paradigm that emphasizes user flexibility and performance over
unreliable automation. Following this paradigm, we present a high-performance,
user-friendly distributed system for dynamic graph processing. Built on the Bulk
Synchronous Parallel (BSP) model, it provides a flexible programming model based
on vertex subsets to control computation flow, along with a simple API offering
powerful abstractions. By enabling direct expression of dynamic logic and
incorporating system-level optimizations, our system achieves significant
performance gains. We demonstrate its expressiveness through six implemented
dynamic graph algorithms, and extensive experiments on eight real-world datasets
validate its superior performance and flexibility compared to state-of-the-art
solutions.
