Title: Rate-Quality-Energy Convex Hull for High Dynamic Range Adaptive Video Streaming
Authors: Amritha Premkumar, Christian Herglotz
ID: 2611.28817
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28817

Abstract:
High Dynamic Range (HDR) video introduces substantially higher decoding
complexity and energy consumption than Standard Dynamic Range (SDR), yet most
adaptive streaming pipelines still rely on bitrate ladders optimized using SDR-
oriented metrics such as VMAF. These ladders fail to capture HDR-specific
perceptual behavior and ignore decoder-side energy, resulting in energetically
inefficient operating points. This work proposes a rate-quality-energy convex-
hull optimization framework that jointly considers bitrate, HDR-aware perceptual
quality (HDRVMAF), and client-side processing energy, including decoding and
upscaling. Experiments on twelve 4K HDR sequences from the LIVE HDR Video
Quality Assessment Database evaluate 72 representations per clip and demonstrate
that traditional VMAF-optimized ladders frequently select high-resolution
encodes that become dominated once energy is accounted for. Compared to the
Default HLS ladder, our HDR-aware method achieves substantial bitrate savings of
up to 54.34% at equal HDRVMAF, while yielding relative client-side energy
reductions of up to 43.10% under higher energy weighting. These results
demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework as a practical
foundation for constructing HDR bitrate ladders that explicitly expose and
control perceptual quality-energy trade-offs.
