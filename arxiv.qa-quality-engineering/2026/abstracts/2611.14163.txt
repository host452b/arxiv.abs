Title: Nonadaptive One-Sided Testing of Binary Rank
Authors: Nader H. Bshouty
ID: 2611.14163
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14163

Abstract:
The binary rank of a binary matrix is the minimum number of disjoint all-one
rectangles that partition its one-entries. We study entry-query testing of
binary rank at most $d$, with one-sided error and nonadaptive queries. We give a
tester using $\tilde O(d^4/ε^2)$ queries, independent of the matrix dimensions
and a general lower bound of $\tilde Ω(d^{3/2}/ε)$.
