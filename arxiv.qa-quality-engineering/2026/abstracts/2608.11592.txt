Title: Video2Track: Steerable Adversarial Scenario Generation from Real-World Interaction Videos to Proving Grounds for Automated Driving System Testing
Authors: Mengjie Tian, Xinrui Zhang, Tianyu Li, Peizhi Zhang, Guirong Zhuo, Haojie Feng, Ziyi Yin, Qixiang Zhang, Lu Xiong
ID: 2608.11592
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11592

Abstract:
Physical proving-ground testing enables controlled and repeatable validation of
automated driving systems (ADSs). However, existing approaches often rely on
manually designed reference routes and scripted vehicle behaviors, limiting
deployment efficiency, real-world interaction reproduction, and interaction risk
steerability. To address these limitations, we propose Video2Track, a framework
that converts real-world driving videos into steerable adversarial scenarios for
proving-ground testing. Video2Track extracts interaction semantics using a
vision-language model, retrieves compatible maneuver pairs on the target road
network, and generates reference trajectories through interaction-guided
diffusion. Game-based online refinement adjusts background vehicle (BV)
trajectories based on real-time feedback from the vehicle under test (VUT) to
regulate interaction risk toward a prescribed anticipated post-encroachment time
(APET). Video2Track achieved an interaction consistency rate of 82.1\% and a
scenario generation success rate of 78.6\%. In proving-ground tests, the mean
minimum APET was within 0.5~s of each of the four target values. Video2Track
also achieved lower mean minimum APET values than the evaluated baselines in
both cut-in and unprotected left-turn scenarios. These results demonstrate the
feasibility of transferring real-world driving interactions to executable
proving-ground scenarios, enabling faithful interaction reproduction and
steerable adversarial testing on an Internet of Things (IoT)-enabled 5G cloud-
controlled platform.
