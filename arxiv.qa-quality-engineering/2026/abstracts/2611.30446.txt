Title: Pudding: A Retargetable Compiler for Processing-Using-DRAM Systems
Authors: Tianrui Zheng, João Paulo Cardoso de Lima, Alexander Kusnezoff, Asif Ali Khan, Jeronimo Castrillon
ID: 2611.30446
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30446

Abstract:
Processing-Using-DRAM (PUD) architectures exploit the physics of DRAM arrays to
perform Boolean operations directly in memory, eliminating the data movement
bottleneck in memory-intensive workloads. Several PUD architectures have been
recently proposed which differ in their primitive operation sets, row allocation
models, and execution metrics. These architectural differences are today hard-
coded in emerging PUD-aware compilers, making crossarchitecture reuse
impossible. We present a retargetable, end-to-end compilation framework for PUD
architectures. The central idea is to make the architecture description a first-
class object in the compilation pipeline: retargeting to a new PUD architecture
only requires supplying its description. Built upon the MLIR framework, our
compiler operates at two coupled levels. At the tensor level, it lowers high-
level programs through bit-slicing, mixed-precision assignment, and integer-
linear-programming (ILP) based placement to reduce the number of PUD commands
and inter-bank data movement. At the logic level, it employs equality saturation
to explore Boolean network implementations, with rewrite rules and cost
functions derived directly from the architecture description. Evaluated on
representative workloads across multiple PUD targets with the same compiler
passes, our framework matches (and in several cases surpasses) architecture-
specific hand-optimized compilers, while delivering retargetability and
extensibility.
