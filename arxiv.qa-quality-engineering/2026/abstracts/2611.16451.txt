Title: HIFC-IQA: Train-Free Cross-Domain Image Quality Assessment via Dual-Process Cognition
Authors: Yu Li, Zhengran Shen, Puchao Zhou, Yukang Ding, Yachun Mi, Boyuan Yang, Xi Zhai, Shaohui Liu
ID: 2611.16451
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16451

Abstract:
The gap between controlled synthetic distortions and complex real-world
degradations introduces a critical domain shift challenge for No-Reference Image
Quality Assessment. Although Unsupervised Domain Adaptation (UDA) approaches
attempt to address this issue, they remain constrained by substantial retraining
overhead and cumulative error propagation inherent in iterative pseudo-labeling.
Inspired by the dual-process theory of human cognition for remarkable
generalization ability, we propose Hierarchical Intuitive and Fuzzy Consensus
(HIFC), a completely train-free Cross-Domain IQA framework. HIFC employs
asymmetric perceptual decomposition to disentangle semantic content from
distortion-sensitive representations. The final quality score is fused through
the synergy of two complementary branches: a memory consensus branch that
retrieves a stable Fuzzy Quality Centroid from a synthetic reference gallery,
and an intuitive branch that captures aesthetic polarization via zero-shot
vision-language alignment. Extensive experiments show that HIFC achieves state-
of-the-art performance across multiple benchmarks, attaining the highest average
SRCC and PLCC among competing UDA methods. Most notably, in the challenging
synthetic-to-authentic cross-domain setting, HIFC achieves a substantial
performance margin over competing methods, demonstrating remarkable robustness
and generalization.
