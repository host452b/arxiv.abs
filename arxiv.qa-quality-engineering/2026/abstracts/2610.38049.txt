Title: Perspective on AI and quantum open-source software
Authors: Changhao Li, Bradley A. Chase, Veena Vijayakumar, Mathys Rennela, Alessandro Cosentino, Naixu Guo, Sebastian Hassinger, Farrokh Labib, Seun Omonije, Patrick Rebentrost et al.
ID: 2610.38049
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38049

Abstract:
Quantum open-source software provides essential infrastructure for quantum
science, and artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping its capabilities and
transforming its development. However, verifying quantum software at scale
remains difficult, and AI-generated contributions can increase the workload of
maintainers. In this Perspective, we examine verification as a central
requirement for reliable AI-assisted quantum software development and research.
We analyze public repositories, records covering 110 repositories from five
annual quantum open-source hackathons that we organize, and responses from more
than 1600 participants in a community survey. Our analysis connects limitations
in automated verification with the review burden on quantum software
maintainers. We propose verification practices and contribution standards that
require contributors to support their changes with reproducible evidence for
maintainer review. We also examine how quantum open-source software can provide
infrastructure for AI-driven research whose results the community can
independently verify and build upon. We maintain an open-source companion
repository to support reproducibility and help projects adopt and refine these
proposals. Together, these contributions help the quantum open-source software
community translate advances in AI into reliable quantum research through
reproducible verification.
