Title: Ephyra: A Property-Based Testing Framework for the LLM Era
Authors: Alperen Keles
ID: 2611.23282
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23282

Abstract:
Coding agents now write a growing share of software tests; however, property-
based testing (PBT) libraries are designed with historical considerations. We
present Ephyra, a PBT library in Rust built on three concepts: customizability,
observability and generator synthesis. Ephyra lets users compose their own
testing loops from generation, mutation and shrinking, and synthesize these
components to satisfy functional predicates. Applying Ephyra to 14 open source
systems, we have found 349 bugs and reported 300 of them, in database management
systems (SQLite, Turso, PostgreSQL, DuckDB, DataFusion), the verified policy
engine (Cedar), the SQL equivalence prover (QED), the URL parser used in Node.js
(ada-url), reimplementations of existing open source systems in Rust (coreutils,
brush, gitoxide), and compilers (binaryen, OCaml, and OxCaml).
