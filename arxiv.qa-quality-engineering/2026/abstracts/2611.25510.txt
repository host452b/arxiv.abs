Title: Life-IQA+: Boosting Blind Image Quality Assessment via GCN-enhanced Layer Interaction and Flexible Hierarchical Expert Specialization
Authors: Long Tang, Guoquan Zheng, Huiyu Duan, Jie Hao, Yucheng Zhu, Liang Yuan, Xiongkuo Min, Guangtao Zhai
ID: 2611.25510
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25510

Abstract:
Blind image quality assessment (BIQA) is essential for evaluating and optimizing
visual experience. Existing BIQA methods typically aggregate features from
multiple backbone stages without accounting for their unequal contributions to
quality prediction. Furthermore, uniformly supervising all experts may constrain
their flexibility and limit the diversity of the learned quality
representations. To address these issues, we present Life-IQA+, a quality
representation decoding framework that combines graph convolutional network
(GCN) enhanced layer interaction and flexible hierarchical expert
specialization. Specifically, the layer interaction module constructs quality
queries from GCN-refined deepest-stage features and uses penultimate-stage
features as keys and values, thereby enabling cross-layer representation
interaction via the cross-attention mechanism. Building on these
representations, we further introduce a flexible hierarchical mixture-of-experts
(MoE) architecture comprising a densely activated shared expert layer and a
sparsely activated routed expert layer. To enhance the representation diversity
and ability of experts, we apply auxiliary classification supervision to a
subset of shared experts using distortion-type labels for synthetic datasets and
discretized quality-level labels for authentic datasets. Experiments on
synthetic and authentic distortion benchmarks, together with controlled
ablations of expert configurations and supervision strategies, show that Life-
IQA+ achieves competitive prediction accuracy with a favorable balance between
accuracy and computational cost. The code is available at:
https://github.com/TANGLONG2/Life-IQA-PlusPlus.
