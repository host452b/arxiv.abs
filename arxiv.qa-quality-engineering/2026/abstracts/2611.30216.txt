Title: EdgeALM: An Efficient and Unified Edge Accelerator for Large Audio Language Models via Full-Stack Hardware-Software Co-Design and Agent-Driven Recursive Optimization
Authors: Chenglin Yang, Feifan Xu, Xingyuan Li, Xinpeng Xing, Dihu Chen
ID: 2611.30216
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30216

Abstract:
Edge deployment of audio language models (ALMs) is constrained by large model
footprints, heterogeneous workloads, and limited software support. This paper
presents EdgeALM, an efficient and unified FPGA accelerator for ALMs that
combines full-stack hardware-software co-design with agent-driven recursive
optimization. An end-to-end ALM compression flow is developed, combining a
unified AWQ-style quantizer with modality-conditioned vocabulary pruning. A
Conv-Matmul Disaggregation architecture is proposed, with dedicated matrix and
convolution engines that operate concurrently on their respective workloads. An
Extra-Coarse-Grained ISA is designed to incorporate regular on-chip data
movement and execution sequences into compute instructions, reducing software
complexity with modest hardware control overhead. Hardware and software
recursive loops are introduced for agent-driven full-stack optimization. The
proposed EdgeALM is implemented on a single Alveo U50 in an ordinary desktop PC
and demonstrates inference on more than 20 models, including ALMs with up to 13B
parameters within the 8-GB HBM capacity. It achieves high-throughput audio-to-
text inference at up to 169.5 tokens/s and sustains real-time streaming audio-
to-audio inference, with 94% bandwidth utilization and 26 W power consumption on
average. Standalone ASR, text-only LLM, and TTS models are also well supported,
and can be combined in cascades. Compared with state-of-the-art FPGA
accelerators (e.g., EdgeLLM), EdgeALM scales to larger models and supports a
wider range of inference workloads extended to the audio modality, while
achieving higher bandwidth utilization at lower board power and cost.
