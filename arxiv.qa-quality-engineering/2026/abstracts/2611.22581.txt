Title: FAST: Functional Adaptive Sensing and Testing for Quickest Change Detection
Authors: Peiyao Liu, Jie Guo, Zhijun Wang, Xueru Zhang, Nan Chen
ID: 2611.22581
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22581

Abstract:
Quickest change detection (QCD) is studied for partially observed functional
data streams. At each sampling epoch, a function is realized over a continuous
domain, but its values can be observed at only a fixed number of locations. At
an unknown change point, the functional mean undergoes a shift that is assumed
to admit a sparse representation in a localized basis. We propose Functional
Adaptive Sensing and Testing (FAST), which jointly estimates the unknown post-
change parameter, selects sensing locations, and performs sequential detection.
A minimax lower bound on the worst-case detection delay is derived under an
average-run-length constraint. A non-asymptotic estimation-error bound is
established for the policy-dependent observations induced by adaptive sensing.
Under the stated regularity conditions, FAST is first-order asymptotically
optimal when sensing subsets are optimized exactly. Simulations and a wildfire
case study demonstrate the performance of the proposed procedure.
