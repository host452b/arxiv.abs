Title: HandiVid: A High-Quality Dataset for Hand-Driven Interactive Video Generation
Authors: Chaoyang Zhang, Shaohao Rui, Xiaofeng Mao, Yuanxun Xu, Haoyuan Xia, Xiaohong Liu, Qinghe Du, Weijie Ma
ID: 2611.29693
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29693

Abstract:
Hand-driven egocentric interactive video generation aims to generate dynamic
scenes with realistic interaction responses conditioned on hand movements and
camera motion. However, existing egocentric video datasets struggle to provide
scene diversity, meaningful hand-object interactions, and reliable motion
annotations simultaneously. To address these limitations, we introduce HandiVid,
a high-quality dataset comprising 200K curated and annotated egocentric
interaction video clips for hand-driven interactive video generation. We develop
a unified data processing pipeline that integrates public datasets, online
videos and synthetic videos. Through video quality assessment, hand detection
filtering, and multi-stage interaction validation using vision-language models
(VLMs), we select high-quality video clips with meaningful hand-object
interactions. Since 3D hand reconstruction relies on initial hand detections, we
integrate SAM 3 into the hand detection pipeline to reduce missed detections and
improve the completeness of 3D hand annotations. Each video clip is provided
with high-quality hand renderings, camera trajectories, and interaction
descriptions. Comprehensive dataset analysis and downstream video generation
experiments demonstrate the effectiveness of HandiVid in terms of data
diversity, annotation quality, and controllable video generation.
