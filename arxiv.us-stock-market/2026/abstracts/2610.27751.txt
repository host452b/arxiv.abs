Title: Market Regime-Based Multi-Asset Portfolio Optimization Using Nested Clustered Optimization for Tactical Asset Allocation
Authors: Supriadi, Bambang Siswoyo
ID: 2610.27751
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.27751

Abstract:
We investigate the interaction between market regime detection and Nested
Clustered Optimization (NCO) for tactical asset allocation across heterogeneous
multi-asset portfolios. The central empirical question is whether conditioning
the NCO pipeline, encompassing covariance estimation, hierarchical clustering,
and weight allocation, on latent market states genuinely enhances risk-adjusted
performance, or conversely, whether estimation uncertainty within regime-
filtered sub-samples induces severe turnover and performance drag. We implement
a causal rolling Gaussian Hidden Markov Model (HMM) coupled with an adaptive
rebalancing mechanism that distinguishes regime shifts from portfolio weight
drift. The framework is evaluated on a monthly Indonesian multi-asset universe
comprising sovereign bond yields (SBN), the LQ45 equity index, spot gold
(XAU/IDR), foreign exchange (CNY/IDR), and Bitcoin (BTC/IDR) across a 56-month
out-of-sample period (January 2021 to August 2025). Rigorous walk-forward
backtesting reveals that standard regime-agnostic NCO achieves remarkable
capital preservation and risk-adjusted efficiency, delivering a 29.97%
cumulative return (5.78% annualized), a maximum drawdown of only 2.18%, a Calmar
ratio of 2.65, and low monthly turnover of 5.41%. In contrast, conditioning NCO
on an unconstrained HMM in a small multi-asset universe amplifies parameter
estimation noise and induces excessive rebalancing (47 events; 24.58% average
monthly turnover), which erodes net cumulative return to 1.60% and increases
drawdown to 9.99%. These findings demonstrate that without explicit transition
persistence or asymmetric downside objectives, regime-switching frameworks can
degrade rather than improve clustered portfolio stability, establishing crucial
empirical boundaries for machine learning asset allocation.
