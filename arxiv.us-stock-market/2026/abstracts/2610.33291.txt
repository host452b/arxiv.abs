Title: Classifying Brazilian Monetary Policy States with Interest Rate Gaps and Markov Models
Authors: Gabriel de Macedo Santos
ID: 2610.33291
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33291

Abstract:
This paper develops a monthly framework for classifying Brazilian monetary
policy that distinguishes the level of monetary restraint, changes in that
stance, and statistical regimes associated with Selic adjustments. The framework
combines a seven-state deterministic classifier, an empirical Markov chain, a
Gaussian hidden Markov model, and Markov-switching regressions. Project outputs
provide 145 classified observations from May 2014 to May 2026, with a partial
final month. Under the implemented nominal neutral rate convention, restrictive
states account for 88.3% of observations. A four-state HMM achieves a lower BIC,
whereas the three-state model records a lower temporal validation loss.
Switching regressions select three Selic adjustment regimes after solutions with
degenerate variances are excluded. An additional sensitivity exercise reduces
the restrictive share to 51.0% when the neutral benchmark is interpreted as
real, demonstrating how strongly the results depend on benchmark measurement.
The contribution is an auditable comparative application for monetary
monitoring. The findings describe historical patterns conditional on the data
and specifications; they do not establish predictive superiority or causal
identification.
