Title: Implied Volatility Expansions for VIX Options in Forward Variance Models
Authors: Ying Liao, Ankush Agarwal, Florian Bourgey
ID: 2604.25123
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.25123

Abstract:
We develop expansions for the implied volatility of VIX options within the class
of forward variance models based on weak-approximation techniques for VIX option
prices. In single-kernel models, such as the standard and rough Bergomi models,
the expansion is fully explicit, and we prove that its error is of the same
order as that of the underlying price approximation. In mixed-kernel models, we
propose two expansions. The first one is fully explicit and relies on a Hermite
expansion of the price, which we rewrite exactly as a Black-Scholes price with a
shifted volatility, up to explicit residual terms. The second one uses the
implied volatility of a mixed log-normal proxy, computed numerically, as its
zeroth-order term, and we prove that it achieves the same order of accuracy as
in the single-kernel case. The resulting formulas yield implied volatilities
directly, rather than by inverting approximate option prices.
