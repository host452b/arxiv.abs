Title: Sign Restrictions and Supply-demand Decompositions of Inflation
Authors: Matthew Read
ID: 2609.06907
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06907

Abstract:
Sign restrictions on the slopes of supply and demand curves are often used to
identify historical decompositions in structural vector autoregressions. I show
that the identifying power of these restrictions depends on both reduced-form
parameters and realised forecast errors. Consequently, unlike many other
structural objects, the strength of identification cannot be assessed from
reduced-form parameters alone. Empirically, identified sets for historical
decompositions of US inflation are typically largely uninformative, both in
aggregate and in most expenditure categories. Existing inflation decompositions
are therefore sensitive to auxiliary assumptions used to select among
observationally equivalent models.
