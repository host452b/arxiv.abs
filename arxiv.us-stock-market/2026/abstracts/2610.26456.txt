Title: Regime-Aware Interpretable Ranking for Sector Rotation
Authors: Kelvin J. L. Koa, Ziling Liu, Xinyang Li, Shuowei Feng, Ruoyu Liang, Mathieu Giroux, Eugene Y. Wen, Xuan Yao, Ke-Wei Huang, Hugo Lamarre
ID: 2610.26456
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26456

Abstract:
Sector rotation is a common decision in benchmark-relative portfolio management,
where portfolio managers rank sectors for overweight and underweight positions.
We formulate this task as ranking sector actions by expected future risk-
adjusted active performance, measured with forward sector-benchmark Sharpe
ratios. Such rankings should preserve temporal information, express model
outputs through economically familiar signal characteristics, account for
changes in signal relevance across macroeconomic environments, and capture
dependencies among sectors. We propose RegimeRank, an interpretable sector-
ranking model that represents fundamental and technical signals through expert-
defined temporal characteristics, conditions them on learned macro factors via
cross-attention, and models cross-sector dependencies through self-attention.
Expected-gradient attributions identify the contributions of individual signal-
transformation pairs, while macroeconomic and cross-sector attention maps
provide diagnostics of the learned conditioning and relational structure. We
evaluate RegimeRank on weekly S&P 500 data from 2002 to 2024 under combinatorial
purged cross-validation, where it outperforms representative ranking and
machine-learning baselines across multiple ranking metrics. Ablation studies
support the contributions of latent macro states, regime conditioning, and
cross-sector modelling, while attribution and attention analyses illustrate the
temporal characteristics, macro states, and sector relationships underlying its
rankings.
