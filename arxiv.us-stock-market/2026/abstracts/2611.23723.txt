Title: When Price Charts Rhyme: Historical Visual Retrieval for Stock Return Prediction
Authors: Hui Chen, Haoyu Liu, Ke Wu, Dexin Zhou
ID: 2611.23723
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23723

Abstract:
We study whether visually similar price histories predict similar future
returns, using a vision model to retrieve each stock's most similar charts from
three decades of U.S. price histories. A representation pretrained only on
natural images already retrieves neighbors whose realized returns predict the
cross-section of stock returns. Aligning the representation with subsequent
returns more than doubles the strategy's Sharpe ratio and outperforms a
convolutional neural network trained directly on chart images. The
predictability is concentrated among stocks with greater limits to arbitrage.
Returns and order imbalances around portfolio formation indicate that the signal
identifies temporary selling pressure that partially reverts. A sparse
autoencoder traces the return-aligned signal to recurring visual states that
determine which historical charts are retrieved. Our approach traces each
forecast to dated historical charts, making it more transparent than direct
image-to-return models and linking rule-based technical analysis with machine-
learning chart reading.
