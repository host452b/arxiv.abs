Title: A Rough Instantaneous Squared VIX Model for Term Structure and Option Pricing
Authors: Tianfeng Yang, Wen Su, Blanka Horvath
ID: 2611.07120
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07120

Abstract:
The VIX term structure provides forward-looking measures of market uncertainty
across horizons, while VIX options allow investors to hedge volatility risk.
This paper proposes a rough instantaneous squared VIX (RISVIX) model by
introducing fractional memory into the conventional Markovian framework. We
derive an explicit representation of the VIX term structure governed by Mittag--
Leffler decay and a semi-closed-form pricing formula for VIX options based on an
affine Fourier--Laplace transform. These results support a two-step calibration
procedure that estimates memory, mean reversion, and date-specific initial
states from VIX term-structure indices before calibrating the remaining
distributional parameters to VIX option prices. Using daily VIX term-structure
indices and VIX option quotes from January 2018 to June 2025, we find that
RISVIX improves upon the Markovian benchmark in fitting the VIX term structure,
with larger gains during elevated-volatility periods and for its slope and
curvature. RISVIX also improves out-of-sample option pricing, reducing MSE and
MAE by 13.06\% and 6.33\%, respectively, with larger gains at shorter maturities
and in high-VIX states. These findings suggest that the gains from fractional
memory are particularly pronounced when volatility is elevated and can be
obtained within a tractable direct model of VIX derivatives. Comparisons with
additional benchmarks from the recent literature also show improvements to
varying degrees in both VIX term-structure fitting and option pricing.
