Title: PortBench: A Correlation-Aware, Full-Pipeline Benchmark for LLM-Driven Portfolio Management
Authors: Yuxuan Zhao, Sijia Chen, Ningxin Su
ID: 2605.27887
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27887

Abstract:
Large language models (LLMs) have shown strong performance across diverse
financial tasks, yet portfolio management (PM) remains poorly evaluated.
Existing benchmarks exhibit two gaps: they are often equity-only and ignore
cross-asset correlations; they provide limited insight into where errors arise
and how they affect subsequent decisions. We introduce PortBench, a benchmark
with market data spanning six asset classes from 2015 to 2025. PortBench
comprises a static QA dataset of 6,269 questions across seven task templates and
a dynamic five-stage portfolio evaluation. Dynamic evaluation uses a class-level
correlation score for allocation and CEPS to summarize reference-based stage
scores. We further evaluate under three stress windows and three risk profiles,
and support real-time evaluation on newly arriving market data. Evaluating ten
LLMs reveals a disconnect: high financial QA scores do not ensure consistent
portfolio outperformance. LLMs concentrate exposure in correlated assets, and
their portfolios achieve higher Sharpe than equal weighting in 54.2\% of 120
evaluations across four market periods. Our source code is available at
\href{https://github.com/AgenticFinLab/portbench}{this https URL}.
