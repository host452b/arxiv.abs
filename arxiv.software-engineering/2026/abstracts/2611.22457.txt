Title: From Ideal to Real: Integrating Language Models into Smart Home for Human-Centric IoT Safety and Security Fuzzing
Authors: Lin Li, Xinbo Ban, Chao Chen, Lei Pan, Ming Ding, Kok-Leong Ong, Jun Zhang
ID: 2611.22457
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22457

Abstract:
Integrating IoT devices into household systems offers enhanced control over
living environments. However, as these devices become pervasive in critical
sectors such as healthcare, transportation, and home automation, they expose
inter-rule vulnerabilities that may lead to privacy breaches, data theft, and
physical harm. Traditional fuzz testing methods, which often operate in
simulated environments assuming ideal human decisions, fail to account for the
complexities of human interactions that can influence device operations in smart
homes, potentially leading to unforeseen consequences. To reveal the impact of
real human decisions and discover unexpected inter-rule vulnerabilities, we
introduce an enhanced version of IoT Fuzz for smart homes by integrating large
language models to emulate human decision-making processes. Our novel approach
adds a layer of realism and unpredictability to the testing environment, more
accurately capturing the diverse and dynamic nature of human interactions in
smart home settings.
