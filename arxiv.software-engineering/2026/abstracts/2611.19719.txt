Title: Found, but Not Fixed : From Software Vulnerability Discovery to Fixes with Iterative LLM Pipelines
Authors: Youssef Gamal, Mohamad Fakih, Halima Bouzidi, Rahul Dharmaji, Gustavo Quiros Araya, Oluwatosin Ogundare, Mst Ayesha Siddika, Mohammad Abdullah Al Faruque
ID: 2611.19719
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19719

Abstract:
Software vulnerabilities persist as a critical threat despite the deployment of
sophisticated detection tools and AI assistants. While detection has advanced,
the subsequent code repair phase remains a primary bottleneck; manual
intervention is inherently slow, costly, and prone to introducing secondary
regressions and issues. In this paper, we investigate whether an iterative, LLM-
based pipeline can provide a robust solution for the automated repair of
vulnerable functions in real-world code. We propose a generalized framework that
leverages the reasoning capabilities of Large Language Models (LLMs) through a
multi-stage feedback loop. We benchmark this approach against state-of-the-art
models, including GPT-4o, GPT-5.1, and Gemini 3 Flash, demonstrating that such a
pipeline can achieve high-quality code repair. To support the broader security
community, we provide our evaluation framework, fine-tuned weights, and
comprehensive experimental dataset on our website:
https://sites.google.com/view/llm4cve
