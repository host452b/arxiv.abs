Title: h-NESDF: An Infinitely Differentiable Signed Distance Between Two Sets with Robotic Applications
Authors: Vinicius Mariano Gonçalves, Pedro Antonio de Souza Campos Rodrigues
ID: 2611.03944
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03944

Abstract:
Computing distances between sets is important in many robotic applications, such
as motion planning and control. In many applications, derivatives of the
distance are needed as well. It is well known that the Euclidean distance is not
always differentiable, which create issues to algorithms that need a derivative.
In this paper, we introduce NESDF, the Normal Euclidean Signed Distance Function
between two general (with mild regularity assumptions but possibly non-convex)
sets. This metric is still not differentiable, but it allows us to introduce a
generalization, $h$-NESDF, which has a smoothing parameter $h$ such that the
metric is infinitely differentiable for all $h>0$ and that reduces to the NESDF
when $h$ goes to $0$. The metric is amenable to GPU parallelization, and thus
can be computed efficiently. We show extensive simulation results to evaluate
its viability in motion planning.
