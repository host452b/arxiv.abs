Title: EnvTrap: Revealing the Environment-Only Attack Surface in Embodied AI via Consequence-Blind Action Execution
Authors: Zehao Liu, Huashuo Lei, Xi Lin, Haoang Li, Yuliang Chen, Huarui Zhang, Yifan Wang
ID: 2611.30004
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30004

Abstract:
As AI models evolve from text and vision to physical agents, embodied AI faces a
fundamentally different attack surface. Prior attacks on embodied systems have
used crafted instructions, adversarial visual inputs, and training-time policy
modifications; we study environment-only object rearrangements with unchanged
models and instructions. We reveal an attack surface in safety-critical spatial
relations among existing objects. An adversary who rearranges objects, without
modifying instructions or accessing models, can cause hazardous consequences. In
this work, we propose EnvTrap, a diagnostic pipeline that constructs paired
safe, trap, and null-trap (benign but misleading) embodied scenarios. We
demonstrate that environment-only perturbations raise hazardous-action rates to
an average of 83.4% across the five evaluated action policies, with similar
vulnerability patterns confirmed in world models. On a consequence-prediction
task, model accuracy remains near chance, while human evaluators achieve high
accuracy. We further propose a consequence-aware defense that reduces trap
trigger rates by an average of 76.3 percentage points across action policies in
simulation and by 62.9 percentage points pooled across physical backends.
Together, these findings link environment-only attacks to a broader deficit in
physical reasoning: embodied models recognize task-relevant objects yet fail to
anticipate how spatial relations change the consequences of acting on them.
EnvTrap makes this gap directly testable and turns hazardous layouts into
training scenarios for safer embodied action. Our code and data are available at
https://github.com/INKSIR/Envtrap.
