Title: Reading between the levels: new tools for the Thorup-Zwick hierarchy and their applications
Authors: Shiri Chechik, Gur Lifshitz, Avi Kadria, Liam Roditty
ID: 2611.01297
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01297

Abstract:
In this paper, we develop three new analytical tools for the Thorup-Zwick
hierarchy: the Mixed-Level Growth, Two-Level Growth, and Long-Short Growth
lemmas. Using these tools, we obtain improved approximate distance oracles
(ADOs), and algorithms for $n$-pairs shortest paths ($n$-PSP), multiple-source
shortest paths (MSSP), and $ST$-shortest paths, as well as new hopsets. One of
our main results is a $(\frac 65 k+O(1))$-stretch ADO with $\tilde O(mn^{1/k})$
preprocessing time and space (where $\tilde O$ hides polylogarithmic factors),
and $\tilde O(n^{1/k})$ query time, breaking, for sparse graphs, the
$(2k-O(1))$-stretch barrier for ADOs with $\tilde O(n^{1+1/k})$ space and
$\tilde O(n^{1/k})$ query time. In unweighted graphs, we also obtain a $(\frac
65 k+O(1),O(k))$-approximate ADO with $\tilde O(n^{1+1/k})$ space and $\tilde
O(n^{1/k})$ query time. This also yields a $(\frac 65 k+O(1))$-approximation
algorithm for $m$-pairs shortest paths ($m$-PSP) in $\tilde O(mn^{1/k})$ time,
answering the question of Chechik, Hoch, and Lifshitz [SODA'25] of whether the
$2k-O(1)$ barrier for $n$-PSP in $\tilde O(mn^{1/k})$ time can be broken. For
unweighted graphs, we give a new analysis of the existing $\tilde
O(mn^{1/k}+n^{1+2/k})$-time $n$-PSP algorithm of [Chechik et al., SODA'25], and
show that, without any modification, it is a $(k,k-1)$-approximation, i.e.,
$d(u,v)\le\hat d(u,v)\le k\cdot d(u,v)+k-1$ for every queried pair. This
improves its multiplicative factor from $\frac 43 k+O(1)$ to $k$, and the
leading coefficient $k$ is tight for this algorithm. Moreover, for graphs with
$m=Ω(n^{1+1/k})$, this answers the question of Dalirrooyfard et al. [FOCS'22] up
to the additive $k-1$.
