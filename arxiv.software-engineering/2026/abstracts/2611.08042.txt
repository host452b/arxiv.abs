Title: Secure Formal Raising: Protecting the Development of AI through Executable Properties
Authors: Rolf Drechsler, Christina Plump
ID: 2611.08042
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08042

Abstract:
Model raising revisits Turing's developmental view of machine intelligence.
Formal Raising (FR) has recently been proposed to complement this view with
explicit behavioral properties. This paper extends that proposal to the security
of the raising environment. Secure Formal Raising (SFR) models policy integrity,
authorization, and confidential data flow using four-valued evidence that
preserves unknown and inconsistent assessments. We prove six security invariants
and show that they force admission to accept only confirmed evidence. A plain-C
reference implementation satisfies the invariants in a bounded exhaustive
exploration, which also exposes a confused-deputy gap for tool calls that a
provenance requirement closes.
