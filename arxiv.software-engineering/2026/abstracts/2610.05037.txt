Title: SparseCraft: Agentic Hardware-Software Co-Optimization for Sparse Computing
Authors: M P Samartha, Rajatabha Chakraborty, Vedant Pahariya, Priyesh Shukla
ID: 2610.05037
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.05037

Abstract:
Sparse-accelerator design spaces are usually searched against analytical models,
so a design is judged by a model's prediction rather than by a measurement of
the hardware. SparseCraft is a closed loop on the CHIA framework in which a
large language model (LLM) agent co-designs the configuration, memories and
sparse-kernel schedule of a Gemmini accelerator. A candidate counts only after
it passes a legality check, cycle-accurate simulation, a bit-for-bit comparison
of every output and synthesis. Post-synthesis timing analysis checks its clock,
and its energy is computed from measured counters, with per-event energies
validated against gate-level and SRAM-macro data. We compare the agent with
expert Gemmini configurations and with random, greedy and multi-objective TPE
search at the agent's budget of 15 evaluations, all measured by the same flow.
On a GraphChallenge sparse-DNN layer, the agent's designs reach 159-164$\times$
the inverse energy-delay-area product of stock Gemmini and 2.07-2.14$\times$
that of the best expert configuration. Their Pareto hypervolume is
0.5557-0.5704, against at most 0.2806 for search. On a power-law graph and a
pruned BERT layer, the agent raises perf/W by 11.45$\times$ and 4.43$\times$,
and held-out transfer shows that its designs specialise to their workload. The
loop is also resilient to faulty designs. When a design fails a check, a repair
agent diagnoses the failure from its evidence and fixes it automatically, or
declines when the only fix would undo the intended change. In a fault-injection
benchmark, it fixed every scorable fault at its first attempt.
