Title: Demo: Matter-Based IoMT Telemetry and Security Analysis
Authors: Muhammad Hassan, Masooda Bashir, Carl Gunter
ID: 2611.16275
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16275

Abstract:
Connected health devices increasingly support health monitoring outside clinical
settings, making secure and accessible physiological data important for patients
and caregivers. However, device-specific applications and interfaces complicate
the integration of readings from different devices. The Matter standard provides
standardized device commissioning and protected communication, but its cluster
library does not represent the health attribute measurement. We present a
preliminary demo study of a Matter-based IoMT testbed with a specific
HealthVitals cluster. Implemented in the connectedhomeip SDK on a
Raspberry~Pi~5, the cluster exposes simulated heart rate, blood oxygen
saturation, and body temperature. We examine commissioning, attribute retrieval,
communication under simulated packet loss, and operational traffic through a
reference controller. The study demonstrates that this custom telemetry model
can operate within the tested Matter configuration without modifying
commissioning or secure-session mechanisms. The resulting testbed gives
researchers and device developers a concrete platform for investigating health-
oriented extensions. It also supports exploration of local telemetry integration
that could help patients and caregivers access readings across connected
devices.
