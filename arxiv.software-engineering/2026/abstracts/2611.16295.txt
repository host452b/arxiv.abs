Title: Polynomial Swap Coboundary Expansion with Applications to LTCs and PCPs
Authors: Yotam Dikstein, Izhar Oppenheim, Inga Valentiner-Branth
ID: 2611.16295
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16295

Abstract:
Swap coboundary expansion, introduced independently by Bafna--Minzer and
Dikstein--Dinur, is a local-to-global property that underlies agreement tests in
the low-acceptance regime and recent constructions of succinct PCPs. Previous
works obtained bounds of $\exp(-O(\sqrt{r}))$ for the $r$-swap coboundary
expansion of Chapman--Lubotzky complexes. We establish the first inverse-
polynomial bounds for bounded-degree complexes: suitable skeleta of Kac--Moody--
Steinberg (KMS) complexes have $r$-swap coboundary expansion at least
$r^{-O(1)}$. Our theorem supplies a key ingredient in forthcoming work of Ben
Yaacov--Dikstein--Liu, which obtains local tests for sparse algebraic codes with
guarantees even at arbitrarily small acceptance probability. These tests use
$\exp(\operatorname{poly}(1/\varepsilon))$ queries and guarantee that any word
accepted with probability at least $\varepsilon$ agrees with a codeword on an
$\varepsilon^{O(1)}$ fraction of coordinates. Separately, substituting our bound
into the existing analysis of Bafna--Minzer--Vyas--Yun improves the soundness
dependence of their two-query PCP construction, giving perfect completeness,
soundness $\varepsilon$, and proof length $n(\log
n)^{\exp(\operatorname{poly}(1/\varepsilon))}$. Our proof exploits the product
structure of the groups arising in KMS complexes. The main new ingredient is a
decomposition lemma for Dehn functions, which separates the analysis of group
relations into independent problems on much smaller factors, with only
polynomial overhead.
