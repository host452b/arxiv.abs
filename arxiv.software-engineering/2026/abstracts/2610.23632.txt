Title: Empirical Insights from AI-Powered Software Engineering in Practice
Authors: Tina Mersch, Jack Johns, Steffen Klepke, Hanya Elhashemy, Artur Signell, Robin Gröpler, Klaus Schmid
ID: 2610.23632
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23632

Abstract:
Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) is becoming indispensable in future
software engineering. Its application spans a wide spectrum - from assisted
development to autonomous software engineering, requiring organizations to
address associated risks and challenges. This paper investigates these issues
through three case studies, highlighting practical problems encountered during
GenAI integration. Based on these findings, it provides targeted recommendations
for managing risks and ensuring reliable outcomes. This paper provides guidance
for effective and responsible GenAI adoption in software engineering based on
empirical insights.
