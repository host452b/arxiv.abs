Title: State-Augmented Learning for Post-Deployment Adaptation of Coexisting O-RAN Applications
Authors: Wonhyeok Ko, Yeonseo Jeong, Songnam Hong
ID: 2611.01376
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01376

Abstract:
Fixed control policies in Open RAN can require retraining when service
requirements, traffic loads, or interacting applications change. We propose
ATTUNE, a state-augmented learning method for an energy saving application that
coexists with a learned mobility load balancing application under a service
constraint. ATTUNE conditions the energy saving policy on a Lagrange multiplier
and updates this scalar during operation using measured service degradation,
while keeping both applications' network parameters fixed. To train a single
policy across service requirements, ATTUNE samples target service levels
uniformly and maps them to multipliers through an adaptive table. This procedure
accounts for policy-dependent multiplier responses and improves energy savings
compared with training over a widened fixed multiplier range. We characterize
the induced distribution of service levels and the convergence and fluctuations
of the online update. An exact finite-window identity establishes convergence of
average measured degradation to the target when the projection corrections
vanish on average. Simulations show that a single ATTUNE policy achieves energy
savings comparable to policies trained separately for each service requirement.
It adapts to changes in the target, load, and partner application without
retraining, increases energy savings at light load, and maintains target
tracking for separately trained application pairs. No statistically significant
increase in cell switching is detected in the evaluated comparisons.
