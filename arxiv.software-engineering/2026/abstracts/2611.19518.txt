Title: Equilibrium measures for anisotropic exponential interaction energies with applications to transformers
Authors: Oleksii Kachaiev, Martin Burger, Samira Kabri, Yury Korolev, Tim Roith
ID: 2611.19518
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19518

Abstract:
We study the minimization of anisotropic exponential interaction energies over
probability measures on the sphere, motivated by mean-field models of self-
attention in transformer architectures. For every symmetric positive definite
interaction matrix, we relate the minimizer to a corresponding anisotropic
Brownian motion and use the Feynman--Kac formula and elliptic regularity theory
to show that it admits a smooth, strictly positive density on the sphere. We
further show that it is the only stationary measure with full support for the
dynamics considered. We use the stochastic viewpoint to derive an algorithm for
sampling from the minimizer. In dimension two, we also propose a numerical
scheme for approximating its density based on a Mathieu series expansion.
