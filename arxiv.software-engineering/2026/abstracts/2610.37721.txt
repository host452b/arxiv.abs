Title: Local-to-Global Theorems Based on Cones with Applications to Graph Coloring
Authors: Anqi Li, Shayan Oveis Gharan
ID: 2610.37721
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37721

Abstract:
Local-to-Global theorems are a cornerstone of many of recent developments in
theoretical computer science from coding theory and PCPs all the way to
analyzing mixing time of Markov chains. Building on trickledown theorems on
simplicial complexes, several techniques have been established to obtain tighter
local-to-global theorems to analyze the spectral gap of the Glauber dynamics,
namely matrix trickledown, ${\mathcal C}$-Lorentzian polynomials and more
recently Bochner type inequalities. In this paper, we adopt non-local local-to-
global/Bochner type inequalities on "cones", corresponding to the local
neighborhood of a given vertex, to show that the Glauber dynamics mixes in
polynomial time to sample proper colorings of maximum degree $Δ$ graphs as long
as $q\geq 1.7408Δ$.
