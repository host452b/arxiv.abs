Title: Digital Security Under Compound Threats: Surveillance, Threat Reasoning, and Habituation Among North Korean Defectors in China
Authors: Yibo Meng, Bingyi Liu, Lionel Z. Wang, Hongkun Yang
ID: 2611.07576
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07576

Abstract:
North Korean defectors residing in China face persistent uncertainty regarding
digital security and privacy. Through semi-structured interviews with 19
defectors, we examine how individuals understand digital threats and adapt their
security behaviors. We identify three interrelated conditions: transnational
surveillance pressure from North Korea, identity-linked digital infrastructure
and intermediary dependency in China, and threat reasoning shaped by prior
surveillance schemas. Participants developed uneven protective practices and
followed two trajectories: adaptive habituation driven by knowledge acquisition
and erosive habituation driven by the prolonged absence of adverse events. Our
findings show how compound threats, attribution uncertainty, and constrained
agency shape security reasoning among undocumented users.
