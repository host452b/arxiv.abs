Title: OS-Wizard: Scaling Computer-Using Agents to Specialized Software via Knowledge-Guided Exploration
Authors: Zhoumianze Liu, Zichen Ding, Bowen Yang, Zehao Li, Zhenyu Wu, Yibo Zhao, Kanzhi Cheng, Yian Wang, Yanan Sun, Qiushi Sun et al.
ID: 2610.33907
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33907

Abstract:
Computer-using agents are increasingly capable of performing complex tasks in
the digital world. To advance such agents, supervised learning on annotated
interaction trajectories has become a common approach to improving their task-
solving capabilities. However, high-quality interaction trajectories remain
scarce for specialized desktop applications, where limited application
knowledge, complex GUI workflows, and incomplete exploration hinder automated
collection. To address these challenges, we introduce \aks{}, a knowledge-guided
automated trajectory collection framework. It combines tutorial-guided
exploration with a hybrid GUI--Code action space, providing application-specific
knowledge and simplifying GUI workflows through direct execution of application-
native commands. The framework also checkpoints and revisits pivotal states
during tree exploration to explore alternative workflows, complementing linear
rollouts for broader coverage. We collect 10.9K automatically validated
trajectories and use them to fine-tune open-source base models. The resulting
model achieves 64.1\% accuracy on a benchmark of specialized desktop workflows,
improving performance by 58.2 percentage points, establishing a new state of the
art. The fine-tuned model also improves performance on general desktop workflows
using GUI actions only, suggesting that the collected supervision benefits
general desktop tasks as well. Comparative experiments and ablations demonstrate
the efficiency of our collection framework and the value of code actions in
specialized workflows.
