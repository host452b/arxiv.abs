Title: Resource-Aware VLA-MPC Co-Location for Efficient Embodied AI
Authors: Zeyu Guo, Hao Lou, Yuhui Hao, Han Zhao, Zihan Liu, Jingwen Leng, Yiming Gan, Yu Feng, Minyi Guo
ID: 2611.30177
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30177

Abstract:
Recent advances in embodied artificial intelligence (EAI) have unleashed new
potential in many frontiers. Current EAI systems often combine vision-language-
action (VLA) inference with high frequency model predictive control (MPC). On
mobile GPUs, these workloads are typically serialized, causing control deadline
misses. We present Dubot, a framework that automatically schedules VLA inference
and MPC on a single mobile GPU. Specifically, Dubot identifies concurrent
opportunities and proposes three co-location mechanisms: spatial partitioning,
block-level co-location, and intra-block fusion. By leveraging different
mechanisms, Dubot enables fine-grained co-location to improve the efficiency of
various VLA-MPC operator pairs. Dubot further proposes a co-location optimizer
that can determine the optimal co-location schedule under workload dependency,
QoS requirements, and hardware-resource constraints. Across representative VLA
inference paradigms on NVIDIA Jetson Orin and Thor, Dubot achieves up to 1.4x
end-to-end speedup and reduces QoS violations by up to 38%.
