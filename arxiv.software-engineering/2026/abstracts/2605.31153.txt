Title: BIAS-ID: A Framework for Analyzing Transformation Biases in AI-Generated Image Detectors
Authors: Jonas Ricker, Asja Fischer, Erwin Quiring
ID: 2605.31153
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31153

Abstract:
Given the surge of harmful AI-generated imagery online, reliably distinguishing
authentic images from generated ones has become an urgent research topic. While
many proposed detection methods demonstrate high accuracy in controlled
settings, their performance often degrades substantially when tested on real-
world data. A potential root cause is subtle bias in the detectors' training
data. As a result, detectors may rely on spurious correlations instead of
learning true forensic artifacts. While recent studies have identified this
problem and developed countermeasures, there is not yet an established protocol
to evaluate how biased a detector actually is. In this work, we therefore take a
step back: First, we discuss what it means for a detector to be biased, and how
this differs from a lack of robustness. Second, we propose BIAS-ID, a
transparent framework for analyzing and quantifying the presence of
transformation biases in AI-generated image detectors. We validate our framework
by evaluating six detectors across two datasets, revealing that several state-
of-the-art detection methods are strongly affected by common, processing-related
biases. Our results highlight the importance of bias-aware evaluation for
developing reliable AI-generated image detectors. We provide an easy-to-use
library and code to reproduce our results at
https://github.com/jonasricker/biasid.
