Title: KLEAR: Knowledge-Matched LLM Evaluation in Korean across Application Requirements via Controlled Benchmark Transformation
Authors: Seoyeon Kim, Jeonghyun Kang, Hamin Koo, Barom Kang, Heondeuk Lee, Jaehyung Kim
ID: 2611.09223
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09223

Abstract:
Existing Korean benchmarks for Large Language Models (LLMs) evaluate performance
across diverse knowledge domains, but their accuracy scores cannot distinguish
whether incorrect responses arise from a lack of required knowledge or a failure
to apply that knowledge correctly. Our analysis of existing Korean benchmarks
reveals that most questions can be answered through factual recall alone, while
those demanding greater application also differ in required knowledge, making it
difficult to isolate application ability. To address this, we introduce KLEAR, a
framework that constructs knowledge-matched contrasts by varying application
demand while preserving the required knowledge. During construction, KLEAR
identifies the knowledge underlying each source question as an explicit anchor
bundle to guide generation of questions requiring more complex application.
Generated candidates then undergo automated and human validation, with iterative
revision based on evaluation feedback. Experiments demonstrate that the
resulting knowledge-matched sets largely satisfy the intended constraints,
enabling controlled evaluation across diverse LLMs. These evaluations reveal
that increasing application demand exposes failures that persist even when the
required knowledge is provided.
