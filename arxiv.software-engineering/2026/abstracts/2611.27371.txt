Title: Balanced Boolean Functions from Sparse Niho-Type Permutation Polynomials: Few-Valued Walsh Spectra and Applications
Authors: Chenxu Wang, Jun Zhang
ID: 2611.27371
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27371

Abstract:
We study balanced Boolean functions parameterized by two-to-one mappings of the
forms $P(x+x^2)$ and $P(x+x^{-1})$, where $P$ is a sparse permutation polynomial
with generalized Niho-type exponents. First, for $q=2^m$ with $m$ even, we
determine the complete four-valued Walsh distribution of the balanced Boolean
function parameterized by $Q(x+x^2)$, where $Q(x)=x(1+x^{q-1}+x^{2(q-1)})$. We
then generalize a permutation polynomial studied by Gupta and Sharma to the
parameterized family $P_λ(x)=x^{3q-1}+λx^{2q}+λx^2$ over $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$. We
prove that $P_λ$ is a permutation polynomial if and only if either $λ=0$, or
$λ\ne0$ and $X^3+X+λ^{-1}$ has no root in $\mathbb{F}_q$. Moreover, the number
of admissible parameters is $(q+2)/3$ when $m$ is even and $(q+4)/3$ when $m$ is
odd. Thus, admissible parameters exist for every $m\geq2$, and approximately
one-third of the elements of $\mathbb{F}_q$ yield permutation polynomials. For
every admissible parameter $λ$, we determine the complete Walsh distribution of
the balanced Boolean function $f_λ$ parameterized by $P_λ(x+x^2)$ and prove that
this distribution is independent of $λ$. For $m\geq3$, these functions have the
five-valued Walsh spectrum ${q,0,-q,-2q,-3q}$, while for $m=2$ the spectrum
degenerates to four values. As applications, their supports yield projective
minimal binary five-weight codes, sets with five hyperplane intersection
numbers, and regular cubelike Cayley graphs with six distinct eigenvalues.
Finally, for mappings of the form $P(x+x^{-1})$, we construct an infinite family
of balanced Boolean functions with simple explicit trace representations.
