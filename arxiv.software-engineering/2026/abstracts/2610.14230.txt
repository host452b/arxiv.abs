Title: An Availability Management Framework for Microservice-Based Safety-Critical CIoT Systems: Architecture and End-to-End Evaluation
Authors: Hassaan Siddiqui, Ferhat Khendek
ID: 2610.14230
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14230

Abstract:
Safety-critical Cellular Internet of Things (CIoT) systems increasingly rely on
microservice architectures orchestrated across heterogeneous edge and cloud
platforms. Although this paradigm improves modularity and scalability, the
fault-tolerance mechanisms of contemporary orchestration platforms remain
predominantly reactive, i.e., failed components are restarted or rescheduled
only after an outage has already occurred. Such recovery can produce multi-
second End to End Service Outage Time (E2E SOT), which can grow further under
container restart backoff. This paper proposes an Availability Management
Framework (AMF), a generic monitor-predict-recover loop that complements
reactive recovery with machine learning-based anomaly detection and make-before-
break switchover to a standby replica. Candidate ML models are evaluated offline
under three protocols, including a change of operating regime, in order to
select a model for live deployment. AMF is instantiated and evaluated on a
three-tier tele-operated driving (ToD) testbed under a staged resource-
exhaustion scenario. Relative to Kubernetes-only recovery, AMF completes
switchover during the anomaly window, reduces E2E SOT, and avoids the tens-of-
seconds outages associated with repeated in-place restarts under backoff. On
this non-real-time testbed, residual E2E SOT lies inside healthy-path jitter and
is therefore limited by path timing rather than by lateness of the recovery
decision. The paper whows that coupling a validated ML based anomaly detector
with proactive switchover can remove restart- and backoff-dominated outage from
a critical path.
