Title: Why Software Engineering Is Indispensable in the Age of Coding Agents
Authors: Alfonso Fuggetta
ID: 2610.10226
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10226

Abstract:
Can AI make Software Engineering (SE) -- the discipline -- obsolete? And can it
make software engineers -- the professionals -- redundant? This paper argues
that the rise of capable AI coding agents makes SE and software engineers
essential, not obsolete: the missing foundation without which AI-assisted
development produces misleadingly plausible, unverifiable, and ultimately
untrustworthy software. Three structural properties of large language models
(probabilistic generation, agnosticism, and semantic statelessness) create a
structural vacuum that no amount of training can eliminate. Filling it requires
four knowledge levers: methodological knowledge, domain knowledge, design
choices, and process choices. All four must be reified as persistent artifacts,
and each requires the software engineer as methodologist, mediator, and
custodian.
