Title: A Security Analysis of Verifiable Credential Verification Pipelines: Attack Surface Arising from JSON-LD/RDFC Canonicalization Compared with SD-JWT VC and mdoc
Authors: Naohiro Fujie, Shigeya Suzuki
ID: 2611.04461
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04461

Abstract:
We evaluate, at implementation level and under an explicit threat model, the
verification pipelines of three Verifiable Credential (VC) formats: SD-JWT VC;
the W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model 2.0 with the Data Integrity
cryptosuite eddsa-rdfc-2022 (W3C VCDM); and mdoc (ISO/IEC 18013-5). For W3C VCDM
we identify and exercise three structural attack surfaces from remote context
resolution and RDFC-1.0 canonicalization: denial of service (DoS) via poison
graphs, context injection through @context, and server-side request forgery
(SSRF) reachable via malicious URLs, all at default settings. A poison graph,
whose blank-node references are symmetric enough to force RDFC-1.0 into deep
recursion, raises it to 278x the baseline from five blank nodes in 527 bytes,
and to 13.5 s from eight. Every experiment ran in four environments sharing
runtime and library versions; the multiple moves with the machine (251--424x for
one input), not with its speed. Cost tracks that symmetry rather than the node
count: an acyclic input with 1,500 blank nodes stays at 462--714x. A call limit
separates the two: every attack family aborts at 16 or below, every legitimate
credential completes at 1, but a shallow, wide recursion, such as the acyclic
control, passes whatever the bound. DoS does not presuppose adversarial input:
with production education schemas, canonicalization costs 23--34x a simple
credential in one environment, 16--49x across four. Effective mitigations are a
static document loader, which removes the retrieval latency and both remote-
context surfaces; a context allowlist; and a call limit with a size bound. SD-
JWT VC and mdoc need neither canonicalization nor external references, and our
verifiers resisted every vector evaluated. These are not format-specific defects
but properties of the pipeline and its configuration.
