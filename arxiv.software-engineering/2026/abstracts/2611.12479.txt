Title: Brief introduction to post-quantum cryptography in industrial applications
Authors: Mateusz Żarski, Jarosław Miszczak, Ilias Cherkaoui, Cédric Crettaz, Indrakshi Dey, Adela Georgescu, Deepak V. Katkoria, Ruxandra F. Olimid, Mihai Prunescu, Maria Slim et al.
ID: 2611.12479
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12479

Abstract:
The development of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) is directly motivated by the
necessity to secure information in the face of the quantum threat. This paper
provides an overview of recent developments relevant to PQC adoption across
several industrial applications, including IoT, healthcare, civil and
infrastructure engineering, and defense. We present a brief, self-contained
overview of PQC and its market positioning. We perform implementation
experiments on selected quantum-safe methods and quantify several aspects, such
as the quantum resources required to break common cryptographic schemes, as well
as the performance of the most well-known PQC algorithms. We also discuss the
resistance of different PQC classes to quantum attacks and the status of PQC
implementations in different programming languages. Finally, we present
benchmark results on KEMs and signature algorithms to help select the
appropriate algorithm for a specific application based on criteria such as
algorithm performance, data lifetime, and level of data sensitivity.
