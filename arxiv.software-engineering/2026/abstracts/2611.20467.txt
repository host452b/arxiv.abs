Title: On the Applicability of $p$-adic Numbers in Computational Modules for Neural Networks
Authors: Vladimir Evgrafov
ID: 2611.20467
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20467

Abstract:
The applicability of $p$-adic arithmetic as an intermediate computational module
in an otherwise unchanged machine-learning pipeline is investigated. A one-
feature linear regression on the scikit-learn Diabetes dataset serves as the
test model. The fitted coefficients are represented in $\mathbb{Q}_p$,
predictions are computed in that field, and rational reconstruction is applied
to the outputs. In the pyAdic experiments, rational reconstruction either
returns no result or produces values that differ from the baseline predictions;
changing computational precision can change both the magnitude and sign of the
reconstructed value. In SageMath, forward conversions succeed, but the
reconstructed predictions differ from the baseline. An alternative
representation based on digit reversal fails to preserve ordinary addition
because of carry propagation. Under the tested settings, none of the test
predictions is reconstructed correctly. These results show that the tested
conversion and reconstruction procedures do not provide a compatible
intermediate module even for linear regression. Models defined directly over
$p$-adic data and operations offer a direction for further investigation.
