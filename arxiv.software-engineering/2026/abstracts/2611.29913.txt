Title: MoMa-NMP: Scene-Scale Neural Motion Planning for Mobile Manipulators
Authors: Adam Kan, Jason Jingzhou Liu, Jiahui Yang, Kenneth Shaw, Deepak Pathak
ID: 2611.29913
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29913

Abstract:
Mobile manipulators operating in homes and other complex environments must
coordinate their base and arm over long horizons, navigating around obstacles,
through narrow passages, and between constrained workspaces. Neural motion
planners can provide fast closed-loop planning from partial observations, but
scaling them to room-sized environments is difficult because generating high-
quality training trajectories is itself a challenging motion-planning problem.
To address this, we introduce MoMa-DemoSmith, a demonstration-generation
pipeline that separates the problem into global path discovery for large complex
scenes and local motion refinement for collision avoidance. This key separation
enables the generation of millions of challenging whole-body trajectories across
our procedurally generated scenes. We train MoMa-NMP on these demonstrations
through behavior cloning (BC) followed by privileged on-policy distillation,
achieving 87.0% success on our simulation benchmark and 77.5% across 80 trials
in unseen real-world scenes. See video results at https://adamhkan.com/moma-
nmp/.
