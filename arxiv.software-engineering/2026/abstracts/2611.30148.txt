Title: STMemoLLM: A Spatial-Temporal Memory-Augmented LLM for Robust Traffic Forecasting
Authors: Jian Zhou, Binqing Wu, Zongjiang Shang, Wanghui Qiu, Dongliang Cui, Chenxi Jin, Yue Yu, Xinyi Fu, Ling Chen
ID: 2611.30148
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30148

Abstract:
Traffic forecasting is essential for intelligent transportation systems.
However, traditional deep-learning models often rely heavily on high-quality
observations. Recent LLM-based approaches incorporate pretrained knowledge to
improve forecasting, but they remain sensitive to input perturbations and
struggle to preserve long-term patterns. To address this, we propose STMemoLLM,
a spatial-temporal memory-augmented LLM for robust traffic forecasting.
STMemoLLM introduces memory prototypes to compress inputs into compact summary
tokens while extracting reliable dynamics from residual signals, achieving a
balance between stability and variability. Specifically, a memory-enhanced
tokenizer encodes prototypes and sensor characteristics into attention biases to
guide adaptive compression of correlated sensors into semantic summary tokens. A
memory-calibrated router further evaluates contextual deviation, compression
deficiency, and prototype alignment to identify reliable residual signals, which
complement the summaries while suppressing noise. Extensive experiments on eight
real-world datasets show that STMemoLLM achieves state-of-the-art performance.
Additional experiments examine point masking, additive Gaussian noise,
structured missingness, and limited training data, demonstrating strong
robustness. Code is available at
https://anonymous.4open.science/r/STMemoLLM-70BF.
