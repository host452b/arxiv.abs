Title: Equal Hamming Weights at Fixed Root Order and Applications to Constacyclic MDS Codes
Authors: Li Zhu, Hongfeng Wu
ID: 2611.00637
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00637

Abstract:
For a fixed positive integer $n>1$, we study the prime powers $q$ coprime to $n$
for which all monic irreducible factors of $Φ_n$ over $\F_q$ have the same
coefficient Hamming weight. Cyclotomic orbit coefficients give a finite,
effective classification of these fields. Outside finitely many computable
characteristics, every factor has the support of its characteristic-zero orbit
polynomial; the uniform condition $p>2^{\varphi(n)}$ suffices. In each remaining
characteristic, extension exponents reduce to divisors of $\ord_n(p)$, and an
integer variance identity expressed by polynomial gcd degrees decides equal
weight. The same coefficient data determine the full weight distribution. As an
application, we prove that an MDS constacyclic code with an irreducible
generator forces every irreducible polynomial of the same root order to have
full support, including factors associated with different constacyclic
constants. This yields a uniform gcd and norm obstruction. We develop its short-
multiplier extension, give a prime-order cubic family, and establish an explicit
quadratic-residue exclusion without a prime-square alphabet or a near-square
length hypothesis.
