Title: NNS-GS: Non-Local Neural Scattering for Relightable Gaussian Splatting
Authors: Shiyu Liang, Yang Gao, Lili Wang, Aimin Hao, Beibei Wang
ID: 2611.30351
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30351

Abstract:
Creating relightable 3D assets of translucent objects is challenging because
changing the illumination at one surface location can affect the appearance
elsewhere through subsurface scattering. Existing Gaussian-based scattering
models typically predict scattering from local features, making this dependence
difficult to capture. We present Non-Local Neural Scattering for Gaussian
Splatting (NNS-GS), which learns non-local scattering together with geometry-
guided local reflection. The scattering module learns how illumination from
light-visible surface regions in light space contributes to the shading point in
view space, conditioned on regional diffuse responses and spatial relationships.
The reflection module supplies these diffuse responses and uses additional
geometric guidance to better capture specular highlights and sharp shadow
boundaries. To reduce component mixing and support controllable scattering
appearance, anchor-based progressive training establishes the diffuse-scattering
base before introducing specular reflection and supervises the components
separately. Under calibrated point lighting, NNS-GS improves mean PSNR over
existing methods by 2.47 dB and 0.81 dB on the synthetic and real translucent
scenes, respectively. Additional synthetic comparisons further show that NNS-GS
better matches the reference when adjusting scattering attenuation.
