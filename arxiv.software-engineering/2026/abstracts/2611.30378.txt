Title: Sparse Constitutive Neural Networks for Hyperelasticity
Authors: Hendrik Geisler, Clément Jailin, Emmanuel Baranger
ID: 2611.30378
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30378

Abstract:
Constitutive model identification aims to recover compact, interpretable models
that accurately describe material behavior. In this work, we identify
hyperelastic constitutive models using artificial neural networks with sparsity-
promoting constraints and regularization. The proposed method combines the
expressiveness of neural networks with the structural interpretability of sparse
constitutive models, such as those identified by EUCLID, by representing the
strain energy density as a sum of products of univariate functions of the
invariants. The resulting decomposition exposes the contributions of individual
invariants and their interactions. The method is demonstrated on synthetic data
in supervised and unsupervised learning scenarios, showing that it can
accurately identify interpretable constitutive models with few active modes.
