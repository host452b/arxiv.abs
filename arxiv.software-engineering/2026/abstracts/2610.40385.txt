Title: Securing the Robotics Supply Chain
Authors: Cindy L. Bethel, Henrik I. Christensen, Odest Chadwicke Jenkins, George Pappas, Holly Yanco, Haley Griffin
ID: 2610.40385
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40385

Abstract:
This paper sets out what a sustained federal research and investment agenda
would need to include to close that gap--not within a single budget cycle, but
over the decade such an undertaking will realistically require. It identifies
where the gap is most pronounced today: in the methods needed to verify and
certify components of uncertain origin, in the foundational hardware research
that has long been underfunded relative to software, in the physical
infrastructure researchers need to test systems at scale, and in the workforce
required to build and sustain all of it. Robotics should not be treated merely
as a downstream beneficiary of a broader artificial intelligence investment
strategy. It constitutes its own supply chain, with its own points of failure,
and merits its own sustained federal commitment.
