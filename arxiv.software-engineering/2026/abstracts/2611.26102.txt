Title: Burnside Problems for Matrix Semigroup Quotients with Applications to Border Complexity
Authors: Roland Guttenberg, Nathan Lhote, Mahsa Shirmohammadi, James Worrell
ID: 2611.26102
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26102

Abstract:
The Burnside theorem for matrix semigroups states that a finitely generated
matrix semigroup is finite whenever each element has only finitely many distinct
powers. We study analogous finiteness questions for quotients of matrix
semigroups by congruences, which need not themselves admit faithful finite-
dimensional matrix representations. We show three finiteness criteria based on
uniform bounds on the quotient images of (rank-)stable subsemigroups and groups
in the Zariski closure. Our proofs use exterior algebra and the Ramsey theorem
to develop variations of Simon's factorisation forest to matrix semigroups
modulo congruences. We also present applications of our results to border
complexity. One such involves separating homogeneous algebraic branching
programs of fixed width and degree from its Zariski closure.
