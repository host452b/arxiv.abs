Title: Solving quantified constraint satisfaction problems with applications to control
Authors: Éric Goubault, Nuwan Herath Mudiyanselage, Sylvie Putot
ID: 2610.39776
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39776

Abstract:
Many problems in control can be expressed as quantified constraints, such as
robust forward and backward reachability, synthesis of controllers, solutions to
motion planning problems. In this paper, we develop a method for computing inner
and outer approximations of global solutions to very general quantified
constraints, generalizing widely the state of the art on similar problems,
usually restricted to at most one alternation of quantifiers or to local
solutions. By global solutions, we mean characterizing accurately the entire set
of solutions, within some prescribed bounds. For this, our approach uses a set
inversion technique relying on oracles for set inclusion using local solutions
to related quantified reachability problems. We exemplify the approach
throughout the paper on different examples related to control problems.
