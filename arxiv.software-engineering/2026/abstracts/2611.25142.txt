Title: Mutable State in Enrich-by-Need Security Protocol Analysis
Authors: Joshua D Guttman, Paul D Rowe, Eben Blaisdell
ID: 2611.25142
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25142

Abstract:
Security protocol analysis has emphasized message-passing, as this is how
different principals interact in authentication, key exchange, and similar
functionalities. However, in many protocols a principal also maintains mutable
state that limits what actions its local sessions may take; this cross-session
state is altered by other local sessions. In this paper we show how to represent
this cross-session mutable state in the strand space approach to symbolic
protocol analysis, and how to implement the resulting ideas in the Cryptographic
Protocol Shapes Analyzer CPSA. The key theory is expressed in two axioms
characterizing state, plus protocol-specific rules. It leads to tight, specific
characterizations of protocol behavior in relevant cases, and aids in developing
correct protocols relying on cross-session mutable state.
