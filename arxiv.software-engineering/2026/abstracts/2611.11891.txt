Title: A Large-Scale Dataset for Cross-Lingual Multi-Jurisdiction Patent Drafting from Patent Cooperation Treaty Applications
Authors: Trung Phuong Le, Hirofumi Nonaka, Koji Marusaki, Katsuhito Sudoh, Seiya Kawano
ID: 2611.11891
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11891

Abstract:
Securing patent protection across countries requires drafting claims separately
for each national patent office, in the relevant language and according to its
legal and stylistic conventions. Patent claims can therefore differ across
languages and jurisdictions because of translation, target-office drafting
practices, and prosecution-stage changes. We formulate cross-lingual patent
drafting as generating plausible candidate claim sets for a specified target
language, jurisdiction, and prosecution stage from a source claim set in another
language. The task models jurisdiction- and stage-specific transformations
beyond content-preserving translation. We construct a large-scale Japanese-
English dataset by linking Japan Patent Office (JPO) and United States Patent
and Trademark Office (USPTO) records through 884,179 Patent Cooperation Treaty
(PCT)-derived patent families. Across six released configurations, these
families yield 4,067,168 directional claim-set instances; four core
configurations form the main benchmark. Commercial machine translation and zero-
and few-shot prompting capture part of the task, while supervised adaptation
provides the strongest overall baseline, showing that task-specific patterns can
be learned from linked cross-jurisdictional claim sets.
