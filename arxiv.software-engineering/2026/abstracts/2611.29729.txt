Title: Rethinking Discovery Research with AI: The promise of transdisciplinarity and the danger of reductionism
Authors: Iulia Georgescu, Venkatesh Narayanamurti
ID: 2611.29729
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29729

Abstract:
In theory, transdisciplinary research focuses on solving real-world problems
without being constrained by disciplinary boundaries and methodologies. In
practice, doing so is challenging, as recognized by the phrase "The world has
problems; universities have departments." As a powerful integrative tool, AI may
help overcome traditional reductionist approaches and enable genuine
transdisciplinarity. In this essay, we discuss how this could happen and why,
within the current system of incentives, it is not happening.
