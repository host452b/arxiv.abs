Title: Irreducible shallow tanh neural networks are a manifold
Authors: Ivan Bioli, Isabella Carla Gonnella, Carlo Marcati, Federico Pichi
ID: 2611.30553
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30553

Abstract:
Neural networks (NNs) have been increasingly used as function approximators,
motivating the study of the geometry of their function spaces. We study the set
of functions represented by shallow neural networks with hyperbolic tangent
activation, fixed input and output dimensions, and $n$ hidden neurons. In
particular, we prove that the subset represented by irreducible networks in the
sense of Sussmann (Neural Networks, 1992), i.e., networks with no redundant
neurons, is a smooth embedded submanifold of the Sobolev spaces
$\mathrm{W}^{\ell,p}$ on bounded sets for any $\ell\in\mathbb{N}$ and
$p\in[1,+\infty]$. The key step is to show that if a sequence of networks
converges in $\mathrm{W}^{\ell,p}$ to an irreducible network with the same
number of hidden neurons, then the parameters of the approximating networks
remain bounded. Finally, we show that this manifold has exactly $n+1$ connected
components when both the input and output are scalar, and is connected
otherwise.
