Title: The Privacy and Security Risks of Edge LLM Optimization
Authors: Mingchen Li, Ren-Yi Huang, Dumindu Samaraweera, Prashant Shekhar, J. Morris Chang
ID: 2610.40543
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40543

Abstract:
Edge deployment of Large Language Models requires optimization techniques that
reduce memory, computation, and energy costs. However, preserving task
performance does not necessarily preserve security. We argue that optimization
is an information transformation that can alter representations, decision
pathways, and trust boundaries, creating security drift and optimization-induced
attack surfaces.
