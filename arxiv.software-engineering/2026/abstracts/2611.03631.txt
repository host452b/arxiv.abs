Title: Supporting Viewpoint Coordination for Compliance by Design with an Artifact Model for Regulatory Requirements Engineering
Authors: Oleksandr Kosenkov, Davide Fucci, Michael Unterkalmsteiner, Daniel Mendez
ID: 2611.03631
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03631

Abstract:
Regulatory compliance has become an essential software requirement Legal demands
for compliance by design CbD call for requirements engineering RE approaches
that derive requirements and support compliance implementation in subsequent
software engineering SE processes such as software design The complexity of the
legal knowledge demanded in regulatory RE makes it important to involve legal
experts and ensure the relevant specifications are valid and usable for them
Regulatory requirements must support compliance implementation in software
design However there is a lack of approaches bridging legal and SE viewpoints
and interaction challenges between the two persist To address engineeringlegal
coordination in specification of regulatory requirements we propose the Artifact
Model for Regulatory Requirements Engineering AM4RRE operationalized through
templates AM4RRE was positively evaluated by most of the 37 practitioners for
coordination in regulatory RE and for supporting requirements quality Results
from a quasiexperiment with 34 of them in 10 groups indicated that
templateguided coordination was associated with on average shorter total
specification time when executed first and higher requirements quality in most
groups in which it varied Existing RE research and practice provide little
attention and support for viewpoint coordination in regulatory RE for CbD
Artifactoriented approaches such as AM4RRE have the potential to guide
coordination in RE for better RE results and contribute to concretizing the role
and contribution of RE to CbD
