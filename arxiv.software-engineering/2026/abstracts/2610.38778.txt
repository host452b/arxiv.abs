Title: Slant: Shaping Traffic for Resource-Efficient Microservices
Authors: Congwei Zhang, Chieh-Jan Mike Liang, Mengbai Xiao, Yifei Zou, Shulun Wu, Xiangyu Gao, Xiuzhen Cheng, Dongxiao Yu
ID: 2610.38778
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38778

Abstract:
Microservice architectures rely on load balancers to distribute requests across
service replicas, yet existing designs largely optimize for balanced routing
without considering how routing shapes autoscaling signals and resource
efficiency. We present Slant, a resource-aware load balancing framework that
intentionally creates safe traffic imbalance to improve resource utilization.
Slant profiles service-specific critical points, computes conservative multi-
dimensional capacity, derives imbalance-aware routing weights that maintain hot
and cold pods, and falls back to safer behavior under anomalies. By exposing
clearer utilization signals, Slant works with existing vertical pod autoscalers
(VPAs) without modification. Experiments on three microservice benchmarks show
that Slant reduces allocated CPU cores by up to 26\% while lowering P99 latency
by up to 18\% compared to Round-Robin. Under dynamic workloads, Slant triggers
VPA scale-up 45\% faster and completes scale-in 40\% faster. Under anomalies, it
recovers 40\% faster from pod failures and limits latency amplification to
1.8$\times$ versus 2.6$\times$ during downstream slowdowns.
