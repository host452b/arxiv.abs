Title: A Reference Architecture for Integrating Agentic AI into Enterprise Software: Responsibilities, Interactions, and Variability
Authors: Mingqin Yu, Fethi Rabhi, Liming Zhu, Qinghua Lu
ID: 2610.14818
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14818

Abstract:
Agentic AI moves generative AI applications toward systems that coordinate
reasoning, enterprise context, model inference, and external actions.
Integrating these capabilities into enterprise software introduces diverse
architectural concerns, from orchestration, knowledge and memory, and model
access to tools and actions, security, runtime infrastructure, and evaluation.
Existing research and industry architectures address many of these concerns but
differ in scope, abstraction, responsibility organization, interaction
representation, and assumptions about which capabilities are required. This
paper presents a technology-neutral reference architecture for enterprise
agentic AI integration. We synthesize architectural capabilities from research
publications, industry architecture guidance, and interoperability
specifications by architectural purpose, organize them into ten
responsibilities, and define boundary invariants, typed cross-responsibility
interactions, and variation points that distinguish responsibilities from
condition-dependent capabilities and replaceable realization mechanisms. We
instantiate the architecture as an ESG portfolio-analysis and pre-trade
compliance agent over an existing service-oriented ESG metric computation and
reporting system, and complement the case with a mapping of an independently
published agentic architecture. The case realizes seven of the ten
responsibilities; different questions take different interaction paths, controls
activate only under their conditions, and the probed boundary invariants hold.
The independent architecture maps onto the proposed responsibilities without
requiring an additional one. These results provide initial evidence for the
architecture's organization and applicability rather than its completeness. The
deployment and recorded results are available as a replication package.
