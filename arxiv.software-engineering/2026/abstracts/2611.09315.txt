Title: Verification of Security Properties of Cryptographic Protocols
Authors: Simona Bernardi, Joaquín Ezpeleta
ID: 2611.09315
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09315

Abstract:
Developing cryptographic protocols that are free from vulnerabilities is very
challenging, as evidenced by numerous design flaws and implementation bugs in
widely used protocols. Recent work has leveraged model checking to generate
negative test cases, that is test cases that include the behavior of Dolev-Yao
attacker. In this paper, we address the problem of analyzing execution traces
produced by testing protocol implementations with such negative test cases. We
propose a method for detecting violations of security properties by model-
checking execution traces. Our approach employs a model checker based on DLTL, a
Linear Temporal Logic with freeze operators, and supports automation of the
testing process through pre- and post-processing steps. These steps,
respectively, bridge the gap between different levels of abstraction and
synthesize the results produced by the model checker.We present experimental
results as a proof of concept for the feasibility of the proposed method.
