Title: Notes on Generative AI for Mathematical Research
Authors: Shurui Liu
ID: 2611.29811
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29811

Abstract:
These notes describe how generative AI is now used to solve and to check
research-level mathematics, with a focus on agents and their harnesses. For the
proving agents Rethlas, Danus, and Pharos and the verification agent OSVerify,
we explain the design principles, compare each with its base model, present case
studies, and show how to use and contribute to them. We also briefly review the
history of AI for mathematics and the community's reactions to it.
