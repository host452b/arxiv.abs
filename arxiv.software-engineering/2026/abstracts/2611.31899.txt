Title: Beyond Average Gains: Probing the Equity--Vulnerability Tension in Structured Visual AI for Learning
Authors: Mingyuan Li, Duan Wang, Erick Purwanto, Yihong Wang
ID: 2611.31899
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31899

Abstract:
Open-ended conversational AI requires learners to formulate questions, organize
learning paths, and track changing problem states. Structured visual interfaces
may ease these demands, but higher average ratings do not establish that support
is experienced consistently across learner backgrounds and devices. We conducted
a randomized study of graph-algorithm learning, comparing VisualCodeChat (VCC),
which combines predefined learning stages, conversational explanations, and
dynamic algorithm visualizations, with a standard text-based chatbot. We
analyzed 282 post-task questionnaires from university students. In unadjusted
comparisons, VCC received significantly higher usability and perceived
effectiveness ratings. We detected no significant between-condition differences
in efficiency, satisfaction, or post-use emotion. Exploratory within-condition
analyses identified differences associated with prior chatbot experience:
infrequent users reported lower adoption and learning-management ratings in the
standard AI condition, while uncomfortable prior chatbot experiences were
associated with lower interaction and layout ratings in the VCC condition. These
findings suggest that improving the average interaction experience is only part
of making AI-supported learning inclusive. Future evaluations should examine
whether learners with different prior experiences can obtain guidance, recover
from difficulties, and demonstrate understanding independently across devices.
