Title: Optimal Average Distortion for Non-Expansive Embeddings of Euclidean Metrics into the Line: With applications to cut-matching games and vertex expansion
Authors: Jack Spalding-Jamieson
ID: 2610.41945
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41945

Abstract:
We show that any $n$-point Euclidean metric admits a non-expansive embedding
into the line with average distortion at most $O\left(\sqrt{\frac{\log
n}{\log\log n}}\right)$, matching a known lower bound. We also present two
applications of this result. First, we give a cut-player strategy with an
optimal spectral guarantee for the undirected cut-matching game. Second, we give
an improved spectral approximation for vertex expansion in high-degree graphs.
