Title: QSoftCore: A Conceptual Foundation for Quantum Software Engineering
Authors: Tao Yue, Yuechen Li, Man Zhang
ID: 2610.39406
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39406

Abstract:
Quantum software engineering (QSE) requires understanding application
requirements, quantum programs and algorithms, quantum computation paradigms,
executions, and deployment. However, relevant quantum computing (QC) concepts
are often defined across physical, mathematical, and paradigm-specific levels of
abstraction, making it difficult for the QSE community and hence proposed
methods to refer to them consistently. To this end, we present QSoftCore, a
conceptual model that reorganizes established QC concepts from a software
engineering perspective and connects quantum applications, computational
problems, algorithms, configurations, programs, and paradigm-specific
computation specifications with state evolution, execution results, and
backends. Our assessment against QSE literature across key software engineering
activities indicates that QSoftCore provides shared QC referents for quantum
computations, executions, deployment configurations, backends, and measurement
evidence across the QSE lifecycle.
