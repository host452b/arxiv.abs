Title: Trustworthy Edge AI: Security, Privacy, and Resilience for Distributed Intelligence
Authors: Jaydip Sen, Subhasis Dasgupta
ID: 2611.31331
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31331

Abstract:
This chapter examines the principles and emerging research directions for
building secure, privacy-preserving, and resilient Edge Artificial Intelligence
(Edge AI) systems. As intelligence shifts from centralized clouds to distributed
edge environments, challenges arise in ensuring data integrity, privacy, and
reliable operation under adversarial and uncertain conditions. The chapter
analyzes Edge AI architectures, emphasizing the expanded attack surface due to
heterogeneous devices, resource constraints, and dynamic networks. It reviews
core security mechanisms, including hardware-rooted trust, zero-trust models,
secure communication, and attestation. Privacy-preserving approaches, such as
federated learning, secure aggregation, and differential privacy, enable
collaborative intelligence without centralized data sharing. Resilience
strategies, including fault tolerance, adversarial robustness, and adaptive
orchestration, are also explored. Case studies from smart cities, autonomous
vehicles, healthcare, and industrial IoT illustrate practical trade-offs. The
chapter advocates a holistic, cross-layer design integrating security, privacy,
and resilience for trustworthy edge intelligence.
