Title: SoK: Assessing Post-Quantum Cryptography Adoption in Software Development: Standards, Lifecycle Coverage, Deployment Maturity, and Evidence of Readiness
Authors: Shakya R. D. N., Craig Costello, Nalin A. G. Arachchilage
ID: 2611.08442
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08442

Abstract:
Standardised post-quantum cryptography (PQC) is critical for protecting software
systems against future quantum threats, but standardised algorithms alone do not
provide a pathway for their secure integration into software. This paper
presents a Systematisation of Knowledge (SoK) of PQC integration in software
development, synthesising existing approaches across implementation, migration,
and developer education. We analyse these approaches against NIST PQC standards
and PQC migration guidance, software development lifecycle coverage,
cybersecurity practices, deployment maturity, and testing and evaluation
evidence. Our analysis shows that existing work establishes important technical
and organisational foundations but provides disconnected support across the
software development lifecycle, with limited coordination between preparation,
implementation, migration, deployment, maintenance, and developer practices.
Evaluation evidence also remains concentrated in controlled or early-stage
settings, providing stronger evidence of technical feasibility than operational
readiness. We identify cross-cutting gaps and research directions for developing
lifecycle-oriented, empirically validated approaches that connect technical,
developer, and organisational requirements for secure PQC integration.
