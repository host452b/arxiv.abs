Title: Enhancing Edge AI System Security with a Stacked Hardware Security Controller for Robust and Energy-Efficient Anomaly Detectio
Authors: Carl Benda, Eren Kurshan, Paul Franzon
ID: 2611.01150
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01150

Abstract:
In recent years, the growing adoption of Edge AI systems has been accompanied by
a surge in security threats. Edge AI deployments face an rapidly evolving
spectrum of security threats, from side-channel information leakage to ad-
versarial model manipulation and physical device tampering. Conventional
security mechanisms such as malware scanners and resource-intensive firewall
solutions are unsuitable due to edge AIs typically limited computational
resources, strict energy budgets and thermal constraints especially in battery-
powered and field-deployed environments. This paper proposes a stacked,
hardware-based security mechanism that enables low-overhead, continuous
detection of anomalous execution patterns. Anomaly detection utilizing hard-
ware performance counters and physical sensors outperforms software-based
alternatives by providing key capabilities such as tamper-resistant root-of-
trust isolation, negligible runtime overhead, and resilience against compromised
operating systems. Experimental analysis shows that adversarial or malicious ap-
plications produce observable deviations in sensor and thermal readings on Edge
AI systems and hardware-based anomaly detection provides effective and low-
overhead anomaly detection.
