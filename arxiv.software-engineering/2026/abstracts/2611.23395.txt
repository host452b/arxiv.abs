Title: Lossless Reversible Watermarking for AIGC with Applications in Authentication
Authors: Yupeng Qiu, Han Fang, Ee-Chien Chang
ID: 2611.23395
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23395

Abstract:
AIGC watermarking is commonly used for tracing, but it can also support
applications such as authentication and tampering detection. We study lossless
reversible watermarking, which enables exact recovery of the original
watermarked image after permissible distortions. By exploiting the relatively
low entropy of the information required for image generation, together with
robust latent-space watermarking, we design a framework that preserves
sufficient information for lossless recovery. We then apply the framework to
AIGC authentication, which generally faces two key challenges. First, the
authentication mechanism must resist adaptive adversaries. Second, the content
relevant to authentication, such as visual designs or expressed messages, may
depend on application requirements that are not known at generation time. To
solve these difficulties, we incorporate cryptographic message authentication to
provide unforgeability against adaptive attacks. Lossless recovery provides the
original image as reference evidence, after which a suitable analyzer can be
selected to make the authentication decision. Experiments on Stable Diffusion
demonstrate exact recovery under a wide range of distortions.
