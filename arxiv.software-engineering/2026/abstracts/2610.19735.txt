Title: MakSafe: A Spatiotemporal Semantic Framework for Formal Specification and Compliance Checking of Road Vehicle Safety Rules
Authors: Maksym Labzhaniia, Julien Deantoni, Marie-Agn{è}s Peraldi-Frati, Fr{é}d{é}ric Mallet
ID: 2610.19735
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19735

Abstract:
Scenario-based validation is essential for automated road vehicles but cannot
cover the full variability of openworld traffic. We present MakSafe, a formal
spatiotemporal framework for specifying and checking road vehicle safety rules
through semantic zones, action-based forecasting, and trace-tree reasoning,
avoiding uniform time discretization. We formalize the road network, vehicle
states, and occupancy zones, then build executable rules for front-vehicle
identification and timeto-collision (TTC) verification. The evaluation combines
a tracedependent case study with a scalability benchmark over traffic density (N
), horizon (H), and branch factor (B). MakSafe yields branch-specific verdicts,
deriving safe and unsafe outcomes from the same initial scene, and re-evaluates
a scenario after a control update. A single rule check stays well below a
millisecond across the reported sweep, and longer horizons slow the TTC check.
