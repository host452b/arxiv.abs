Title: More Secure Software Development via Cultural Analysis: How could Log4Shell happen?
Authors: Lutz Prechelt, Lasse Fischer
ID: 2611.18565
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18565

Abstract:
There is little progress avoiding vulnerabilities. Cultural analysis may help:
For a given team, find the specific cultural values that led to a past
vulnerability, then modify the values accordingly for the future. After enough
such studies, patterns will likely emerge that are useful for the development
community at large.
