Title: Two barriers for minimal time firing squads, with applications to four and five states
Authors: Kia Keng Giam
ID: 2611.11080
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11080

Abstract:
A minimal-time solution of the firing squad synchronization problem synchronizes
every line of n cells at time 2n minus 2. Six states suffice; whether five do
has been open since 1987. Every line agrees with the half line until its right
ends echo returns. For minimal time solutions with any number of states, we
prove that cells at distance j behind the half lines front become time-periodic
only after time (1.5 minus epsilon)j, for every epsilon greater than 0 and
infinitely many j, and that the half line is never periodic in time and space on
a wedge at its left border, hence not eventually regular in our sense. With
certified satisfiability checks, we exclude four states using the lines of
lengths 2 to 5 and parts of the next five, and show that every five state
minimal time solution uses at least 15 non quiescent neighbourhoods on its half
lines first 78 anti-diagonals. We give five state rules synchronizing exactly
the lengths 2 to 14.
