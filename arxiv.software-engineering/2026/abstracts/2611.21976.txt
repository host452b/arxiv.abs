Title: Sparse Integer Fourier Transform and Its Application In Machine Learning
Authors: Jinyang Li, Jie Chen
ID: 2611.21976
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21976

Abstract:
The Discrete Fourier transform, mainly implemented by Fast Fourier Transform
(FFT), has been essential for numerous domains, such as digital signal
processing and feature extraction in materials design. However, current FFT
methods suffer from extensive non-trivial multiplications and twiddle-factor
operations in the big data era. To improve the computational efficiency of FFT,
this study proposes Sparse Integer Fourier Transform (SIFT), a method that
leverages the fast computing of sparsity and small integers, and applies the
Radix-n method to derive an FFT. The 2N-point DFT matrix in Radix-n FFT is
decomposed into four sparse integer matrices. A diagonal matrix containing
complex twiddle factors is efficiently approximated using an optimization
strategy while the remaining matrices retain sparsity and integer entries. For
radix-32 FFT, SIFT reduces the estimated arithmetic cost by 75%, with RMSE below
$2.3765\times10^{-8}$ and MAPE below 0.01089%. We evaluate frequency extraction
in a numerical test and an engineering case. In the numerical test, MAPE and
RMSE are below 2.016% and $2.536\times10^{-6}$, respectively. For wind turbine
fault diagnosis, SIFT extracts compact spectral features that retain fault
information while reducing the classifier's input dimensionality relative to the
full time series.
