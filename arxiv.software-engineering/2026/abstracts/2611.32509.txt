Title: Analysis of Federated Graph Neural Networks for Predicting Nuclear Core Properties
Authors: Oliver Kožul, Mihailo Ilić, Julia Bartos, Tuan Tran, Miloš Savić, Vladimir Kurbalija, Edo Frederix, Dušan Jakovetić
ID: 2611.32509
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32509

Abstract:
Federated learning has grown in popularity owing to the increasing data privacy
and safety concerns. It allows multiple parties to collaboratively train a model
without exchanging their raw data. As an example of highly sensitive
infrastructure of national importance, nuclear reactors benefit from this
approach, where---while pooling multiple large datasets from different
institutions could improve machine learning solutions---the confidentiality of
the datasets is paramount. In this paper, we demonstrate that a graph neural
network (GNN) outperforms the convolutional neural network (CNN) baseline in a
federated setting for a use case concerning nuclear reactor core optimization.
The GNN architecture is better suited for the nuclear reactor geometry as,
unlike the CNN, it does not require padding the cylindrical core and does not
assume translation invariance. The key finding of this work is that input
representation is more important than model size and complexity. More
sophisticated federated algorithms were employed, but the improvements derived
were of lower magnitude. Experimenting with training corpus volume led to the
discovery that greater fuel inventory variance is more beneficial than
arrangements of existing cases, which is a direct consequence of data
heterogeneity. Going forward, focus will remain on enhancing the input and
readout representation.
