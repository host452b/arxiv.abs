Title: Integrating of Attack Patterns and Threat Modeling to Support Security Analysis in the Automotive Domain
Authors: Eduardo Santos de Oliveira Marques, Saulo Moraes Villela, Heder Soares Bernardino, Alex Borges Vieira, Bernardo Martins Rocha, André Luiz de Oliveira
ID: 2610.40944
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40944

Abstract:
The increasing connectivity of cyber-physical systems in the automotive domain
demands systematic approaches for identifying and mitigating cybersecurity
threats. While ISO 21434 defines a structured workflow for Threat Analysis and
Risk Assessment (TARA), it does not prescribe a specific method for identifying
attack paths or evaluating risks. This paper proposes a conceptual model
grounded in ISO 21434 that integrates the STRIDE threat classification with the
CAPEC attack pattern database, focusing on the identification of attack
scenarios and risk assessment. Its applicability is demonstrated through a case
study involving Bluetooth-based threats to an automotive headlamp system.
