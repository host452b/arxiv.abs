Title: $β$-number and Its Applications to Data Science
Authors: Pengxu Wen, Ziwei Nie, Liwen Zou, Ying Cao, Xiaoping Yang
ID: 2611.19379
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19379

Abstract:
In this paper, we introduce a novel metric, $β$-number, designed to measure the
"$p$-th order $d$-dimensional oscillation degree" of a set. For a given set $K$
in the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^n$, $β$-number is computed by identifying an
optimal $d$-dimensional plane $L$ that minimizes the average $p$-th order
distance of all points in $K$ to $L$. This metric serves as a versatile tool in
data science, functioning both as a dimensionality reduction technique to find
the optimal $p$-th order $d$-dimensional subspace and as a measure to
characterize data oscillation for classification. We conduct a thorough
exploration of $β$-number through theoretical analysis and numerical
experiments. Initially, we establish the upper and lower bounds of $β$-number
for any set $K$, along with its scaling law. We then elucidate the relationships
between $β$-number and its related parameters. A key finding of this study is
that in discrete settings, when $p=2$, the $d$-dimensional plane determined by
$β$-number coincides with that obtained via Principal Component Analysis (PCA),
positioning $β$-number as a generalization of PCA. Furthermore, we propose two
algorithms for calculating $β$-number in both continuous and discrete settings.
We validate our theoretical results through a series of numerical experiments in
low-dimensional cases. Finally, we demonstrate the potential of $β$-number in
practical data analysis through a dimensionality reduction task and an image
classification task.
