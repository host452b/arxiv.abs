Title: Implicit Additively Weighted Geodesic Voronoi Diagrams and Applications
Authors: Bruce W. Brewer, Haitao Wang
ID: 2610.42075
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.42075

Abstract:
Let $P$ be a simple polygon with $m$ vertices. After $O(m)$-time preprocessing
of $P$, for any set $S$ of $n$ additively weighted point sites, we compute an
implicit additively weighted geodesic Voronoi diagram in $O(n\log n+n\log^2 m)$
time, supporting nearest-neighbor queries in $O(\log(n+m))$ time. In contrast,
explicitly constructing the diagram requires $Ω(m)$ time. Our algorithm
introduces a new sweep-line paradigm that avoids explicitly constructing the
diagram, together with a novel technique for point location in implicit
subdivisions. As applications, we obtain new dynamic data structures for
additively weighted geodesic nearest-neighbor searching and the first efficient
algorithms for shortest paths in geodesic disk graphs. After the same
$O(m)$-time preprocessing, single-source shortest paths in a geodesic disk graph
of $n$ sites can be computed in $O(n\log n+n\log^2 m)$ time in the unweighted
case and $O(n^{3/2}(\log n+\log^2 m))$ time in the weighted case. We also extend
our techniques to implicit farthest geodesic Voronoi diagrams for unweighted
sites, obtaining analogous bounds and dynamic farthest-neighbor data structures.
