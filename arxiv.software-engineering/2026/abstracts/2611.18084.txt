Title: On Applications of Polynomial-Time Group Isomorphism Algorithm
Authors: Wenkang Zhang, Zhinan Wang, Jincheng Zhuang
ID: 2611.18084
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18084

Abstract:
The group isomorphism problem is a fundamental problem in computational group
theory and is closely related to many other problems. Recently, Qiao and Zhang
proposed a polynomial-time algorithm for finite group isomorphism in the
multiplication-table model that computes the coset of all isomorphisms. Based on
the algorithm of Qiao and Zhang, we study applications to finite associative
ring isomorphism, bilinear map isotopism over finite commutative rings and free
three-tensor isomorphism over finite commutative rings. First, we obtain a
polynomial-time algorithm that returns the coset of all isomorphisms for finite
associative rings. Second, we extend efficient isotopism and isomorphism
algorithms for bilinear maps and free three-tensors from fields to general
finite commutative rings.
