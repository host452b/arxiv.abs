Title: Secure Wireless Communications with Flexible Reflectors: Joint Position and Rotation Optimization for Physical Layer Security
Authors: Miao Jiang, Xike Wang, Yiqing Li, Guangchi Zhang
ID: 2610.39290
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39290

Abstract:
By exploiting the spatial selectivity of wireless channels, physical layer
security (PLS) protects confidential wireless transmissions. Flexible reflectors
(FRs), which are passive metallic plates mounted on movable platforms, offer a
low-cost and low-complexity solution for reshaping the propagation environment.
However, their potential for secrecy-oriented signal propagation shaping has not
been explored. In this paper, we study an FR-aided secure communication over
non-line-of-sight links and jointly optimize the positions and rotations of FRs
to maximize the secrecy rate under geometric reflection and mutual-blocking
constraints. For the single-FR case, we propose a Fibonacci segmentation search
(FSS) method, which partitions the feasible region into multiple unimodal
intervals and obtains the optimal solution with Fibonacci search over the
unimodal intervals. To reduce the computational overhead, we also propose a low-
complexity cross-entropy search (CES) method, which employs Gaussian mixture
model-based adaptive importance sampling to localize promising search intervals
before deterministic refinement. After deriving the blocked placement interval
induced by the deployed FRs and integrating it into a sequential deployment
scheme, we extend the FSS and CES methods to the multi-FR case. Simulation
results show that our proposed FSS method achieves significant secrecy rate
performance gains over various benchmark schemes, and our proposed CES method
achieves a favorable runtime-performance tradeoff. These results indicate that
FRs provide a low-cost and reconfigurable mechanism for PLS.
