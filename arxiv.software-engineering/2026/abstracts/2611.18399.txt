Title: A Framework for Cost-Preserving Rounding with Applications to Laminar Thin Trees
Authors: Jannis Blauth, Rico Zenklusen
ID: 2611.18399
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18399

Abstract:
In the context of finding thin trees, like laminarly-thin trees or globally-thin
trees, it turned out to be challenging to round a fractional solution to an
integral one without losing cost, while only having a constant-factor violation
of the thinness constraints. We provide a general framework for this problem, in
a much broader context, that reduces the problem to finding a certain type of
rounding algorithm with only an approximate prize guarantee. We show that, up to
a change in constants, the rounding problem with approximate prize guarantee
that we use is equivalent to the original question. As an application, we obtain
a polynomial-time algorithm that finds a laminar constantly-thin tree of cost no
higher than the one of the natural linear programming solution, thus obtaining
the first result without cost loss of this type (even for the special case of
chain-constrained trees). Moreover, our framework gives a new perspective on how
to approach the problem of finding globally-thin trees without cost loss.
