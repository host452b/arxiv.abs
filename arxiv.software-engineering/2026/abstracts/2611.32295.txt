Title: Norn: Context-Length-Aware Optimization of Weight and KV Cache Placement for Offloaded LLM Inference
Authors: Naoto Sugiura, Hiroki Matsutani
ID: 2611.32295
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32295

Abstract:
Offloading model weights and the key-value (KV) cache to host memory enables
large language models (LLMs) to run on a single memory-constrained GPU. As the
KV cache grows during decoding, GPU memory requirements and the costs of KV
cache transfers and attention computation change. However, existing offloading
methods generally use static computation assignments and placements of model
weights and the KV cache, preventing them from adapting to these changes and
degrading inference performance. We propose Norn, a context-length-aware
offloaded LLM inference method. Based on offline profiling, Norn co-optimizes
computation assignments and placements at the sublayer level, precomputing
multiple execution plans and their switching points. During decoding, Norn
switches plans as context length increases, overlapping computation and data
transfers without knowing the output length in advance. For prefill, Norn
employs operator-major chunked prefill to avoid repeated weight transfers and
automatically selects the chunk size. Experiments with Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-32B
on two single-GPU systems show up to a 16.8$\times$ speedup in end-to-end
latency over Pipelined Sharding and a 4.2$\times$ speedup over its oracle
variant with known input and output lengths.
