Title: Systematic Detection of Energy Regressions in Software Version Histories
Authors: Jérôme Maquoi, François Bechet, Luís Cruz, Benoît Vanderose, Xavier Devroey
ID: 2610.19895
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.19895

Abstract:
Context: Green software engineering is emerging as a crucial response to the
rising energy impact of information technology, especially in continuous
development. However, challenges remain in devising automated methods to
efficiently identify energy regressions across commits. In particular, little
effort has been put into automatically detecting regressions at the commit level
by identifying statistically significant changes in energy consumption.
Objectives: This paper presents EnergyTrackr, an approach for detecting energy
regressions across multiple commits. Methods: We empirically evaluated
EnergyTrackr on 1,932 commits from five Java projects, assessing its ability to
identify significant energy changes. We also compared a straightforward linear
algorithm that analyzes the entire commit history with a more efficient
dichotomous search algorithm. Results: The evaluation demonstrates the
effectiveness of EnergyTrackr in identifying significant energy changes across
commits. Our findings highlight the efficiency of the dichotomous algorithm in
uncovering overall software energy trends, achieving results comparable to the
linear approach while reducing resource consumption and processing time.
Conclusion: We expect EnergyTrackr to help developers accurately monitor energy
regressions and improvements in their projects and optimize their source code to
reduce software energy consumption.
