Title: A New Formulation of the Elastica Model with Application to Image Inpainting
Authors: Zuoqiang Shi, Xue-Cheng Tai
ID: 2611.00110
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00110

Abstract:
Curves and interfaces play a central role in computer vision and image
processing, and Euler's elastica energy is one of the most successful tools to
model and manipulate them. The elastica energy is usually computed through a
level-set or a phase field representation of the curve, in which the curvature
term makes the energy nonlinear, non-convex and, in the level-set case, non-
differentiable, so that its minimization is notoriously difficult. In this
paper, we propose a new formulation of the elastica model based on a binary
representation of curves. The key ingredient is a very simple formula for the
curvature: if $φ$ is the $\pm1$ binary function associated with a curve $Γ$,
then along $Γ$ the curvature is given by
$-\frac{1}{C_0ε}\left(R_ε*φ\right)+O(ε)$, where $R_ε$ is a radial kernel
supported in a ball of radius $ε$ and $C_0$ is an explicit constant. No
derivative of $φ$ is involved and the convolution is evaluated by FFT. The
resulting energy is a weighted total variation, so that formally the new
formulation is a generalization of the well-known ROF model; a phase field type
model is obtained from the same curvature formula. Combining FFT with the split
Bregman iteration, which for the present problem coincides with an augmented
Lagrangian method (ALM), we obtain efficient algorithms whose subproblems are
either linear systems solved by conjugate gradient or explicit pointwise
formulas. Numerical experiments on the inpainting of binary images are presented
to demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed algorithms.
