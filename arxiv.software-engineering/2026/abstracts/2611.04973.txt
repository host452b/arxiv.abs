Title: Software Design Patterns for Latency Measurements with eBPF in SDN Environments
Authors: Pedro Lucas M. S. Rosa, Bruno L. Dalmazo, Leticia S. Machado, Jéferson Campos Nobre
ID: 2611.04973
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04973

Abstract:
This work proposes, implements, and evaluates a modular architecture based on
software design patterns applied to latency measurement in Software-Defined
Networks (SDN) using the Extended Berkeley Packet Filter (eBPF) and the Express
Data Path (XDP). Unlike approaches that focus solely on measurement precision,
this study examines how design patterns can structure observability systems in
an extensible and maintainable way while preserving data-plane efficiency. The
proposed architecture applies the Observer, Strategy, and Factory Method
patterns to modularize the metric collection and analysis pipeline, allowing
dynamic integration of policies and data sinks. The experimental environment was
implemented with Mininet, Python agents, and eBPF/XDP programs, and was
evaluated in terms of processing time, overhead, and evolution effort. The
results show that the introduction of design patterns shows no clear evidence of
measurable performance degradation, maintaining continuous measurements with a
minimum of 5% overhead. The findings suggest that software engineering practices
may be applied to the construction of eBPF-based monitoring and observability
systems.
