Title: An Experimental Apparatus for Embodied Conversational AI: A Simple Implementation Coupling a Commercial Voice AI with an Avatar
Authors: Hiroshi Hamada, Lin Gu
ID: 2611.30552
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30552

Abstract:
As generative AI becomes integrated into everyday life, understanding how
avatars shape human-AI interaction and the perception of AI as a social partner
is becoming increasingly important. This paper builds a research instrument on
audio-output coupling, which connects the audio output of a generative AI to an
external avatar. There are two implementations: the browser implementation
captures the audio of a tab selected through screen sharing without installing
additional software, and the desktop implementation captures the audio of the
service embedded in the application. Both work with the voice AI as people
ordinarily use it, and as a method neither depends on a particular service. The
implementation reported here, Deskmate, generates speech state, mouth shape, and
expression from low-level features such as voice-band mean amplitude, RMS, peak,
and transients. Because it uses no microphone, speech recognition, or
transcription, Deskmate acquires neither the user's voice nor the content of the
conversation. Records of the conversation remain with the voice AI provider, but
none are kept automatically on the side of Deskmate. Three interchangeable
backends are provided for rendering the avatar: 3D (VRM), 2D (Live2D), and CSS.
Deskmate allows researchers to manipulate avatar presentation as an experimental
condition, without changing the commercial service's interface, voice, or
response generation. Because conversation content is not held fixed, the
estimand is the total effect, including the changes in the participant's speech
and the AI's responses that the presentation brings about. A server edition,
served from the researcher's server for online experiments, is also provided.
The effectiveness of avatar presentation has not yet been evaluated; this paper
reports the design and implementation.
