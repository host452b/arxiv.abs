Title: RuralShield: Super Intelligence-Assisted Security Triage and Serverless Infrastructure for Resource-Constrained Healthcare
Authors: Mohammad Arman Ullah, Asif Ahmed
ID: 2611.17887
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17887

Abstract:
Resource-constrained healthcare providers must connect cloud security controls
to an operational response process. Introducing language-model assessments adds
an authorization problem: a recommendation must be converted into a permitted
action against a specific resource. RuralShield combines a configurable
serverless application foundation with structured model assessment,
deterministic routing, and human-approved containment. Its design separates
workload access, assessment validation, and action authorization so that each
boundary produces inspectable records. We evaluate the implementation through a
frozen 120-hour non-AI workload and separate desktop and AWS AI cohorts. The
workload recorded 999,861 authenticated requests and 999,860 successful
responses, with one HTTP 404 and one missing minute representing 139 scheduled
requests. Median, 95th-percentile, and 99th-percentile Ohio client latencies
were 28.57, 53.01, and 110.77 ms. Eight successful model requests exercised
assessment and reporting in two four-case cohorts. The AWS cohort and five
scripted controls produced nine completed workflows, including two human-
approved Lambda containment cases with verified restoration of the original
concurrency setting. Source-linked, checksum-verified archives preserved the
records before scoped cleanup. The study demonstrates an inspectable integration
of application controls and human-mediated AI response under the evaluated
synthetic conditions.
