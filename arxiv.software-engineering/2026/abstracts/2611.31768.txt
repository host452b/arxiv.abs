Title: Joint Trajectory and Power Optimization for Movable-Antenna-Aided Secure Transmission
Authors: Jiacheng Liao, Fengchao Zhu, Guojie Hu, Ying Zhang, Tong-Xing Zheng
ID: 2611.31768
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31768

Abstract:
This paper investigates joint trajectory and power optimization for movable
antenna (MA)-aided secure transmission, where the MA can transmit during its
movement. Specifically, the average secrecy rate is maximized subject to
movement and power constraints, which couple the MA positions and transmit
powers over time, respectively. To tackle this nonconvex problem, we first
decouple the transmit powers via Lagrangian duality, where the optimal power for
a given trajectory is derived in semi-closed form and shown to depend only on
the current MA position. Accordingly, we decouple the MA positions by
equivalently reformulating the problem on a discretized linear rail in terms of
the farthest grid point and the number of time slots spent at each grid point.
Then, from strong duality of the convex relaxation for each farthest point, we
construct an asymptotically optimal solution in which the MA dwells at no more
than two positions. Finally, a low-complexity dual-based branch-and-bound
algorithm is developed to select the farthest grid point and certify the
optimality gap. Simulation results show that the proposed design outperforms the
benchmark schemes.
