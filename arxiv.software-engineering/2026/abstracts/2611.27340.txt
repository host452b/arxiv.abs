Title: CoGITAtion: Collaborative Gaze-based Interpretable Task-adaptive Representation for Privacy-sensitive Applications
Authors: Mayar Elfares, Yasmine Elfares, Andreas Bulling
ID: 2611.27340
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27340

Abstract:
Gaze-based analysis provides rich behavioural information for privacy-sensitive
applications, but deployment is challenged by heterogeneous, scarce, and
distributed data. We present CoGITAtion (Collaborative Gaze-based Interpretable
Task-Adaptive Representation), a framework for privacy-preserving inference over
vertically distributed gaze data. CoGITAtion transforms variable-length gaze
recordings into compact, interpretable gaze-transition graphs and descriptors
computed locally by each data owner. Task-adaptive models accommodate
heterogeneous and missing feature blocks through task and availability gating,
avoiding unnecessary private computation. Results show that shared components
capture patterns that generalise across tasks, while task-specific components
provide capacity without increasing model size. Public availability gating
further reduces encrypted computation by removing branches determined by
publicly known task and owner availability without altering predictions.
Overall, CoGITAtion enables gaze model reuse, efficient privacy-preserving
deployment, and robust inference under incomplete owner availability while
retaining interpretable gaze representations.
