Title: AI Policies: Help or Hindrance? A Software Developer's Perspective
Authors: Samuel Ferino, Rashina Hoda, John Grundy, Christoph Treude, Hashini Gunatilake
ID: 2609.16496
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.16496

Abstract:
AI policies introduced by software organisations to mitigate LLM-related risks
such as sensitive information leaks and unauthorised usage are not useful if
software developers do not engage with them. We draw on 19 software developer
interviews to show how AI policies help and hinder developers. We suggest
approaches to support managers and decision makers with a developer-centric
approach to introducing AI policies.
