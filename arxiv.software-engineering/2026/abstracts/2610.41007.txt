Title: Adaptive Dynamic Security for Operational Technology Against AI-Powered Attacks
Authors: Mohamed Chahine Ghanem
ID: 2610.41007
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41007

Abstract:
Operational technology now faces attackers whose tempo is set by AI. We present
ADS-OT, an adaptive dynamic security framework modelling defensive posture as a
partially observable decision problem. A reinforcement-learning policy adapts
monitoring, access, deception, and segmentation under a safety-governance layer,
reducing attack-path completion, detection, and containment versus static
baselines.
