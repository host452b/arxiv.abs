Title: Formal Specifications with Data-Driven Signal Analysis
Authors: Roderick Bloem, Benedikt Maderbacher, Cristinel Mateis, Dejan Ničković, Sergii Tkachov, Björn Ziegler
ID: 2610.39007
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39007

Abstract:
Developing analog and hybrid systems requires substantial domain knowledge that
is hard to articulate in a formal specification; without additional effort, this
domain knowledge is also inaccessible to machine learning methods. We propose a
hybrid approach for analyzing and monitoring real-valued signals that integrates
specification-based and data-driven techniques. We use regular expressions with
interval operations to identify regions of interest in a signal, classical
signal processing methods to extract semantically meaningful features, and semi-
supervised machine learning to infer complex relationships that distinguish
correct from faulty behaviors. The relationships learned from normal behavior
data can automatically detect incorrect behavior in the same system or variants
thereof. We evaluate the method on a voltage regulator, demonstrating its
effectiveness in detecting undesired system behaviors.
