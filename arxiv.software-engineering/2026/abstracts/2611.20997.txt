Title: OpenMAS-GApp: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Domain-Adaptive Multi-Agent Application Construction
Authors: Xunkai Li, Kairui Yang, Jiaxi Li, Yuecong Mao, Zekai Chen, Xinle Zhang, Rong-Hua Li, Guoren Wang
ID: 2611.20997
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20997

Abstract:
Constructing multi-agent system (MAS) applications across domains requires
translating diverse requirements into executable systems, involving task
decomposition, agent and tool selection, and execution organization. Existing
studies have explored individual aspects of MAS construction, including agent
generation, architecture design, workflow generation, and component selection.
However, these methods are often developed and evaluated independently, using
different task specifications, component resources, and execution settings. This
makes it difficult to compare their construction capabilities or determine how
they can be applied to different domains. To address this problem, we propose
OpenMAS-GApp, a One-for-All benchmark for MAS application construction across
diverse domains. It standardizes task inputs, component resources, construction
interfaces, and execution environments to support MAS construction under
consistent conditions. Within OpenMAS-GApp, we provide Graph Harness as a
reference construction method that derives task dependencies, selects compatible
agents and tools, and organizes their interactions into executable graphs. We
evaluate construction methods across 19 benchmark datasets under 20 evaluation
settings spanning four application types and four professional domains.
