Title: Handoff at Frontier AI Companies
Authors: Matteo Pistillo, Isaak Mengesha, Charlotte Stix, Annika Hallensleben, Alejandro Ortega, Marius Hobbhahn, Alexander Meinke
ID: 2611.30711
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30711

Abstract:
The term "Handoff" captures frontier AI companies' ongoing delegation of all AI
Research & Development (AI R&D) functions, including high-level decisions such
as safety assessments and deployment decisions, to internally deployed AI
agents. Handoff is progressing rapidly and will be complete once no human
remains in the loop. This report aims to alert policymakers to the national
security threats and Loss of Control risks that Handoff can create, and to
advise them on how to monitor and respond to those risks.
