Title: When Single Agent Is Enough: Rethinking Multi-Agent Systems for Automated Code Review
Authors: Jiawei Li, Hoa Khanh Dam, Iftekhar Ahmed
ID: 2611.23283
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23283

Abstract:
Code review plays a critical role in identifying software quality issues and
ensuring system robustness. Prior work has proposed techniques for Review
Comment Generation (RCG) and Code Refinement (CR), with recent Multi-Agent (MA)
approaches achieving state-of-the-art results by coordinating agents specialized
in different quality dimensions. However, MA systems introduce substantial
architectural complexity and runtime overhead, and recent evidence suggests they
may underperform Single-Agent (SA) approaches in certain tasks. In this work, we
systematically compare MA and SA designs for RCG and CR. Surprisingly, our
results show that SA approaches perform comparably to- and sometimes outperform-
MA systems, despite their simpler design. These findings challenge the
prevailing assumption that MA architectures are the default choice for complex
software engineering tasks.
