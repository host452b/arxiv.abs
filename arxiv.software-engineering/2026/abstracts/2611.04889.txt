Title: NP-Hardness and a PTAS for Maximum Interval Multicover, with Applications to Dense Subgraphs
Authors: Morteza Alimi, Tobias Mömke, Alexandru Popa, Michael Ruderer
ID: 2611.04889
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04889

Abstract:
Given intervals and points on a line, each point with a demand, and a budget
$K$, Maximum Interval Multicover asks for at most $K$ intervals that satisfy as
many points as possible, a point being satisfied if it lies in at least as many
chosen intervals as its demand. One application is committee elections in which
each candidate is approved by consecutive voters and voters have individual
demands. Xu, Ran and Zhang introduced the problem and gave a
$1/2$-approximation; the objective is not submodular when demands exceed one.
Li, Tu, Zhang and Cao gave a polynomial-time approximation scheme (PTAS) for the
minimization counterpart, unweighted partial interval multicover, and asked for
its complexity. We prove that both problems are NP-hard, even for intervals of
equal length; as their optima are integers bounded by the input size, neither
has a fully polynomial-time approximation scheme unless
$\mathrm{P}=\mathrm{NP}$. We then give a PTAS for Maximum Interval Multicover,
also for arbitrary nonnegative nondecreasing rewards for the coverage of each
point. Building on a decomposition from Nonner's PTAS for Densest $k$-Subgraph
on interval graphs, our scheme cuts the line into pieces at a small loss and
solves every piece exactly. The key dynamic program handles intervals crossing a
common point by choosing an interleaving of their left and right endpoints
rather than sweeping along the line; it needs only two counters to track these
intervals. The same algorithm yields PTASes on convex bipartite graphs for
Bipartite Dense Subgraph, improving on the ratio $2/3$ of Backer and Keil for
the subclass of bipartite permutation graphs, and on interval graphs for Maximum
$k$-Vertex Cover and weighted clique counts.
