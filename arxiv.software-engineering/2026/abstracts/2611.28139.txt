Title: Dynamics-Guided Conditional MeanFlow from Regional Interaction to Internal Revision and Its Application to CT Imaging
Authors: Md Shifatul Ahsan Apurba, Md Selim, Jin Chen
ID: 2611.28139
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28139

Abstract:
Conditional image enhancement requires spatially varying corrections, yet fixed-
depth models allocate similar computational budgets regardless of where
additional refinement is needed. Although one-step flow models reduce inference
costs and iterative restoration methods introduce subsequent corrections, how
predicted transformation dynamics can guide internal feature refinement remains
underexplored. We introduce IRCMF, a dynamics-guided Conditional MeanFlow
framework that combines regional interaction with recurrent internal revision.
The decoder predicts signed interval velocity and a learned velocity-change cue.
A soft-region graph aggregates contextual information for velocity estimation,
while a dynamics-conditioned graph establishes regional relationships to guide
recurrent bottleneck updates. The revised features are processed by the shared
decoder to recompute velocity, establishing feedback between transformation
dynamics and internal representations. An actor-critic policy controls regional
updates and further revisions to balance enhancement quality and computational
cost. We evaluate IRCMF on two computed tomography (CT) datasets, NLST for
STANDARD-to-LUNG kernel enhancement and LDCT-and-Projection-data for simulated
low-to-routine-dose enhancement. With nine decoder evaluations, IRCMF achieves
slice-level PSNR of $36.24 \pm 3.49$ dB and SSIM of $0.97 \pm 0.01$ on NLST, and
PSNR of $32.45 \pm 1.97$ dB and SSIM of $0.84 \pm 0.05$ on LDCT-and-Projection-
data. The learned policy reduces average decoder evaluations to 4.42 and 3.45,
respectively, lowering per-slice latency from 97.00 to 51.82 ms and from 97.12
to 41.95 ms, with PSNR reductions of only 0.20 and 0.06 dB.
