Title: Wreath products of distributive quasi relation algebra frames and their application to representability
Authors: Wilmari Morton, Claudette Robinson
ID: 2610.38984
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38984

Abstract:
We adapt a construction by Comer (1983) for polygroups to define the wreath
product of the dual frames of two distributive quasi relation algebras
satisfying certain conditions and show that it is itself a frame. Frames play a
role analogous to that of atom structures for relation algebras. After
introducing the notion of a frame network, we establish a correspondence between
frame networks satisfying certain conditions and representability of finite
distributive quasi relation algebras. Next we describe a frame network for the
wreath product of two finite representable distributive quasi relation algebras
that satisfies the aforementioned conditions, as well as additional conditions,
and use this to conclude that the complex algebra of the wreath product is a
representable distributive quasi relation algebra. Thus, our construction
provides a method for obtaining representable DqRAs from certain finite DqRAs
that are already known to be representable.
