Title: Procrustes-Bering-Sheaft: Automated Model Discovery and Virtual Crash Tests for Microservices
Authors: Anatoly A. Krasnovsky
ID: 2610.31444
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31444

Abstract:
Procrustes-Bering-Sheaft automates lightweight model discovery and virtual crash
tests for microservices. Each test estimates operation availability by Monte
Carlo sampling of service and communication failures. Procrustes checks
deployment and tracing evidence; Bering discovers dependencies and incorporates
replica declarations; Sheaft evaluates declared operation-success conditions.
Independent command-line interfaces exchange reusable, versioned artifacts. The
toolchain extends an earlier graph-based approach with mandatory-call contracts,
failure-tolerance sweeps, baseline comparison, component-importance analysis,
and CI/CD gates. We demonstrate the workflow on one HTTP operation. Six
controlled execution states exposed two mismatches between reachability and
observed success; an explicit required-call predicate matched all six. Two
modelled one-replica additions produced different decisions: recommendation
retained an availability-target violation, whereas catalog restored the baseline
estimate of 0.979900 and grid tolerance of 0.12. Analytical oracles and
controlled tests verify the implementation; archived replay checks numerical
continuity with two earlier studies.
