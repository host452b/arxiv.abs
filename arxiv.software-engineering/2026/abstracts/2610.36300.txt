Title: Building Mathematical Software -- Lessons from the Development of GAP
Authors: Alexander Hulpke
ID: 2610.36300
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36300

Abstract:
Mathematical software has particular needs that are not all covered by the
existing literature on software engineering or algorithm design. This paper
looks at some of these aspects, in the concrete context of the computer algebra
system GAP. It also studies the potential impact AI systems could have on the
development process for mathematical software.
