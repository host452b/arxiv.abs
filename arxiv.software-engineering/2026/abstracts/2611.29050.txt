Title: Certified Comparative Statics for Conservation-Law Equilibrium Layers, with an Application to RNA Interference
Authors: Shadi Khodagholi, Sara Karimi, Niloofar Yousefi
ID: 2611.29050
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29050

Abstract:
Implicit and equilibrium neural layers define their output as the fixed point of
a map. Existing theory certifies that this fixed point exists; it does not
certify how it responds to its inputs. For a conservation-law layer with order-
preserving coupling we give a form-independent answer: every input-output
comparative static is sign-certified if and only if the residual Jacobian is a
nonsingular M-matrix, and the certified sign depends only on the sign of the
occupancy derivative, so one guarantee covers Langmuir, Hill, and saturating-
exponential occupancies. The M-matrix machinery is classical; the contribution
is form-independence as the portable invariant, the first certified-sign
sensitivity result for a layer of this class, and a sharp phase boundary at
which certification is lost while the equilibrium remains well posed. Three
consequences follow: the phase boundary, which is strictly stronger than the
M-matrix condition; competitive derepression, whose derivative is identically
zero for any independent per-target scorer and provably nonzero here; and non-
submodularity of resource-coupled suppression. We instantiate on siRNA off-
target competition and validate on real public data only, across seventeen
microRNAs, two titration studies, and a paired chemical-modification experiment;
a second, non-biological instantiation shows the same certified sign on measured
physical data. As a trainable layer the operator learns end-to-end while
remaining certified-monotone by construction, reaching magnitude parity with a
parameter-matched feedforward baseline while expressing a competition effect
that baseline cannot represent for any weights. The single-pool instantiation
validates the mechanism but does not rank off-target magnitude or resolve
position-specific design. Code: https://github.com/shadi97kh/certified-
equilibrium-layers
