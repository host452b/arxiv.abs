Title: CSI-Agnostic End-to-End Neural Decoding of LDPC Codes under Synchronization Errors
Authors: Yi Kuang, Shu Liu, Ningning Wang
ID: 2611.30037
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30037

Abstract:
Correcting synchronization errors (insertions, deletions, and substitutions) in
concatenated coding schemes, typically comprising an inner marker code and an
outer Low-Density Parity-Check (LDPC) code, poses a significant challenge.
Traditional algebraic decoding fails entirely under sequence misalignment. While
recent neural pipelines attempt to resolve this, they typically demand exact
channel state information (CSI) for inner synchronization and suffer from error
propagation caused by premature syndrome binarization before outer decoding. In
this paper, we propose ASDecoder, a data-driven, CSI-agnostic end-to-end neural
decoding architecture specifically designed for LDPC codes over insertion-
deletion-substitution (IDS) channels. ASDecoder features a CNN-Transformer
front-end that implicitly aligns variable-length sequences and fuses multi-read
redundancies without requiring explicit CSI. Tightly integrated into this
pipeline is a novel \emph{syndrome-free} BiDecoder back-end. By mapping
continuous soft features directly into a sparse-masked graph attention network,
it strictly avoids intermediate hard decisions and effectively captures
topological constraints. Experiments demonstrate that a single universal
ASDecoder outperforms the BCJRFormer baseline, providing consistent error
correction across highly asymmetric channels. Furthermore, it generalizes
robustly to real-world deployment scenarios, including the industrial CCSDS
$(128,64)$ QC-LDPC standard and non-linear DNA biological constraints.
