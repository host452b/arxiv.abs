Title: Patterns for Designing CI/CD Workflows
Authors: Leandro Sousa, Filipe Correia
ID: 2611.17276
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17276

Abstract:
CI/CD pipelines are difficult to build and maintain, and information about them
is scattered across empirical studies, practitioner books, and catalogues of
'smells'. We collected candidate practices and anti-practices converted them
into patterns, and then consolidated these into patterns. These patterns address
issues such as security, pipeline scheduling, workflow structure, performance
and failure and release management. Each pattern is contextualised and describes
the problem it solves, the forces at play, the solution and the consequences.
