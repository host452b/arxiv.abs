Title: Enhancing Metaverse Security using Explainable AI-based Intrusion Detection Framework
Authors: Esha Kuppanda, Shalaka Mahadik, Pranav M. Pawar, Mithun Mukherjee, Raja Muthalagu
ID: 2611.27199
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27199

Abstract:
The MoT virtual environment, called the Metaverse of Things (MoT), combines
machine learning (ML) and Internet of Things (IoT) to offer significant business
opportunities in various sectors such as entertainment, healthcare, and
education. In addition to its benefits, the MoT poses risks to privacy and
security through unauthorized access, which threatens trustworthiness. While
traditional intrusion detection systems (IDS) exist to handle this challenge,
they struggle with high-dimensional data and poor interpretability. To address
this challenge, the research conducted a thorough analysis of different ML
models and proposed the optimal model to secure the MoT environment. The
research employed explainable artificial intelligence (XAI) to identify the most
effective features that contributed to optimal model selection. The research
presented an optimal framework combining ML and XAI to safeguard MoT networks
from the most recent cyberthreats. The research used three advanced benchmark
security datasets: ToN-IoT, CIC-IoT-2023, and BoT-IoT, to examine the XAI-based
intrusion detection framework. The XAI-based intrusion detection framework
achieved 99.83% accuracy on ToN-IoT and 96.45% on CIC-IoT-2023 using the XGBoost
model. On the BoT-IoT dataset, the Random Forest (RF) and Extra Trees (ET)
classifiers reached 99.99% accuracy. The Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP)
and Local Interpretable Model-Agnostic Explanations (LIME) techniques used for
XAI further enhanced feature relevance and user confidence in the optimal model.
