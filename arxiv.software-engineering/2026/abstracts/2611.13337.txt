Title: VC-Dimension and Generalization in Geometric Machine Learning with Applications to Active Learning
Authors: Gokul Bhusal, Delanna Do, Ekaterina Merkurjev, Kevin Miller, Stephan Wojtowytsch
ID: 2611.13337
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13337

Abstract:
We compute the VC-dimension of metric ball classifiers in the model spaces of
Riemannian geometry (Euclidean, spherical, hyperbolic) and present upper and
lower bounds on the VC-dimension of metric balls in several other situations,
including general normed vector spaces (potentially infinite VC-dimension),
vector spaces with $\ell^p$-norms (VC-dimension grows at least linearly in the
dimension, faster than Euclidean for $p=1$), flat tori, and general Riemannian
manifolds (arbitrarily large VC-dimension after a conformal change). In several
situations where the VC-dimension is large or even infinite, we prove
generalization bounds under additional assumptions on the data distribution
using covering arguments. Both in the VC-dimension and covering setting, we
consider data on low-dimensional subsets of high-dimensional Euclidean spaces.
As an application, we consider coverage-based algorithms in active learning.
