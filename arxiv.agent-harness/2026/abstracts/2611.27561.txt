Title: Exploring the Potential of Ultra-Low-Bit Inference on LLM Accelerators
Authors: Hao Wang, Yang Shi, Jiageng Shi, Qiuran Li, Yaohua Wang
ID: 2611.27561
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27561

Abstract:
Vector quantization (VQ) enables ultra-low-bit compression of large language
models (LLMs) by encoding groups of values as compact codebook indexes. However,
reconstructing these values for computation requires frequent codebook lookups,
which conflict with the dataflows of conventional LLM accelerators and limit the
performance gains from VQ compression. Consequently, prior work has resorted to
VQ-specific accelerator designs, which either incur substantial additional
hardware overhead or support only a limited range of inference scenarios. In
this work, we argue that existing systolic-array-based LLM accelerators already
possess the essential hardware resources for efficient VQ inference, but lack
the mechanisms to effectively exploit them. To bridge this gap, we present
VQACC, an architectural enhancement that jointly redesigns the existing operand
buffer's access logic and data mapping to reuse its storage and port resources
for codebook lookups, seamlessly integrating dequantization into the normal
operand-addressing dataflow. Evaluation shows that VQACC achieves a 2.63$\times$
speedup over the EVA baseline, with additional hardware occupying only 0.17\% of
the compute array's area.
