Title: Boltz-Flow: Generating High-Quality Robot Action from Boltzmann Machine
Authors: Hengzhi Jiang, Haixu Liu, Jindou Jia, Tinglan Wang, Shaobo Chen, Yiren Zhao, Ziyang Rao, Laiyuan Cui, Jiaxin Dong, Yin Ma et al.
ID: 2611.31038
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31038

Abstract:
Flow-matching policies integrate a velocity field from a fixed, observation-
independent Gaussian source. However, since the source is unimodal and identical
across all tasks, the limited representational capacity of Gaussian
distributions constrains how far a policy can scale with growing task diversity.
Moreover, the source factorizes over time steps and control dimensions,
discarding the coordination that manipulation requires. We introduce Boltz-Flow,
which replaces Gaussian initialization with a more powerful, observation- and
instruction-conditioned Boltzmann source while leaving the base VLA unchanged.
Boltz-Flow parameterizes this source with a conditional Boltzmann machine that
assigns an energy value to each binary source state. A listwise objective,
supervised entirely by offline demonstrations, aligns this energy with the
quality of actions produced after frozen flow integration. At inference, the
energy defines an Ising instance whose solution is decoded into continuous noise
before a single standard integration. We use a physical coherent Ising machine
during training. Experiments show that Boltz-Flow improves $π_{0.5}$ across
three benchmarks, outperforming the compared strong policy baselines and
competing noise optimization methods on LIBERO and RoboTwin 2.0 and ranking
second only to LEAP on LIBERO-Plus. Additional experiments show gains under
noise-adapted fine-tuning, with action-to-action warm starts, and on the SmolVLA
backbone.
