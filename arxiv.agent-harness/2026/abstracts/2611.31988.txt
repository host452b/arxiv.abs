Title: Describe, Don't Decide: Correcting Answer Bias in Multimodal LLMs
Authors: Shuwei Wu, Longin Jan Latecki
ID: 2611.31988
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31988

Abstract:
Comparing two entities in an image, multimodal LLMs (MLLMs) often answer
wrongly. We find that the answer bias enters not when the model sees, but when
it decides with the image in view: the model's own descriptions of the two
entities often already hold the right answer. We therefore propose D$^3$
(Describe, Don't Decide): the model describes each entity alone, decides from
these descriptions without the image, and merges this decision with its direct
answer. Without any training, D$^3$ improves all 79 model-benchmark pairs across
ten benchmarks and eight 7-9B MLLMs. On held-out GQA comparison questions, the
mean accuracy of these models rises from 72.4% to 83.2% (up to +21.5% per
model), higher than that of Qwen2.5-VL-32B answering directly (82.8%). Code will
be available at https://github.com/paul-swu/describe-dont-decide.
