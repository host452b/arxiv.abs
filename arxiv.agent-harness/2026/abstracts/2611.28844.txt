Title: AgriManager: A Framework and Benchmark for LLM-RL Generalization in Agricultural Management
Authors: Chi Gui, Junwen Zheng, Ritwik Nigam, Qianlan Yang, Meagan Lang, Vardhan Dongre, Jeremiah Barr, Yu-Xiong Wang, Vikram S. Adve
ID: 2611.28844
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28844

Abstract:
Agricultural management is heterogeneous along two axes. The first is
distributional shift within a fixed task interface: weather, crops, and prices
vary across years and locations while sensors, actions, and objective stay
fixed. The second is structural shift across interfaces: different farms expose
different observed variables, available actions, and management objectives. We
formalize the second axis as schema shift: variation in the policy-facing schema
$Σ= (Σ_O, Σ_A, Σ_R)$ between training and deployment. We test whether LLM-RL
generalizes across both axes with AgriManager, to our knowledge the first
framework that trains LLM policies with RL across multiple process-based crop
simulators, unifying DSSAT, WOFOST, and Cycles. We design a three-tier
generalization ladder: Tier 1 within-schema distributional shift, Tier 2 single-
axis schema shift along $Σ_O / Σ_A / Σ_R$, Tier 3 compositional schema shift
across simulators, all scored on a frozen held-out test suite with three
training seeds per method. Within fixed schemas, Qwen3-4B trained by RL with
explicit reasoning exceeds neural-network PPO trained with the same interaction
budget; without reasoning, training stalls near no-action where the reward
penalizes input use. Under single-axis schema shift, $Σ_O$ transfer needs
decision-critical state, $Σ_A$ results are consistent with joint action-menu
coverage, and with reasoning a policy trained on yield alone transfers to unseen
objectives. Under compositional schema shift, single-source training mostly
hurts zero-shot transfer across simulators, and a unified policy trained only on
other combinations of the same components recovers about 86% of the gain of a
specialist trained on the target combination on DSSAT and stays below the
specialists on the WOFOST and Cycles profit targets. Project page:
https://agrimanager-framework.github.io
