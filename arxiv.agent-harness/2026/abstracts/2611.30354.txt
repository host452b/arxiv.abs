Title: STAR: Structure-Aware Temporal Action Recovery for Point Supervised Temporal Action Localization
Authors: Yawar Rehman, Min Young Kim
ID: 2611.30354
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30354

Abstract:
Point supervised temporal action localization is becoming the need of time in
this era of ever growing and non-stop video data. Existing method mainly rely on
snippet level action response to generate pseudo labels. However, these labels
often suffer from boundary ambiguity, over extended proposals, and weak temporal
structure. To address these issues, we propose STAR, a structure-aware Temporal
Action Recovery framework for point supervised temporal action localization.
STAR works by enhancing the base S model with temporal difference interaction
with attention module to capture forward and backward snippet changes, and
introduces the learning of start, core, and end roles to model the internal
temporal structure of actions. In addition, STAR further improves the pseudo
label generation through All point proposal ranking and Rolebank based
structural re-ranking, which suppress neighbor crossing and structurally
inconsistent proposals. Finally, temporal action detector to STAR feedback
supervision transfers high quality detector prediction back to the STAR model
through soft start, core, end, action, and background targets. This helps to
further refine the pseudo labels. Extensive experiments on four standard
benchmarks shows the effectiveness of STAR. By using I3D features, STAR achieves
67.2% at AVG@0.1:0.7 on THUMOS'14, 58.1% on GTEA, 74.6% on BEOID, and 31.7% at
AVG@0.5:0.95 on ActivityNet-1.3, outperforming previous point supervised methods
across multiple benchmarks.
