Title: PseuDRAGON: Knowledge-Guided Local LLMs for Financial-Data Pseudonymization in Closed Networks
Authors: Gwangui Hong, Seonho An, Seonkyu Lim, KyungTae Lim, Min-Soo Kim
ID: 2611.30353
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30353

Abstract:
Staff pseudonymizing financial data in closed networks must interpret
abbreviated column names, identify personal information, and choose processing
methods. Local language models can assist, but may give different labels for the
same input. PseuDRAGON keeps confirmed judgments and corrections outside a fixed
model and reuses them through exact-name lookup and learned rules. For expert
review, the workflow searches institutional policy, national privacy law, then
international sources. Across 92 columns from one institution, rules increase
reuse under the same approval checks from 75 to 83 labels without reuse errors
when all training judgments are available. With 25% of the training judgments,
withholding non-PII rules for unseen names eliminated the extra PII misses in
replay at the cost of lower reuse. Revising or withdrawing approvals rebuilds
derived rules, and tests on synthetic records verify that selected methods
produce the specified outputs. In three paired runs, the history-first baseline
cut automated identification time by 83.0% on average versus no reuse.
