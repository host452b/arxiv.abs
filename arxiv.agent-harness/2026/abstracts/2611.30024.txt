Title: FlexWAM: Learning Flexible Visual Routing for World Action Models
Authors: Mingyu Mei, Haojie Xu, Zibo Dai, Hongjie Fang, Shirun Tang, Zhengrui Lv, Yang Liu, Chen Guang, Xingyue Zhao, Huiliang Shen et al.
ID: 2611.30024
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30024

Abstract:
Humans dynamically adjust their reliance on sensory modalities across
manipulation stages. Although existing World Action Models (WAMs) fuse multi-
camera observations, they struggle to adapt their reliance on individual views
based on the current observations. This raises a central question: how can the
action expert integrate complementary visual evidence while prioritizing the
views most relevant to the current action? FlexWAM addresses this challenge
through three modules. First, we alternate within-view and cross-view attention
to capture view-specific dynamics and complementary cues. For action prediction,
View-Routed Action Attention uses an observation-conditioned router to
prioritize relevant views. To calibrate routing, we train a critic using
counterfactual view credit, estimated from action loss sensitivity upon
suppressing each view's contribution to action attention. We evaluate FlexWAM
with two video backbones on five challenging real-world manipulation tasks
across two robot platforms. The two variants improve mean task success by 16.4
and 23.0 percentage points over the strongest baseline. Project website:
https://flexwam.github.io/.
