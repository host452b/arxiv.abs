Title: PromptSMC: Enabling Particle Exploration in Flow Matching via LLM-Based Prompt Rejuvenation
Authors: Wenyi Lian, Zheng Zhao, Thomas B. Schön
ID: 2611.31471
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31471

Abstract:
This paper is concerned with prompt-image alignment at inference time through
exploration of generative trajectories and dynamical prompt rejuvenation. We
leverage sequential Monte Carlo (SMC), to offer a gradient-free and principled
framework for this purpose through reward-guided trajectory selection. More
specifically, we introduce LLM-based prompt rejuvenation, allowing for efficient
particle exploration in SMC samplers along the diffusion time in flow matching
models. The proposed method, PromptSMC, retains the selected latent states and
uses a pretrained LLM to dynamically revise the prompts of duplicated particles
under semantic-preservation constraints. These revised prompts induce
alternative conditional velocity fields, allowing particles to explore different
continuations from a shared intermediate state. The method further uses a reward
model to evaluate each particle's alignment with the original prompt, and the
resulting scores guide particles through resampling. PromptSMC thus integrates
prompt-based exploration with reward-guided sampling, achieving faithful prompt-
image alignment without additional noise injection, model training, or reward
gradients. Our experiments and ablation studies across multiple benchmarks and
backbones demonstrate the effectiveness of the method for text-to-image flow
matching. Code is available at https://github.com/shermanlian/PromptSMC.
