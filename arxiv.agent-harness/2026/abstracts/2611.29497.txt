Title: Gossamer: A Utility-Driven Architecture for Constant-Budget KV Cache Compression in Reasoning LLMs
Authors: Feng Cheng, Cong Guo, Junyao Zhang, Haoxuan Shan, Chiyue Wei, Hong Wang, Hai Li, Yiran Chen
ID: 2611.29497
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29497

Abstract:
Transformer-based large language models (LLMs) are increasingly applied to
complex reasoning tasks, yet their inference efficiency is fundamentally
constrained by the rapid growth of the KV cache. As reasoning contexts extend to
hundreds of thousands of tokens, the key-value (KV) cache becomes the dominant
contributor to both memory footprint and bandwidth demand, tightly coupling
computation with memory utilization and thereby limiting scalability. Existing
compression methods, such as quantization and eviction, alleviate capacity
pressure but remain purely algorithmic, often introducing additional passes and
compute overhead that reduce system efficiency. This paper presents Gossamer, a
KV cache compression architecture for reasoning LLMs that bridges the algorithm
--system--hardware gap. Gossamer introduces a unified utility-based action
framework that dynamically selects compression operations, quantization,
merging, or eviction, under a constant KV cache budget. Through cross-domain co-
design, we fuse cosine-similarity estimation directly into the attention
computation, exploiting idle compute cycles to perform similarity evaluation
without latency or bandwidth cost. We call this mechanism Free-Lunch
Utilization. This enables real-time, fine-grained KV management with near-
constant memory usage and negligible overhead. Under tight memory constraints,
Gossamer sustains reasoning quality comparable to uncompressed inference while
improving end-to-end throughput by up to 16.7$\times$ over uncompressed
baselines, 5.5$\times$ and 4.9$\times$ over the quantization-based Oaken and
Ecco, and 1.8$\times$ over the eviction-based R-KV.
