Title: AgentBank: Banking on Blockchain Agentic Payments
Authors: Yuexin Xiang, Martin Perešíni, Yuchen Lei, Yuquan Li, Yuanzhe Zhang, Andreas Deppeler, Tsz Hon Yuen, Shiping Chen, Qin Wang, Burkhard Stiller
ID: 2611.32389
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32389

Abstract:
AI agents increasingly pay for data and computation through protocols such as
x402 and AP2, but individually funded wallets idle and require asset transfers
for every payment. We present AgentBank, a smart-contract banking layer that
lets agents spend bank liabilities under owner-issued mandates and on-chain
spending limits. Internal payments update account balances without transferring
assets, while pooled backing funds are allocated between reserves and yield-
bearing strategies. We bound losses from compromised agent keys and analyze how
correlated demand affects reserve requirements. Experiments show that AgentBank
reduces idle liquidity by 52-77% relative to per-agent wallets and lowers gas
costs for batched internal payments. Using historical DeFi liquidity data, we
evaluate how reserve policies balance treasury returns against payment
availability during market stress. We further examine the risks of automated
bank runs and evaluate mechanisms to mitigate them.
