Title: OVOGO: Offline Value-Guided On-Policy Generative Optimization for Flow-Based Vision-Language-Action Models
Authors: Rongzheng Yang, Jiaming Guo, Yuxuan Guo, Zihao Zhang, Yunkai Gao, Siming Lan, Shaohui Peng, Rui Zhang, Tianyun Ma, Xing Hu et al.
ID: 2611.30477
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30477

Abstract:
Flow-based vision-language-action models can benefit from robot experience
collected during deployment, but repeatedly acquiring new physical interactions
for policy improvement is costly. Offline reinforcement learning provides a
natural way to reuse collected experience, while flow-based policies offer an
additional opportunity because fresh generation trajectories can be cheaply
resampled from the current policy at replay observations. This motivates
separating offline value learning from on-policy optimization of the generative
process. We propose OVOGO, an offline reinforcement learning framework that
realizes this separation for flow-based VLAs. A central challenge in this
formulation is to learn a critic that remains grounded in logged robot
experience while providing useful value signals for newly generated actions.
OVOGO introduces a Return-Anchored Ranking Critic that combines in-sample
distributional value learning with guarded local ranking, improving
discrimination among generated actions without assigning them artificial return
targets. The frozen critic then provides group-relative value signals for
selected-transition optimization of fresh action candidates sampled from the
current flow policy. Experiments on RoboTwin 2.0, DexJoCo, and LIBERO, as well
as a real-robot dexterous manipulation task, show that OVOGO improves the
initial VLA across all four settings and achieves higher success rates than the
evaluated offline adaptation and value-guided baselines. These results suggest
that offline robot experience can be repeatedly reused for value learning while
inexpensive generative sampling supports policy optimization, providing an
effective approach to experience-based post-training of flow-based VLAs.
