Title: Proper and Optimal Scoring Rules for Risk-Averse Agents
Authors: Ying Liu, Raimundo Saona
ID: 2611.30563
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30563

Abstract:
Scoring rules are used to elicit probabilistic beliefs through payments that
depend on reports and realised outcomes. We ask how these rules should be
designed to induce truthful reporting and incentivise costly information
acquisition when the agent is risk averse. For agents with entropic risk
preferences in finite state spaces, we characterise proper scoring rules through
an exponential transformation, derive optimal structures for information
acquisition under bounded payments, and obtain explicit optimal coefficients in
the binary case. The risk-neutral limit preserves properness and, in the binary
case, optimality, while recovering the optimal betting structure for nonbinary
states. For optimal design in the binary model, it suffices to focus on rules
whose associated concave function is affine on each side of the prior. The
optimal coefficients depend only on the prior and the risk-aversion parameter.
The binary optimal rules we construct also admit an exact transformation from a
particular risk-neutral optimal rule. In the nonbinary model, optimal design
reduces to choosing the best prior bound in a transformed betting mechanism.
