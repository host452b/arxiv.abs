Title: STEGOGUARD-LM: Evaluating End-to-End Protection Against LLM Covert Channels
Authors: Martha Dimgba, George Williams
ID: 2611.28152
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28152

Abstract:
A defense that disrupts hidden messages may still provide little protection if
its detector rarely triggers an intervention. We present StegoGuard-LM, a
controlled framework for evaluating defenses against covert messages in large
language model outputs. We examine token-rank and synonym channels, with Unicode
concealment as a structural control, and track messages through detection,
intervention, and recovery. Deterministic rewriting prevented full recovery of
103 of 104 synonym-encoded messages when applied unconditionally, but only one
when intervention depended on a detector set to limit false alarms. In a four-
round replay on a 48-response subset, an attacker selecting from a fixed catalog
recovered more bits by switching channels, although paired gains were not
statistically significant after correction for multiple comparisons. Randomized
rewriting showed no sustained advantage in this pilot. A more sensitive detector
improved protection but modified about one-third of clean responses. These
findings show how uneven detection can undermine effective rewriting and why
protection must be evaluated alongside attacker adaptation and intervention on
legitimate text.
