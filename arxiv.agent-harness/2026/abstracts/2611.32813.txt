Title: Atomic Policy: Composing Atomic Manipulation Skills with Agentic Reasoning
Authors: Kaidi Zhang, Pengyuan Guo, Zhengtong Xu, Caiyi Zhang, Huayi Gou, Guanlin Huang, Aaron Lim, Anas Rafei, Yu She
ID: 2611.32813
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32813

Abstract:
Long-horizon manipulation requires robots to translate task intent into physical
interactions as the scene changes. Vision-language models (VLMs) offer semantic
and spatial reasoning, while visuomotor policies provide fine-grained control.
Yet ambiguous goals and dependencies between actions make their integration
challenging. We introduce Atomic Policy, a framework that composes learned
manipulation skills through grounded steering and execution feedback. A VLM
estimates the symbolic state and goal, and a PDDL solver constructs a skill
plan. An agentic harness steers each skill through language, visual pointing, or
both, and verifies its outcome to guide phase transitions and replanning. A
common phase-level interface supports specialized policies and generalist action
models. Experiments in simulation and on a real robot show how Atomic Policy
improves task following and supports recovery in complex, long-horizon
manipulation. Project page: https://aaronzkd.github.io/atomic-policy/
