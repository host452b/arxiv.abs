Title: HiPerfCC-KVCache: Accelerating Confidential LLM Serving with Hierarchical KV Caching
Authors: Haoxuan Xu, Shaoyu Wang, Minghao Lin, Beijie Liu, Potung Yu, Yongqin Wang, Vincent Quentin Ulitzsch, Seo Jin Park, Murali Annavaram, Mengyuan Li
ID: 2611.28702
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28702

Abstract:
Hierarchical key-value (KV) caching reduces repeated prefill computation in
large language model serving by retaining reusable state beyond GPU memory.
Under GPU confidential computing (CC), however, protected transfers can erase
this benefit. We show that bulk KV copies hold driver locks during prolonged
CPU-side processing, stalling independent serving operations despite separate
threads and streams. Our characterization on Hopper and Blackwell GPUs shows how
lock scope, copy granularity, and allocation ownership affect foreground
progress and transfer parallelism. Guided by these findings, we present
HiPerfCC-KVCache, which manages resource lifetimes, prioritizes ready token
readback between bounded backup submissions, and parallelizes KV restores
through auxiliary contexts with shared GPU staging. Implemented in SGLang,
HiPerfCC-KVCache uses existing protected transfer interfaces without hardware or
driver changes. Across three models and three workloads on H200, HiPerfCC-
KVCache achieves a geometric-mean output-token throughput speedup of
$1.74\times$ (up to $2.72\times$) over the staged KV-transfer HiCache-Workers
baseline at the highest tested loads. Mean time to first token decreases by 36%
on average and up to 59%. The benefits generalize to RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell,
with a geometric-mean throughput speedup of $1.28\times$ (up to $2.20\times$)
over the same baseline. A capacity analysis further models and explains
variation in gains and remaining gaps to CC-off serving.
