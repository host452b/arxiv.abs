Title: Place-Revisit Consistency Evaluation of Reference Poses
Authors: Jianzhu Huai, Yujia Zhang, Grzegorz Jozkow
ID: 2611.30741
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30741

Abstract:
Reference trajectories are widely used to evaluate odometry and simultaneous
localization and mapping systems. However, local inconsistencies in benchmark
reference poses can bias such evaluations. This paper presents a place-revisit
consistency evaluation method for reference poses in mobile sensing datasets.
When a platform revisits a previously observed place after a sufficient temporal
gap, the relative pose implied by the reference trajectory should agree with
relative poses estimated directly from onboard sensors. We instantiate this idea
with lidar scan registration and stereo-image pose estimation, and apply it to
the KITTI Odometry and SNAIL Radar. The KITTI analysis identifies several
revisit regions where the public reference poses disagree with sensor-derived
relative poses, with median translation discrepancies up to 6.60 m.
Representative point-cloud overlays further localize these discrepancies and
support the use of place revisits as a benchmark-validation tool.
