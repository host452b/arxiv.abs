Title: Towards Agentic Video Corpus Moment Retrieval
Authors: Chenwei Xu, Shang Wu, Zhenyu Pan, Jianshu Zhang, Pranav Maneriker, Lie Lu, Fan Du, Manling Li, Han Liu
ID: 2611.29877
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29877

Abstract:
Video corpus moment retrieval (VCMR) requires finding the video and time
interval that match a text query in a corpus of videos. We present RELAY, a
training-free framework for VCMR that shares memory across queries. RELAY
processes a batch of queries in three stages: search with a query bank, ranking
with video notes, and localization with video prompt caching. In the search
stage, an embedding model scores each video by its best-matching frame. The
query bank penalizes videos that score highly for many other queries in the
batch. In the ranking stage, a vision-language model (VLM) writes notes on each
candidate video's scenes, people, objects, and actions once. Then, a large
language model (LLM) reranks the candidates from these notes. In the
localization stage, a VLM predicts the time interval of the moment in the top-
ranked video. When multiple queries select the same video, prompt caching lets
the VLM reuse its frames. On the three VERIFIED benchmarks, RELAY outperforms
the trained baselines in video retrieval R@1 without training. Without any
labeled moment, its predicted intervals are shorter than the annotated ones. In
VCMR, it achieves similar performance to VideoSearch-R1 on one benchmark and
trails it at IoU 0.5 on the other two. Human annotators also mark shorter
moments than the original annotations of the benchmarks. A single margin,
calibrated on a few labeled queries, widens the predicted interval to the
annotation convention of the benchmarks. With it, RELAY outperforms the trained
baselines in VCMR at IoU 0.5.
