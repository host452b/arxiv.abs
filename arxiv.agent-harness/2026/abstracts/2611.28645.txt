Title: PROSPER: LLMs as Sparse Retrievers for First-Stage Product Search
Authors: Hongru Song, Yu-An Liu, Ruqing Zhang, Jiafeng Guo, Maarten de Rijke, Sen Li, Wenjun Peng, Fuyu Lv, Xueqi Cheng
ID: 2611.28645
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28645

Abstract:
Product search is a crucial component of large-scale e-commerce platforms, where
first-stage retrieval should achieve high recall while ensuring efficient online
deployment. Sparse retrieval remains attractive for this setting due to its
interpretability and deployability, yet it suffers from severe vocabulary
mismatch challenges, especially for short queries and product titles. Large
language models (LLMs) offer strong semantic capabilities, but directly applying
them to sparse retrieval in product search introduces new challenges: (i)
semantic expansion may distort critical literal signals such as brand names and
model identifiers; and (ii) learning meaningful sparse representations is
difficult in the high-dimensional vocabulary space induced by LLMs.To address
these challenges, we propose PROSPER, a framework that uses LLMs as sparse
retrievers for product search while explicitly preserving lexical fidelity.
PROSPER combines (i) a literal residual network that alleviates lexical
expansion hallucination by reinforcing underweighted literal terms through a
residual compensation mechanism; and (ii) a lexical focusing window that uses
top-k selection for rapid early sparsification and effective training
initialization. Extensive offline evaluations and large-scale online A/B tests
demonstrate that PROSPER consistently improves over existing sparse retrievers,
and provides revenue gains for industrial hybrid retrieval systems.
