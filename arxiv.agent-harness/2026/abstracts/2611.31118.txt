Title: TideSand: Performance-Accurate Sandbox Multiplexing for LLM Agents
Authors: Shulu Li, Hanchen Li, Xiaokun Chen, Qiuyang Mang, Hanfei Chen, Jacky Kwok, Hanfei Yu, Alvin Cheung, Edward A. Lee, Joseph E. Gonzalez et al.
ID: 2611.31118
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31118

Abstract:
We propose TideSand, a sandbox system that intelligently multiplexes resources
for performance-sensitive LLM agents, such as auto research agents working on
system measurement tasks. TideSand combines three key ideas: agent-aware
scheduling, which schedules sandbox execution intelligently at the tool call
level to avoid resource contention; speculative swapping, which offloads the
memory of sandboxes waiting on inference and restores it before their next tool
call; and selective rollback, which checkpoints selected sandboxes when
contention occurs and restores them when machines are idle. We evaluate TideSand
across six model-workload pairs on AlgoTune, Terminal-Bench 4.0, and Terminal-
Bench Science. TideSand achieves 3.8-10x higher throughput than strict
reservation while maintaining tool-call fidelity, or 7-44% higher fidelity than
overcommit baselines at similar throughput. Moreover, TideSand reduces the
evaluation error on Algotune by 19x through improved tool-call fidelity. We hope
the work motivates future research to maintain tool call fidelity while keeping
resource utilization high through statistical multiplexing.
