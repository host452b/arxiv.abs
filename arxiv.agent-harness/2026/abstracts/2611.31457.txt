Title: Taxi: Analyzing Agents at (Real) Scale
Authors: Guy Dar
ID: 2611.31457
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31457

Abstract:
In this technical report, we present Taxi -- a system for debugging, clustering,
and monitoring agents at scale. Taxi scales to millions of steps efficiently.
This is made possible by a combination of scalable analysis methods. Taxi relies
primarily on clustering to generate a taxonomy over steps for a structured
overview of the data, and provides full SQL access for engineers and automated
agents to ask complex questions. Clustering and querying are best used together
in a hybrid pipeline guided by a coding agent. Taxi also includes an
experimental integration of Jev-style decision models: models that map state and
bounded questions directly to typed, probabilistic decisions. They add low-cost
semantic routing and evaluation where embeddings or literal queries alone are
insufficient. In addition, Taxi provides an extensible ecosystem, including
built-in evaluators and daily reports. To accommodate growth, Taxi is designed
to be modular, hackable, and easy to plug-and-play for individuals and teams.
