Title: Quantizing in the Loop: Drift-Aware Ultra-Low-Bit Vision-Language-Action Models
Authors: Yanlong Chen, Thongthornpatch Chamadol, Yichen Guo, Yunfei Gao, Mohan Liu, Addison Lin Wang, Yawei Li
ID: 2611.30178
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30178

Abstract:
Deploying large vision-language-action (VLA) models on resource-constrained
robots demands extreme compression, yet existing post-training quantization
(PTQ) methods frequently collapse at ultra-low bit-widths ($\le$ 2 bits). We
trace this failure to two mismatches in standard pipelines. First, calibrating
on static offline demonstrations induces distribution shift: minor quantization
perturbations drive the policy into unseen states where uncalibrated errors
compound. Second, minimizing the layer-wise weight reconstruction error is a
flawed proxy, as action generation depends only on the representations consumed
by the action head. We present LoopQ, an on-policy ultra-low-bit quantization
framework. LoopQ closes the loop by calibrating on states from the quantized
policy's own rollouts. It then adds readout-aligned fine-tuning, which freezes
the trellis codes and optimizes only lightweight lookup tables and scaling
factors to align the backbone readout with the full-precision teacher. With all
linear layers at 2 bits, LoopQ achieves a 60.4% success rate on LIBERO-Plus with
GR00T N1.7, close to the 61.8% of full precision. Even at 1 bit per weight,
$π_{0.5}$ still completes 80.0% of LIBERO tasks. With 8-bit activations, the
2-bit $π_{0.5}$ runs on integer kernels on an embedded NVIDIA Jetson AGX Thor,
with 4.1$\times$ smaller weights and 44% less memory. On a real AgileX Piper
arm, it stays within one success of full precision on each of three tasks. Code
and checkpoints are available at https://github.com/ForeverBlue816/LoopQ
