Title: PAMS: Preconditioned Activation-Metric Subspace for Zeroth-Order LLM Fine-tuning
Authors: Pradeep Kumar Ragu Chanthar, Saksham Sahai Srivastava, Jaewoo Lee
ID: 2611.28476
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28476

Abstract:
Zeroth-order (ZO) optimization provides a computationally efficient approach to
fine-tuning large language models (LLMs), but conventional methods typically
rely on isotropic perturbations that ignore the anisotropic structure of neural-
network parameter spaces. We introduce Preconditioned Activation-Metric Subspace
(PAMS), a ZO optimization framework that uses forward-pass activation statistics
to construct structured, layer-wise perturbation geometries without computing
gradients or forming dense curvature matrices. PAMS tracks a low-rank
approximation of the dominant activation subspace and its associated spectrum
online, and uses the spectrum to softly reweight perturbation variance while
preserving exploration in directions outside the retained subspace. A trace
normalization separates the perturbation geometry from its overall scale,
enabling consistent perturbation magnitudes across layers. We show theoretically
that the resulting perturbation covariance induces right-side preconditioning of
the gradient of the corresponding Gaussian-smoothed objective. We further
characterize the estimator's smoothing bias and second moment and derive a
nonconvex convergence guarantee that captures the effects of the perturbation
geometry and smoothing radius. The proposed low-rank tracking procedure requires
$\mathcal{O}(n_l r)$ persistent memory for a layer with input dimension nl and
retained rank r, avoiding the $\mathcal{O}(n_l^2)$ memory required by a dense
activation covariance. Experiments on transformer-based language-model fine-
tuning tasks across GLUE and SuperGLUE demonstrate that PAMS improves the
effectiveness and stability of zeroth-order optimization while retaining its
gradient-free and objective-evaluation-based advantages.
