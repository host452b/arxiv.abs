Title: Jagged Robustness: Uneven Safety Transfer and Emerging Vulnerabilities under LLM Quantization
Authors: Ramneek Agnihotri, Yingte Xu, Hans Vath
ID: 2611.27424
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27424

Abstract:
Quantization enables efficient inference in language models through model
parameter compression. This work audits the impact of quantization on capability
and safety domains including model safeguards, instruction-following, ethics and
bias. Through benchmarks, we find that model safeguards and bias can degrade
under quantization, while other capabilities such as truthfulness remain more
stable in Qwen3-4B. We define this uneven transfer of LLM capabilities under
quantization as Jagged Robustness. We find multiple factors driving Jagged
Robustness: task complexity, evaluation method and generation coherence.
Moreover, we verify the degradation of model safeguards by observing increased
compliance with unsafe requests under quantization, and demonstrate universal
adversarial suffix attacks that are benign for the full model but jailbreak
quantized models. The findings indicate that it is risky to assume that the
performance and robustness can transfer evenly across different capability
domains from full models to quantized models.
