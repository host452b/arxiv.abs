Title: Analytical Modeling and Optimal GPU Provisioning for Disaggregated LLM Inference
Authors: Akshat Pandey, Dominic Cheng, Sidharth Sharma, Ashwin Gumaste
ID: 2611.28760
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28760

Abstract:
We present OSplit, an exact analytical method that enables efficient
partitioning of a GPU L1 domain into prefill and decode workers, including
sharding for classical and advanced GPUs. OSplit takes into consideration model
parameters, enabling any inference model to be partitioned into prefill and
decode workers with a goal of producing the maximum throughput possible. OSplit
is based on a capacity model that takes as inputs the model, the loading scheme
(batch size, max sequence length), and models an inference serving framework
(vLLM) and computes the efficiency of each GPU. For this, we model H100 and
GB300 GPUs, and model a scale-up domain, along with FlashAttention-based
kernels. OSplit first computes an optimal collective generation policy, which
gives inputs to the capacity model that enables optimal split of prefill and
decode GPUs. We evaluate our scheme over H100 (NVL8) and GB300 (NVL72) using
vLLM inference serving and show throughput, predicted and measured latency,
showing near-perfect theoretical-to-measured values over leading LLMs (Llama and
Qwen)
