Title: Categorical Foundations of AI Agents
Authors: Yong Wang
ID: 2611.29998
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29998

Abstract:
We follow the idea of separating intelligence from execution and give the
categorical foundations of AI agents: the transformers, the agents, and the
orchestrations and choreographies of AI agents are categorized, and which also
include their coalgebras and algebras.
