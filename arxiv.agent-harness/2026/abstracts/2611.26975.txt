Title: Run One, Get Two: Near-Free Streaming Safety Checks for Local LLMs
Authors: Soheil Zibakhsh, Pedram Aghazadeh, Farinaz Koushanfar
ID: 2611.26975
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26975

Abstract:
Local LLM assistants decode for one user at a time, so decoding is bound by
memory bandwidth and leaves compute idle. Streaming safety checks today trade
accuracy against cost. A dedicated guard model is accurate but nearly doubles
the latency per token, while a hidden-state probe is cheap but less accurate. We
present ToRA, an adapter that runs a second model as an extra row in the
generator's decode step, sharing its weights and KV cache. Dual-row kernels read
each weight once for both rows. On Qwen3-0.6B, ToRA matches or beats a dedicated
streaming guard, with higher F1 on 12 of 17 benchmarks. It detects 11.8 points
more unsafe responses than the probe at a similar cost, under 4% added latency
per token.
