Title: Native Block-Quantized Matrix Multiplication and Explicit K/V Residency with Dual-View Access for LLM Inference on RISC-V Vector Processors
Authors: Weiying Wang, Yuanyang Xiang, Zhiwei Zhang
ID: 2611.31937
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31937

Abstract:
Some key execution states in large language model inference persist across
multiple instructions and are difficult to express and exploit within RVV
semantics centered on individual operations. We define specialization boundaries
according to data-state lifetimes and propose Quantized Block Streams (QBS) and
Attention/KV Context (AKV) while preserving RVV programmability. QBS organizes
native-block format processing, block traversal, integer computation, and
ordered FP32 accumulation into a unified execution process, whereas AKV
maintains local K/V residency and supplies operand views aligned with the
computation direction while leaving Attention arithmetic on RVV. Cycle-accurate
RTL evaluation shows that QBS achieves geometric-mean speedups of 35.68x and
51.34x for projection Compute and Matmul, respectively, while AKV achieves a
1.45x geometric-mean speedup over Tiled RVV for Decode Attention. Compared with
the baseline, the complete design increases total area by 12.65%; on the
evaluated projection workloads, throughput per area and energy efficiency
improve by 46.63x and 19.78x, respectively. Further evaluation demonstrates
applicability across multiple block-quantization formats and across models with
different Attention organizations.
