Title: Improving Repair Agents with Cause-Chain Diagnoses
Authors: Marius Smytzek, Martin Eberlein, Lars Grunske, Andreas Zeller
ID: 2611.30574
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30574

Abstract:
Repair agents resolve a growing share of real-world issues. Starting with the
same information as a developer reading a bug report, they have to find out how
the program fails before actually fixing it. We present FORECAST, which provides
repair agents with a diagnosis of the failure. From the executions of passing
and failing tests, FORECAST infers a cause chain, a short sequence of execution
features, such as conditions or data flow, that explains how the failing runs
diverge. FORECAST appends this cause chain to the issue an agent receives. It
thus works with any agent that builds its prompts from the issue, without
modifying the agent. We evaluate FORECAST on all 300 issues of SWE-bench Lite
with five repair agents and two models, the commercial Claude Haiku 4.5 and the
open-weight Qwen3.5. With the cause-chain diagnosis, every agent resolves
significantly more issues. Over all agents, the share of resolved issues rises
from 48.5% to 70.9% for Haiku 4.5 and from 31.2% to 56.1% for Qwen3.5. The open-
weight model with the diagnosis thus surpasses the commercial model without it.
The gains persist across repeated runs and for agents that already run the tests
the diagnosis is derived from.
