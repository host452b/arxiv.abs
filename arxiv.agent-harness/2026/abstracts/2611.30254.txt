Title: Innata-0: A Native Embodied World System with Model-Agent Co-Design
Authors: Innata Team, Yu Bai, Ziyi Bai, Yuxuan Cai, Chenrui He, Wenhao Li, Zhe Lin, Heng Wang, Hai Yuan, Zhiwei Yu et al.
ID: 2611.30254
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30254

Abstract:
The physical world is open-ended, dynamic, and partially observable. While
foundation models have advanced embodied intelligence, standalone models
struggle with long-horizon execution, and simply integrating models with agents
may lead to inconsistencies between planning and physical control. To support
reliable and adaptive behaviors in the physical world, we introduce Innata-0, a
native embodied world system. Innata-0 jointly designs an embodied world-action
model and an agent to combine their complementary strengths. The model,
Innata-0-WAM, adopts a tri-tower Mixture-of-Transformers architecture that
integrates understanding, prediction, and action generation. The agent,
Innata-0-Agent, coordinates long-horizon execution through memory, verification,
and recovery. A co-design protocol is further proposed to align model training
with agent execution and leverage validated experiences to support subsequent
adaptation and model improvement. Extensive evaluations demonstrate strong
embodied capabilities across understanding, world prediction, and robotic
manipulation. Innata-0 represents our first step toward physical intelligence,
and we see it as the beginning of a much longer journey.
