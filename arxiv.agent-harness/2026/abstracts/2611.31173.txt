Title: EmboEagle: An Efficient Agentic Embodied Framework for Long-horizon Robotic Manipulation
Authors: Fan Xu, Zhongtao Wang, Hao Wu, Yitong Zhu, Wenzhuo Ma, Long Li, Zhuohao Li, Zhisheng Chen, Rui Su, Kun Wang et al.
ID: 2611.31173
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31173

Abstract:
Long-horizon robotic manipulation demands reliable execution across
interdependent stages, yet standalone World-Action Models (WAMs) suffer from
compounding physical errors, while stateless Vision-Language Model (VLM)
supervision incurs prohibitive chunkwise latency and lacks 3D spatial
persistence. We present EmboEagle, a closed-loop agentic framework that unifies
subtask-conditioned WAM control, latent-space adaptive supervision, and memory-
guided geometric recovery. First, we adapt sequential WAMs via decoupled two-
stage flow matching that preserves cross-stage visual history while calibrating
truncated action supervision at subtask boundaries. Second, without an auxiliary
vision encoder, we distill teacher intervention decisions into a lightweight
routing gate driven solely by the WAM Action Expert's pre- and post-chunk latent
transitions and subtask embeddings. Third, upon invocation, the VLM decouples
diagnostic status from discrete routing actions and executes parameterized
recovery primitives grounded in structured memory (initial references, active
grasp bindings, and tool traces), followed by mandatory post-repair
verification. Across RMBench and LIBERO-Mem, EmboEagle achieves 91.3\% and
90.6\% average success, surpassing the strongest baselines by 8.0 and 9.8
percentage points, while boosting real-world success over VoLoAgent under
identical executor weights. Moreover, adaptive gating cuts VLM calls by 74.2\%
versus chunkwise monitoring (91.3\% vs. 93.7\% success).
