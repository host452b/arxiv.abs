Title: CarrierKV: An Edge LLM Accelerator with Positional Carrier Decomposition for Low-Bit KV Cache
Authors: Junyi Luo, Xinting Jiang, Ruichen Qi, Gregory Kielian, Quan Cheng, Mehdi Saligane
ID: 2611.26857
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26857

Abstract:
Long-context decoding on edge devices is limited by DRAM, because every
generated token reads the whole, growing KV cache. 4-bit block-scaled formats
such as NVFP4 reduce this traffic but degrade accuracy: on Qwen3-4B, NVFP4
lowers 32K RULER (seven tasks) from 92.6 to 79.4, and a Hadamard rotation lowers
it further to 57.7. We observe that most of the key energy is a positional
carrier, one per-head vector that rotary position embedding (RoPE) rotates with
position. In NVFP4, it sets the shared scale of most key blocks and lowers the
signal-to-quantization-noise ratio of the content by about 5 dB, nearly one bit,
yet it depends only on that vector and the position. We therefore introduce
positional carrier decomposition, which removes the carrier before RoPE and
stores only the content residual. We design a KV path that rotates and quantizes
keys only after this removal and computes each attention score directly on the
4-bit code. We also design a positional phase engine that regenerates the
carrier's score term for one key per cycle. At the same cache size as NVFP4,
CarrierKV raises 32K RULER on Qwen3-4B from 79.4 to 91.1 and lowers the 32K PG19
perplexity increase of NVFP4 by 1.8 to 5.9 times on four models, outperforming
SageAttention and P3-LLM on all four. Its loss also stays nearly flat as the
cache grows, while that of NVFP4 rises. In a 22 nm FDSOI process at 600 MHz,
CarrierKV's hardware increases the area of the NVFP4 accelerator by 2.9% and the
energy of a 20K-token reasoning request on Qwen3-4B by 0.12% at the same decode
latency. On Qwen3-4B at 32K, CarrierKV has a better RULER score than FP8 keys
(91.1 against 89.3) and uses 13.7% less energy per token. The decomposition also
lowers the 32K perplexity increase of 3-bit TurboQuant keys on Qwen3-4B from
69.7 to 0.76.
