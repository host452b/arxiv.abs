Title: Domain advantage in two-dimensional quantum-device design with multi-modal agentic harness
Authors: Shenhao Miao, Yizhou Li, Liqiao Xia, Ruihao Liu, Dingke Wang, Pablo Jarillo-Herrero, Eli Zeldov, Jiaqi Cai
ID: 2611.30043
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30043

Abstract:
High-quality two-dimensional quantum devices are commonly fabricated from
mechanically exfoliated crystals, whose irregular shapes and placement require a
lithographic layout tailored to each specimen. Drawing these layouts remains
largely manual. We introduce qlaybench, a benchmark that tests whether language-
model agents can turn microscope images and experimental requirements into
device layouts that fit the specimen. Agents with conventional tools often
construct devices when material outlines are supplied, but struggle to identify
those outlines from images. Modelled on the human workflow, qlaybot, a
multimodal agentic harness, connects image interpretation to the tools used to
align images, draw device features and inspect the resulting layout. We evaluate
each layout with checks drawn from practical design experience, asking whether
it fits the specimen and connects its intended components. With greater
computational effort, an agent using qlaybot passes 81.5% of the benchmark
tasks, compared with 24.1% when it uses conventional tools. This domain
advantage highlights the importance of connecting scientific interpretation of a
specimen to geometric design and provides a route towards reducing manual effort
in two-dimensional quantum-device fabrication.
