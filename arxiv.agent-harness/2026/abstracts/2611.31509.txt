Title: TokenJoule: Energy Modeling for Distributed Training and Inference of LLMs
Authors: George Karfakis, Lime Yao, Joyjit Kundu, Debjyoti Bhattacharjee, Puneet Gupta
ID: 2611.31509
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31509

Abstract:
As large language models (LLMs) scale, energy dictates operating cost, cooling,
and cluster capacity across training and inference. Early hardware design-space
exploration (DSE) requires predicting energy before silicon exists, yet
performance models miss hidden component work while prior energy predictors
calibrate to deployed GPUs. We present TokenJoule, an open-source analytical
tool that derives device kernel sequences, tiled compute, shared-memory staging,
cache residency, and collective traffic, pricing activity directly with
component energies. With zero workload fitting, TokenJoule achieves 3.3-6.6%
activity error (151 A100/H100 runs) and 3.0-6.8% A100 energy error, guiding
parallelism, memory packaging, and 3D-DRAM chip co-design.
