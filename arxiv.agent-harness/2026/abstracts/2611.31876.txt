Title: AgentDRC: Multi-Agent Algorithms for Spatially-Aware Automated DRC Correction
Authors: Pengju Liu, Nuo Xu, Winso Chen, Peijun Li, Yang, Zhao, Yu Cao, Caiwen Ding
ID: 2611.31876
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31876

Abstract:
Design Rule Checking (DRC) error fixing remains a time-consuming bottleneck in
physical design, often requiring manual intervention despite advanced EDA
optimizations. Existing LLM-agent approaches fall short due to redundant
contextual shapes, limited spatial reasoning over raw coordinates, and
inefficient flat layout representations. We propose AgentDRC, which formulates
iterative DRC correction as a multi-agent algorithm and introduces a spatial-
aware verification and correction algorithm that reconstructs structured spatial
relations among critical shapes and validates fixes through geometric and
semantic constraint checking. The system coordinates four specialized agents to
perceive multi-level, multi-modal geometric features, filter irrelevant shapes,
and iteratively correct violations. Experiments show that AgentDRC achieves up
to 30.4% pass@1 and 55.7% pass@5 on challenging violations, with 13.8% pass@5
improvement over the best baseline. We will open-source all code and benchmark
tasks upon acceptance.
