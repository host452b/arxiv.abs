Title: ReVAgent: Multi-Agent Reasoning-Intensive Video Generation
Authors: Yehui Yang, Jing Fan, Jun Hu, Shangyao Chen, Jinhua Bian, Song Wang, Ruize Han
ID: 2611.30163
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30163

Abstract:
A central challenge in generating instructional videos for reasoning-intensive
topics is to show how and why the state changes at each step. Even when an
animation renders successfully, the depicted object relationships and state
transitions can conflict with the explanation, making the reasoning difficult to
follow. We present ReVAgent, a multi-agent framework that solves a problem,
generates keyframes for its explanation, and synthesizes an instructional video.
The agents share the execution record of a solution program, reuse intermediate
results and constructions in the scenes, and check rendered keyframes to guide
revisions to the solution or the scenes. We also introduce a multidimensional
rubric for assessing whether generated videos present the problem clearly,
organize the steps coherently, and use visuals to explain the reasoning. We
calibrate a multimodal judge using human ratings and apply it to instructional
videos for mathematics and algorithms. In the evaluated samples, ReVAgent
presents multi-step reasoning coherently and uses diagrams to show how key
operations lead to subsequent states.
