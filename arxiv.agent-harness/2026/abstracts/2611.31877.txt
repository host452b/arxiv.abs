Title: Don't Miss the Forest for the Trees: Zero-Shot Skeleton Action Recognition with Hyperbolic Hierarchical Semantic Prototypes
Authors: Zixiao Jin, Siyu Zhu, Feng Ding, Haisheng Fu, Xin Dong, Shankai Chen, Jie Liang
ID: 2611.31877
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31877

Abstract:
Zero-shot skeleton-based action recognition is evaluated almost exclusively on
flat leaf labels, which leaves open whether a representation also grounds the
broader concepts that contain an action in skeleton motion. We find that strong
leaf alignment does not carry over to them: when ancestor concepts are scored
directly under independent decoding, eight flat methods with 57.86-73.29% mean
leaf Top-1 reach only 6.15-31.21% mean full-path accuracy across four NTU RGB+D
splits. We propose the Hyperbolic Hierarchical Semantic Prototype ($H^2SP$)
framework, which represents every node of an action taxonomy in the Lorentz
model of hyperbolic space. First, instead of learning a free vector per node,
$H^2SP$ synthesizes hierarchy-aware prototypes by recursive parent-child
geodesic interpolation under a single tree-wide gate. Second, tangent-space
semantic and distributional alignment on seen classes anchor the leaf prototypes
in skeleton motion. Third, a soft entailment-cone regularizer encourages
directional parent-child inclusion, propagating this motion grounding to
ancestor concepts. We evaluate hierarchical concept prediction under independent
and cascade decoding, and transfer to an alternative taxonomy without
retraining. Across the four splits, $H^2SP$ attains the best mean independent
and cascade full-path accuracy among the evaluated methods, at a comparable leaf
Top-1 of 72.40%. Transferred to the alternative taxonomy, it outperforms both
flat baselines and the same-tree Euclidean control across every split and
decoding mode, indicating that its concept-level correspondence extends beyond
the training taxonomy.
