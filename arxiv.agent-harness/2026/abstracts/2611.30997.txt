Title: Generalizing Agentic Robotics via Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation
Authors: Xinxin Liu, Anh Dao, Wenrui Bao, Rafid Ahmed, Bingxin Xu, Ming Li, Chen Chen, Yuzhang Shang
ID: 2611.30997
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30997

Abstract:
Building general-purpose robots is a central goal in robotics. Agentic robotics
pursues this goal with planners that control robots through tool calls. However,
existing methods either rely on proprietary models accessed through APIs, which
incur high costs and latency, or post-train a planner with reinforcement
learning (RL) in a single domain, requiring a separate specialist for each
domain with incompatible tools. In large language model (LLM) post-training,
multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) has been used to consolidate domain
specialists into a single generalist. Yet whether MOPD can build a generalist
planner for agentic robotics, how well it performs, and how it compares with
other consolidation methods remain unexplored. In this paper, we systematically
study these questions: we introduce Robo-MOPD, which trains a single student on
closed-loop rollouts in simulation under token-level supervision from matching
frozen specialists, and compare it with weight merging, off-policy specialist
supervised fine-tuning (SFT), and multi-domain online RL. Experiments on LIBERO-
PRO and RoboCasa365 show that a single 9B Robo-MOPD planner reaches 72.9% and
44.8% success (58.9% average), surpassing all consolidated baselines in average
success and both 9B specialists on their respective benchmarks. Robo-MOPD excels
on long-horizon tasks, outperforming multi-domain online RL by 13.0 percentage
points on LIBERO-10.
