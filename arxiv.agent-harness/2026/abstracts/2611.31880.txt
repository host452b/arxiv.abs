Title: Multi Agent Reinforcement Learning enhanced Federated Learning for Predicting Remaining Useful Lifetime
Authors: Rajarshi Chattopadhyay, Chen-Khong Tham
ID: 2611.31880
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31880

Abstract:
Remaining useful life (RUL) prediction is central to Prognostics and Health
Management (PHM). The growth of industrial IoT has unlocked large multivariate
sensor streams and enabled deep models - especially Transformers - for long-
horizon sequence learning. However, transformers are parameter-heavy and data-
hungry, while operational datasets are often siloed and privacy-sensitive.
Federated Learning (FL) addresses this by training across clients without raw
data sharing, but in practice many clients connect over lossy wireless links
where UDP is preferred for low latency, making model exchange vulnerable to
packet drops. We present a cooperative multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL)
framework that adapts FL to lossy channels by selecting per-client layer subsets
to transmit and capping retransmissions. This framework is trained with QMIX
value-function factorization for scalability. We instantiate the approach with a
Graph Convolution Position-Aware Transformer (GCPATr) for RUL forecasting. On
the CMAPSS dataset, our MARL-enhanced FL (RL-FL) consistently improves mean
square error over standard FL, with the largest gains at high packet loss (e.g.,
up to 18% improvement on FD003 at 50% loss). These results show that MARL is an
effective countermeasure to wireless degradation in FL-based RUL prediction.
