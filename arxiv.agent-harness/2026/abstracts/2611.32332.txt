Title: GUI-WEdit and GUI-SkillEvo: Gradient-Free Adaptation of Small GUI Agents
Authors: Haixia Bi, Yingtian Chen, Sihan Yang, Zuzheng Kuang, Yuzhen Tong, Kai Fu, Zhengyong Wan
ID: 2611.32332
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32332

Abstract:
Small graphical user interface (GUI) agents can execute desktop workflows, but
repeated local errors and fragile procedural decisions limit their reliability.
We present GUI-WEdit-4B and GUI-SkillEvo-2B, two gradient-free adaptations of
GUI-Owl-1.5 backbones. For the 4B model, we use trajectory feedback to construct
guarded, closed-form updates to a late multilayer perceptron (MLP), retaining
successful states as protection constraints. The final checkpoint changes
23,739,095 parameter values, or 0.535\% of the 4,437,815,808-parameter backbone,
within one weight tensor. For the 2B model, an assistant iteratively proposes
and refines procedural skills while the model weights remain fixed. On the
361-task OSWorld subset excluding Google Drive tasks, the frozen systems obtain
final aggregate scores of 48.02\% and 45.47\%, respectively.
