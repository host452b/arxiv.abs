Title: FOREST: Coordinating Tree-based HBF, RRAM, and HBM for Efficient Speculative LLM Inference
Authors: Hao Ding, Zheng Shi, Dongxue Zhao, Huiying Bian, Meng Li, Xu Zheng, Kun Wang, Nanjian Wu, Jie Chen, Yimao Cai
ID: 2611.27701
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27701

Abstract:
Speculative decoding (SD) accelerates large language model (LLM) inference by
combining lightweight drafting with parallel target-model verification. However,
its stages impose distinct resource demands: target verification requires high-
throughput access to large weight sets, sequential drafting executes a compact
model under tight latency constraints, and long-context attention processes
growing key-value (KV) caches. Conventional GPU-centric SD systems serve these
workloads through shared HBM-centric memory and general-purpose compute,
creating mismatches in capacity, bandwidth, and computation. These mismatches
constrain deployable model-context configurations and limit the efficiency of
bandwidth-bound and latency-sensitive operations. We propose FOREST, a
heterogeneous memory-centric architecture coordinating HBF, 3D-stacked RRAM, and
HBM memory-compute trees for efficient SD. It features: (1) compute-aligned 3D
RRAM, combining ultra-wide, low-frequency reads with clock-decoupled interfaces
and overlapped SRAM-DCIM weight refills for latency-sensitive drafting; (2)
cross-tree numerical co-design, moving block-scaled Q/K/V preprocessing to HBF
and RRAM root plates and enabling block-floating-point (BFP) attention with
pipelined softmax reduction in the HBM tree; and (3) HBF-aware SD design-space
exploration, revealing how bandwidth-compute provisioning and model scale
determine performance, energy, and cost trade-offs. Evaluated across six SD
configurations spanning 32B-405B target models, FOREST achieves up to 5.5x end-
to-end speedup, 11.46x higher decode throughput, and 16.5x higher decode energy
efficiency over NVIDIA A800 GPU-based SD baselines. Under a modeled total cost
of ownership, FOREST delivers up to 6.73x higher token-generation cost
efficiency.
