Title: ConsisX: Cross-View Consistency Thinking in 3D for Vision-Language-Action Models
Authors: Yunhe Zhang, Long Gao, Yuedong Fu, Gang He, Li Xu, Chang Wu, Yuqi Zhu, Pengyu Li
ID: 2611.30308
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30308

Abstract:
Vision-language-action (VLA) models integrate visual perception and language
understanding from vision-language models (VLMs) with action prediction for
robotic manipulation. Although multi-view observations provide complementary
scene information, VLMs model cross-view interactions based on 2D feature
similarity, which can lead to physical inconsistency in the form of cross-view
incorrect association between distinct entities and cross-view information
fragmentation about the same entity. To address these challenges, we propose
ConsisX, a novel VLA framework that integrates cross-view association and entity
coherence to enable cross-view consistency thinking in 3D for physically
consistent robotic manipulation. Specifically, Cross-View Association Modeling
(CAM) is proposed to introduce cross-view spatial proximity constraints in a
shared 3D space to reduce cross-view incorrect association. Entity Coherence
Organization (ECO) is designed to organize cross-view local information into
coherent 3D entity evidence, thereby addressing cross-view information
fragmentation. Consistent Action Conditioning (CAC) is introduced to condition
action prediction on task-relevant 3D entity evidence for cross-view consistent
action execution. Experiments on 3 real-world manipulation tasks and 4
simulation benchmarks establish ConsisX as an effective and competitive
framework for robotic manipulation.
