Title: Training Vision Models for LLM-Driven Weight-Space Programming
Authors: Timo Ropinski, Sebastian Kranz
ID: 2611.26408
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26408

Abstract:
It has been demonstrated that, in certain settings, large language models can
perform weight-space programming to directly modify the weights of neural
networks. However, it is unknown how dedicated training of these networks can
contribute to the successes of weight-space programming. We present a benchmark
of LLM-driven weight-space programming on vision models, in which five LLMs edit
convolutional networks and vision transformers on relabeling and error-
correction tasks on Waterbirds, CIFAR-100, PACS and Entity-30. We report more
than 2,600 such edit processes, whereby each edit is scored on held-out images
for success, side effects and accuracy loss. We compare standard training with
label smoothing, gradient-based editable training, and our Edit-Ready Training
(ERT), a novel method that rehearses the direct, closed-form weight edits LLMs
perform. We show that preparing networks for editing raises the success of LLM
edits substantially where standard networks are hard to edit. ERT further
achieves the highest mean score for GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 across all
evaluated baselines. We will release our LLM testing framework alongside all
tasks, editing trajectories and scores. Project page: https://viscom.uni-
ulm.de/wsp
