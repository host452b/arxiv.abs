Title: SOARS: System One Assisted Reranking for Agentic Search
Authors: Amin Bigdeli, Liye Fu, Fattane Zarrinkalam, Joel Stremmel, Elizabeth Lingg
ID: 2611.32917
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32917

Abstract:
Deep search agents address complex information needs through iterative retrieval
and reasoning, where the relevance of retrieved evidence influences downstream
performance. Existing rerankers improve retrieval effectiveness but introduce
substantial latency and cost when invoked throughout the search trajectory. We
introduce SOARS, a training-free framework leveraging a System One model for
fast, calibrated relevance estimation. At each search step, SOARS selects query-
relevant passages from long documents and reranks candidates in a single call,
jointly considering the original question and agent-generated sub-query. Across
six LLM backbones on BrowseComp-Plus and WebShaper, SOARS consistently improves
answer accuracy and evidence recall while reducing search calls, end-to-end
latency, and inference cost. On BrowseComp-Plus, it improves GPT-5.4 Mini's
accuracy from 73.9% to 84.7% while reducing search calls from 23 to 14,
demonstrating the potential of System One reranking for more effective and
efficient deep search.
