Title: Towards Effective Fuzzing of EDK2 Components
Authors: Datta Manikanta Sri Hari Danduri, Connor Everett Glosner, Aravind Machiry
ID: 2611.03854
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03854

Abstract:
Securing bootloaders is critical to establishing the trusted computing base of
modern systems. TianoCore's edk2, the de facto UEFI-based bootloader, is widely
deployed but has recently been shown to contain numerous high-severity
vulnerabilities. Existing approaches either focus on post-boot interfaces or
rely on heavyweight simulation, limiting their scalability, throughput, and
coverage of boot-time components. We present Metis, an automated technique for
fuzzing edk2 protocols (i.e., boot-time interfaces) by rehosting them as user-
space applications. Our key insight is that effective fuzzing must exercise not
only protocol interfaces but also their dependency interactions, which serve as
additional input channels. Metis employs a Direct-dependency Component Rehosting
(DCR)-based strategy to construct semantically meaningful mocks for
dependencies, enabling controlled input injection across all channels. It
further leverages a hybrid approach combining static analysis and Large Language
Model (LLM)-guided synthesis to generate and refine fuzzing harnesses. We
implement Metis on the Host Based Firmware Analyzer (HBFA) framework and
evaluate it on 60 drivers covering 162 protocols. Metis achieves 73.1% coverage,
approximately 1.7 times higher than FuzzUEr, the state-of-the-art edk2 fuzzing
technique, and discovers 137 previously unknown bugs. We have already reported
26 of them, including 12 that are remotely triggerable, and all reported
vulnerabilities have been acknowledged by the vendors.
