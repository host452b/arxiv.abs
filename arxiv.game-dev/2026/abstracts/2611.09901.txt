Title: CaregiverConnect: A Mobile Application for Stress Tracking and Support in Dementia Care
Authors: Yagna Manasa Boyapati, Arshia Khan
ID: 2611.09901
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09901

Abstract:
The rising global prevalence of dementia places escalating demands on paid
professional caregivers, who frequently experience severe stress and
professional isolation. Existing mobile health (mHealth) applications rarely
integrate real-time stress monitoring with multimodal relief strategies.
CaregiverConnect, a Flutter/Firebase mobile application comprising nine feature
modules, was evaluated in a single-group, pre-post pilot study with 10 paid
professional dementia caregivers at two nursing home facilities over two weeks.
Descriptive analysis yielded an exploratory effect estimate for self-reported
stress change (Cohen's d = 1.60; interpreted with caution given N = 10 and no
control group), an improvement in emotional support responses from 60% to 100%
of participants, and near-perfect usability ratings (UI Navigation: 4.90/5.0).
These preliminary findings support the feasibility and acceptability of
CaregiverConnect and identify end-of-shift stress peaks as a priority target for
future adaptive design. Larger randomized controlled trials are needed to
establish efficacy.
