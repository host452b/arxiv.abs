Title: A Stackelberg Game Framework with Drainability Guardrails for Pricing and Scaling in Multi-Tenant GPU Cloud Platforms
Authors: Junji Yan, Asrin Efe Yorulmaz, Hanchen Zhou, Tamer Başar
ID: 2604.16802
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16802

Abstract:
Modern Graphics Processing Unit (GPU)-backed services must satisfy strict
latency service-level objectives (SLOs) while controlling spare-capacity costs.
In multi-tenant GPU cloud platforms, this trade-off is inherently dynamic
because workload demand is endogenous; specifically, pricing shapes the
submissions of heterogeneous tenants, which subsequently impact congestion and
delay. We formulate the joint pricing-and-scaling problem as a large-population
Stackelberg game problem, and we derive an explicit equilibrium demand map. The
resulting closed-loop model reveals a structural failure mode in which delay-
insensitive workloads sustain a residual demand floor, making the backlog
undrainable under bounded price and service capacity. This observation motivates
a computable drainability guardrail that certifies uniformly negative backlog
drift in the residual-demand regime. For any fixed price-capacity pair
satisfying the drainability guardrail, we establish global convergence to a
unique operating point under a checkable step-size condition. Building on this
fixed-pair analysis, we further develop an optimizer-agnostic action shield that
provides a negative-drift certificate for shielded execution in the residual
regime of the dynamic problem and show empirically that it improves safety and
robustness for model-free reinforcement learning (RL) in this setting.
