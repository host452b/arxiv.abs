Title: A CPU-Only Framework for Mining Collectively-Behaving Bots in MMORPGs
Authors: Hyunsoo Kim, Jaeman Son, Sangdeok Seo, Ilhan Bang, Man Jae Cho
ID: 2611.30502
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30502

Abstract:
MMORPGs continually face automated programs (bots) that disrupt gameplay balance
and in-game economies. An unsupervised Transformer framework can mine
collectively behaving bot groups, but its multi-GPU training is costly in live
operations. We show that such GPU-trained sequence models are not strictly
necessary for this task. Our CPU pipeline pairs zone embeddings learned from
movement context, which place teleport-linked zones close together, with a
training-free coordinate kernel, and then applies density clustering. It trains
3-5$\times$ faster than the GPU-based Transformer (56-86$\times$ on the same
CPU) while matching its mining quality on two commercial MMORPGs, and has been
deployed since late 2024. We also share deployment lessons on how to set the
clustering threshold and when to retrain.
