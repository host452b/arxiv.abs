Title: Inverse Kinematics in Endoscopic Scenarios using MoveIt
Authors: Marc Bestmann, Timon Engelke, Rebecca Rodeck, Gerko Wende
ID: 2611.20978
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20978

Abstract:
Inverse kinematics are critical in many robotic applications, yet standard
MoveIt-based solvers struggle in confined scenarios. This paper provides a
benchmark of the available solvers and compares multiple practical modifications
for improving the performance of the solvers. Splitting the IK problem into two
parts results in the best performance, leading to a 100% success rate under 100
ms in an endoscopic tank scenario.
