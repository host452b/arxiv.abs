Title: On rankings in multiplayer games with an application to the game of Whist
Authors: Alexis Coyette, Charles Modera, Candy Sonveaux, Judicaël Mohet, Francçois-Grégoire Bierwart, Sylverio Pool Marquez, Jarod Ketcha Kouakep, Cédric Simal, Komlan Fiagbe, Violaine Piengeon et al.
ID: 2604.00641
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00641

Abstract:
We propose a novel extension of the Bradley-Terry model to multiplayer games and
adapt a recent algorithm by Newman [1] to our model. We demonstrate the use of
our proposed method on synthetic datasets and on a real dataset of games of
cards.
