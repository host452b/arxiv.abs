Title: DIFUZZI: A Differential Fuzzing Tool for Code Functional Similarity Assessment
Authors: Simantika Bhattacharjee Dristi, Matthew B. Dwyer
ID: 2610.38337
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38337

Abstract:
A range of software engineering tasks, such as optimization and refactoring,
seek to preserve the behavior of software components and require methods to
assess functional similarity. Research has explored formal approaches to prove
functional equivalence and used machine learning methods to approximate it, but
the former does not scale and does not quantify the extent of similarity and the
latter struggles when subtle semantic differences are present. Differential
fuzzing offers a scalable approach for differencing programs semantically, but
applying it often requires substantial manual effort to construct input
generators that are well-suited to a target pair of programs. To address this
challenge, we introduce DIFUZZI, which incorporates an LLM-based generator
synthesis step that is iteratively validated and repaired. By automating this
step, DIFUZZI paves the way for broader applicability of differential fuzzing.
We release DIFUZZI at https://github.com/Dristi123/difuzzi_tool, with a
demonstration video at https://youtu.be/FlHNOHuo9WE
