Title: DruMR: A Feedback Driven Mixed Reality Air Drumming System
Authors: Omar Elgohary, Viktor Radev, James Herbert, Akul Manoj
ID: 2611.02447
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.02447

Abstract:
This paper introduces DruMR, a mixed reality (MR) drumming system designed to
make learning drums more accessible, responsive, and feedback-driven. By
combining Aerodrums hardware with the Meta Quest 3 headset, DruMR allows users
to drum in a virtual environment without a physical drum set. Unlike existing VR
drumming applications, DruMR emphasizes instructional value through quantitative
feedback and future integration of AI-generated insights, refined quantitative
and qualitative visualizations, reactive environment modes and more. Overall,
this paper reviews the current state of the DruMR system, related works that
influenced the systems development, planned user study comparing physical and
virtual training, and future work to be added to the system.
