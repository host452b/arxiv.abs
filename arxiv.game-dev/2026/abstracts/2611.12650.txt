Title: Explicit LOD-Conditioned Anisotropic Filtering for Neural Texture Compression
Authors: Shin Fujieda, Takahiro Harada
ID: 2611.12650
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12650

Abstract:
Neural texture compression (NTC) stores material textures compactly, but the
dominant feature-pyramid design receives no filtering from the GPU texture unit:
it learns each material at a fixed UV and level of detail and has no explicit
anisotropic-filtering (AF) mechanism. Setting the filter from the footprint
aspect ratio alone is not enough: a single input cannot carry both the shape of
the footprint and its size, so the image is too blurry near the camera by the
same amount at every distance. Our method, AF-NTC, instead conditions the
decoder on the same three bounded values a hardware sampler uses: minor-axis
scale (LOD), aspect ratio, and major-axis orientation. These drive a tap line
along the major axis and a level blend across the minor axis, and we train
against anisotropically filtered ground truth. At $25\%$ of a full BC7 mip
chain, AF-NTC beats the no-AF trilinear-BC7 baseline on all ten material--scene
pairs we compare ($+1.3$ to $+7.6$\,dB), and it is $0.0$ to $3.4$\,dB below the
$4\times$-larger anisotropic-BC7, depending on the material.
