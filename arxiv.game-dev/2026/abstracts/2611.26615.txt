Title: ScaleWorld: High-Resolution World Generation through Shared Prediction and Refinement
Authors: Yansong Qu, Wesker Zheng, Quanjian Song, Zhiyuan Zhang, Xiao Cao, Shengchuan Zhang, Liujuan Cao
ID: 2611.26615
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26615

Abstract:
High-resolution generation enables video world models to render fine-grained
scene structures and textures, but directly generating high-resolution video
incurs substantial computational and memory costs. We present ScaleWorld, a
causal video model that unifies low-resolution world prediction and high-
resolution refinement within a single shared backbone. By refining its own
predictions, ScaleWorld produces high-resolution world videos without requiring
a separate refinement model. For each video chunk, the shared backbone first
completes a low-resolution prediction and then performs one-step high-resolution
refinement conditioned on that prediction. To support efficient streaming
generation, we maintain separate causal states with a long low-resolution
history for prediction and a short high-resolution history for refinement. To
address task interference during distillation, we separate specialist
optimization from capability consolidation and introduce task-asymmetric on-
policy distillation (TA-OPD). This procedure transfers prediction and refinement
behaviors from specialized teachers into a shared student through local velocity
matching under their respective sampling and history policies. {Extensive
experiments demonstrate that ScaleWorld achieves high-quality world generation
with improved computational and memory efficiency.
