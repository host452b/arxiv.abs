Title: A Model-Centric DevOps Architecture for DEVS-Based Digital Twin Simulation Services
Authors: Arnis Lektauers, Gusts Linkevičs, Guntis Mosāns, Arina Fokina, Rasa Gulbe
ID: 2609.11122
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11122

Abstract:
Digital twin simulation models evolve and are redeployed like software. Engines
based on the Discrete Event System Specification (DEVS) have a sound formal
basis but offer little support for versioning, automated validation, or
continuous delivery in cloud-native environments. This paper proposes a model-
centric DevOps architecture that deploys DEVS-based digital twin simulations as
managed services. Models are first-class DevOps artefacts written in a
declarative language based on YAML (YAML Ain't Markup Language) with a tuple-
level mapping to the multiPDEVS formalism. Repository continuous integration
(CI) validates publishing manifests, the engine loader performs syntactic,
structural, and static semantic checks before every simulation, and published
model artefacts are versioned and immutable, so that an earlier validated
version can be selected by pinning its identifier. The platform runs as
containerised microservices on Kubernetes with engine adaptations that
externalise inputs and results and expose lifecycle control. An initial case
study on bus route 22 in Riga exercises the lifecycle on an offline model with
roughly 115,000 DEVS atomic components and reports single-container throughput
and concurrent job execution on one Kubernetes node. Behavioural validation and
multi-node evaluation remain future work.
