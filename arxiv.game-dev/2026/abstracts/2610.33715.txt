Title: Quantum Closed Loop Inverse Kinematics Through Hilbert-Space Encoding of Robot Kinematics
Authors: Andrea Morghen, Pierluigi Arpenti, Roberto Schiattarella, Giovanni Acampora, Bruno Siciliano
ID: 2610.33715
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33715

Abstract:
The integration of quantum computing into robotics requires mathematical
representations that preserve the geometric structure and configuration
dependence of robot motion. This paper introduces a structure-preserving quantum
representation of rigid-body transformations and serial-manipulator kinematics,
establishing a correspondence between classical kinematic models and
parameterized quantum circuits. Building on the Bloch-sphere representation,
spatial directions are encoded into single-qubit states, while quantum rotations
reproduce the ordered composition of reference-frame transformations.
Translational contributions are combined through quantum-state aggregation, with
their metric scales retained classically, enabling the reconstruction of end-
effector position and orientation from Pauli expectation values. The formulation
preserves the original joint variables and accommodates different kinematic
conventions and joint types. Beyond forward kinematics, we establish that the
manipulator geometric Jacobian can be reconstructed from quantum-observable
derivatives using the parameter-shift rule, with analytical equivalence to its
classical counterpart under ideal readout. To demonstrate the applicability of
this representation to robot-level algorithms, we introduce Quantum Closed Loop
Inverse Kinematics, a hybrid closed-loop method that exploits quantum-derived
pose and Jacobian information. The proposed framework provides a geometrically
interpretable foundation for developing and investigating quantum and hybrid
computational methods for robotic kinematics and motion.
