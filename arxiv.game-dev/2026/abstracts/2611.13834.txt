Title: MusicFlux: Flexible Conditioning for Text-Guided Music Generation and Editing
Authors: Teysir Baoueb, Leonardo Boulitreau de Menezes Martins Marques, Paraskevas Stamatiadis, Ga{ë}l Richard
ID: 2611.13834
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13834

Abstract:
Music editing transforms existing recordings while preserving their original
attributes. Recent approaches adapt pretrained text-to-music models for tasks
like inpainting, event insertion, or restoration by conditioning them with
context audio. It was recently proposed for image editing to unify generation
and editing by treating the source image as in-context information. However,
applying this directly to music is computationally expensive due to long audio
sequences. In this work, we develop MusicFlux, a text-guided music editor built
on an efficient pretrained flow-matching text-to-audio model. We introduce two
conditioning approaches that trade off editing performance and computational
efficiency. Experiments show that both are competitive with strong baselines,
with one achieving better editing quality and the other preserving the original
sequence length and substantially improving computational efficiency.
