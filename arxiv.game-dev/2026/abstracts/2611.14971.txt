Title: Can Music Generation Models Tap Along? Evaluating Generative Approaches to Beat Tracking
Authors: Kun Fang, Ziyu Wang, Ichiro Fujinaga
ID: 2611.14971
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14971

Abstract:
Existing studies have shown that music generative models can generate
synchronized tracks that play alongside an input song. Can we extend this
capability to develop a generative approach to beat tracking? To answer this
question, we equip a pretrained MusicGen model with an audio-conditioning
adapter to generate percussion tracks synchronized with the input music, from
which we extract the beat positions. We train separate models with percussion
targets of varying rhythmic complexity and compare them with two other
generative models and conventional beat trackers. On GTZAN, our model achieves
performance close to state-of-the-art beat trackers. Our model also demonstrates
greater robustness: it shows the smallest drop in F-measure when drums are
removed from the input at inference time. In certain genres, such as metal and
disco, our model is also less likely to be misled by strong onsets or to miss
beats with weak onsets. These results suggest that percussion generation has the
potential to become a viable approach to beat tracking, and that its output is
not merely a sequence of beat positions but an audible percussion track.
