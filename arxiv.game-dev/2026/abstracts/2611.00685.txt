Title: Text-to-music Generation via Structured Captions AND Interpretable Evaluation
Authors: Junkai Wu, Zhepei Wang, Ge Zhu, Sarah Hye-yeon Lee, Mari Ostendorf, Nicholas J. Bryan
ID: 2611.00685
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00685

Abstract:
Text descriptions of music are central to the performance of text-to-music
generation (TTM) methods, but creation of paired text information for music
generation is relatively under explored. Here, we propose training latent
diffusion/flow-based music generation models conditioned on structured captions
(SCAPs) and compare with typical free-form captions via a set of controlled
ablations. We compare free-form vs. structured captions, partial vs. full
information coverage, and global vs. temporal text information during training,
by fine-tuning different configurations of a pretrained flow-based diffusion
transformer (DiT) and using a multi-stage inference-time prompt rewriter.
Further, we introduce a new text-alignment evaluation protocol inspired by
Davidsonian Scene Graphs (DSGs) for a fine-grained, interpretable measure of
faithfulness of TTM methods. With multiple metrics, we show 1) that fine-tuning
our DiT on SCAPs significantly improves text-alignment on music generation
outputs while also improving quality, and 2) our proposed Music DSG metric
better correlates with human preference than CLAP.
