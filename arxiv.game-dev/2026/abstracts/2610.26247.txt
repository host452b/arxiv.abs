Title: Bridging In-Situ Visualization to Game Engines: From ParaView Catalyst via ANARI to Unreal Engine
Authors: Thomas George, Severin A. T. Klapproth, Jens Henrik Göbbert, Jonathan Windgassen, Andreas Adelmann, Sriramkrishnan Muralikrishnan
ID: 2610.26247
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26247

Abstract:
In-situ high-performance computing (HPC) simulations produce massive datasets
that remain difficult to stream into game engines for interactive visualization.
We present an in-situ, disk-free pipeline that couples simulation data to Unreal
Engine 5 through ParaView's ANARI rendering API. A modified ANARI-USD device
captures Universal Scene Description (USD) scene graphs in memory at the ANARI
layer and streams the engine-agnostic USD format through an embedded ZeroMQ
broker to visualization clients, where JuSync, middleware with a companion
Unreal Engine 5 plugin, parses the geometry as it arrives and routes it to
engine rendering components at runtime, producing scenes that are camera-
navigable once received. The decoupled design keeps simulation execution
independent of visualization load, and clients may connect or disconnect at any
time. The pipeline is demonstrated on the JURECA-DC supercomputer at
Forschungszentrum Jülich, streaming Free Electron Laser (FEL) simulation data
with point clouds of tens of millions of particles.
