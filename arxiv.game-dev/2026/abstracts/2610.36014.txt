Title: Immersive Commons: Designing Ephemeral Spatial Dialogue for Co-Located Mixed Reality Environments
Authors: Wuwu Ran, Mohammed Faraj Al-Suwaidi
ID: 2610.36014
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36014

Abstract:
Head-mounted mixed reality (MR) often prioritises individual immersion,
potentially drawing attention away from others sharing the room. This paper asks
how MR can support co-located spatial dialogue through embodied interaction and
activity visible to non-headset observers. We introduce Immersive Commons, a
design framework, and Atmos Messages, a prototype combining gesture-based,
ephemeral spatial messages with voice transcription, preset text, and a public
floor-plan overview. Following a Research through Design approach, fifteen
participants created 236 messages across two sessions, including 48 voice-
authored messages. Questionnaire responses highlighted playfulness and social
awareness, while observations suggested opportunities for exchange grounded in
the architectural setting. Interaction clustered near the public display,
although this was also where participants put on their headsets. These findings
suggest the potential of ephemeral spatial messaging to support co-located
exchange, with playfulness, shared visibility, and architectural context
informing the design of more social and situated MR experiences.
