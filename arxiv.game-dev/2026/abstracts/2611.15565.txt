Title: Generalized Inverse Kinematics via Conditional Flow
Authors: Kaitlyn Zhang, Tomás Lozano-Pérez, Leslie Pack Kaelbling
ID: 2611.15565
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15565

Abstract:
Inverse kinematics (IK) is a foundational primitive in robotics, mapping the
task-space objectives expressed by higher-level planners to robot configurations
that realize them. Traditional IK methods generally treat each query as an
isolated pose-to-configuration problem, but planners often need a distribution
over valid realizations that accounts for future reachability and preferences
such as collision avoidance and stability. We introduce GenGenIK, a generative
modeling approach that supports mobile bases, multiple end-effectors and
targets, scene awareness, and preferences over robot behavior within a common
interface. Experiments show that GenGenIK outperforms existing methods in both
solve rate and runtime across tasks such as bimanual lift and flip-up, while
allowing flexible control over the sampled distribution and remaining effective
under partial scene observations. Website:
https://icra-2027-gengenik.github.io/.
