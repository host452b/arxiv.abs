Title: How Close to the Theme? Similarity-Label Conditioning for Game Music Generation
Authors: Shangxuan Luo, Zixun Guo, Jinjie Shi, Joshua Reiss
ID: 2611.01047
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01047

Abstract:
Composers develop music from an original idea, controlling how similar each new
section is to it, from near repetition to new material. Game music needs this
often, as its material repeats over long play. Music models, however, rarely
learn to control similarity from real pieces. We further train a pretrained MIDI
model on section pairs from Super Mario pieces. A label states each pair's
similarity, and a context vector summarises the given section, or context. For
157 test contexts, the model generates six outputs per label. Twelve measures
score the outputs; one main measure each covers melody, pitch content, rhythm
and note length. On average, outputs requested as similar are more similar to
their context than those requested as dissimilar, and not to other pieces. All
twelve measures favour them; each main measure stays significant after
correction for multiple tests. On melody, the gap comes mainly from more
frequent restatement of the context's melody. Against an otherwise identical
model without a label, only the request for high similarity makes outputs more
similar. In a listening test, ratings followed melodic overlap with the context
most closely of all measures.
