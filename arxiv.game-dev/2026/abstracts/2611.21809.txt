Title: Evaluating the Evaluation of Music Generation Models with Generative Psychometrics
Authors: João Carlos Santana Silveira, Caio Von Rondow Morais, Frederico Pedrosa, Lucas N. Ferreira
ID: 2611.21809
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21809

Abstract:
Subjective user studies are one of the major methods for evaluating music
generation models. However, subjective evaluation protocols vary widely and are
rarely specified in enough detail to be reproduced, hindering comparison across
studies. In this paper, we map subjective evaluation methods in ISMIR music
generation papers from 2020-2025 and apply generative psychometrics to assess
the stability of their items. We extract 284 items from the papers'
methodological descriptions, using an LLM and manual validation to standardize
them. We represent these items as contextual embeddings and apply Unique
Variable Analysis to remove redundant, locally dependent items and Bootstrap
Exploratory Graph Analysis to estimate their latent dimensions. Results show the
network converges onto a taxonomy of six stable quality dimensions: Rhythm,
Melody, Audio, Composition, Structure, and Lyrics, pointing to a unified
methodology for conducting user studies to evaluate music generation models.
