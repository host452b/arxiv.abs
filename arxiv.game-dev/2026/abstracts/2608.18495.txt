Title: Physics-Unrolled Neural Operator for Accelerating High-Fidelity Wireless Ray Tracing
Authors: Rafid Umayer Murshed, Saif Ur Rahman, Mingyue Tang, Elahe Soltanaghai
ID: 2608.18495
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18495

Abstract:
Site-specific wireless design relies on ray tracing to predict how signal
strength varies across a floorplan, but a converged indoor radio map takes tens
of seconds to simulate and planning a single building requires thousands of
them. A learned surrogate can remove most of that cost, yet training one runs
into a second problem: high-fidelity labels are exactly what is too expensive to
produce at scale, so the affordable labels come from finite-ray simulations that
carry residual Monte Carlo noise. We address this with RayNO, a physics-unrolled
neural operator that reconstructs the converged output of a ray tracer from one
cheap low-fidelity run and scene priors. Rather than treating a radio map as a
generic image, RayNO refines the field through three stages matched to
reflection, diffraction, and scattering, each supervised by a label of matching
physical complexity. We prove that when the label error is conditionally
unbiased the learned operator can end up closer to the high-fidelity reference
than the labels it was trained on, and we verify that condition directly in the
simulator using repeated independent ray seeds. Across diverse floorplans, RayNO
outperforms image-to-image baselines, wireless learning models, monolithic
neural operators, and classical multi-fidelity regression on both image-quality
and wireless deployment metrics, while reducing the cost of a high-fidelity map
from roughly 28 s to 0.11 s.
