Title: artist: A Python package for differentiable ray tracing and gradient-based optimization of solar tower power plant operation
Authors: Marlene Busch, Marie Weiel, Kaleb Phipps, Daniel Maldonado Quinto, Max Pargmann, Robert Pitz-Paal, Markus Götz
ID: 2611.20851
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20851

Abstract:
Solar tower power plants provide dispatchable, renewable energy by concentrating
sunlight with thousands of mirrors, called heliostats, onto a central receiver.
Efficient operation requires accurate modeling of the optical path from each
heliostat to the receiver and compensation for manufacturing imperfections,
kinematic misalignment, and material degradation that distort the flux
distribution. Conventional ray tracers predict the flux from an assumed plant
model but are limited to forward simulation. They cannot recover unknown plant
parameters from measured flux nor directly optimize plant operation. We present
artist, an open-source, FAIR4RS-compliant Python package implementing a fully
differentiable digital twin of solar tower power plants. By making the optical
pipeline, comprising heliostat surface representation, kinematics, blocking, and
ray tracing, end to end differentiable, artist turns the digital twin from a
passive forward model into an active physics-based tool for inverse modeling and
data-driven optimization. The resulting gradient-based framework unifies tasks
usually handled by separate solutions, such as reconstructing heliostat surfaces
and kinematics from operational flux images without costly deflectometry,
predicting irradiance at the receiver, and optimizing aim points to realize a
desired receiver flux density distribution. Built on PyTorch, artist scales to
real plants through GPU acceleration, a structure-of-arrays memory layout, and
nested data parallelism. It interfaces with open data sources such as the PAINT
database. We describe the software architecture, physical models, and
optimization framework, and demonstrate the workflow on representative tasks. By
integrating modeling, prediction, and optimization into a single open,
interpretable package, artist provides a principled foundation for data-driven
research toward autonomous solar tower operation.
