Title: GI-RTL-Fuzz: Grammar-Informed RTL Generation with LLMs for End-to-End Differential Simulator Fuzzing
Authors: Ziyue Zheng, Zhi Qu, Yangdi Lyu
ID: 2610.38489
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38489

Abstract:
Register-Transfer Level (RTL) simulators are fundamental to modern IC design
workflows, yet like any complex software, they frequently contain critical bugs
that compromise simulation accuracy. To systematically stress-test these tools,
we introduce GI-RTL-Fuzz, a grammar-informed fuzzing framework that leverages
Large Language Models (LLMs) to generate high-quality RTL test cases. Our key
innovation is guiding the LLM's generation process using Backus-Naur Form (BNF)
grammar coverage information, enabling the exploration of complex and rarely-
tested language features. By performing differential testing against different
RTL simulators, our framework uncovered a significant number of previously
unknown inconsistencies in both the linting process and simulation results of
major Verilog simulators.
