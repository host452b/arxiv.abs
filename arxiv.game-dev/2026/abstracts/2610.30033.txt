Title: RLMuscle: Muscle-Driven Character Animation via Reinforcement Learning
Authors: Chunlei Li, Siyuan Yu, Yang Gao, Shuai Li, Peng Yu, Aimin Hao, He Wang
ID: 2610.30033
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30033

Abstract:
Body deformation caused by muscle contraction plays a vital role in the visual
realism of character animation, yet in most 3D animation pipelines artists have
to manually specify muscle activations, which is time-consuming and gives no
guarantee of physical realism. To address this, we propose a new method in which
a reinforcement learning (RL) controller discovers per-muscle activations that
track reference motions without manual tuning. Existing RL muscle controllers
stop at 1D muscles; we go one step further, keeping the 1D model inside the RL
control loop as a fast surrogate, and using a 3D volumetric simulation to
produce the final deformation. Because both stages share the very same
constitutive law, the muscle deformation is produced by the same activations
that generated the motion. We further add a measured electromyography (EMG)
prior that steers the redundant activation solution toward real human muscle
recruitment. To achieve stable, efficient, and realistic simulation, we propose
a novel geometric multigrid for Extended Position-Based Dynamics (XPBD) that is
built on a coarse tetrahedral cage with barycentric-coordinate prolongation and
closes a V-cycle on tetrahedral meshes. Within this GPU-parallelized solver, we
inject the same Hill-type force-length law as an energy-based fiber constraint
that turns per-fiber activation into realistic muscle contraction and bulging.
We demonstrate volumetric muscle simulation on a 194-muscle human body with a
diverse set of motions (walk, kick, swim, overhead-squat, etc.), simulating the
full-body model at 18 ms per frame on a single RTX 4090. Our work takes a step
toward character animation that is not only visually convincing but also
biomechanically grounded. The website with code and videos is at
https://chunleili.github.io/project-page-rlmuscle/.
