Title: MIDI-REPA: Improving Text-to-Music Generation with Symbolic Representation Alignment
Authors: Song-Ze Yu, Fang-Duo Tsai, Yi-Hsuan Yang
ID: 2610.20967
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20967

Abstract:
State-of-the-art text-to-music models increasingly rely on scaling model
capacity, data, and compute, while still learning musical structure directly
from dense audio. Yet melody, rhythm, harmony, and structure can be represented
compactly in symbolic form. We introduce MIDI-REPA, a representation-alignment
framework that derives MIDI supervision from automatic music transcription (AMT)
to align intermediate representations of a flow-matching DiT. Building on the
122M-parameter FluxAudio-S baseline from the 2026 ICME Academic Text-To-Music
Challenge, we investigate what, where, and when to align. Aligning a MuScriptor-
derived piano roll with an early DiT layer throughout the full flow trajectory
performs the best. Without additional training data or techniques, MIDI-REPA
remains competitive on the ATTM objective metrics while remaining trainable on a
single RTX 3090 in about 31 hours. Our results highlight symbolic supervision as
a simple and inexpensive learning signal for improving text-to-music generation.
We release our transcription pipeline, JamendoMIDI, training code, and audio
samples at https://midi-repa.vaclis.net.
