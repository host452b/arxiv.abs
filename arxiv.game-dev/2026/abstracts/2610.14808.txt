Title: Testuary: Agentic Test Generation with Zero-Shot Parallelism
Authors: Kevin Kammler, Dominik Sobania
ID: 2610.14808
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14808

Abstract:
Agentic approaches for automated test generation can reach high-quality results,
but they improve a given test suite incrementally, so a weak starting point may
lead to many iterations or insufficient tests. To address this, we present
Testuary, which provides the agentic phases with an improved starting point.
Testuary first generates multiple test suites through independent, parallel
zero-shot requests, checks and repairs them deterministically, and combines them
into a single suite with a novel structural merging approach. A runtime-weighted
set-cover reduction removes redundant tests without losing covered lines,
branches, or killed mutants. After that, two agentic phases target the remaining
gaps in coverage and mutation score. We compare Testuary with established search
and LLM-based test generation approaches. The non-agentic first phase alone
already significantly outperforms the current state-of-the-art. After the
agentic phases, Testuary achieves on average more than 99.9% line and branch
coverage as well as a mutation score of 98.6%, significantly outperforming all
compared baseline approaches. Testuary needs fewer model requests and its test
suites are on average smaller and faster than those of the compared agentic
approach.
