Title: SCALE: Real-Time Latent Communication for Cyber-Physical Agents
Authors: Shanghao Shi, Jian Xiao, Ching-Hsiang Chan, Sanjoy Baruah, Sastry Kompella, Yevgeniy Vorobeychik, William Yeoh, Ning Zhang
ID: 2611.08390
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08390

Abstract:
Latent communication enables agents to collaborate efficiently by directly
exchanging intermediate model representations rather than raw sensor data or
text messages. Existing approaches, however, are primarily designed for LLM
agents and do not consider the bandwidth constraints and heterogeneous task
deadlines faced by cyber-physical agents. In this work, we propose SCALE, a
real-time latent communication method tailored for cyber-physical agents.
Motivated by the spatio-temporal continuity of the physical world, SCALE matches
tokens across consecutive latent messages using similarity scores to identify
reusable information and avoid redundant transmission. It then profiles the
semantic gain and communication cost of transmitting different amounts of latent
information. Based on these profiles, SCALE employs a real-time scheduler that
jointly determines which pending messages to transmit and how many latent tokens
to send, subject to available bandwidth and task deadlines. We evaluate SCALE
across three cyber-physical benchmarks and a physical spider robot case study
under diverse network conditions and heterogeneous task deadlines. Our results
show that SCALE significantly improves both task performance and on-time
delivery in bandwidth-constrained networks. Our code is available at:
https://anonymous.4open.science/r/SCALE-4E80/.
