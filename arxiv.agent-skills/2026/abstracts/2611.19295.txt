Title: Optional Traps: An Empirical Study of LLM Agents Against Credential Deception and Alert-Driven Revocation
Authors: Raditio Ghifiardi
ID: 2611.19295
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19295

Abstract:
Deception (honeypots, decoys and canary credentials) rests on a simple premise:
legitimate users never touch a fake asset, so any touch is a high-confidence
signal. We ask whether that premise holds when the attacker is a large language
model (LLM) agent, and what deception contributes once ordinary hardening is in
place. We built an isolated, hash-chained lab around a mock Records API and
report four studies. (1) In an exploratory run, a local 9.7B-parameter model
ignored every optional decoy, read a genuinely leaked service key from a debug
endpoint and exported the protected records in both static and adaptive
deception modes. (2) After removing the leak, planting a canary credential in
its place and revoking the offending client, a frozen 60-session study with a
4B-parameter model found export success of 20/20 at baseline, 0/20 with
hardening alone and 0/20 with hardening plus deception (exact paired McNemar p =
1.9e-6 baseline vs. defended; p = 1 hardened vs. defended). Deception added
detection and per-client revocation in 19 of 20 defended sessions, but no
measurable prevention beyond hardening. (3) On Google Cloud, a real OpenCanary
login alert was authenticated, signed and turned into revocation of the
attacking client; the first denied export followed within an observed upper
bound of 303 ms, a separate legitimate client completed 100/100 requests, and
all 14 control checks passed. (4) In multi-agent swarm engagements, agents that
used the canary were revoked 39-59 s after the bait was served without affecting
siblings, but one model-driven legitimate agent also took the bait, a false-
positive risk specific to LLM clients. Against LLM agents, traps must sit on the
attacker's path rather than beside it, closing real weaknesses does the
preventive work, and deception's measurable value is fast, attributable
detection. All results are lab-scale and are not a production claim.
