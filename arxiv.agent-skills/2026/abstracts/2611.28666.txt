Title: RecallWorld: Episodic Memory for Persistent Embodied Video World Models
Authors: Sarosij Bose, Gitika Karumuri, Chenqi Li, Allen Tu, Srikumar Sastry, Mellon Zhang, Avinash Ravichandran, Alexander Schwing, Konstantinos Karydis, Amit K. Roy Chowdhury
ID: 2611.28666
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28666

Abstract:
Video world models generate plausible novel views but often lack persistent
memory, failing to preserve a place's geometry and appearance when an agent
revisits a scene. A central challenge is how previous views should be
effectively used for creating memory. Reconstructing them independently
underutilizes geometric constraints between nearby views, while repeatedly
reconstructing a growing history can propagate later prediction errors into
earlier geometry. We introduce RecallWorld, a persistent embodied video world
model that jointly reconstructs short multi-view episodes into Scenelets while
keeping geometry independent across episodes. Starting from a single source
image, camera-aware ray lifting places perspective and native fisheye images in
a common world frame, retaining wide-field content for perspective generation.
Each generated segment extends the Scenelet memory and conditions subsequent
generation. Coverage-based retrieval selects complementary Scenelets from the
full history, while target-view visibility is resolved geometrically before
appearance fusion, providing consistent memory conditioning for guiding
subsequent generation. Across four datasets, RecallWorld improves revisit PSNR
over the strongest evaluated baseline by 2.6 dB on partial revisits and 3.9 dB
on full cycles, demonstrating substantially stronger preservation of scene
identity across exploration and return.
