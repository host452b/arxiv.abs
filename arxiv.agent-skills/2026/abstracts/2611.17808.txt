Title: CarryOn: Learning What to Keep in Context for Long-Horizon LLM Agents
Authors: Lihuan Li, Ruiyi Yang, Jiru Wang, Yifan Duan, Xiachong Lin, Hao Xue, Yang Song, Flora Salim
ID: 2611.17808
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17808

Abstract:
Long-horizon LLM agents can coordinate tools and applications to complete
complex, multi-step tasks. As their interaction histories grow, effective
context management becomes essential for reliable and efficient execution.
Existing methods reduce context through summarization, pruning, or history
selection. However, shorter contexts do not necessarily support better
decisions: summaries can omit exact details needed for later actions, while
retaining entire interactions can carry substantial irrelevant information. We
propose CarryOn, a lightweight context manager that pairs compact execution
state with fine-grained, source-linked evidence. A learned selector and adaptive
retention policy keep relevant evidence available without additional LLM calls
for context management. We introduce two complementary evaluation metrics: $S_α$
prioritizes task success while accounting for resource use, and normalized cost
per success (NCS) measures the resources spent per successful task. Across
AppWorld, OfficeBench, and Tau2 Telecom with three LLM Actors, CarryOn achieves
75.7% macro-averaged task success, exceeding the strongest baseline by 6.7
percentage points. It also achieves the best average scores on both metrics.
