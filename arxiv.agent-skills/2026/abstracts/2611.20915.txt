Title: Multi-LLM Agentic AI at the Edge: Joint Agent Scheduling and Resource Allocation
Authors: Yuanyan Song, Kezhi Wang, Xinmian Xu, Yongmin Li
ID: 2611.20915
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20915

Abstract:
In this paper, an edge-enabled multi-large language model (LLM) agentic AI
architecture is proposed, where one supervisor AI agent and multiple worker AI
agents cooperatively execute users' requests at the edge. The supervisor AI
agent decouples the user request into a series of sub-tasks and dynamically
dispatches them to LLM-equipped worker AI agents via two agentic workflows,
i.e., parallel and serial workflows, with a two-level retry mechanism to enhance
execution reliability. To prolong the service duration of worker AI agents, the
energy minimization problems are formulated by jointly considering AI agent
selection and resource allocation, subject to task execution latency and quality
of service (QoS) constraints. A resource allocation scheme based on alternating
optimization (AO) is proposed. Specifically, for the parallel workflow, the
scheme decouples the original problem into bandwidth allocation, retrieval
selection and AI agent selection sub-problems, which are iteratively solved by
the proposed successive convex approximation (SCA), Lagrangian relaxation (LR),
and Gale-Shapley (GS) or LLM-based algorithms, respectively. For the serial
workflow, the problem is similarly decoupled into service time allocation,
retrieval selection and AI agent selection sub-problems; the optimal service
time allocation is obtained via Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) conditions, while the
latter two sub-problems are addressed by extending the proposed algorithms to
the serial scenario. Simulation results show that the proposed resource
allocation scheme can reduce system energy consumption compared with other
benchmarks, while experiments on a multi-LLM agentic AI system based on Qwen and
Llama demonstrate improved task execution success rates.
