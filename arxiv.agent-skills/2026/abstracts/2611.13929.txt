Title: ReACT: Reflection-Consistent Modulation and Cross-Load Consistency for Humanoid Velocity Tracking Under Asymmetric Loads
Authors: Xianghui Wang, Aoran Fu, Liyou Luo, Shanze Wang, Xiaoyu Shen, Dan Zhang, Wei Zhang
ID: 2611.13929
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13929

Abstract:
In recent years, humanoid loco-manipulation has progressed to whole-body
grasping, lifting, and transport. A basic requirement underlying these tasks is
carrying the object to its destination, which depends on the locomotion
controller accurately executing velocity commands from the navigation policy.
However, carrying an object in one hand introduces a persistent asymmetric
upper-body load that can degrade velocity tracking. Persistent tracking biases
can accumulate into position and heading deviations, increasing the risk of
collisions and navigation failure. To address this problem, we present ReACT, a
framework for accurate velocity tracking under asymmetric upper-body loads.
Reflection-consistent disturbance modulation uses the estimated wrench to
modulate actor features for load compensation while preserving action
correspondence under reflected observations. Cross-load tracking consistency
training compares loaded and unloaded conditions under the same velocity
commands to promote consistent velocity tracking across loading conditions.
Simulation experiments on a Unitree G1 humanoid robot show improved velocity
tracking and reduced directional bias under varying asymmetric loads. Real-world
experiments demonstrate collision-free goal reaching in all 40 tested navigation
trials under unilateral hand loads with a fixed mapless navigation policy.
