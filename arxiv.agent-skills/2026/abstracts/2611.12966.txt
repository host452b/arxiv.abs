Title: BuySideFlow: A Point-in-Time Benchmark for LLM Agents in Investment Research
Authors: Dong Bo, Fanjin Zhang, Ke Cheng, Xinxin Zhao, Xiaowei Su, Yang Wang, Jinghang Xu, Cuiping Li, Hong Chen, Jing Zhang
ID: 2611.12966
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12966

Abstract:
Large language models (LLMs) can generate SQL and SQL--Python workflows for
investment research, but ensuring point-in-time (PIT) validity remains
challenging. Agents must enforce disclosure cutoffs and select the appropriate
historical states and record versions throughout data retrieval and financial
analysis. We introduce \textsc{BuySideFlow}, a benchmark of 408 practitioner-
derived Chinese-language tasks spanning equities, funds, fixed income, and
macroeconomics over a fixed snapshot from a licensed production financial
database. We provide executable references and a taxonomy of temporal errors,
pairing result checks with expert audits of executed workflows. We evaluate nine
agent configurations on all tasks, with result accuracy ranging from 16.2\% to
36.8\%. An audit of 900 runs on 100 shared tasks confirms PIT violations in 193
of the 380 runs that pass result checks (50.8\%), showing that output agreement
alone does not establish temporal validity.
