Title: An LLM-Agentic Framework for Intelligent Work Order Management in Industrial Maintenance
Authors: Nianjun Zhou, Dhaval Patel
ID: 2611.25070
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25070

Abstract:
This paper presents an AI-driven framework, CodeReAct agent, designed to enhance
industrial equipment maintenance through natural language (NL) interactions. The
framework employs an advanced AI agentic approach to interpret human
instructions, providing knowledge extraction, asset status summarization, and
work order recommendations. It identifies patterns and trends and predicts work
orders by standardizing industrial data into business objects (BOs) and
utilizing analytical function API calls. The AI agent operates through a
Thought-Action-Observation process similar to human problem-solving, automating
complex analytical tasks typically performed by data scientists. Notably, the
Action phase generates executable code, replacing manual coding efforts. A case
study focusing on chiller maintenance demonstrates the framework's capability to
analyze multiple years of work orders, and alerts and anomalies are presented.
The results indicate that the AI agent framework effectively processes human
utterances to analyze data, extract valuable insights, and support informed
decision-making, reducing the need for data scientist involvement. By
recontextualizing NLP agent frameworks in industrial engineering, the approach
enables autonomous function generation and task execution across a range of
domain-specific maintenance scenarios, with chillers used as a representative
case study.
