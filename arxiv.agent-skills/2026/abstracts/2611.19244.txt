Title: Agentic On-Policy Distillation Implicitly Reshapes LLMs' ReAct Patterns
Authors: Deyang Kong, Zhaoyi Li, Sijian Ren, Ying Wei, Defu Lian, Shikun Zhang, Wei Ye
ID: 2611.19244
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19244

Abstract:
On-policy distillation (OPD) transfers specialized capabilities to language-
model agents, but its cross-domain effects on interaction remain unclear. We
investigate how OPD reshapes ReAct interaction patterns across mathematics,
ALFWorld, and $τ^2$-bench, focusing on reasoning, actions, and observation
acquisition. General-reasoning supervision reduces mathematical tool attempts
from 70.4% to 14.4% while increasing accuracy from 16.7% to 38.0%; code-
interpreter supervision improves household actions and retail queries. In
contrast, ALFWorld supervision reduces $τ^2$-bench accuracy from 41.5% to 6.6%,
accompanied by frequent tool calls that violate user-information dependencies.
To address negative transfer using only source-domain training trajectories, we
explore selective OPD guided by teacher preference changes before and after
domain-specific RL. Retaining the top 95% of positions raises $τ^2$ accuracy
from 11.5% to 29.9% with a source-learning trade-off.
