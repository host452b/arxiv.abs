Title: Review the Episodes, Refine the Lessons: Simple Memory Evolution for Embodied Agents
Authors: Yifei Yao, Yude Zou, Xing Gao, Guanbo Jiao, Yukai Wang, Jianming Ma, Weinan Zhang, Chunhua Shen
ID: 2611.11509
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11509

Abstract:
Recent studies of embodied agents based on frontier multimodal large language
models (MLLMs) develop harness engineering around the MLLM, such as harnesses
with post-trained policies and recursive self-improvement loops. Yet whether
these additions exceed the native capability of the MLLM remains unexplored. We
introduce SimplEvo to explore how far an MLLM can improve under direct control
when it retains its execution experience in self-written text memories. It
requires no weight updates, post-trained policies, or privileged information.
The MLLM writes a coordinate-free task memory once from a single demonstration.
Under a strict train-test protocol, it then creates a bounded working memory on
training episodes only and refines it after each episode with lessons on
procedure, completion, and recovery. Both memories stay fixed at test time. On
EBench, SimplEvo lifts the success rate (SR) of GPT-6-Astra under direct control
from 46.7% to 81.7%, and tops the RoboDojo leaderboard with 69.63% SR and a
score of 75.32. Compared with memories that also store past visual frames,
SimplEvo reaches a similar SR with text memory alone while using fewer tokens.
With its memories fixed, SimplEvo also generalizes to unseen layouts and
randomized scene configurations. Moreover, the working memory transfers across
models: written by GPT-6-Astra, it can be reused as is by weaker MLLMs and
improve their performance without any adaptation of their own.
