Title: SurveyWeave: An Agentic Framework for Automated Survey Generation
Authors: Franklin Zhang, Xiao Yang, Xin Luna Dong, Juhua Hu
ID: 2611.19029
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.19029

Abstract:
Surveys help researchers keep up with fast-moving fields, but they take weeks to
months to write. Automated survey generation (AutoSurvey) can alleviate this
situation, but it is challenging to draw on hundreds to thousands of precisely
scoped papers, choose among many valid structures, and stay faithful to its
sources while offering insights. We present SurveyWeave, an agentic architecture
that decomposes the problem into four tasks coordinated by three agents, each
reviewing others' work and conducting revisions based on feedback. We also
introduce WeaveEval, a five-dimension evaluation grounded in prior surveys. A
comprehensive comparison shows that SurveyWeave outperforms Codex by 8 points
and the strongest AutoSurvey baseline by 6 points.
