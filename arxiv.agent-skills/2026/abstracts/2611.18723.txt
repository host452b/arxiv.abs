Title: Acting by Intent, Reacting by Sight: Visual Conditioned Reflexes for Real-Time VLM Agents
Authors: Qiancheng Zhou, Wenhua Zhang
ID: 2611.18723
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18723

Abstract:
Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown strong capabilities as generalist
agents that perceive, reason, and act in interactive environments. Yet real-time
environments such as video games demand both deliberate control for exploration
and split-second reactions to fast-moving hazards. Existing agents struggle to
handle both: relying solely on direct VLM actions is too slow for sudden events,
whereas delegating execution to fixed scripts leaves the agent blind at the
moment of action, leading to frequent misfires whenever the scene shifts. Humans
overcome this by pairing normal conscious control with conditioned reflexes-
while the mind handles deliberate actions and shapes reflex rules, the reflex
itself still relies on live vision to make the final instant decision. Emulating
this mechanism, we propose ViCoR (Visual Conditioned Reflex). Alongside normal
gameplay actions, the main VLM defines and updates lightweight situational
guidance for recurring events, while a fast visual reflex branch (invoking the
VLM in no-thinking mode) inspects live local views with motion cues to make the
final sub-second action decisions. By offloading 67.8% of high-frequency
reactions to the reflex branch (680 ms per check), ViCoR reduces average per-
action decision latency by 2.6x (from 14.0 s to 5.4 s) and cuts token
consumption by 36.9%. Across 17 diverse 2D and 3D games in the GameWorld
benchmark, ViCoR achieves state-of-the-art performance, improving task success
rate by +11.8% (from 52.9% to 64.7%) and average normalized progress by +13.9%
(from 66.5% to 80.4%) over the strongest deliberative VLM and script baselines.
In fast-paced real-time games, ViCoR refines its reflexes across episodes to
boost progress by 1.67x, and even with a low-effort main planner, surpasses
high-effort deliberative baselines by +8.1% at one-third of the token cost.
