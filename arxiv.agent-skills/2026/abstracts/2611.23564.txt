Title: LProp: Propagating Latent Uncertainty through Evidence-Conditioned Belief Revision in LLM Agents
Authors: Kaiwen Shi, Zheyuan Zhang, Weixiang Sun, Han Bao, Colby Nelson, Yanfang Ye
ID: 2611.23564
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23564

Abstract:
When LLM agents pass intermediate judgments downstream without uncertainty,
later decisions may treat tentative assumptions as facts. Effective uncertainty
propagation must preserve information for correction and update inherited
beliefs as new evidence arrives. Yet scalar scores omit information needed for
revision, while detailed descriptions and reflection incur additional costs. We
introduce Latent Uncertainty, a latent-space estimator that preserves
uncertainty-relevant information without lengthy descriptions. Building on this,
we propose LProp (Latent Uncertainty Propagation), which revises inherited
answer distributions using latent representations and new evidence without
additional trajectory sampling. We evaluate both methods across multiple-choice,
yes/no, open-ended question answering, and mathematical reasoning tasks. Our
approach outperforms baselines on most metrics, while LProp achieves the lowest
mean inference-token usage in all eight propagation settings. Matched feature
ablations further demonstrate that inherited latent content improves error
recovery beyond answer probabilities or model capacity alone.
