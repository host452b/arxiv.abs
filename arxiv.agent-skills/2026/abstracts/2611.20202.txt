Title: DAPPT: Domain-Aware Policies from Procedural Trajectories for Sim-to-Real Dexterous Tool Use
Authors: Beining Han, Jia Deng
ID: 2611.20202
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20202

Abstract:
Sim-to-real RL has achieved impressive results in dexterous manipulation but
remains limited to tasks with accurate rigid-body simulation. However, these
methods cannot be applied to skills that involve deformable and particles, with
which the simulation speed will become too slow for large-scale RL learning. We
present DAPPT, a sim-to-real framework for end-to-end dexterous tool-use
manipulation skills, e.g., sand scooping, play-dough rod-rolling, without
simulating non-rigid materials. Our method learns a domain-aware RL policy by
tracking procedural trajectories parameterized by domain parameters, and
distills it into an end-to-end domain-aware sensorimotor policy. Furthermore,
explicit domain represen-tation enables fast selection of domain parameters that
can maximize downstream task performance. We evaluate DAPPT end-to-end on six
dexterous manipulation tasks, including sand scooping, play-dough rod-rolling,
sand scraping, mouse-wheel scrolling, dispenser pressing, and spray triggering.
Our method achieves 1.5-3.8x in task metrics through the best domain parameter
and transfers zero-shot to the real world.
