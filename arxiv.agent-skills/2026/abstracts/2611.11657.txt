Title: Inside the Trading Loop: Markets, Models, and Moves with LLM Agents
Authors: Renee Jia, Di Mu, Yao Yu, Tianrui Liu
ID: 2611.11657
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11657

Abstract:
We built a hybrid multi-agent trading system that combines large language model
(LLM) analysts with momentum-based portfolio construction and rule-based
execution. Company-news and macroeconomic agents return structured reports to a
coordinator that sets stock weights and cash reserves. We evaluate the portfolio
engine using its broker-recorded paper account, which operated with
deterministic fallbacks, and compare LLM-enabled configurations through
retrospective replays. From March 11 to August 28, 2026, the account returned
34.5%, compared with 14.4% for the S&P 500 fund SPY over the same dates. This
raw return advantage came with a larger maximum drawdown. Reconstructed policies
also finish above matched SPY and QQQ references when assessments are used in
the same session or deferred to the next. Design comparisons examine cash
authority, selective invocation, and response contracts. A constant-cash control
gives a return estimate close to stance-based sizing, and shorter report
contracts improve observed parsing but can change recommendations.
