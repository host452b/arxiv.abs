Title: Direct Feedback Policy Optimization for AI Coding Agents
Authors: Fei Ding
ID: 2611.29193
URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.29193

Abstract:
AI coding agents generate, modify, and validate code through multi-turn
interactions. Existing reinforcement learning methods often rely on sparse
terminal rewards, making it difficult to assess how intermediate decisions
affect task outcomes, and group-relative methods also require repeated sampling
for each task. We propose Direct Feedback Policy Optimization (DFPO), a single-
trajectory reinforcement learning algorithm that trains directly from a single
trajectory. DFPO achieves higher task resolution rates than GSPO under matched
base models and execution harnesses.
